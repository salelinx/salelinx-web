# Version 1.1.7

Ten days of work: presets everywhere, a long pass on Depop relisting, and
some honesty about what the panel is doing.

## New

- **Presets follow your account.** Saved presets on every settings panel now
  sync through your SaleLinx account, so a reinstall or a second computer
  picks them up. Relist and Auto-Accept gained the same preset row the other
  tabs already had.
- **Page animations toggle.** A button in the panel header turns the page
  animations off and on, and shares its state with the Display setting.
- **Relist updates live.** Finished items un-grey and their replacements
  appear as a run progresses instead of all at once at the end, and Linking
  has an Include sold toggle that filters both columns and the recommended
  pairs.
- **Restocker shows restock counts** and a feed that reflects what actually
  happened, and it now checks Depop on every cycle.
- **A Manage account link** in Settings takes you straight to your account
  page.

## Fixed

- **Depop relisting.** Relisting a sold Depop item now recovers its size,
  fills in the product type, and creates the listing correctly. Draft edits
  are written where Depop keeps them, the editor shows the live listing rather
  than the cloud copy, and the quantity field is prefilled with the real
  number.
- **Depop stock.** An item with stock 0 is shown as Sold out rather than
  Hidden, sold listings are locked out of the editor, and a quantity change
  is written into the variants Depop reads.
- **Vinted details.** The editor brand picker searches Vinted's own brand
  list, condition is sent when crosslisting to Vinted, and when Vinted refuses
  to extend a posting deadline the panel says why.
- **My Listings refreshes after each crosslist**, so the new listing shows
  without a manual refresh, and the grid updates reused rows correctly after
  a bulk edit.
- **Refresh All says the real count** and greys out when nothing is due. The
  Stop control is back on the Refresh progress bar.
- **Auto-Offers needs a linked Vinted account**, and now says so instead of
  showing an empty tab.
- Smaller fixes: the crosslist toolbar no longer jumps when you switch
  filters, the pagination bar stays put so the search bar stops moving, and
  drag-select no longer drags card images.

## Improved

- **A cloud outage banner.** If our servers are unreachable the panel shows a
  banner, and the login screen makes clear you have not been signed out.
- **Crosslist grid.** Platform filters switch without a round trip, the grid
  fades between filters, and every marketplace logo tile is rounded.
- The relist prompts are stacked and their choices have icons.

## Plans and billing

- **New accounts start on a 14-day trial** rather than a free plan. A trial
  needs a card and moves onto a paid plan when it ends.
- **A failed renewal payment keeps your access for seven days** while the
  card is retried. A trial whose first payment fails ends straight away.
