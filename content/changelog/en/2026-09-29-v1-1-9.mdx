# Version 1.1.9

A quick follow-up to 1.1.8 that puts sign-in right, with a week of currency,
Price Drops and shipping label work alongside it.

## New

- **Your own currency everywhere.** SaleLinx now works out your shop's
  currency from your listings and uses it on every tab, including the
  animations, threshold inputs and empty states that used to show a fixed £
  or $. It knows 16 currencies, covering every Vinted market, and puts the
  symbol after the amount where your currency does (12,00 zł).
- **A look-back window for completed orders** on the Labels tab: 7, 14, 30,
  60 or 90 days, saved as soon as you pick it. Narrowing it is instant, and
  widening it only fetches the orders you have not loaded yet.

## Fixed

- **Signing in works again.** Version 1.1.8 from the Chrome Web Store could
  not reach our servers, so the panel said it was not configured and would
  not let you sign in. 1.1.9 restores it.
- **Vinted labels without a default address.** If none of your saved Vinted
  addresses was marked as the default, every label order was refused.
  SaleLinx now picks from your address list instead, preferring one with a
  phone number.
- **Price Drops dates.** A listing older than its own drop interval showed
  every step of its schedule as due now. The first drop is now counted from
  today, which matches how drops really run: one per listing per run, then a
  full interval before the next.
- **Prices in the wrong currency.** The relist overlay showed dollars for any
  currency outside five majors, so Polish and Swedish sellers saw their real
  prices with a $ sign, and some tabs said £ while others said $.
- **Dead Stock totals.** The capital banner added up only your most common
  currency while counting every item, so £300 and €500 across 18 items read
  as "£300 stuck in 18 items". It now shows one total per currency.
- **Auto-Offers without a linked Vinted account** now blurs the whole tab,
  stats included. The stats used to stay readable and could show
  real-looking numbers from before you unlinked.
- The completed orders list no longer goes blank when it cannot load. It
  keeps the orders it has, and says why it is empty when it is.

## Improved

- **A redesigned Price Drops row.** Each tracked listing reads as one
  sentence, "Drop 15% every 3 days down to £11", with its schedule drawn
  underneath as a ladder: the live price, the next drop highlighted, and the
  floor where the schedule ends. The main switch is now called Run price
  drops, to match the tab.
- **The Labels tab stops reloading itself.** Orders are cached for 20
  minutes, the cache survives the extension restarting in the background,
  and orders that have not changed are not fetched again, so going back to
  the tab is quick. The progress bar only counts work it is really doing.
- The Labels tab description now says it covers Depop as well as Vinted.
