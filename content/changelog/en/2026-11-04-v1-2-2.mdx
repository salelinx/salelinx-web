# Version 1.2.2

Answer eBay offers and messages from the panel, drop eBay prices on a
schedule, and stop an item selling twice.

## Offers on eBay

- **An eBay column.** eBay Best Offers sit beside Depop and Vinted. Accept,
  decline or counter without opening eBay.
- **Auto-Accept works on eBay.** Switch eBay on in its settings. eBay offers
  are checked at most once an hour.
- **Counters stay below your asking price.** The panel says Too high before
  you send.

## Messages on eBay

- **One inbox** for Depop, Vinted and eBay, with whoever is waiting first.
- **Reply to eBay buyers** with up to 5 photos, and see the photos they send.
- **Filters** for unread, read and each marketplace. A Needs reply tag marks
  the conversations waiting on you.

## Price Drops on eBay

- eBay listings saved to the cloud drop on the same schedule as Depop and
  Vinted.
- Save listings to the cloud straight from the Price Drops tab.
- Auctions are skipped, since their price can't drop.

## Sold once, removed everywhere

- When a linked listing sells, its other copies come down after the next
  sync. Depop is set to sold out, eBay is ended, and Vinted is hidden by
  default.
- A notice says what came down and anything that couldn't.
- Switch it off in the Linking tab's settings.

## Improved

- **A faster Offers tab.** Depop offers load in about a second instead of 15
  or more, each marketplace shows as soon as it answers, and your last offers
  appear straight away while new ones load.
- **A cleaner Offers tab,** with the price first on every offer, a single
  price box for counters, and a Start button for Auto-Accept with its
  settings beside it.
- **Select a range in My Listings.** Click a listing, then Shift-click
  another to select everything in between.
- **Bulk edit** can turn eBay offers on or off for many listings at once.
- **Updates arrive sooner.** SaleLinx checks for a new version when you open
  it.
- **Keyboard access** on the sign-in, trial and account linking screens, and
  clearer messages when linking fails.
- **Create listing:** arrow keys work in its dropdowns and colour pickers,
  and Escape closes an open dropdown first.

## Fixed

- A Depop offer could be missed when Depop's offer count was out of date.
- The marketplace popup could ask you to open eBay when an eBay tab was
  already open but asleep.
- Sync status could stay on Not synced. It now tries again.
- Turning page animations off now also stills the Linking animation.
- Photos in the listing editor no longer flicker while listings load.
