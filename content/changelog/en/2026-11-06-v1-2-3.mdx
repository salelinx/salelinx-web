# Version 1.2.3

See what isn't selling on one board, eBay included.

## Dead Stock on eBay

- **eBay listings beside Depop and Vinted.** Your fixed-price eBay listings
  are checked with the others, and there is an eBay filter. Auctions are left
  out, since they end on their own.
- **Watchers instead of likes.** eBay listings are judged on watchers and
  views, and the views cover the last 90 days. A dash means eBay has reported
  none.
- **Act on them from the panel.** -10% works on eBay listings without
  variations, Relist ends a listing and lists it again as new, and Delete ends
  it.
- **eBay on its own.** With only eBay switched on, the Dead Stock tab opens
  instead of showing Coming soon.

## A new Dead Stock board

- **Four columns:** Dead, Stale, Slow and Price dropped, oldest first. A
  headline says how many listings aren't selling, with the money tied up and
  the oldest one's age.
- **A whole column at once.** Relist all, -10% all and Delete all act on every
  listing in a column, and ask first. Listings on a marketplace with no tab
  open are left out, and the window says so.
- **Select several** by dragging across them or with Shift + click, then press
  Relist Selected.
- **Act from any page.** -10%, Relist and Delete work on every marketplace,
  not only the one you are browsing.
- **Dead Stock settings.** Set the days listed, likes (watchers on eBay) and
  views for each level and each marketplace, and whether either number or both
  have to be low. Save them as presets.

## Improved

- **Dark mode in Settings.** A Dark mode switch under Display. The sun and
  moon button at the top of the panel still works too.
- **Updates install themselves** once nobody is using SaleLinx: when nothing is
  running and no marketplace tab is open, or after 5 minutes away from the
  computer. Open marketplace tabs then reload, apart from a page where you are
  part-way through a listing.
- **Ended eBay listings in My Listings** show as Deleted or Auction ended, and
  the editor says End listing instead of Unlist.
- **The account linking screen** uses Continue and Log in, and says what is
  left to do before you can carry on.

## Fixed

- Deleted eBay listings could all come back in My Listings at once.
- An eBay listing that ended during a relist that then failed could disappear
  from My Listings.
- Dead Stock totals counted marketplaces you had switched off.
- A Messages reply refused for sending too many photos now keeps your text and
  photos, and says when you can try again.

## Plans and billing

- **Plan cards on the welcome screen.** A new account sees the free trial and
  Pro side by side, with each plan's allowances. When a plan ends, the screen
  shows Starter, Pro and Business.
- **Straight to checkout.** The plan buttons open checkout directly, or the
  pricing page if checkout can't open.
- **No Refresh needed.** Coming back to the tab after checkout unlocks the
  panel by itself. Refresh now says No plan found yet, or Couldn't check. Try
  again.
