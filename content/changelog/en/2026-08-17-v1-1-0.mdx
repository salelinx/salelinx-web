# Version 1.1.0

The first substantial update since launch. Google sign-in, a home for the
referral programme, and a set of fixes, one of which matters a lot if you
sell outside the UK.

## New

- **Sign in with Google.** No separate SaleLinx password to remember. Email
  and password still works exactly as before if you prefer it.
- **Refer &amp; Earn tab.** The referral programme now has its own tab in the
  panel: share your link, and see who has signed up and what you have earned
  without leaving the extension.
- **Reload listings buttons** on Relist and Dead Stock, so you can pull fresh
  data without switching tabs.
- **Open platform buttons.** Where the panel tells you a marketplace session is
  missing, the platform name is now a link that opens it.

## Fixed

- **Depop Messages.** Conversations that failed to load, failed silently, or
  showed blank avatars now work. This was the largest single fix in the
  release.
- **Crosslisting to Depop used the UK regardless of where you sell.** Every
  listing the extension created was stamped with a United Kingdom address and
  country, and sizes were resolved against the UK size set. For a US seller
  that meant a `US 8` could be published as a `UK 8`, which is two sizes
  smaller, with no warning. Listings now use your own country and size region,
  and where the region cannot be matched the size is left unset rather than
  guessed.
- **Account linking showed the wrong state.** The panel could report a
  marketplace as connected when the session had actually expired. It now
  distinguishes a live session from an expired one and tells you which.
- **Unlinked accounts are surfaced** across the panel rather than features
  failing quietly further down the line.
- **Price Drops** no longer keeps a stale selection after the list refreshes,
  and separates items that are not due for a drop yet from those that were
  skipped.
- **Shop Designer previews** now render inside phone and laptop mockups, so the
  mobile and desktop grids are easier to tell apart.

## Also

- The free trial is now **14 days**, up from 7, for new accounts.
- A concurrent-device limit now applies, so one subscription covers one person
  rather than a shared login.

## Updating

Chrome updates extensions automatically, usually within a few hours of a
release. When the update is staged you will see a banner in the panel with a
**Reload to update** button, which applies it immediately without restarting
your browser.
