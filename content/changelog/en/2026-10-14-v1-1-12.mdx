# Version 1.1.12

A smaller release on the way to eBay: price tools, steadier Vinted messages
and a new look.

## New

- **Bulk price changes.** Bulk Edit can set a new price, or raise or lower
  prices by an amount or a percentage.
- **Buying chats in Messages.** Conversations where you are the buyer now
  show too, tagged Buying.
- **A new logo.** The lynx mark appears in the panel, the toolbar popup and
  the toolbar icon.
- **A clear message when SaleLinx is down.** If our servers can't be
  reached, the whole panel says SaleLinx is temporarily down, with a Close
  SaleLinx button. It clears itself once the service is back.

## Improved

- **Crosslists are always saved as drafts.** The Auto-post switch is gone.
  Check your new Depop and Vinted drafts, then post them from Post drafts in
  My Listings.
- **Add from URL** shows a progress bar while it fetches, takes links
  separated by commas, and fails straight away for a listing that is no
  longer up instead of opening a tab. Closing the window cancels it.
- **One reload warning.** When a Depop or Vinted tab needs reloading, a
  single warning says so, with a button that reloads it for you. Failed syncs
  no longer pop up an alert.
- **No Vinted tab open?** My Listings now offers to open one, instead of
  asking you to reload a tab that isn't there.
- Refresh All and automatic refreshes skip marketplaces you have switched
  off.
- Items you change in the crosslist review are marked Edited.
- The Settings sections are now called SaleLinx account and Marketplace
  accounts, and the Page animations switch lives under Display.
- In a narrow window, fullscreen scales the panel down instead of squashing
  it.

## Fixed

- **Vinted Messages showing nothing.** When Vinted's security check lapsed,
  Messages said No new messages and Vinted features switched off. SaleLinx
  now reloads the Vinted tab once and tries again.
- Bulk-deleted rows no longer flash back as selectable just before they
  disappear.

## Plans and billing

- **Only crosslists that succeed count.** Each crosslist is counted as it
  lands, and your allowance is checked before every item, so a batch that
  reaches your monthly limit stops and keeps the rest for later. Failed items
  don't count towards your allowance.
