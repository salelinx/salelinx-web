# Version 1.1.2

A quality-of-life release. The headline change is for everyone who sells on
only one of the two marketplaces, and the rest is a broad polish pass across
the panel.

## New

- **Choose your marketplaces.** If you only sell on Depop or only on Vinted,
  you can now switch the other one off in Settings. The panel hides its
  features, stops asking you to link the account, and skips its background
  work entirely.
- **Support ticket replies.** You can now reply to your support tickets from
  the Support tab, including tickets that have already been closed, instead of
  switching to the website. The tab also links to the docs, FAQ, and status
  page.
- **Keyboard shortcut.** Alt+S opens and closes the panel. The shortcut is
  shown in Settings, where you can change it, and Escape now closes the
  topmost open dialog.
- **Offline notice.** If your connection drops, the panel now says so instead
  of letting actions fail quietly.

## Improved

- **Account linking.** You now pick which marketplace you are linking, see
  exactly which account is involved, and get a clear reason when a link is
  refused instead of a generic error.
- **Device switching is instant.** Signing in on a new device now signs the
  old one out right away, rather than the old device lingering until its next
  action.
- **Items queued in a bulk job are locked everywhere.** Something waiting in a
  Relist, Refresh, Crosslist, Dead Stock, Auto-Markdown, or Restocker queue is
  now marked as busy on every other surface, so the same item cannot be queued
  twice or edited mid-job.
- **A panel-wide consistency pass.** Unified colours, tidier dialogs, a
  smoother close animation, and readable messages where raw error codes used
  to appear. Upload failures now log the real reason.
- **Queue history** rows can be cleared one at a time, and Dead Stock
  refreshes itself after a relist run finishes.

## Also

- The panel no longer opens automatically in every new Depop or Vinted tab;
  open it with the toolbar button or Alt+S when you want it.
- New installs land on a short getting-started page.
- After 25 successful runs the extension asks, once, whether you would leave a
  review.
