# Version 1.2.4

Post eBay orders from the same list as Depop and Vinted.

## eBay in Labels

- **eBay orders waiting to be posted** sit in the same list as Depop and
  Vinted, with an eBay filter and a countdown to eBay's post-by date.
- **Label prices in the panel.** Get label shows eBay's drop-off services for
  your parcel size and weight, with their prices, the buyer's pick first, the
  arrival dates and what the buyer paid for postage. Open on eBay takes you to
  eBay's own label page to buy it.
- **Mark posted** tells eBay and the buyer the order is on its way, with a
  carrier and tracking number if you have them.
- **Cancel order** works on eBay orders too, with eBay's own reasons.

## Better labels

- **Vinted A4 labels are cropped** to the label itself for every carrier. This
  also fixes blank InPost prints after Vinted moved the label on the sheet.
  Relay QR codes print on a 4x6 slip.
- **Faster downloads** when you print several Vinted labels.
- Cropped labels carry a small SaleLinx mark under the label, as QR slips
  already did.
- The list shows 20, 50 or 100 orders a page, and the search box is bigger.

## Create listing from a draft

- **Start from a draft.** Select one draft in My Listings and click Create
  listing. The form opens with the draft's title, description, price, brand,
  condition, colours and photos, plus the category, size and parcel size of a
  Vinted draft.
- **Nothing lost.** If you had already started a listing, the form asks before
  replacing it.
- **The draft becomes the listing.** On Depop and Vinted the draft itself is
  used and stays linked to the new copies. An eBay draft is replaced.

## Offers

- **A new card layout,** with the buyer first, then the offer beside your
  crossed-out asking price and the percentage off.
- **Sort offers** by Best offers, Newest first or Oldest first.
- **Earlier** shows past offers and counters on Depop and Vinted.
- A counter typed as 12,50 is read as 12.50.

## Messages

- Vinted conversations show once the buyer has written something.
- Conversations about sold or deleted listings are left out.
- Unread conversations load first.
- A buyer who only says thanks or ok, or sends an emoji, is no longer marked
  Needs reply.

## Improved

- **Counts in the side menu** for Offers, Messages and Labels.
- **One Relist window for sold items** in My Listings, with Marketplaces,
  Changes and Cloud save, plus Save as draft. A sold eBay listing can be
  relisted with the rest of its linked group.
- **A sold linked listing's notice** shows its photo, a View on button for
  the marketplace it sold on, and a Take down button.
- **My Listings:** Filter & Sort is now Advanced, with Presets inside it,
  Hidden and Delayed sit behind a More statuses arrow, and photos have a Rotate
  90° button. Save to Cloud takes drafts too.
- **Post drafts** checks drafts faster, asks for a Depop size or brand when it
  needs one, and shows Depop's reason when a post fails.
- **Support** links to rating SaleLinx on the Chrome Web Store and reviewing
  it on Trustpilot.
- Brighter buttons and readable errors in dark mode, better keyboard and
  screen reader support in Offers, Labels and Relist, and proper singular and
  plural wording in every language.

## Fixed

- Depop drafts failed to save or post after a change on Depop's side.
- Cancelling a Vinted order failed.
- Label already ordered showed on every first Vinted label.
- Vinted drafts lost their brand and size in My Listings, and CSV import
  dropped the condition, size and brand on Vinted drafts.
- After an update, Vinted actions such as bulk delete could do nothing.
- A Depop description such as "Please ask for size" was read as a size.
- Create listing now says why a price is refused.
- The colour picker was cut off at the bottom of Create listing, and some
  colours, such as Turquoise, were dropped.
- New eBay orders could be hidden after a refresh, and missing photos showed
  as broken images.
