# SaleLinx launches

After a private beta, SaleLinx is available to everyone with a Chrome or
Chromium browser. This release ships the core crosslisting flow between
Depop and Vinted, the unified dashboard, the offer and message inbox, and
the Depop Shop Designer.

## What&rsquo;s in the first release

- **Bi-directional crosslisting** between Depop and Vinted, with auto-mapped
  categories, sizes, conditions and brands.
- **Unified dashboard** that merges listings from both marketplaces into one
  sortable grid with bulk actions.
- **Offer and message inbox** with rule-based auto-replies.
- **Listing refresher** with a configurable schedule (4h to 24h).
- **Shop Designer** drag-and-drop layout tool for the Depop shop profile.

## What&rsquo;s next

More crosslisting and automation features are in the works. See
