# Version 1.2.1

Make a listing once for all your marketplaces, and relist on eBay as well as
Depop and Vinted.

## Create listing

- **A new button in My Listings.** Fill in the photos, title, description,
  price and details once, pick your marketplaces, and SaleLinx makes the
  listing on each of them.
- **One category and size.** Choose them from the first marketplace's lists
  and the others are matched for you. Press Edit on a marketplace to correct
  its match.
- **Drafts first.** Listings are saved as drafts by default, so you can check
  them before they go live. You can switch that off to post straight away,
  and eBay's fee is shown first.
- **Back up and link.** On a plan with cloud sync, new listings can be backed
  up to the cloud and linked together.
- **Nothing to fill in twice.** The form keeps what you've entered, and if a
  marketplace fails, Try again makes only what's missing.

## Relist on eBay

- **eBay in the Relist tab.** eBay listings now sit alongside Depop and
  Vinted, and linked copies stay linked after a relist.
- **What you can change.** The title, description and price options work on
  eBay, and you can save the relist as a draft. The photo options stay Depop
  and Vinted only.
- **Auctions stay auctions,** with the same starting price, length and any Buy
  It Now price. If an auction has bids, the window warns you first, because
  relisting ends it early and cancels every bid.
- **Drafts keep their offers.** A relist saved as a draft keeps its Best Offer
  settings.
- **A new Relist window,** laid out like the crosslist review: options For
  every item, then a row per item with a tick to skip it and its own options
  and price.

## Improved

- **eBay auctions in My Listings.** A live auction shows as Active with an
  Auction tag beside its price. Hover over the tag for the bids, end date,
  starting price and Buy It Now price. To change an auction, the editor links
  you to eBay, and bulk changes skip it.
- **Post drafts lets you choose.** Each draft has a tick. Untick one to leave
  it as a draft.
- **One popup when a marketplace needs attention.** If a tab needs a
  marketplace that is closed, signed out or on the wrong account, a single
  popup shows what to fix, with a button for each. The tab carries on once
  everything is ready.
- **SaleLinx no longer opens eBay tabs by itself.** If it needs a signed-in
  eBay tab, it asks you to open one.
- **What's new after an update.** A short card shows what a release added,
  once per release.
- **More of the panel is translated** into French, Spanish and German,
  including the task queue, Grow, the upgrade window and tooltips. The toolbar
  popup follows your panel language.
- **Support** is now just a message to send, with no type to pick.
- **Shipping Labels** shows its filters and search while your orders load.

## Fixed

- **Depop shipping labels.** Depop moved its label files, which made every
  Depop label download fail. They work again. Chrome asks for permission the
  first time. If you decline, that download leaves out the Depop labels and
  keeps the Vinted ones.
- **eBay drafts going up as auctions.** A posted draft could become a 7-day
  auction with eBay's own postage. New eBay drafts are set to fixed price, and
  post with your saved postage when you have it saved.
- **Listings relisted by eBay showing twice.** When eBay relists an item, or
  you relist it on eBay, the row, cloud copy and links now move to the new
  listing.
- **Relist window.** Items ticked and then hidden by a filter no longer drop
  out. A saved bulk price change shows as soon as the window opens and can be
  cleared, and eBay prices are rounded properly.
- A draft picked through two selected rows in Post drafts now posts once.
- Photos in the Relist and Refresh tabs no longer blink when the list updates
  in the background.

## Plans and billing

- **Create listing and your crosslists.** The first marketplace a listing is
  made on is free. Each further one counts as a crosslist, checked against
  your allowance before it's made, and the form tells you if you don't have
  enough left.
- **Relist on eBay** uses the same monthly relist allowance.
