# Version 1.1.10

Two weeks of work, most of it on crosslisting between Depop and Vinted.

## New

- **Review before you crosslist.** The Crosslist button now opens a review
  list before anything is posted. Edit any item to change its title,
  description and price, or its category, size, brand, condition and colours
  in the target platform's own terms. Your edits are sent with the batch;
  the old single-item preview threw them away.
- **Add from URL takes many links.** Paste as many as you like. Each item
  joins the list as soon as it is fetched, and links that fail stay in the
  box. Vinted links are read straight from the page instead of loading a
  background tab, which is faster and picks up the category, condition and
  colours too.
- **A floating Crosslist button** docks at the bottom right once the toolbar
  scrolls out of view, with the same count and target.
- **Breaks in the queue popup.** When a job pauses for a break or a rate
  limit, its status says so with a countdown to when it resumes, and pauses
  that cover a whole platform get a chip in the summary.
- **Dead Stock rests a listing after a price drop.** After a -10% drop the
  listing moves to a new Price dropped filter for seven days, out of the
  tiers, Worst Performers, counts and capital banner, with a chip showing
  the old and new price and the days left.
- **Listing times.** The Listed column in My Listings shows the time under
  the date.

## Fixed

- **Vinted to Depop categories.** Every Vinted catalog now maps to an exact
  Depop category, instead of a guess from the wording. The title is read
  before the description, so a blazer no longer lists as a jumper because
  its description says "knitwear".
- **Vinted to Depop sizes.** Combined labels like "M / UK 12-14" match the
  right size, one-size items such as bags and books get One size, kids items
  are sized by age, and men's chest sizes like 42R fall back to the matching
  letter.
- **Depop to Vinted titles, prices and brands.** Titles are cut to Vinted's
  100 characters at a sentence or word break, and toned down when written
  mostly in capitals. Prices are converted to your Vinted account's currency
  (a $75 item used to list at £75). Brands are matched more loosely, so
  "levi-s" finds Levi's, and Depop's Like new now maps to Very good rather
  than New without tags.
- **Depop drafts** post with their own photos instead of re-uploading them,
  kids drafts post as kids listings, and a draft priced in another currency
  is converted at the day's rate.
- **My Listings keeps categories** through background syncs. A Vinted sync
  used to wipe the category a crosslist had set.
- **No more false "not linked" or "sold".** A failed check no longer tells
  you your accounts are not linked, and after an update, listings from a
  platform with no open tab show Not synced instead of being marked sold.
- **Your Depop tab is left alone** when a background read fails. It used to
  reload, wiping whatever you were doing in it.
- The My Listings sort arrow sits beside its column label again.

## Improved

- **Drag-select scrolls.** Hold a selection near the top or bottom of a list
  and it scrolls, faster the closer you get to the edge, selecting rows as
  they pass. This works in My Listings, Relist, Refresh and Crosslist.
- **A cleaner start.** The link-accounts screen and marketplace picker share
  the welcome screen's style, and the tutorial popup at login is gone.
- **A new toolbar icon**: the SaleLinx lynx on a transparent background.
- **A choice about marketing email.** Signing up in the extension offers an
  unticked box to opt out of tips and offers. On the website, signup asks
  yes or no, and your account page has an Email preferences card where you
  can change your answer.

## Plans and billing

- **A welcome screen instead of a padlock.** A new account without a plan
  now sees Welcome to SaleLinx and a button to start the 14-day free trial,
  rather than "This account has no active plan". The panel also waits for
  your plan to load, so the wrong screen no longer flashes up first.
- **Ended plans are worded correctly.** The screen after a plan ends told
  paying customers their free trial had ended. It now says your plan has
  ended, with a See plans button.
- **Changing plan from the pricing page** takes subscribers straight to the
  billing portal, rather than back to the account page with no explanation.
