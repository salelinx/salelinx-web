# Version 1.1.8

A short release: drafts join the crosslist picker, settings get a cleaner
layout, and a round of category, size and brand fixes.

## New

- **Crosslist Depop drafts to Vinted.** Drafts now appear in the crosslist
  picker with a badge, and a draft source always creates a draft on Vinted.
  Any field a draft is missing is a warning, not a failure, so a half-filled
  draft crosslists with whatever it has.
- **An All / Active / Drafts filter** on the crosslist picker, next to the
  link filter, with its own empty states.

## Fixed

- **Categories come from Depop.** Crosslisting now matches category slugs
  against Depop's own list instead of a legacy id, tops get a category group,
  and if a category cannot be resolved the panel says so rather than creating
  a listing with no category.
- **Sizes and brands.** The Vinted size is read from where Vinted keeps it,
  the Depop size is sent as the variants map Depop reads, and a brand lookup
  tries both apostrophe forms before giving up.
- **My Listings updates after a crosslist batch.** The panel polls the
  platform you wrote to once the batch finishes, so new listings show without
  pressing Refresh All.
- A Vinted listing with no condition is now flagged, and the price badge no
  longer shows through the queued pill.

## Improved

- **Redesigned settings panels.** The preset dropdown and Save button are
  replaced by one small pill at the top right of each panel. Its menu lists
  your presets and, once you edit a value, offers Save changes, Save as new
  or Revert. Rows are grouped under short captions, and the forms no longer
  stretch across the page on Refresh and Relist.
- The draft marker and filter on the crosslist picker match the rest of the
  panel.

## Plans and billing

- **Trials and complimentary periods end on their end date.** A trial that
  has passed its end date no longer keeps its allowances while the plan
  change catches up.
