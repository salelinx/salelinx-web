# Version 1.1.11

A short release: Vinted editing fixes and a round of visual polish.

## Fixed

- **Vinted size and condition edits stick.** Vinted accepted a size change
  from the quick editor and then ignored it. Size and condition are now
  saved where Vinted reads them.
- **Vinted brands stay put.** Saving a quick edit without touching the brand
  could make Vinted drop it or match a different one. The brand is now sent
  back exactly as it was.
- **Sizeless categories.** Bags, homeware and other categories without sizes
  show Not available instead of a size list.
- **Parcel sizes fit the category.** The editor lists each category's own
  parcel sizes, a bulk Small, Medium or Large maps onto that category's
  scale, and publishing keeps your draft's size when the category allows it,
  otherwise the smallest it offers.
- **Depop "everything else" items** get their category when crosslisted.
- **Bulk jobs release each listing as they go**, rather than holding every
  listing until the whole job ends.

## Improved

- **Faster cloud saves.** Photo backups no longer pause between photos.
- **One Cloud button** in the listings toolbar opens Save, Remove and
  Update. Remove asks first, then deletes the cloud copy and its photo
  backups.
- **A calmer toolbar popup** in neutral tones, and a toolbar icon that
  follows Chrome's light or dark mode. The icon updates once a Depop or
  Vinted tab loads or you open the popup.
- **A how-it-works card for Crosslist**: three points and a short animation
  (tick items, press Crosslist, drafts appear).
- "Failed to load listings" now shows as a proper card, and the sign-out and
  unlink dialogs cover the whole panel, sidebar included.
- The brand picker lists Zara, Nike, H&M, Adidas and Shein first, and the
  arrows on price inputs move by 1.

## Plans and billing

- **Start your plan before the trial ends.** During a trial, your account
  page lets you start paying for your plan straight away. It shows the price
  before charging, and if your card is declined you can update it and try
  again.
- **A confirmation page** after checkout or a plan change says your plan has
  started and what to do next.
- **Signing out of the website** no longer signs you out of the extension or
  your other devices.
