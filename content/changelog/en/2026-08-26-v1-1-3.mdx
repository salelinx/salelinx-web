# Version 1.1.3

A correctness release, mostly aimed at the Restocker and at what happens when
a browser tab is signed into a different marketplace account than the one
linked to SaleLinx.

## Fixed

- **Restocker stock handling.** Restock quantities now count as remaining
  stock, quantities are converted correctly on the Depop side, and the final
  item in stock is no longer relisted after it sells. Several sale-detection
  bugs across backlog and live modes are fixed too.
- **Account mismatch no longer blocks the whole tab.** If a tab is signed into
  a different marketplace account than the one linked to SaleLinx, you now get
  a warning on each affected feature, per platform, instead of the entire tab
  being walled off. Listings picked up from a mismatched account are ignored
  rather than mixed into your data.
- **Price Drops** no longer acts on stale cloud data; two bugs found while
  building out its test coverage are fixed.
- **Offers** opens on the marketplace you are currently viewing, and a column
  ordering bug is fixed.

## Improved

- **Refer &amp; Earn.** The leaderboard is gone. In its place, a clear
  explanation of what you earn, when it is granted, and where it is applied.
