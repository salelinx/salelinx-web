# Version 1.1.6

A fortnight of work: a big pass on Depop editing, plus stability and design.

## New

- **See what you have used.** Membership now shows every allowance with an
  icon, a bar and how much is left, so you can tell at a glance how much of
  your plan is spent.
- **Crosslisting is no longer capped** by how many listings you select, and
  the listings grid gained a shared toolbar and a faster bulk editor.
- **Support tickets collapse**, and every queued job can now be cancelled from
  See more, with a confirmation before anything is removed.

## Fixed

- **Depop editing.** Brand, category, size, department, colour and condition
  all save correctly again.
- **Expired shipping labels are hidden.** Depop leaves sold orders marked as
  awaiting shipment indefinitely, so months-old orders offering a label that
  will no longer scan were still showing in the tab.
- **The extension no longer stops working under load.** A caching bug meant
  accounts with nothing linked queried our servers on every panel render,
  which could exhaust the database and stop anyone signing in.
- Vinted account checks now read the live session, and the shop designer falls
  back to an initial-letter avatar when a profile has no picture.

## Improved

- The panel opens and closes with a cleaner fade.
- The Grow tab was redesigned, the shipping payout card now leads with the
  total, and the Offers, Auto-Offers and Crosslist animations were reworked.
- Critical updates can now apply themselves while the extension is idle,
  instead of waiting for a browser restart.
