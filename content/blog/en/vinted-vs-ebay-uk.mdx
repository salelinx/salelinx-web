Vinted is better for everyday clothes and kidswear you want gone quickly, and eBay for anything with a collector, a model number or a price above about £50. Both leave a UK private seller the full price they set, so the choice is about who buys, how long it takes and how much work each sale is.

- **Electronics, games, collectables, trainers, books and anything people search by model number:** eBay.

## Vinted vs eBay: what does each cost?

**Neither charges a UK private seller a selling fee. The buyer pays on both.**

- **Vinted:** sellers pay nothing. Buyers pay a Vinted fee at checkout, &ldquo;usually 3% to 8% + £0.3 to £0.8&rdquo; per Vinted&rsquo;s [price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist) (last updated 8 October 2026). See our [Vinted fees guide](/blog/vinted-fees-uk).
- **eBay:** private sellers pay no selling fee and get 300 free listings a month, then 35p each ([private seller fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4364)). Buyers pay a [Buyer Protection fee](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594) of 10p plus 7% of the price up to £20, then 4% of the part from £20 to £300. See our [eBay fees guide](/blog/ebay-fees-uk).

On a £20 item a private seller keeps £20 on either. The buyer pays £21.50 on eBay and £20.90 to £22.40 on Vinted. Fees do not separate them; the audience does.

**If you buy stock to resell, eBay expects a business account,** and then you pay the fee instead of the buyer: 11.9% on clothing, 0.35% regulatory fee, and 30p or 40p per order, all before VAT ([business seller fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/business-selling-fees?id=4809)). Vinted has no equivalent charge, so for a reseller the gap is wider.

## Which has better buyer protection?

**Vinted closes the book in days; eBay leaves a month open.**

- **Vinted:** a buyer has 2 days after Vinted says the parcel should have arrived to press &ldquo;I have an issue&rdquo;. If they do not, the order completes. Change of mind is the seller&rsquo;s call: [returning an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/272) says &ldquo;it&rsquo;s the seller&rsquo;s choice whether to accept the return&rdquo;, and the buyer pays return postage.
- **eBay:** almost every sale paid through checkout is covered by the [Money Back Guarantee](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/ebay-money-back-guarantee-policy/ebay-money-back-guarantee-policy?id=4210). A buyer has 30 calendar days from the estimated delivery date to report an item that has not arrived, and 30 days from delivery to return one that is not as described. Writing &ldquo;no returns&rdquo; does not stop that: the buyer can return an item that is faulty, damaged or not as described &ldquo;even if the seller doesn&rsquo;t offer returns&rdquo;, and the seller pays the return postage.

A casual Vinted seller rarely sees a claim. eBay suits buyers of higher-value items and costs you more if you describe badly. Photograph every flaw on either.

## How does postage differ?

**Vinted is less thinking. eBay gives more control and more ways to get the parcel size wrong.**

- **Vinted:** [the buyer pays for shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753). You get a prepaid label to print, or a QR code to show at a drop-off point. You never set a postage price.
- **eBay:** private sellers use [Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575) for eligible items priced £750 or less. Carriers are Evri, Royal Mail, DHL and InPost, and the label is valid for 7 days. You choose whether you or the buyer pays. Free postage can make a listing more attractive, but the label cost comes out of what you keep, so price it in.

## Which pays out faster?

**An established eBay seller can be paid faster than anyone on Vinted. A new one is on about the same timetable.**

- **Vinted:** the order completes when the buyer confirms all is well, or 2 days after delivery. You &ldquo;will receive your payment within 2 days&rdquo; of that ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/235)). Withdrawing to your bank can then take up to 5 business days, with a £0.10 minimum ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/68)).
- **eBay:** for private sellers, funds typically become available 2 days after delivery on tracked items, and 14 calendar days after the order date on untracked ones. Sellers with at least 10 completed sales worth £150 or more in the last 5 years, and no more than 2 open requests, cases or defects in the last 12 months, typically get paid within 24 hours of the buyer paying ([eBay](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814)).

## Who buys, and what sells where?

This is general guidance from how the two work, not a published figure.

- **Vinted** is a search box with filters. Buyers type a brand and a size and buy at the price shown. Clothes, shoes, kidswear in bundles, handbags and homeware sell well, and so does anything priced to go.
- **eBay** buyers often search by exact name or model number and compare. That suits consoles, games, electronics, trainers, LEGO, books, collectables, parts, and branded items where size or condition is less of a guess.

## Auctions or fixed price?

**Only eBay has auctions.** In an [auction](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/listings/creating-managing-listings/choosing-selling-format?id=4107) you choose a starting price and buyers bid; with Buy It Now a buyer purchases straight away. An auction can beat your price on something rare you cannot value, and a fixed price is safer for something common. Vinted sells at your price, with buyers free to send an offer. Nothing to time or watch.

## Which takes more effort?

**Vinted is faster to list; eBay repays the extra work on higher-value items.**

- **Vinted:** photos, a title, brand, size and condition, and short category fields. Sales are small and frequent.
- **eBay:** item specifics, a decent title and a parcel size ([item specifics guide](/blog/ebay-item-specifics)). One sale covers a lot of effort.

## Which should I use for my stuff?

- **Everyday clothes and shoes:** Vinted. No fee for you, a short problem window and buyers who search for exactly this. Bundle the cheap items.
- **Kidswear:** Vinted, in bundles by age and season.
- **Games, consoles, electronics:** eBay. Buyers search by model, and you can state condition and what is included. Test and photograph it first.
- **Collectables, LEGO, books, vinyl:** eBay, where buyers look for the exact item. Try an auction if you cannot value it.
- **Branded mid-range clothes and trainers:** both, or Vinted first and eBay if it has not sold.
- **Anything over about £50:** check eBay sold prices first. A higher price on Vinted is possible, but offers tend to come in low.
- **Stock you bought to resell:** read eBay&rsquo;s business seller rules and HMRC&rsquo;s guidance first. Our [tax guide](/blog/tax-on-vinted-and-depop-sales) covers where the line is.

## Can I sell on both?

**Yes, but end the other listing the moment one sells.** eBay&rsquo;s [selling practices policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/selling-policies/selling-practices-policy?id=4346) says not to list an item you are simultaneously selling outside eBay without ensuring you have enough product available. For one-off items, that means ending the other listing straight away. See [selling the same item twice](/blog/stop-selling-same-item-twice) for a routine, and our [crosslisting apps guide](/blog/best-crosslisting-apps-uk) for tools.

## Questions

### Is Vinted or eBay better for selling clothes?

Vinted, for most everyday clothes. Sellers pay nothing, buyers search by brand and size, and the problem window is short. eBay suits branded or vintage items that people search for by name, and anything worth more than about £50.

### Does Vinted have lower fees than eBay?

For a private seller, both are free to sell on. Vinted buyers pay usually 3% to 8% plus £0.30 to £0.80, and eBay buyers pay 10p plus 7% of the first £20 of the price. If you sell as a business on eBay you pay 11.9% on clothing plus other fees, before VAT, which Vinted does not charge.

### Which pays out faster, Vinted or eBay?

An established eBay seller can be paid within 24 hours of the buyer paying, if they meet eBay&rsquo;s conditions. Otherwise, eBay funds on tracked items arrive 2 days after delivery. Vinted pays within 2 days of the order completing, then a bank withdrawal takes up to 5 business days.

### Can I sell the same item on Vinted and eBay?

Yes, but eBay&rsquo;s policy says you must have enough stock available when you sell elsewhere at the same time. For a one-off item, end the other listing the moment it sells, or you may have to cancel an order.

### Is eBay or Vinted safer for sellers?

Both protect the buyer and have a process for problems. Vinted&rsquo;s window is 2 days from delivery notification. eBay&rsquo;s Money Back Guarantee leaves 30 days, and a buyer can return an item that is not as described even if you offer no returns. Describing and photographing flaws is your best protection on either.
