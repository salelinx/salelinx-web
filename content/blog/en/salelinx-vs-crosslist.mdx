## Where Crosslist is stronger

- **More marketplaces,** including Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Grailed, Facebook
  Marketplace, Shopify and WooCommerce.
- **Mobile apps** on iOS and Android.
- **Delisting without your computer on** for Depop, eBay, Etsy, Shopify and
  WooCommerce. Crosslist detects those sales in the cloud.
- **Higher caps:** 200 new listings a month on its cheapest plan.

## Where SaleLinx is stronger

- **Vinted for new accounts.** SaleLinx crosslists between Vinted, Depop and
  eBay in any direction, and delists from Vinted when an item sells.
- **The EU.** SaleLinx is sold worldwide and priced in euros as well as
  pounds and dollars.
- **Price.** SaleLinx starts at $9.99 or £7.99 a month, against $29.99 for
  Crosslist&rsquo;s Bronze plan. Delisting needs SaleLinx Pro at $18.99, or
  Crosslist Gold at $39.99.
- **Routine jobs on Depop and Vinted:** relisting, refreshing Depop listings,
  offers to likers, price drops and shipping labels.

## Delisting when an item sells

- **Crosslist** detects Depop and eBay sales in the cloud, so your computer can
  be off. Vinted, Mercari, Poshmark and others need the extension with Chrome
  open. It skips multi-variant listings, and Vinted listings must have a
  quantity of 1.
- **SaleLinx** checks Depop and Vinted about every 5 minutes, with a tab open
  for each, and eBay about every 15, while Chrome is open. It works on listings
  you have [linked](/docs/automate/linking) as the same item.

## Who should not pick SaleLinx

- **You already have Vinted connected on Crosslist** and also sell on Etsy,
  Poshmark or Mercari. Crosslist covers more of your shops.
- **You want sales detected with your computer off.** Crosslist does that for
  Depop and eBay. SaleLinx needs Chrome open.
- **You list mostly from your phone.** SaleLinx needs a desktop or laptop.

## Questions

### Can new Crosslist accounts use Vinted?

No. Crosslist's Vinted FAQ says that since 23 September 2026 new accounts can no longer connect Vinted. Accounts that already had Vinted connected keep it.

### What is a Crosslist alternative for Vinted?

SaleLinx crosslists between Vinted, Depop and eBay and delists from Vinted when an item sells. List Perfectly also lists Vinted, as a LITE marketplace without automatic sale detection. Vendoo does not list Vinted.

### Does Crosslist work in the EU?

No. Crosslist's pricing page says it supports the UK, US, Canada and Australia and does not support users residing in the European Union.
