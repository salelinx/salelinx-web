## Marketplaces: the deciding question

Vendoo&rsquo;s help centre lists eBay, Etsy, Depop, Whatnot and Facebook
Marketplace for its UK and EU customers. Vinted is not on that list, and
Vendoo&rsquo;s UK pricing page does not mention it either.

SaleLinx does two marketplaces: Depop and Vinted. You can copy a listing from
Depop to Vinted or from Vinted to Depop, and the category, size, brand,
condition and colour are translated into what the other site expects. Copies
are saved as drafts, so you can check them before they go live. See the
[crosslisting guide](/docs/inventory/crosslist) for exactly how it works.

So the honest split is this. If eBay or Etsy is a big part of your business,
SaleLinx does not cover them and Vendoo does. If Vinted is a big part of your
business, Vendoo&rsquo;s UK site does not list it and SaleLinx covers it.

## Pricing

Both tools charge in pounds in the UK, but they price in different ways.

- **Vendoo** charges by the number of new items you add each month. When we
  checked, its UK plans started at £16.99 a month for 125 new items, with a
  free plan for 5 items a month.
- **SaleLinx** has three monthly plans: Starter at <PlanPrice tier="starter" />,
  Pro at <PlanPrice tier="pro" /> and Business at <PlanPrice tier="business" />.
  Each plan includes a monthly allowance for crosslisting and relisting, and a
  daily one for things like follows and Depop refreshes. There is no free plan,
  but there is a 14-day free trial of Starter. The current allowances are on
  the [pricing page](/pricing).

The plans are not like for like, so compare them on what you would actually
use. If you list on several marketplaces, Vendoo&rsquo;s wider coverage may be
worth paying for. If you mainly want to keep a Depop and Vinted shop moving,
check that the SaleLinx allowances cover your volume and compare from there.

## How you use it

Vendoo runs in a browser and also has iOS and Android apps, so you can list
from your phone.

SaleLinx is a Chrome extension. It opens as a panel next to Depop and Vinted on
your computer and does its work in the background while those sites are open.
There is no phone app. If you do all your listing on your phone, that is a real
point in Vendoo&rsquo;s favour.

## Automation for Depop and Vinted

This is where the two tools differ most. Vendoo is a crosslister first. Its UK
plans list bulk delisting and relisting and reminders for stale listings, but
not offer or sharing automation.

SaleLinx is built around keeping a Depop and Vinted shop busy once the listings
exist:

- **On every plan:** [crosslisting](/features/crosslist-depop-vinted),
  [relisting](/features/relist-depop-vinted), refreshing your Depop shop by
  hand, following and unfollowing to grow your audience, and a
  [dead stock](/docs/automate/dead-stock) report that flags listings that have
  gone quiet.
- **On Pro and Business:** a [Depop refresh](/features/depop-auto-refresh)
  scheduler that runs every 4 to 24 hours, auto-accepting offers above a
  threshold you set, sending offers to people who liked your Vinted items, and
  linking the same item across both marketplaces.
- **On Business:** scheduled price drops, and the Restocker, which reposts
  multi-quantity Vinted items and takes the other side down when a linked item
  sells out.

## Marketplace rules apply to every tool

Depop and Vinted each have their own rules, and they change. Vinted&rsquo;s
[terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions), updated on 8 October
2026, restrict external software tools unless Vinted allows them, and say
members must not delete and re-list the same item multiple times or in bulk.
Whichever tool you use, read the current terms of each marketplace you sell on
and decide how you want to use it.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Vendoo if:**

- you sell on eBay, Etsy, Whatnot or Facebook Marketplace as well as Depop;
- you want to list from a phone app;
- you add a lot of new stock each month and want pricing based on that.

**Choose SaleLinx if:**

- your shop is on Depop and Vinted, or you want to start selling on both;
- you want automation on those two marketplaces, not just crosslisting;

## Questions

### Does Vendoo work in the UK?

Yes. Vendoo has a UK site with prices in pounds. Its help centre lists eBay,
Etsy, Depop, Whatnot and Facebook Marketplace for UK customers.

### Does Vendoo support Vinted?

Vinted was not among the marketplaces on Vendoo&rsquo;s UK site or help centre
when we checked on 14 October 2026. If Vinted matters to you, ask Vendoo
directly before you sign up.

### Does SaleLinx work with eBay?

No. SaleLinx supports Depop and Vinted only. If eBay is your main marketplace,
a tool that covers it, such as Vendoo, is the better fit.

### Is there a free version of SaleLinx?

There is no free plan. Every account starts with a 14-day free trial of
Starter. A card is needed to start it, and you can cancel before the trial ends
without paying.
