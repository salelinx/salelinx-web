Vinted has no relist button. When sellers talk about relisting, they mean
uploading an item again as a new listing and deleting the old one, so it gets
a fresh upload date and shows up as new stock. It works because Vinted gives
new listings a lift. It is also something Vinted&rsquo;s terms limit, so the
honest answer is: do it by hand, do it rarely, and only for items that have
genuinely gone quiet.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
Relist one item at a time, once, after it has sat for weeks with few views.
Vinted&rsquo;s terms say you must not delete and re-list the same item
multiple times, or multiple items in bulk. For an item that is still getting
views, a price drop, better photos or a Bump will usually do more than a
fresh listing.
</Callout>

## What relisting on Vinted actually means

Three different things get called relisting, and only one of them gives you a
new upload date.

- **Editing the listing.** Changing the photos, title or price keeps the
  same listing and the same likes. Vinted&rsquo;s
  [help centre](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/60) says nothing about an edit
  changing where the item ranks, and sellers do not report a lift from it.
- **Unhiding or unreserving.** Vinted lets you hide a listing and bring it
  back later. It is the same listing, so it does not count as new.
- **Deleting and uploading again.** The old listing goes, a new one with a
  new link takes its place. This is the only version that resets the upload
  date, and the one the rest of this guide is about.

## What Vinted&rsquo;s rules say

Read this part before you relist anything. Vinted&rsquo;s
[terms and conditions](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions),
checked on 30 October 2026, say members must not &ldquo;delete and re-list
the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;. The same
section bans external software tools such as bots unless Vinted has allowed
them. A new version of the terms applied from 8 October 2026, so check the
current text if you are reading this later.

Two more pages matter:

- The [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say
  &ldquo;You should only have one listing per item.&rdquo; Upload the new copy
  and then delete the old one, never both live at once.
- The help page on
  [hidden or removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) lists
  &ldquo;listed multiple times and is a duplicate&rdquo; as a reason for
  removal, and says repeat breaches of the catalogue rules can lead to a
  temporary or permanent ban.

So a single relist of a stale item, done by hand, is the ordinary seller
behaviour Vinted expects. Relisting the same item every week, or clearing out
and re-uploading your whole wardrobe, is the behaviour the terms name.

## Why a fresh listing gets more views

Vinted&rsquo;s page on
[recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) lists &ldquo;time
since item upload&rdquo; as one of the signals its ranking uses, and says it
promotes &ldquo;some segments of content, such as new listings&rdquo;. It does
not say how long that window lasts. Our guide to the
[best time to post on Vinted](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-vinted) goes
through what is known about it.

That is the whole mechanism. A relisted item is not special; it is simply new
again. It gets the same window every new upload gets, then settles back to
wherever its photos, price and likes put it.

1. **Open the old listing and save what you need.** Download the photos, and
   copy the title, description, size, brand and price. Vinted keeps deleted
   listing data for up to 6 months, but you cannot get the photos back from
   it.
2. **Decide what to change.** If the item got no interest first time round,
   repeating it exactly is a waste of the fresh window. Retake the first
   photo in better light, tighten the title to brand, item, colour and size,
   and look at what the same item has actually sold for.
3. **Upload the new listing.** List it in the evening if you can, when the
   most buyers are scrolling.
4. **Delete the old listing straight away.** Open it, choose Delete, and
   confirm. Leaving both up is a duplicate.
5. **Note the date.** If the new copy also sits for weeks, the problem is the
   item or the price, not the listing date. Do not relist it again.

Expect to lose the likes on the old listing. They do not carry over, and
anyone who liked it gets no notification about the new one. If an item has
plenty of likes, send those likers an offer before you consider relisting.

A relist costs you the likes, the watchers and a few minutes of work. It
pays off when the only thing wrong with a listing is its age. When something
else is wrong, fix that first.

## What Vinted offers instead

Vinted sells two ways to get an old listing seen without deleting it. Both
are priced at checkout, and both are listed on Vinted&rsquo;s
[price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist).

- **Bump.** A [Bump](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340) shows the item
  higher in the newsfeed and search for 3 or 7 days, or until it sells. The
  fee depends on the item&rsquo;s price and the length you pick.
- **Showcase.** [Showcase](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452) promotes up to
  15 of your listings for 7 days to members likely to buy from you. You need
  at least 5 items listed, and the price depends on how many listings you
  have and what they are worth.

Then there are the free moves: a price drop, an offer to likers, or a better
first photo. On an item with likes, a 10% drop or an offer usually beats a
Bump, because the people who already want it are the ones who see it.

## Questions

### Is relisting allowed on Vinted?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms do not ban a single relist. They ban deleting and
re-listing the same item multiple times, and deleting and re-listing multiple
items in bulk. Keep it to one item at a time, and do not keep relisting the
same item.

### How often can I relist the same item on Vinted?

Vinted gives no number. The terms say &ldquo;multiple times&rdquo; is the
problem, so treat a second relist of the same item as the line. If one relist
with better photos and a checked price did not sell it, change the price or
move on.

### Does editing a listing bump it on Vinted?

Vinted has not said that it does, and sellers do not report a lift from an
edit alone. An edit is still worth doing when the photos or title are the
problem, because those affect whether people click, which Vinted does rank
on.

### Do I lose my likes if I relist?

Yes. The new listing starts from zero likes and nobody who liked the old one
is told about it. If the item has likes, send them an offer before you
relist.

### Is it better to relist or to Bump?

Bump if the item is getting likes and just needs more eyes. Relist if it has
gone completely quiet and you are going to improve the photos or price at the
same time. Neither will sell an item that is priced above what it goes for.
