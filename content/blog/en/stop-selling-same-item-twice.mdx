The only way to avoid overselling when you crosslist is to take the other listings down the moment an item sells, and since you cannot do that instantly, you also need a plan for the day two people buy it in the same hour.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **Sold means delist first,** before you pack, on every other marketplace.
- If a second buyer gets in first, message them straight away, apologise, and cancel before the parcel ships.
- **Vinted:** tell the buyer first so the cancellation can be agreed and you avoid an automatic bad rating.
- **eBay:** cancelling because you sold it elsewhere counts as a defect against you.
- **Depop:** you cancel by refunding the sale in the app.

</Callout>

## Why do double sales happen?

Selling on two or three marketplaces at once is allowed, but one-off items can only be sold once. **A double sale needs only a gap:**

- **The sale happens while you are out:** two notifications arrive an hour apart and you see them both at lunchtime.
- **You deal with the first sale and forget to delist:** packing, labelling and messaging fill the next ten minutes.
- **Notifications are late or missed:** email and app alerts do not always arrive when the sale does.
- **A tool is slower than a buyer:** any app that takes a listing down for you works on a delay, and a buyer can get in during it.

The longer an item is live on several sites, the more likely it is. Fast sellers and popular items are the usual victims.

## What to do if you sell the same item twice

Do not ship to one buyer and ignore the other. Pick the buyer to keep, then cancel the other order the same day.

1. **Keep the first buyer** (or the one who has paid in full).
2. **Message the other buyer before you cancel.** Say plainly that the item sold elsewhere at the same time, apologise, and say you will refund them in full.
3. **Cancel through the marketplace,** not by going quiet. The steps differ on each, below.
4. **Take the item down everywhere else,** so it cannot happen a third time.

**Cancel before the parcel ships.** After that, the marketplace&rsquo;s return or refund route applies instead.

### Vinted

Vinted&rsquo;s help page on [cancelling an order](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/58) says an order &ldquo;can only be cancelled (by either the buyer or the seller) if the item hasn&rsquo;t been shipped yet&rdquo;. It warns that if you cancel manually, &ldquo;you might receive automatic negative feedback&rdquo;, and suggests you &ldquo;inform the other member before cancelling the order and reach a mutual agreement&rdquo;.

**Message first, get a yes, then cancel.** Afterwards you can press &ldquo;Re-upload item&rdquo; in the conversation to list the item again.

### eBay

**eBay is the strictest.** Its [seller performance policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/selling-policies/seller-performance-policy?id=4347) lists as a transaction defect a seller who &ldquo;cancels the order unexpectedly (e.g. because it was out of stock, or because they sold it to someone else)&rdquo;. Its [order cancellation policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/order-cancellation-policy?id=5298) says an out-of-stock cancellation &ldquo;will result in a transaction defect&rdquo; and adds that a seller &ldquo;may be eligible for fee credits for cancelled orders&rdquo;.

One defect is not a disaster:

- eBay says &ldquo;you&rsquo;re allowed up to 2% of transactions with defects&rdquo; in an evaluation period.
- You are only rated Below Standard if your defects involve more than 4 different buyers.
- Below Standard can mean listings ranked lower in search, lower selling limits, no Promoted Listings and held payments, and for business sellers higher final value fees.

Do not choose a false reason when you cancel to dodge the defect. Ask the buyer to request the cancellation if that is what has happened, and otherwise choose the honest reason.

### Depop

**Depop has no separate cancel button for sellers.** You cancel a sale by refunding it in the app, as Depop&rsquo;s [refund a buyer](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001772068-How-do-I-refund-a-buyer) page sets out:

- **iOS:** My Depop, the Sold tab, the order, then Refund sale.
- **Android:** Selling Hub, Sold items, the order, then Refund sale.

Refunds can only be sent from the app, and the money comes from your Depop balance or, if that is short, your top-up card, so you need one added first. If the buyer paid by PayPal, refund them in PayPal instead, then ask Depop to refund its selling fee through the [fee refund form](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001790827-How-do-I-get-a-Depop-fee-refund).

Depop does not publish any rating or account penalty for a seller refund, so message the buyer first, apologise, and refund promptly. Checked 6 November 2026.

## A manual routine that works

You do not need software to avoid most double sales. You need a habit.

1. **Sold means delist, before anything else.** Before you pack or print the label, remove the item from the other marketplaces. Delete or end it, or set it as sold, depending on what each allows.
2. **Keep one list of what is live where.** A spreadsheet is enough: one row per item, a column for each marketplace, the price, and a status. Mark the row the moment it sells.
3. **Sweep at set times.** Once in the morning and once in the evening, check every marketplace for sales and delist anything sold. Turn on sale and message notifications on each app.
4. **Number your items.** Put a short code (A12) in each listing&rsquo;s description or in your own notes, so you can match the same item on each site without scrolling.
5. **List fewer items on several sites.** A one-off with a buyer waiting in the wings is the risky one. Items you hold several of are safer, if you track the count.
6. **Mind eBay&rsquo;s rule.** Its [selling practices policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/selling-policies/selling-practices-policy?id=4346) says not to list items you are &ldquo;simultaneously selling outside of eBay without ensuring beforehand that you have enough product available&rdquo;. For a one-off, end the eBay listing as soon as it sells elsewhere.

Our guides to [Depop or Vinted](/blog/depop-vs-vinted-uk) and [Vinted or eBay](/blog/vinted-vs-ebay-uk) help you decide which items to list on more than one site at all.

## Where do tools fit?

Spreadsheets get tiring past a few dozen items, and the sweep always leaves a gap. **A tool can shorten that gap but cannot close it,** because it also works on a delay. Keep your cancellation message ready either way.

Vinted&rsquo;s [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) restrict external software tools and deleting and re-listing items in bulk. Read them before you let anything act on your Vinted account, and keep your own activity to what a person would do by hand.

## Questions

### What happens if I sell the same item to two people?

Keep one order and cancel the other before the item ships. Message the second buyer first, apologise and refund in full. On Vinted, agree the cancellation with them first to avoid an automatic bad rating. On eBay, expect a transaction defect.

### Does cancelling an order hurt my seller rating?

It can. Vinted warns you might receive automatic negative feedback if you cancel without agreement. On eBay, cancelling because you sold the item elsewhere is a transaction defect, but you are allowed defects on up to 2% of transactions and are only Below Standard if more than 4 different buyers are involved. Depop cancels a sale through a refund and publishes no rating penalty for it.

### Can I list the same item on Vinted, Depop and eBay?

Yes. eBay&rsquo;s policy says you must ensure beforehand that you have enough stock if you sell the item elsewhere at the same time, so for a one-off item end the eBay listing as soon as it sells. Vinted restricts external tools, so check its terms before using any.

### Can software stop me selling an item twice?

It can reduce the risk but not remove it. Any tool that takes down listings checks for sales on a schedule, so a buyer can still get in during the gap. Keep a manual routine and a ready message for the day it happens.

### Can I cancel a Vinted order after the buyer has paid?

Yes, if the item has not been shipped. Vinted says either the buyer or the seller can cancel until then. Tell the buyer first and agree it, which Vinted suggests as the way to avoid automatic negative feedback.
