## Where List Perfectly is stronger

- **Many more marketplaces,** including Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Grailed,
  Facebook Marketplace and Shopify.
- **Unlimited listings** on every plan.
- **A web app** for managing drafts, photos and prices, including from a phone
  browser. Crosslisting itself still needs a browser that runs extensions.
- **Built-in extras** such as background removal, AI listings and barcode
  scans, with monthly quotas by plan.

## Where SaleLinx is stronger

- **Outside the US.** List Perfectly&rsquo;s FAQ says it supports US
  marketplaces only, that &ldquo;Depop UK and other regional versions are not
  supported&rdquo;, and that support may not be able to fix region problems.
  SaleLinx is sold worldwide, priced in pounds, euros and dollars.
- **Full Vinted support,** including taking the other copies down when an item
  sells on Vinted.
- **Price.** SaleLinx starts at $9.99 a month. List Perfectly&rsquo;s cheapest
  plan, Simple, is $29 and crosslists title, description and price only.
- **Delisting on a mid plan.** SaleLinx does it on Pro and Business. List
  Perfectly&rsquo;s pricing table puts auto delist on Pro Plus, from $99 a
  month.

## Delisting when an item sells

Both need the extension running with your marketplaces connected, so neither
is instant.

- **List Perfectly** covers eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook
  Marketplace, Depop, Grailed and Vestiaire Collective, for listings with a
  quantity of 1. Vinted is not covered, because Vinted is a LITE marketplace.
- **SaleLinx** checks Depop and Vinted about every 5 minutes, with a tab open
  for each, and eBay about every 15. It works on listings you have
  [linked](/docs/automate/linking) as the same item.

## Who should not pick SaleLinx

- **You sell on US marketplaces beyond Depop and eBay.** List Perfectly
  covers them.
- **You list mostly from your phone.** SaleLinx needs a desktop or laptop.
- **You want unlimited crosslisting on a cheaper plan.** SaleLinx caps it at
  150 or 500 a month below Business.

## Questions

### Does List Perfectly work in the UK?

Its FAQ says it supports US marketplaces only and that Depop UK and other regional versions are not supported, checked 6 November 2026. It also says support may not be able to troubleshoot region-specific issues.

### Does List Perfectly delist from Vinted when an item sells?

No. List Perfectly treats Vinted as a LITE marketplace, which its pricing page says means no automatic sale detection. You can crosslist and delist Vinted items, but a Vinted sale will not take the other copies down by itself.

### Is SaleLinx cheaper than List Perfectly?

Yes. SaleLinx costs $9.99 to $31.99 a month and List Perfectly $29 to $99 or more. List Perfectly includes unlimited listings and more marketplaces.
