Vinted has not published a formula, but its help pages do say how search and the feed rank items: filters apply first, then listings are ranked by relevance to the search and to the buyer. That is all the official &ldquo;Vinted algorithm&rdquo; there is to cite. This guide keeps what Vinted says apart from what sellers say, and everything was checked against Vinted&rsquo;s pages on 6 November 2026. It is general guidance, not our own test data.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
Vinted says search filters come first, then the remaining listings are ranked using item attractiveness (including how recent a listing is, its interactions, price and condition), how well it matches the query, and the buyer&rsquo;s own history. To get more views, fill every field correctly, use specific words in the title and description, and price and photograph the item well. New listings and Bumps get extra visibility under rules that limit how often they appear. Anything more precise than that, such as exact keyword weights, is seller opinion, not Vinted fact.
</Callout>

## What Vinted says about the Vinted algorithm

Vinted&rsquo;s page on [recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) is the main source. It says, for search results, that once filtered, &ldquo;our system ranks the listings based on relevance to the query and to you.&rdquo; It names the most important factors as the filters a buyer applies (such as size) and the filters the system applies (such as items available in the buyer&rsquo;s country).

After filters, it lists item attractiveness criteria: how recent the listing is, how many interactions it has, its price and its condition. Personal history matters too, though the page notes past activity may not reflect current interests. A longer parameter list includes item descriptions and seller type.

Vinted&rsquo;s [Our platform](https://www.vinted.co.uk/our-platform) page adds that ranking reflects how long ago an item was uploaded, the price and the brand, plus &ldquo;the number of impressions (views), clicks, or how often an item is added to favourites&rdquo;, and a buyer&rsquo;s earlier activity with similar items.

What these pages do not give: a weighting for each factor, a list of the keywords that rank, or a rule that says what raises an item in search. Anyone quoting exact numbers is guessing.

## How the feed differs from search

The homepage feed is not a search. Vinted&rsquo;s recommended content page says the feed uses two kinds of algorithms: recommenders that select the listings shown to a buyer, and re-rankers that order them by estimated relevance to that buyer. Its [personalisation page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/31) says the signals include preferred sizes, brands the buyer follows, and their purchases, favourites and clicks. A buyer can switch interaction-based recommendations off in privacy settings, after which recommendations use only the item&rsquo;s data and size.

So two buyers see different feeds. You cannot rank number one for everyone. You can make an item match as many relevant buyers as possible by getting the details right.

## How titles, brand, category and descriptions feed search

Buyers filter and search, so your fields decide who can find the item.

- **Category, size and brand.** Vinted&rsquo;s search pages call filters the most important parameters. If the category or size is wrong, the item is filtered out for the buyers it should reach. Use the brand field for the brand. Vinted&rsquo;s [describing an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/49) page says not to hashtag or add other brand names in the description.
- **Title.** Vinted advises specific details such as brand, colour, type or unique features, and avoiding words like &ldquo;nice&rdquo; or &ldquo;pretty&rdquo;. Its example is &ldquo;New pink Zara mini evening dress&rdquo; over &ldquo;Very nice dress&rdquo;. Vinted&rsquo;s page lists item descriptions and brand among ranking inputs but does not name titles. Buyers search by keyword, so it is sensible to put the words they would type in the title. That is our reading, not a Vinted statement.
- **Description.** It is on Vinted&rsquo;s parameter list. Describe flaws, measurements and extra materials. The [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say descriptions cannot link to external sites, and using unrelated brands in the brand field, title, description or as a hashtag &ldquo;is not allowed&rdquo;. So padding a description with popular brand names can get a listing hidden.
- **Photos.** The Catalogue Rules say the first photo should clearly represent the whole item. It is what a buyer sees before anything else, and engagement feeds attractiveness.

Our diagnostic guide, [why Vinted items are not selling](/blog/vinted-items-not-selling), turns these into a fix-in-order checklist.

## Filters buyers use

Vinted&rsquo;s [finding items](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/35) page says buyers can search by keywords and add filters, and that search filters are saved and suggested next time. The recommended content page names category, brand, price range, size, and condition among parameters. I could not find an official list of every filter, so check the filter panel in the app. The [saved searches page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/435) says buyers can save unlimited searches and see a counter of newly uploaded matching listings. That means a correct, specific listing can reach a buyer who is hunting for it later.

## New listings, freshness and Bumps

Vinted says directly that new listings and paid promotions get extra visibility. The recommended content page says &ldquo;These items get increased visibility through rules that limit how often they appear.&rdquo; Its list of attractiveness criteria includes how recent a listing is.

On Bumps, Vinted&rsquo;s [Bump page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340) says a Bump makes the item appear higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 consecutive days, or until it sells, shown to members &ldquo;most likely to be interested in them, based on their preferences&rdquo;. Showcase highlights up to 15 listings for 7 days ([Showcase page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452)). Our [Bump guide](/blog/vinted-bump-cost-and-does-it-work) covers when it pays.

Two limits are documented. First, sellers who upload many items in a short window are shown to buyers &ldquo;in a proportionate way&rdquo;, so a bulk upload day does not let one seller dominate. Second, the terms forbid deleting and re-listing the same item multiple times or in bulk, and the [help page on removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) says the same item cannot be listed twice. Relisting to look new is therefore a rule risk, not a free trick. See [how to relist on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) for the careful version.

## A practical order for more views

1. Fix the fields: category, size, brand, condition, colour.
2. Write a specific title and an honest description in the words a buyer would use.
3. Make the first photo show the whole item.
4. Price against similar live listings.
5. Only then think about Bumps, which Vinted says give extra visibility for a limited time.

Vinted gives no guarantee about views for any of this, and neither do we.

## Questions

### Does Vinted have an algorithm?

Yes, in the sense that it ranks search results and feeds. Vinted says filters apply first, then listings are ranked by relevance to the query and to the buyer, using item attractiveness and the buyer&rsquo;s history. It does not publish the weightings.

### How do I get more views on Vinted?

Fill every field correctly, use specific brand, colour and type words in the title and description, make the first photo show the whole item, and price against similar listings. Vinted also says new listings and Bumps get extra visibility, but gives no guarantee.

### What keywords should I use on Vinted?

Use the words a buyer would type: brand, item type, colour, size, material. Vinted advises specific details over vague words like nice. Do not add unrelated brands or hashtags, because the Catalogue Rules do not allow them and the listing can be hidden.

### Does editing a listing push it up in Vinted search?

Vinted does not say so. Some sellers report it, but we found no Vinted page that confirms it. Vinted does list how recent a listing is as a ranking factor, which refers to the upload date, so treat edit tricks as unproven.

### Do Bumps change Vinted search ranking?

Yes, for a limited time. Vinted says a Bump makes an item appear higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 days, or until it sells. It affects visibility, not what the buyer sees once they open the listing.

### Does relisting reset the Vinted algorithm?

A new upload has a new date, and Vinted counts recency in ranking. But Vinted forbids listing the same item twice and deleting and re-listing in bulk, so relisting is a rule risk. If you do it, do it by hand and rarely.
