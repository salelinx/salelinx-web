Vinted has not published a formula, but its help pages say how search and the feed rank items. Checked against those pages on 6 November 2026; general guidance, not our own test data.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **Filters apply first,** then listings are ranked by item attractiveness (recency, interactions, price, condition), match to the query, and the buyer&rsquo;s own history.
- **To get more views,** fill every field correctly, use specific words in the title and description, and price and photograph the item well.
- **New listings and Bumps get extra visibility,** under rules that limit how often they appear.
- Anything more precise, such as exact keyword weights, is seller opinion, not Vinted fact.

</Callout>

## What does Vinted say about its algorithm?

Vinted&rsquo;s page on [recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) is the main source. For search results it says that once filtered, &ldquo;our system ranks the listings based on relevance to the query and to you.&rdquo; **The most important factors are the filters a buyer applies (such as size) and the filters the system applies (such as items available in the buyer&rsquo;s country).**

After filters, the page lists item attractiveness criteria:

- How recent the listing is
- How many interactions it has
- Its price and condition

Personal history matters too, though past activity may not reflect current interests. A longer list includes item descriptions and seller type.

Vinted&rsquo;s [Our platform](https://www.vinted.co.uk/our-platform) page adds that ranking reflects how long ago an item was uploaded, the price and the brand, plus &ldquo;the number of impressions (views), clicks, or how often an item is added to favourites&rdquo;, and a buyer&rsquo;s earlier activity with similar items.

**What the pages do not give:** a weighting for each factor, a list of keywords that rank, or a rule for what raises an item in search. Anyone quoting exact numbers is guessing.

## How is the feed different from search?

The homepage feed uses two kinds of algorithms, says Vinted: recommenders that select the listings a buyer sees, and re-rankers that order them by estimated relevance to that buyer.

The [personalisation page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/31) lists signals such as preferred sizes, followed brands, purchases, favourites and clicks. A buyer can switch interaction-based recommendations off in privacy settings, after which only the item&rsquo;s data and size are used.

**Two buyers see different feeds, so you cannot rank number one for everyone.** You can match as many relevant buyers as possible by getting the details right.

## How do titles, brand and descriptions affect search?

**Buyers filter and search, so your fields decide who can find the item.**

- **Category, size and brand:** Vinted&rsquo;s search pages call filters the most important parameters. A wrong category or size filters the item out for the buyers it should reach. Use the brand field for the brand, and do not hashtag or add other brand names in the description ([describing an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/49)).
- **Title:** Vinted advises specific details such as brand, colour, type or unique features, not words like &ldquo;nice&rdquo; or &ldquo;pretty&rdquo;: &ldquo;New pink Zara mini evening dress&rdquo; over &ldquo;Very nice dress&rdquo;. Its ranking list names descriptions and brand but not titles, so putting buyers&rsquo; search words in the title is our reading, not a Vinted statement.
- **Description:** it is on Vinted&rsquo;s parameter list. Describe flaws, measurements and extra materials. The [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) bar links to external sites, and using unrelated brands in the brand field, title, description or as a hashtag &ldquo;is not allowed&rdquo;, so padding with popular brand names can get a listing hidden.
- **Photos:** the Catalogue Rules say the first photo should clearly represent the whole item. It is what a buyer sees first, and engagement feeds attractiveness.

Our diagnostic guide, [why Vinted items are not selling](/blog/vinted-items-not-selling), turns these into a fix-in-order checklist.

## Which filters do buyers use?

Vinted&rsquo;s [finding items](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/35) page says buyers search by keywords, add filters, and get their filters saved and suggested next time. The recommended content page names category, brand, price range, size and condition. I found no official list of every filter, so check the filter panel in the app.

**Buyers can save unlimited searches** and see a counter of newly uploaded matching listings ([saved searches](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/435)). A correct, specific listing can reach a buyer who is hunting for it later.

## Do new listings and Bumps get a boost?

**Yes, Vinted says so directly.** The recommended content page says &ldquo;These items get increased visibility through rules that limit how often they appear.&rdquo; Its attractiveness criteria include how recent a listing is.

- **Bump:** the item appears higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 consecutive days, or until it sells, shown to members &ldquo;most likely to be interested in them, based on their preferences&rdquo; ([Bump page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340)).
- **Showcase:** highlights up to 15 listings for 7 days ([Showcase page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452)).

Our [Bump guide](/blog/vinted-bump-cost-and-does-it-work) covers when it pays.

Two limits are documented:

- **Bulk uploads are shown proportionately.** Sellers who upload many items in a short window are shown to buyers &ldquo;in a proportionate way&rdquo;, so one seller cannot dominate on upload day.
- **Relisting to look new is a rule risk.** The terms forbid deleting and re-listing the same item multiple times or in bulk, and the [help page on removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) says the same item cannot be listed twice. See [how to relist on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) for the careful version.

## What order should I fix things in?

1. Fix the fields: category, size, brand, condition, colour.
2. Write a specific title and an honest description in the words a buyer would use.
3. Make the first photo show the whole item.
4. Price against similar live listings.
5. Only then think about Bumps, which Vinted says give extra visibility for a limited time.

Vinted gives no guarantee about views for any of this, and neither do we.

## Questions

### Does Vinted have an algorithm?

Yes, in the sense that it ranks search results and feeds. Vinted says filters apply first, then listings are ranked by relevance to the query and to the buyer, using item attractiveness and the buyer&rsquo;s history. It does not publish the weightings.

### How do I get more views on Vinted?

Fill every field correctly, use specific brand, colour and type words in the title and description, make the first photo show the whole item, and price against similar listings. Vinted also says new listings and Bumps get extra visibility, but gives no guarantee.

### What keywords should I use on Vinted?

Use the words a buyer would type: brand, item type, colour, size, material. Vinted advises specific details over vague words like nice. Do not add unrelated brands or hashtags, because the Catalogue Rules do not allow them and the listing can be hidden.

### Does editing a listing push it up in Vinted search?

Vinted does not say so. Some sellers report it, but we found no Vinted page that confirms it. Vinted does list how recent a listing is as a ranking factor, which refers to the upload date, so treat edit tricks as unproven.

### Do Bumps change Vinted search ranking?

Yes, for a limited time. Vinted says a Bump makes an item appear higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 days, or until it sells. It affects visibility, not what the buyer sees once they open the listing.

### Does relisting reset the Vinted algorithm?

A new upload has a new date, and Vinted counts recency in ranking. But Vinted forbids listing the same item twice and deleting and re-listing in bulk, so relisting is a rule risk. If you do it, do it by hand and rarely.
