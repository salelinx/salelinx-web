If you sell on Vinted, Depop and eBay, pick SaleLinx or List Perfectly,
the two apps here that list Vinted: SaleLinx costs less and covers only those
three, while List Perfectly covers more marketplaces but treats Vinted as a
lighter integration. If you sell mostly on eBay, Etsy, Poshmark or Mercari and
do not use Vinted, Vendoo is the better fit.

A crosslisting app copies a listing you have made on one marketplace to
another, so you do not type the same item out twice. Most of the well-known
ones were built for American marketplaces like Poshmark and Mercari, and UK
sellers mostly sell on Vinted, Depop and eBay. So the first question for a UK
seller is which apps cover the marketplaces you actually use.

We make SaleLinx, one of the apps below, so read this with that in mind. Every
figure for the other apps comes from their own pricing or help pages, linked
in the table note, and was checked again on 6 November 2026. Prices change, so check
the current page before you pay.

## What each one is good at

**SaleLinx** is a Chrome extension built only for Depop, Vinted and eBay. It
crosslists between all three in any direction, and also handles the routine jobs on
Depop and Vinted: relisting, refreshing Depop listings, price drops, offers
and printing shipping labels. On eBay it adds bulk editing. Copies to Depop and Vinted are
saved as drafts for you to check; copies to eBay go live, after it shows you
eBay&rsquo;s fee for each item. If you also sell on Etsy, Poshmark or
Facebook, it does not cover them.

**Vendoo** covers the most marketplaces of the four and has a mobile app. Its
help centre lists eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Facebook
Marketplace, Shopify, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot, but not Vinted, and
its old Vinted page now redirects to its marketplace list. It suits sellers
whose stock goes to eBay, Depop and Etsy rather than Vinted.

**List Perfectly** gives unlimited listings on every plan and lists Vinted,
but as a &ldquo;LITE&rdquo; marketplace. Its pricing page says that means no
automatic sale detection on Vinted, so a Vinted sale will not delist the item
elsewhere by itself.

**Crosslist** is sold in the UK, US, Canada and Australia (its pricing page
says the EU is not supported), and runs through a browser extension plus
mobile apps. Its plans are capped by new listings a month, from 200 up to
unlimited. It has closed Vinted to new accounts: its
[Vinted notice](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces/vinted), last updated 29
October 2026, says new accounts cannot connect to Vinted or import from it,
while existing users keep access.

## Who should not pick SaleLinx

- **You sell on Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or Facebook Marketplace.** SaleLinx
  does not list them. Vendoo or List Perfectly do.
- **You work mostly from a phone.** SaleLinx is a Chrome extension, so it needs
  a desktop or laptop browser. Vendoo has a mobile app.
- **You want a free plan.** SaleLinx has a 14-day free trial, then paid plans.
- **You only sell on eBay.** You do not need a crosslister, and eBay&rsquo;s
  own tools will do.

## Crosslisting from Vinted to Depop

Of the apps above, SaleLinx and List Perfectly cover
both. SaleLinx copies Vinted listings to Depop as drafts, so you can check
each one before buyers see it, and the other direction works the same way.
Crosslist is closed to new Vinted accounts, and Vendoo does not list Vinted.

## What to check before you pick one

- **Directions.** Moving Vinted stock onto eBay and moving eBay stock onto
  Vinted are separate features in some apps. Check the one you need.
- **Drafts or live.** eBay has no drafts, so any app posting to eBay posts
  live listings. For Depop and Vinted, an app that saves drafts lets you check
  each copy before buyers see it.
- **eBay item specifics.** eBay refuses many clothing listings without a size,
  department or brand. A good crosslister fills these in and tells you what is
  still missing before it posts.
- **Marketplace rules.** Vinted&rsquo;s
  [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) say members must not
  &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in
  bulk&rdquo;, and its
  [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) ask for one
  listing per item. Read them before you use any tool on Vinted.

## Questions

### What is the best crosslisting app for Vinted in the UK?

It depends on where else you sell. Of the apps above, SaleLinx and List
Perfectly list Vinted. SaleLinx covers Vinted, Depop and eBay only; List
Perfectly also covers Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari and others, with Vinted as a
lighter &ldquo;LITE&rdquo; integration.

### Can I crosslist from Vinted to eBay?

Yes, with an app that supports both. eBay has no drafts, so the copy goes live
on eBay straight away, and eBay will ask for item specifics such as size and
brand.

### Is there a free crosslisting app?

Most offer a free trial or a few free listings rather than a free plan.
SaleLinx and Vendoo both have 14-day free trials.
