not type it twice. Most were built for Poshmark and Mercari, so a UK seller
should first check which apps cover Vinted, Depop and eBay.

## What each one is good at

### SaleLinx

A Chrome extension built only for Depop, Vinted and eBay.

- **Crosslists between all three** in any direction.
- Also handles routine jobs on Depop and Vinted: relisting, refreshing Depop listings, price drops, offers and shipping labels. On eBay it adds bulk editing.
- Copies to Depop and Vinted are saved as drafts. Copies to eBay go live, after it shows you eBay&rsquo;s fee for each item.
- It does not cover Etsy, Poshmark or Facebook.

### Vendoo

Covers the most marketplaces of the four and has a mobile app.

- **No Vinted.** Its help centre lists eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot. Its old Vinted page now redirects to that list.
- Suits sellers whose stock goes to eBay, Depop and Etsy rather than Vinted.

### List Perfectly

- Unlimited listings on every plan.
- **Vinted is a &ldquo;LITE&rdquo; marketplace.** Its pricing page says that means no automatic sale detection, so a Vinted sale will not delist the item elsewhere by itself.

### Crosslist

- Sold in the UK, US, Canada and Australia (its pricing page says the EU is not supported). Runs through a browser extension plus mobile apps.
- Plans are capped by new listings a month, from 200 up to unlimited.
- **Closed to new Vinted accounts.** Its [Vinted notice](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces/vinted), last updated 29 October 2026, says new accounts cannot connect to Vinted or import from it. Existing users keep access.

## Who should not pick SaleLinx

- **You sell on Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or Facebook Marketplace.** SaleLinx
  does not list them. Vendoo or List Perfectly do.
- **You work mostly from a phone.** SaleLinx is a Chrome extension, so it needs
  a desktop or laptop browser. Vendoo has a mobile app.
- **You want a free plan.** SaleLinx has a 14-day free trial, then paid plans.
- **You only sell on eBay.** You do not need a crosslister, and eBay&rsquo;s
  own tools will do.

## Crosslisting from Vinted to Depop

**Only SaleLinx and List Perfectly cover both.** SaleLinx copies Vinted
listings to Depop as drafts, so you check each one before buyers see it, and
the other direction works the same way. Crosslist is closed to new Vinted
accounts, and Vendoo does not list Vinted.

## What to check before you pick one

- **Directions.** Vinted to eBay and eBay to Vinted are separate features in
  some apps. Check the one you need.
- **Drafts or live.** eBay has no drafts, so any app posting there posts live.
  For Depop and Vinted, drafts let you check each copy first.
- **eBay item specifics.** eBay refuses many clothing listings without a size,
  department or brand. A good crosslister fills these in and flags what is
  missing before it posts.
- **Marketplace rules.** Vinted&rsquo;s
  [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) say members must not
  &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in
  bulk&rdquo;, and its
  [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) ask for one
  listing per item. Read them before you use any tool on Vinted.

## Questions

### What is the best crosslisting app for Vinted in the UK?

It depends on where else you sell. Of the apps above, SaleLinx and List
Perfectly list Vinted. SaleLinx covers Vinted, Depop and eBay only; List
Perfectly also covers Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari and others, with Vinted as a
lighter &ldquo;LITE&rdquo; integration.

### Can I crosslist from Vinted to eBay?

Yes, with an app that supports both. eBay has no drafts, so the copy goes live
on eBay straight away, and eBay will ask for item specifics such as size and
brand.

### Is there a free crosslisting app?

Most offer a free trial or a few free listings rather than a free plan.
SaleLinx and Vendoo both have 14-day free trials.
