## UK support: the first thing to check

List Perfectly&rsquo;s [FAQ](https://listperfectly.com/faq/) is clear about
where it works: &ldquo;At this time, List Perfectly supports U.S. marketplaces
only&rdquo;, and &ldquo;Depop UK and other regional versions are not
supported&rdquo;. It also says it cannot guarantee how it performs outside the
US.

Its pricing page does list Vinted, as &ldquo;Vinted (LITE)&rdquo;, which
covers crosslisting and delisting but not automatic sale detection. It does not
say which Vinted country site that means, and given the US-only policy above,
we would not assume it is Vinted UK. If that matters to you, ask List Perfectly
before you pay.

SaleLinx works with Depop and Vinted as UK sellers use them. You can copy a
listing from Depop to Vinted or the other way round, with the category, size,
brand, condition and colour translated into what the other site expects. If
your two shops use different currencies, the price is converted too. See the
[crosslisting guide](/docs/inventory/crosslist) for the details.

## Pricing and currency

List Perfectly&rsquo;s plans are priced in US dollars and billed monthly:
$29, $49, $69, and $99 or more, all with unlimited listings. There is no free
trial. Instead, its help centre describes a money-back guarantee if you ask
within 5 days or 100 listings, whichever comes first. Paying in dollars from a
UK card can also mean a foreign transaction fee from your bank, depending on
your card.

SaleLinx bills in pounds: Starter at <PlanPrice tier="starter" />, Pro at
<PlanPrice tier="pro" /> and Business at <PlanPrice tier="business" /> a month.
Each plan has monthly and daily allowances for things like crosslisting,
relisting and follows, so check the [pricing page](/pricing) against the volume
you sell. Every account
starts with a 14-day free trial of Starter.

## Automation

List Perfectly&rsquo;s automation is aimed at US marketplaces. Its higher plans
add Poshmark sharing, offer sending and automatic delisting.

SaleLinx&rsquo;s automation is all about Depop and Vinted:

- **On every plan:** [crosslisting](/features/crosslist-depop-vinted),
  [relisting](/features/relist-depop-vinted), refreshing your Depop shop by
  hand, following and unfollowing to grow your audience, and a
  [dead stock](/docs/automate/dead-stock) report.
- **On Pro and Business:** a [Depop refresh](/features/depop-auto-refresh)
  scheduler, auto-accepting offers above a threshold you set, sending offers to
  people who liked your Vinted items, and linking the same item across both
  marketplaces.
- **On Business:** scheduled price drops, and the Restocker, which takes the
  other side down when a linked item sells out.

## Marketplace rules apply to every tool

Depop and Vinted each set their own rules, and they change. Vinted&rsquo;s
[terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions), updated on 8 October
2026, restrict external software tools unless Vinted allows them, and say
members must not delete and re-list the same item multiple times or in bulk.
Whichever tool you use, read the current terms of each marketplace you sell on
and decide how you want to use it.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose List Perfectly if:**

- you sell on US marketplaces such as eBay US or Poshmark;
- you want unlimited listings on every plan;
- you want Poshmark sharing built in.

**Choose SaleLinx if:**

- you sell on Depop and Vinted in the UK;
- you want to pay in pounds and try it free before you pay;
- you want automation built for those two marketplaces.

Its help centre says no: a card or PayPal is needed at sign-up, with a
