Crosslisting means listing the same item on Vinted and eBay at once, so both
sets of buyers see it. Neither site has a button for it: you copy it by
hand, or use an app.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- Copy the title, photos, description, size, brand and condition.
- Fill in eBay&rsquo;s item specifics: brand, size, colour, department and type.
- Pick a postage option and list. The same price works on both.
- **When it sells on one, take it down on the other that day.**

</Callout>

## Is the item allowed on both?

**Vinted is the stricter side.** Its
[catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) ban:

- Items &ldquo;specifically bought or sourced to resell&rdquo; on a standard
  account. Commercial sellers need Vinted Pro.
- Uploading &ldquo;an item that is not available or already sold&rdquo;.
  That is why keeping stock in sync matters.

Your own second-hand clothes are fine. Stock you bought to flip is not, even
if it was on eBay first. eBay lets private sellers resell; what it cares
about is the category and the details you give.

## Vinted to eBay by hand

### 1. Gather the listing

Copy the title, description, brand, size, condition and colour from your
Vinted listing. Use the original photos from your phone if you still have
them, since Vinted compresses its copies.

### 2. Pick the eBay category

Start a new eBay listing, search the item&rsquo;s name and check the
category eBay suggests. **The category decides which details eBay asks for
next.** A jumper under Women&rsquo;s Clothing gets different questions from
one under Vintage.

You can save a draft and finish later. eBay&rsquo;s
[listing guide](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/listings/creating-managing-listings/creating-listing?id=4105)
says drafts expire 75 days after your last edit.

### 3. Fill in the item specifics

This is the step Vinted never asks for. eBay&rsquo;s
[Seller Centre](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/listings/item-specifics)
calls Brand, Size and Colour &ldquo;critical&rdquo; in Fashion, and warns:
**&ldquo;New or revised listings that don&rsquo;t include required item
specifics will be blocked.&rdquo;**

For clothes, expect:

- Brand, size and size type
- Department (men, women, kids) and type
- Colour, and often material, style and pattern

Most of it is already in your Vinted description. eBay just wants it in
boxes.

### 4. Set the price

**The same price works on both.** Neither marketplace takes a cut from a UK
private seller. The buyer pays a fee on top instead.

- **Vinted:** &ldquo;There are no fees for selling on Vinted&rdquo;
  ([selling basics](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/26)). The buyer pays the
  Vinted fee, usually 3% to 8% plus £0.30 to £0.80
  ([price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist)). It was
  [renamed](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1653) from Buyer Protection in
  October 2026.
- **eBay:** no final value fee, and 300 free listings a month, then 35p each
  ([private seller fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822),
  updated 30 September 2026). The buyer pays a
  [Buyer Protection fee](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594)
  of £0.10, plus 7% of the price up to £20, 4% from £20 to £300 and 2% from
  £300 to £4,000.

Our [eBay fees guide](/blog/ebay-fees-uk) has worked examples, including
for business sellers.

### 5. Sort out postage

- **Vinted:** the buyer always pays and picks the carrier, and Vinted emails
  you a prepaid label. You have 5 business days to send
  ([shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753)).
- **eBay:** [Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575)
  gives you a prepaid label on eligible items up to £750. In the &ldquo;Who
  pays?&rdquo; box you choose: the buyer pays, or you offer free postage and
  the label comes out of your account. Your own custom postage is only
  allowed in certain categories at £20 and under.

**If you offer free postage on eBay, raise the price to cover the label.**

### 6. Keep a list

Note which items are on both marketplaces, even just on your phone. You need
it for the next step.

## Keep stock in sync

**The real risk is selling the same item twice.** It sells on eBay on
Tuesday, you post it Wednesday, and on Thursday a Vinted buyer pays for it
too. Cancelling on Vinted hurts your account, and listing an item you no
longer have breaks its rules.

**Take the other listing down before you post the parcel.**

- **On Vinted,** hide it if the eBay sale might fall through, and delete it
  once the parcel has gone.
- **On eBay,** end it from your active listings.

## eBay to Vinted

The reverse is easier on data and harder on rules.

**Easier:** an eBay listing already has more detail than Vinted needs.
Brand, size, colour and condition drop straight in, and there are no item
specifics. eBay photos are fine as long as they show the real item.

**Harder:**

- **One listing per item.** The catalogue rules say &ldquo;You should only
  have one listing per item.&rdquo;
- **No resale stock on a standard account.** Business sellers need
  [Vinted Pro](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pro), and some categories, such as
  cosmetics, are closed to Pro.
- **Postage does not carry over.** Vinted sets it from the parcel size, and
  the buyer pays.
- **No bulk tools.** Vinted&rsquo;s
  [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) (from 8 October
  2026) say members must not use &ldquo;any kind of external software tools
  (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs, crawling programs,
  spiders)&rdquo; unless Vinted allows it, or &ldquo;delete and re-list the
  same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;. Keep Vinted
  activity to the pace of a person listing by hand.

More in our guide to [relisting on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted).

## Using an app instead

By hand, one item takes about ten minutes, so fifty is a weekend. A
crosslisting app reads the listing, maps the category, brand, size and
condition, copies the photos and creates the copy. Our
[best crosslisting apps](/blog/best-crosslisting-apps-uk) post compares the
few that cover Vinted.

**Check the app ends one listing when the other sells.** If it does not, the
sync rule above is still your job.

## Questions

### Can I crosslist from Vinted to eBay for free?

Yes, by hand. Vinted charges sellers nothing, and eBay gives UK private
sellers 300 free listings a month with no final value fee. Apps that do the
copying charge a monthly fee.

### Why does eBay refuse my clothing listing?

Almost always a missing required item specific, such as brand, size, colour
or department. Fill in the fields marked required. If they look wrong for
the item, change the category first, since it decides which are required.

### Do I need to charge more on eBay than on Vinted?

Not for fees, as neither takes a cut from a UK private seller. Only if you
offer free postage on eBay, since the label cost comes out of your account.

### What happens if the item sells on both at once?

Cancel the later order as soon as you see it and message the buyer. A
cancelled Vinted order counts against you, so hide the Vinted listing the
moment an eBay sale comes in.

### Is it against Vinted&rsquo;s rules to use a crosslisting app?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms restrict external software tools and ban bulk delete
and re-list. They do not name crosslisting. Keep activity on your Vinted
account to what a person would do by hand, and never let a tool delete and
re-list in bulk.
