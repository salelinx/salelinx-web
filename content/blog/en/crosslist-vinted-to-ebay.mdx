Crosslisting from Vinted to eBay means making a second listing for the same
item on eBay, so it is in front of both sets of buyers at once. There is no
button for it on either site. You either rebuild the listing by hand in
eBay&rsquo;s sell form, or use a crosslisting app that reads your Vinted
listing and creates the eBay copy for you. The hard part is not the copying.
It is the three things eBay does differently: item specifics, fees and
postage, and the discipline of taking the item down on the other marketplace
the moment it sells.

This guide walks through the Vinted to eBay direction first, then eBay to
Vinted, with the rules on each side. Every fee and rule links to the
marketplace&rsquo;s own page, checked on 30 October 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
Copy the title, photos, description, size, brand and condition across, then
fill in eBay&rsquo;s item specifics (brand, size, colour, department and
type), pick a postage option, and list. UK private sellers pay no final
value fee on eBay, and Vinted charges sellers nothing, so the same price
works on both. Keep one rule above all: when it sells on one marketplace,
remove it from the other that day.
</Callout>

## Before you start: is the item allowed on both?

Vinted is the stricter side. Its
[catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say a standard
account cannot list items &ldquo;specifically bought or sourced to
resell&rdquo;, and that commercial sellers need a Vinted Pro account. Your
own second-hand clothes are fine. Stock you bought to flip is not, however
it got onto eBay first. The same rules forbid uploading &ldquo;an item that
is not available or already sold&rdquo;, which is the rule that makes stock
sync matter.

eBay has no equivalent restriction on private sellers reselling, but it does
care what category you list in and which details you give. That is where
most Vinted to eBay copies go wrong.

## Vinted to eBay by hand

### 1. Gather the listing

Open your Vinted listing and copy the title, description, brand, size,
condition and colour somewhere you can see them. Save the photos at full
size from the listing page; the app compresses them, so the originals on
your phone are better if you still have them.

### 2. Rebuild it in eBay&rsquo;s sell form

Start a new listing on eBay, search the item&rsquo;s name and pick the
category eBay suggests, then check it. Vinted categories are broad (Women,
Jumpers and sweaters); eBay&rsquo;s are narrower and the one you pick
decides which details eBay demands next. A jumper filed under Women&rsquo;s
Clothing asks different questions from one filed under Vintage.

eBay&rsquo;s [listing guide](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/listings/creating-managing-listings/creating-listing?id=4105)
says &ldquo;some fields are required and some are not, depending on which
category you&rsquo;re selling in&rdquo;. It also lets you save a draft,
which expires 75 days after you last edited it, so you can build the eBay
copy in stages.

### 3. Fill in the item specifics

This is the step Vinted never asked you for. eBay&rsquo;s
[Seller Centre](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/listings/item-specifics)
says item specifics are required in &ldquo;the Fashion, Home &amp; Garden,
Toys &amp; Games, and Vehicle Parts &amp; Accessories categories&rdquo;,
calls Brand, Size and Colour &ldquo;critical&rdquo;, and is blunt about what
happens if you skip them: &ldquo;New or revised listings that don&rsquo;t
include required item specifics will be blocked.&rdquo;

For clothes, expect to be asked for brand, size, size type, department
(men, women, kids), type, colour, and often material, style and pattern. Most
of it is already in your Vinted listing in prose. eBay wants it in boxes.

### 4. Set the price with both fee structures in mind

Vinted charges sellers nothing. Its
[selling basics](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/26) page says &ldquo;There
are no fees for selling on Vinted, and you can list as many items as you
like.&rdquo; The buyer pays a separate charge at checkout, which Vinted
[renamed](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1653) from the Buyer Protection fee
to the Vinted fee in October 2026, and its
[price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist) puts at usually 3% to 8%
plus £0.30 to £0.80.

eBay now works the same way for UK private sellers. Its
[private seller fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)
page, last updated 30 September 2026, says &ldquo;You won&rsquo;t pay final
value fees or regulatory operating fees when your items sell&rdquo;, and
gives you 300 free listings a month with 35p for each one after that. The
buyer pays a
[Buyer Protection fee](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594)
instead: £0.10 per item, plus 7% of the price up to £20, 4% of the part from
£20 to £300 and 2% of the part from £300 to £4,000.

So the price you type in is what you get on both, and the buyer sees a
slightly higher total on each. Our
[eBay fees guide](/blog/ebay-fees-uk) works through the eBay side with
examples, including what changes if you register as a business.

### 5. Sort out postage

On Vinted you never think about postage. Its
[shipping page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753) says &ldquo;The shipping
is always paid by the buyer&rdquo;, the buyer picks the carrier, and Vinted
emails you a prepaid label after the sale. You have 5 business days to send.

On eBay, most private sellers now get something similar through
[Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575).
It covers eligible items priced £750 or less, and after a sale you click
&ldquo;Get prepaid label&rdquo; on the order. The difference is a choice
Vinted never gives you: in the listing&rsquo;s &ldquo;Who pays?&rdquo; box you
decide whether the buyer pays for the label at checkout or you offer free
postage and have the label cost taken from your account. Opting out for your
own custom postage is only possible for certain categories at £20 and under.

If you offer free postage on eBay, raise the price to cover the label, or
the eBay copy nets you less than the Vinted one.

### 6. List it, then note it down

Once you press list, the item is live on both. Write down which items are
on both marketplaces, even if it is just a note on your phone. That list is
what saves you in the next section.

## Keep stock in sync, or sell the same thing twice

The one real risk of crosslisting is a double sale. The item sells on eBay
on Tuesday, you post it Wednesday, and on Thursday a Vinted buyer pays for
it too. Now you are cancelling on Vinted, which hurts your account, and the
Vinted buyer has waited for nothing. Vinted&rsquo;s catalogue rules make
listing an unavailable item a breach in its own right.

Make taking the other listing down the first thing you do after a sale, not
the last. On Vinted, hiding the listing is enough if you think the eBay sale
might fall through; delete it once the parcel has gone. On eBay, end the
listing from your active listings page.

## eBay to Vinted

The reverse direction is easier on data and harder on rules.

Easier, because an eBay listing already holds more structured detail than
Vinted asks for. Brand, size, colour and condition drop straight into
Vinted&rsquo;s form. Vinted&rsquo;s categories are broader, so picking one is
quick, and there is no item specifics step. The eBay photos will do on
Vinted as long as they show the real item, not a stock image.

Harder, because of what Vinted will and will not accept:

- **One listing per item.** The catalogue rules say &ldquo;You should only
  have one listing per item.&rdquo; If an item is already on Vinted in any
  form, do not create a second copy.
- **No resale stock on a standard account.** If your eBay shop is a
  business, or you sell things you bought to sell on, Vinted expects a
  [Vinted Pro](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pro) account, and some
  categories (cosmetics, for example) are closed to Pro sellers.
- **Postage.** Your eBay postage settings do not carry over. Vinted sets
  postage from the parcel size you pick, and the buyer pays it.
- **No bulk tools.** Vinted&rsquo;s
  [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions), in the version
  applicable from 8 October 2026, say members must not use &ldquo;any kind of
  external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping
  programs, crawling programs, spiders)&rdquo; unless Vinted allows it, and
  must not &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple
  Items in bulk&rdquo;. Read them before you use any app that touches your
  Vinted account, and keep what you do on Vinted to the pace of a person
  listing by hand.

Our guide to [relisting on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) goes
further into what those terms mean in practice.

## Using an app instead

Doing all of the above for one item takes about ten minutes. For fifty
items it is a weekend, and that is what crosslisting apps exist for. They
read your listing on one marketplace, map the category, brand, size and
condition to the other marketplace&rsquo;s lists, copy the photos, and create
the listing. The [best crosslisting apps](/blog/best-crosslisting-apps-uk)
post compares the ones that cover Vinted, which is fewer than you would
expect.

## Questions

### Can I crosslist from Vinted to eBay for free?

Yes, by hand. eBay gives UK private sellers 300 free listings a month and
charges no final value fee, and Vinted charges sellers nothing, so the only
cost is your time. Apps that do the copying for you charge a monthly fee.

### Why does eBay refuse my clothing listing?

Almost always a missing required item specific. eBay blocks new listings in
Fashion that leave out details like brand, size, colour or department. Open
the listing, look for the fields marked required, and fill them in. The
category you picked decides which ones are required, so if the list looks
wrong for the item, change the category first.

### Do I need to charge more on eBay than on Vinted?

Not for fees. Neither marketplace takes a cut from a UK private seller.
You may need to if you offer free postage on eBay, since the label cost
comes out of your account. With buyer-pays postage, the same price works on
both.

### What happens if the item sells on both at once?

You have to cancel one order. Cancel the later one as soon as you see it and
message the buyer. On Vinted, a cancelled order counts against you and
listing an item you no longer have breaks the catalogue rules, so it is
better to spend the thirty seconds hiding the Vinted listing when the eBay
sale comes in.

### Is it against Vinted&rsquo;s rules to use a crosslisting app?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms restrict external software tools, and separately ban
bulk delete and re-list. They do not mention crosslisting by name. Read the
current terms, keep activity on your Vinted account to what a person would
do by hand, and never let a tool delete and re-list your Vinted items in
bulk.
