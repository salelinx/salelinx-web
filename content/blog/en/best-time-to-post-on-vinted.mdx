The usual advice is to list in the evening and save your best pieces for
Sunday, but this is general guidance from Vinted, consumer sites and sellers,
not our own tested data.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **List in the evening, roughly 7pm to 10pm.**
- Save your best pieces for Sunday evening or the days around payday.
- That is the consensus advice, and the only timing figure Vinted has published points the same way.
- We have not tested it, and nobody outside Vinted has published hour-by-hour UK data. Treat timing as a tiebreaker: photos, price and a clear title still do most of the work.

</Callout>

## Does posting time matter?

**Yes, a little: Vinted ranks new listings higher.** Its explanation of
[recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) lists &ldquo;time since
item upload&rdquo; as a ranking signal. It says it promotes some content,
&ldquo;such as new listings&rdquo;, alongside paid promotions like Bumps and
Showcase.

Vinted does not say how long the window lasts or how big the boost is. The
logic is simple: if your item&rsquo;s freshest hours fall while many buyers are
scrolling, more of them see it.

## What does the evidence say?

Everything we could find with a source, strongest to weakest.

- **Vinted: activity peaks around 9pm.** Its review of 2022 said people are
  particularly active in the evening, around 9pm
  ([reported by NSS Magazine](https://www.nssmag.com/en/lifestyle/32059/things-vinted-2022)).
  It is a few years old and covers Vinted as a whole, not the UK, but it is
  the only hour-of-day figure Vinted has made public.
- **Vinted: the first Sunday in January is the busiest listing day.** Vinted
  named Sunday 4 January 2026 &ldquo;Sell-It Sunday&rdquo; and said new listings
  that day run at about three times the normal daily rate
  ([ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2025/12/vinted-pinpoints-4th-january-as-sell-it-sunday/)).
  That counts sellers listing, not buyers browsing, so expect more competition.
- **Consumer and shipping experts: evenings, weekends and payday.** Which?
  suggests listing around payday and at weekends
  ([Which?, January 2026](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/expert-tips-for-buying-and-selling-on-vinted-ad7Pi9r6UddI)).
  Parcel2Go recommends evenings after 7pm, and higher-value items close to
  payday ([Parcel2Go](https://parcel2go.com/content-hub/boost-your-vinted-listings)).
  Neither cites data, but the advice is consistent and fits the 9pm figure.
- **Sellers on forums: all over the place.** Some swear by 7am, lunchtimes or
  Sunday nights. One seller&rsquo;s results are hard to separate from luck.

**Any article that gives you an exact minute without showing data is guessing.**

## What is a good posting routine?

1. **List in the evening.** Roughly 7pm to 10pm on weekdays. If you can only
   list at one time of day, make it this.
2. **Save your best items for Sunday evening.** It is the most repeated advice
   and lines up with the evening peak.
3. **Time pricier pieces for payday.** Many UK workers are paid on the last
   working day of the month, so list items above your usual price in the few
   days after.
4. **Spread new listings across the week.** Ten items over five evenings each
   get a fresh window. Ten at once compete for the same one.
5. **List seasonal stock ahead of the season.** Coats in early autumn, festival
   wear in spring.
6. **Avoid the first Sunday in January.** Lots of sellers list on Sell-It
   Sunday, so list a strong piece the week before or after.

## Will timing fix a weak listing?

**No.** Recency is one ranking signal. Vinted&rsquo;s help page also names
item attractiveness, such as interactions, price and condition. Check the
basics first:

- **Photos.** Good light, plain background, the whole item first, then
  close-ups of labels, fabric and flaws.
- **Title.** Brand, item type, colour and size, in the words a buyer would
  search.
- **Price.** Check what the same item has sold for, not what it is listed at.
- **Replies.** Answer questions and offers quickly. A waiting buyer often buys
  something else.

## Should you delete and re-upload old listings?

**No, Vinted&rsquo;s rules say not to.**

- Its [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) say members must
  not &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items
  in bulk&rdquo;.
- Its [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say you should
  only have one listing per item, and its
  [help centre](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) says a listing can be removed
  as a duplicate.

The terms changed on 8 October 2026, so check the current version. For an item
that has gone quiet, Vinted offers:

- **Lower the price.** A small drop is often all a watcher needs.
- **Bump it.** A [Bump](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340) is paid. It shows
  the item higher in the newsfeed and search for 3 or 7 days, or until it
  sells. The price depends on the item and is shown before you pay.
- **Use Showcase.** [Showcase](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452) (formerly
  Wardrobe Spotlight) is a paid 7-day promotion for up to 15 listings. You need
  at least 5 items listed.
- **Freshen the listing.** Better photos or a clearer title can help it show up
  in more searches.

## What about Depop?

**On Depop you do not need to delete anything.** Editing and re-saving a
listing moves it to the top of your shop and gives it a fresh &ldquo;last
edited&rdquo; time, which helps in search. The same evening routine is a
sensible starting point there.

## Questions

### What is the best day to post on Vinted?

Sunday, in the evening, is the most common answer, and the advice from Which?
adds weekends and payday. There is no published UK data that settles it, so
compare your own views and favourites across a few weeks.

### Does Vinted show the newest items first?

Not strictly. Vinted ranks items using several signals, including how
recently they were uploaded, how people have interacted with them, price and
condition, and the buyer&rsquo;s own browsing. Newer listings get a lift, but
the feed is not a simple newest-first list.

### Is it worth paying to bump an item on Vinted?

It depends on the item. Vinted prices a Bump by the item&rsquo;s price and
how long it runs, so it makes most sense for pieces with a healthy margin and
good photos that just need to be seen. If an item has had plenty of views but
no sales, the price or photos are usually the problem, and a Bump will not fix
that.

### Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

No. Vinted says there are no fees for selling. Buyers pay a separate fee at
checkout, which Vinted renamed from the Buyer Protection fee to the
&ldquo;Vinted fee&rdquo; in October 2026. Vinted&rsquo;s
[price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist) has the current rates.
