When a Vinted item is not selling, the cause is almost always the price, the first photo or a vague title. Fix them in that order before you pay for anything.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **Work down this list and stop when the item sells.**
- Compare your price with similar listings, buyer total included.
- Make the first photo show the whole item.
- Rewrite the title with brand, colour and type, then fill every field.
- Check postage, send offers to people who favourited, and relist or Bump only an item that has passed those steps. Timing is a tiebreaker, not a fix.

</Callout>

General guidance from Vinted&rsquo;s own help pages, checked 6 November 2026. It is not our sales data and guarantees nothing.

## Why is my Vinted item not selling?

**Most unsold items fail at one of two moments.** Either nobody clicks (photo, title, price in search) or people click and leave (price, condition detail, checkout total).

Vinted&rsquo;s [recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) page says search ranking weighs item attractiveness, including price, condition and how many interactions a listing gets. A listing nobody opens tends to fall further down, so we start with what a buyer sees first.

If you can see views and favourites, they show which moment is failing:

- **Few views:** people are not finding or opening it.
- **Plenty of views, no favourites:** the price or the detail is turning them away.

## 1. How should I price it?

**Compare your price with similar listings.** Vinted&rsquo;s [pricing help page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/376) says to search the catalogue to see how others price similar items, and that &ldquo;Setting a price that&rsquo;s lower than our catalogue average price might help you sell faster.&rdquo; It gives rough starting points by condition:

- **New or like new:** 60% to 80% of retail price.
- **Very good:** 40% to 60%.
- **Good or satisfactory:** 20% to 40%.

These are a starting range, not a rule: a rare brand can beat it, a common high street piece often sits below. Compare the same brand, type, size and condition, and aim for the lower half.

I could not confirm that Vinted shows buyers the final price of sold items, so use live listings as your guide, and do not treat a high asking price on a listing that has sat for months as proof of value.

The same page says a discount may help sell out-of-season items. Our [clothing pricing guide](/blog/how-to-price-second-hand-clothes) has the maths.

## 2. What makes a good first photo?

**The first photo should show the whole item.** Vinted&rsquo;s [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say &ldquo;The first photo should clearly represent the whole item.&rdquo; Photos must show the item as it is, with no image editing, and cannot be stock photos, photos from ads or watermarked images. You can upload up to 20.

It shows in search and the feed, so it does the selling:

- **First photo:** a flat lay or hanger shot in daylight, whole item in frame, plain background.
- **Then:** close-ups of labels, fabric and every flaw. Vinted&rsquo;s [describing an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/49) page asks you to show flaws in the photos as well as the description.

## 3. How should I write the title?

**Use specific details: brand, colour, type, unique features.** The same help page says to avoid words like &ldquo;nice&rdquo;, &ldquo;pretty&rdquo; or &ldquo;good&rdquo;. Its example prefers &ldquo;New pink Zara mini evening dress&rdquo; to &ldquo;Very nice dress&rdquo;.

Buyers search in plain words, so put brand, item, colour and size in the title and description.

Do not add other brand names or hashtags to look like a bigger search term. The Catalogue Rules say using unrelated brands in the brand field, title, description or as a hashtag &ldquo;is not allowed&rdquo;, and &ldquo;inspired by&rdquo; followed by a brand is generally prohibited too. See our guide to the [Vinted algorithm and search](/blog/vinted-algorithm-and-search).

## 4. Which fields matter?

**Search filters work on the fields, not your title.** Vinted says on its [recommended content page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409) that filters are applied first and the remaining listings are then ranked. A wrong category, blank size or missing brand means a buyer who filters never sees the item.

Check each field against the label and photos:

- **Category:** upload each item in its corresponding category, as the Catalogue Rules require.
- **Size:** use the label size, and say in the description if it runs small or large.
- **Brand:** pick it in the brand field. Do not add other brands in the description.
- **Condition:** choose it honestly. Buyers can return an item that is significantly different from the listing.
- **Colour and materials:** fill them in, and note extra materials in the description.

## 5. Is postage putting buyers off?

**The buyer compares your price plus the fee plus postage, not your price alone.** Vinted says postage is always paid by the buyer, who also pays a Vinted fee on top. A £4 item with £3 postage and a fee can look poor value next to a £6 item in a bundle. Our [Vinted fees guide](/blog/vinted-fees-uk) has the figures.

The [selling faster page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/57) gives two levers:

- **More shipping options:** Vinted says more delivery choices may attract more buyers.
- **Bundle discounts:** a buyer taking several items pays one fee on the bundle, which makes cheap stock easier to move.

## 6. Should I send offers?

**Offer to buyers who favourited.** A favourite means interest. The [selling faster page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/57) says: &ldquo;If a buyer favourites your item or asks about it, you can send them an offer. This may help them decide to buy.&rdquo;

On its [offers page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/258), Vinted says:

- Sellers can offer up to 40% less than the original cost.
- There is no limit on the number of seller offers.
- The new price is only visible to the person you send it to. Other buyers still see the original price.

Start with a modest cut, not the full 40%. Our guide to [sending offers on Vinted](/blog/how-to-send-offers-ebay-vinted-depop) shows the steps.

## 7. Can I relist a stale item?

**Relist by hand, once per item, and only after fixing the real cause.** If an item has had a fair run with a good photo, title and price and still sits, a fresh listing can help. Changing nothing just repeats the old one.

Vinted has no relist button, so this means uploading again and deleting the old one. Two rules apply:

- Vinted&rsquo;s [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) forbid external software tools such as bots, and deleting and re-listing &ldquo;the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;.
- The [hidden and removed listings page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) says &ldquo;The same items can&rsquo;t be listed twice on Vinted&rdquo;, so never leave the old and new listing live together.

Our [relisting guide](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) covers the safe way.

## 8. Are Bumps and Showcase worth it?

**Only as a last step.** Vinted says an item Bump is a paid feature you can add to any listing &ldquo;to increase your chance of selling the item&rdquo;. It makes the item appear higher in member newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 consecutive days, or until it sells, at a price shown at payment ([Bump page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340)). Showcase highlights up to 15 listings for 7 days ([Showcase page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452)).

## 9. Does timing matter?

**Least of all, so use it as a tiebreaker.** Vinted&rsquo;s help pages do not give an hour-by-hour guide to listing times. The usual advice is to list in the evening, and our [best time to post on Vinted](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-vinted) guide separates what Vinted has said from received wisdom.

## Questions

### Why are my Vinted items not selling?

Usually price, the first photo or a vague title. Compare your price with similar listings including the buyer&rsquo;s total, make the first photo show the whole item, and put brand, colour and type in the title. Fill every field too, because buyers filter by them. Paid promotion comes last.

### Should I lower my price on Vinted?

If similar items are listed for less and yours has had views but no favourites, yes. Vinted says a price below its catalogue average might help you sell faster. Lower it in steps, or send an offer to people who favourited, rather than one large cut.

### Does Vinted hide items that do not sell?

Vinted does not say that it hides them. Its help pages say search ranking considers item attractiveness, such as how recent a listing is, its interactions, price and condition. An older listing with few interactions can sit lower, so fix the listing rather than waiting.

### Is it worth paying for a Bump on an item that is not selling?

Only after the price, first photo and title are right. According to Vinted, a Bump makes an item appear higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 days, or until it sells. It does not change what the buyer sees when they open the item.

### Can I relist an item that has not sold?

You can upload it again by hand and delete the old one, but not keep both live. Vinted does not allow the same item to be listed twice, and its terms forbid deleting and re-listing in bulk or using bots. Relist rarely, and fix the cause first.

### Do offers help items sell on Vinted?

They can. Vinted suggests sending an offer to a buyer who favourites your item or asks about it. Sellers can offer up to 40% off the original price, and only the person who gets the offer sees the new price.
