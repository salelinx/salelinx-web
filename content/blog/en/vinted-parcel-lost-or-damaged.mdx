When a Vinted parcel goes quiet, what you get back depends on the label, the packing and the proof you kept. This pulls the seller&rsquo;s side together from Vinted&rsquo;s help centre, with a link to the page for every rule.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **With a Vinted label and a carrier-confirmed loss or damage, Vinted can compensate you, up to £500** ([seller compensation](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)).
- A parcel counts as lost once tracking says &ldquo;Parcel seems to be lost&rdquo; or more than 25 business days have passed since you shipped it ([Refund Policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)). Both pages checked 6 November 2026.
- Your payment is held while Vinted investigates, which can take several weeks.
- Damage claims need proper packing, so photograph the parcel before you post.
- With your own postage, you claim from the carrier yourself.

</Callout>

## How do I protect myself before posting?

**Nearly every claim comes down to what you can show.** Do these on every order:

1. **Use the Vinted label.** Compensation only applies to parcels sent with a prepaid Vinted-generated label ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)). With a different label or the wrong shipping option, Vinted says the order is cancelled automatically ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234)).
2. **Pack it properly.** Vinted &ldquo;can only compensate you for a damaged parcel if it was properly packed&rdquo;. See its [packing rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65) in the checklist below.
3. **Keep proof.** Vinted&rsquo;s [carrier pages](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706) say to keep proof of shipping until the order completes (a copy of the label, the drop-off receipt or an email confirmation) and to photograph how the parcel is packed. It also suggests photos of the item and the packed parcel, inside and out.

<Callout variant="warn" title="Royal Mail postboxes">

Vinted&rsquo;s [Royal Mail page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528) asks you not to drop Vinted parcels in a postbox. A postbox gives no tracking events to prove you sent it, and Vinted says no compensation will be offered.

</Callout>

## What if the parcel is lost?

**Vinted gives a parcel time to turn up first.** Under the [Refund Policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465), the investigation can only start once:

- the tracking changes to &ldquo;Parcel seems to be lost&rdquo;, or
- more than 25 business days have passed since you shipped it.

From there:

1. Vinted tells the buyer the parcel seems lost, and the buyer has 2 days to make a claim ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1127)).
2. If they claim, the order is suspended and your payment is held until the issue is resolved.
3. Vinted investigates with the carrier. This &ldquo;may take several weeks&rdquo;.
4. If the loss is confirmed, the order is cancelled and the buyer gets a full refund. With a Vinted label, you can be compensated, up to £500 ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)).

If the buyer does not claim in time, or presses &ldquo;Everything is OK&rdquo;, the order completes and you are paid as normal.

Vinted does not publish exactly how compensation is worked out. Its terms say the amount &ldquo;may depend on which carrier was used&rdquo;, and £500 is the ceiling.

If the parcel turns up after you have been compensated, it is no longer yours: accepting compensation passes ownership to Vinted Go, Vinted&rsquo;s shipping arm.

## What if tracking says delivered but the buyer says it never came?

**The tracking record and your proof of postage carry real weight.** A buyer can ask for a refund when an order was &ldquo;reportedly delivered, but you never received it&rdquo; ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)). They have 2 days from the status update to report it, and the order is then suspended while Vinted investigates.

Vinted&rsquo;s terms say carrier information &ldquo;will be considered accurate&rdquo; unless the other side provides contrary evidence before the order completes. Vinted&rsquo;s help pages do not say what the seller receives if the buyer is refunded here.

## What if it arrives damaged?

**Your compensation depends on the parcel having been properly packed.** The buyer must report the damage within the same 2-day window, with photos of the item and the problem, the outer and inner packaging, and any packaging damage ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)).

- **Single item, or every item in a bundle damaged:** the buyer is refunded without sending anything back.
- **Only some bundle items damaged:** the buyer may be asked to return the whole bundle.

Vinted says you &ldquo;may not receive a refund or compensation&rdquo; if you ignored its packing rules and the parcel was damaged ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65)). This is where your before-posting photos earn their keep.

## What if the buyer never collected it?

**Collect returned parcels quickly.** Parcels sent to a locker, shop or depot are only held for a while. Carriers typically hold them for 2 to 15 calendar days, and the parcel should come back to you within 25 business days ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1445)).

When it arrives, mark it as received and cancel the order, and the buyer is refunded ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/731)). If it has not reached you within 15 business days, Vinted investigates. If it never reaches you, contact Vinted once 25 business days have passed since it was sent back, and you can be compensated as for a lost parcel.

Two things catch sellers out:

- **Missed deadline:** if a returned parcel is not collected by the deadline (plus any extra holding period), ownership can pass to Vinted Go.
- **Wrong address:** if the return shows as delivered somewhere other than your address, contact Vinted within 2 days of the &ldquo;delivered&rdquo; update, or it cannot investigate ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1450)).

## What if I used my own postage?

**You are responsible for lost or damaged parcels and claim from the carrier yourself.** That is Vinted&rsquo;s rule for anything sent other than with its prepaid shipping. Also, an order with no tracking information is cancelled automatically 5 business days after purchase ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/243)). For almost every order, the Vinted label is the safer choice.

## How should I pack a Vinted parcel?

From Vinted&rsquo;s [packing rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65):

- Use sturdy outer packaging that fits the item snugly.
- Do not use bags without padding inside.
- Fill any empty space so the item cannot move around.
- Seal it with strong tape.
- Stick the label flat on the largest side, and remove any old labels.
- For anything fragile, always use a sturdy box, never a plastic bag.

For clothes, Vinted&rsquo;s own example is a padded bag or a cardboard box.

## Questions

### How much compensation does Vinted pay sellers?

Up to £500, for parcels sent with a Vinted-generated label that the carrier
confirms were lost or damaged. Vinted does not publish how the amount is worked out, and its terms say it may depend on the carrier.

### How long does a lost parcel claim take?

Vinted says investigations &ldquo;can take several weeks&rdquo;. A parcel is not treated as lost until its tracking says so or 25 business days have passed since shipping.

### Do I still get paid if the buyer opens a claim?

Not straight away. Your payment is held while Vinted investigates. If the claim
fails, or the buyer never makes one, the order completes and you are paid. If
the buyer is refunded, whether you receive anything depends on the case, as
set out above.

### Can I claim from Evri or InPost directly?

If you used a Vinted label, go through Vinted. InPost&rsquo;s own help says
that for labels bought through a marketplace you need to contact the
marketplace. If you used your own postage, the claim is between you and the
carrier.
