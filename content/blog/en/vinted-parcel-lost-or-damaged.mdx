A parcel goes quiet, the tracking stops moving, and the buyer starts asking
questions. Most of what you need to know is in Vinted&rsquo;s help centre, but
it is spread over a dozen pages and most of them are written for buyers. This
guide pulls the seller&rsquo;s side together, with a link to Vinted&rsquo;s own
page for every rule.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
If you sent the parcel with a Vinted-generated label and the carrier confirms
it was lost or damaged, Vinted can compensate you, up to £500. Your payment is
held while Vinted looks into it, which can take several weeks. Damage claims
depend on the parcel having been properly packed, so take photos before you
post. If you used your own postage, you claim from the carrier yourself.

## Before you post: three things that protect you

Nearly every claim comes down to what you can show. Do these on every order.

1. **Use the Vinted label.** Compensation only applies to parcels sent with a
   prepaid Vinted-generated label
   ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)). If you use a different label
   or the wrong shipping option, Vinted says the order is cancelled
   automatically ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234)).
2. **Pack it properly.** Vinted &ldquo;can only compensate you for a damaged
   parcel if it was properly packed&rdquo;. Its
   [packing rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65) are summed up in the
   checklist below.
3. **Keep proof.** Vinted&rsquo;s carrier pages say to keep your proof of
   shipping until the order is completed (a copy of the label, the drop-off
   receipt or an email confirmation) and to take a photo of how the parcel is
   packed ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706)). Vinted also suggests
   photos of the item and the packed parcel, inside and out.

<Callout variant="warn" title="Royal Mail postboxes">
Vinted&rsquo;s [Royal Mail page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528) asks you
not to drop Vinted parcels in a postbox. A postbox gives no tracking events to
prove you sent it, and Vinted says no compensation will be offered.
</Callout>

## If the parcel is lost

Vinted gives a parcel time to turn up before it treats it as lost. Under its
[Refund Policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465), the investigation can only
start once:

- the tracking changes to &ldquo;Parcel seems to be lost&rdquo;, or
- more than 25 business days have passed since you shipped it.

From there:

1. Vinted tells the buyer the parcel seems lost, and the buyer has 2 days to
   make a claim ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1127)).
2. If they claim, the order is suspended and your payment is held until the
   issue is resolved.
3. Vinted investigates with the carrier. This &ldquo;may take several
   weeks&rdquo;.
4. If the loss is confirmed, the order is cancelled and the buyer gets a full
   refund. If you used a Vinted label, you can be compensated, up to £500
   ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)).

If the buyer does not claim in time, or presses &ldquo;Everything is
OK&rdquo;, the order completes and you are paid as normal.

Vinted does not publish exactly how your compensation is worked out. Its terms
say the amount &ldquo;may depend on which carrier was used&rdquo;, and the
£500 is the ceiling.

If the parcel turns up after you have been compensated, it is no longer
yours: when you accept compensation for a lost parcel, ownership of it passes
to Vinted Go, Vinted&rsquo;s shipping arm.

## If tracking says delivered but the buyer says it never came

The buyer can ask for a refund when an order was &ldquo;reportedly delivered,
but you never received it&rdquo;
([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)). They have 2 days from the
status update to report it, and the order is then suspended while Vinted
investigates.

Vinted&rsquo;s terms say the information from the carrier &ldquo;will be
considered accurate&rdquo; unless the other side provides evidence to the
contrary before the order completes. So the tracking record and your proof of
postage carry real weight. Vinted&rsquo;s help pages do not say what the seller
receives if the buyer is refunded in this situation.

## If it arrives damaged

The buyer has to report the damage within the same 2-day window and send
photos of the item and the problem, the outer and inner packaging, and any
damage to the packaging ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)).

- If it was a single item, or every item in a bundle was damaged, the buyer is
  refunded without sending anything back.
- If only some items in a bundle were damaged, the buyer may be asked to
  return the whole bundle.

Your compensation depends on the parcel having been properly packed. Vinted
says you &ldquo;may not receive a refund or compensation&rdquo; if you ignored
its packing rules and the parcel was damaged
([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65)). This is where the photos you took
before posting earn their keep.

## If the buyer never collected it

Parcels sent to a locker, shop or depot are only held for a while. Vinted says
carriers typically hold them for 2 to 15 calendar days, and the parcel should
come back to you within 25 business days
([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1445)).

When it arrives, mark it as received and cancel the order, and the buyer is
refunded ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/731)). If it has not reached
you within 15 business days, Vinted investigates. If it never reaches you,
contact Vinted once 25 business days have passed since it was sent back, and
you can be compensated in the same way as a lost parcel.

Two things catch sellers out:

- **Collect returned parcels quickly.** If a returned parcel is not collected
  by the deadline (plus any extra holding period), ownership can pass to
  Vinted Go.
- **Wrong address?** If the return shows as delivered somewhere other than
  your address, contact Vinted within 2 days of the &ldquo;delivered&rdquo;
  update, or it cannot investigate
  ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1450)).

## If you used your own postage

Vinted&rsquo;s terms are plain: if you send a parcel any other way than with
Vinted&rsquo;s prepaid shipping, you are responsible for lost or damaged
parcels and for claiming from the carrier yourself. On top of that, an order
with no tracking information is cancelled automatically 5 business days after
purchase ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/243)). For almost every
order, the Vinted label is the safer choice.

## Packing checklist

From Vinted&rsquo;s [packing rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65):

- Use sturdy outer packaging that fits the item snugly.
- Do not use bags without padding inside.
- Fill any empty space so the item cannot move around.
- Seal it with strong tape.
- Stick the label flat on the largest side, and remove any old labels.
- For anything fragile, always use a sturdy box, never a plastic bag.

For clothes, Vinted&rsquo;s own example is a padded bag or a cardboard box.

## Questions

### How much compensation does Vinted pay sellers?

Up to £500, for parcels sent with a Vinted-generated label that the carrier
confirms were lost or damaged. Vinted does not publish how the amount is worked
out, and its terms say it may depend on the carrier.

### How long does a lost parcel claim take?

Vinted says investigations &ldquo;can take several weeks&rdquo;, and a parcel
is not treated as lost until its tracking says so or 25 business days have
passed since shipping.

### Do I still get paid if the buyer opens a claim?

Not straight away. Your payment is held while Vinted investigates. If the claim
fails, or the buyer never makes one, the order completes and you are paid. If
the buyer is refunded, whether you receive anything depends on the case, as
set out above.

### Can I claim from Evri or InPost directly?

If you used a Vinted label, go through Vinted. InPost&rsquo;s own help says
that for labels bought through a marketplace you need to contact the
marketplace. If you used your own postage, the claim is between you and the
carrier.
