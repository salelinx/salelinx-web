## Should I discount for Black Friday?

**Only where it helps you sell something that was going to sit.** Buyers expect deals in late November, but a Vinted or Depop sale price comes straight out of your take. A cut on every listing mostly gives away money from people who would have paid full price.

Better places to cut:

- **Items that have sat for weeks.** If a listing has had views but no sale, the price is usually the cause. Our guide to [pricing second-hand clothes](/blog/how-to-price-second-hand-clothes) shows how to work out your floor.
- **Bundles.** A buyer who takes three items pays postage once, and you post one parcel in the busiest fortnight of the year.
- **Buyers who already showed interest,** with a private offer rather than a public price drop.

Check what each sale leaves you after fees first. See our guides to [Vinted fees](/blog/vinted-fees-uk), [Depop fees](/blog/depop-fees-uk) and [eBay fees](/blog/ebay-fees-uk).

## Should I send offers to people who liked my items?

**Yes, and it is the lowest-risk discount you can run.** A like, a watch or a basket add is someone telling you they are interested. A private offer costs you only on that one sale, and nobody else sees the lower price.

Start modestly, because a buyer can counter and you can still agree a middle price. On Vinted the new price is visible only to the person you send it to, and on eBay offers go to interested buyers rather than the whole marketplace.

## What should I list for Christmas?

**Anything a person would be glad to unwrap, with a photo and a title that say so.** Gift-friendly stock is clean, boxed or easy to wrap, and sensibly priced for someone buying for another person.

- **Say what it is for in the title and description:** brand, item, size and condition. Buyers searching for gifts still filter by those fields.
- **Show condition honestly.** A gift that arrives different from the listing becomes a refund, and December refunds are slow to turn into a resale.
- **Get new listings up by early December,** while a sale can still be posted in time.
- **Think small and light.** Cheaper postage helps because buyers compare the total, as we explain in [why Vinted items do not sell](/blog/vinted-items-not-selling).

Treat those as the last possible day, not the target. Evri says winter weather and heavy seasonal volume &ldquo;can sometimes cause unexpected delays&rdquo;. If a buyer pays on Friday 11 December, post it that week.

Two Vinted rules affect when you can safely accept a late order. You have [5 business days to ship](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/740-i-cant-send-the-item-on-time) after a purchase, or the order is cancelled, and you can ask the buyer to extend the deadline by 3 or 5 business days. ## How do I stop orders while I am away over Christmas?

**Use each marketplace&rsquo;s away setting before you travel, not after the first order arrives.**

- **Vinted holiday mode:** it hides all your listings for up to 90 days, which &ldquo;will prevent buyers from starting new orders with you&rdquo;. It does not extend purchased Bumps or Showcase ([Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/33-using-holiday-mode)).
- **Depop vacation mode:** according to the [Depop help centre](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12267530258833-Vacation-Mode), it pauses purchases from your shop for 7 days and then switches itself off, so set it again if you are away longer.
- **eBay Time Away:** you can pause item sales, which hides fixed price listings from search, or allow sales with a message and updated delivery dates. Auction-style listings stay visible either way. It can take a few hours to hide listings, so set it in advance ([eBay](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/selling-tools/time-away?id=5137)).

## Are there longer Christmas returns windows?

**Not from any of the three that we could find.** We found no announcement of an extended Christmas returns period from Vinted, Depop or eBay.

On Vinted, a buyer has [2 days from when the parcel should have been delivered](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465-the-vinted-refund-policy) to report an item that is significantly not as described. eBay&rsquo;s peak season guide lists 30-day returns as something buyers look for, as part of its Retail Standards for business sellers. If you offer longer returns for gifts, say so in the listing.

## Questions

### When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November 2026.

### What is the last posting date for Christmas 2026 in the UK?

For Royal Mail 2nd Class it is Friday 18 December. Royal Mail Tracked 48 is Saturday 19 December, 1st Class and Tracked 24 are Monday 21 December, and Special Delivery Guaranteed is Wednesday 23 December. Evri courier collections end on Friday 18 December. Dates are subject to change, so check the carrier&rsquo;s site.

### Have DPD and InPost published Christmas 2026 posting dates?

Not on their own sites when we checked on 8 November 2026. DPD lists its closed days, including Christmas Day and Boxing Day, but no last posting dates. Check again in December and post early.

### Should I put my Vinted, Depop or eBay shop on holiday over Christmas?

If you cannot post parcels, yes. Vinted holiday mode hides your listings for up to 90 days, Depop vacation mode pauses purchases for 7 days, and eBay Time Away can pause sales or keep listings live with a notice.

### Can private eBay sellers run a Black Friday sale?

Not with sale events. eBay says that since 20 January 2025 coupons, sale events and order discounts are no longer included in Shop subscriptions for private sellers. You can still send offers to interested buyers.

### Is it worth discounting everything on Black Friday?

Usually not. A site-wide cut gives money away on items that would have sold at full price. Target stale stock, bundles and offers to people who liked or watched an item instead.
