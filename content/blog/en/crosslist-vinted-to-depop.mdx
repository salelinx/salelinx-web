Crosslisting means listing the same item on Vinted and Depop, so both sets of buyers see it. Neither site has a button for it: you copy it by hand, or use an app.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- Copy the photos, title, description, brand, size and condition.
- Add what Depop wants: a postage price, up to five hashtags and a style.
- **Vinted takes no seller fee, but Depop takes 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing.** Price the Depop copy a little higher to keep the same.
- **When it sells on one site, take it down on the other that day.**

</Callout>

## Is the item allowed on both?

**Vinted is the stricter side.** Its [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say:

- A standard account cannot list items you have &ldquo;specifically bought or sourced to resell&rdquo;. That is a Vinted Pro matter, wherever the item is also listed.
- You should &ldquo;only have one listing per item&rdquo;.
- It is &ldquo;forbidden to upload an item that is not available or already sold&rdquo;. That makes stock sync a requirement.

Your own second-hand clothes are fine. Depop&rsquo;s [selling guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360009418014-Selling-on-Depop) asks for original photos, so use your own.

The details behind that table:

- **Photos.** Vinted takes far more than Depop. If yours has 12, pick the best four to eight and lead with the clearest full-item shot. Neither help page states a required shape. Depop&rsquo;s [web listing page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/8608273715217-Listing-on-web) lets you choose and crop a cover image, so check the first photo after cropping.
- **Hashtags and description.** Depop&rsquo;s tips ask for words a buyer would search, measurements, condition and flaws, kept &ldquo;short and to the point&rdquo;. Vinted has no hashtag field, so there are no tags to copy. Neither page publishes a character limit, so I have not quoted one.
- **Category and size.** They do not match word for word. Pick the closest Depop category, then check the size reads the same as the label.
- **Condition and style.** Choose the nearest Depop condition and describe flaws in words. Depop&rsquo;s style field is extra, so pick the one that fits.

## How to crosslist Vinted to Depop by hand

### 1. Gather the listing

Copy the title, description, brand, size, condition and colour from Vinted. Use the original photos from your phone if you still have them.

### 2. Start a new Depop listing

Depop&rsquo;s [listing guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360032716413-How-to-list-an-item) has the steps. In the app, tap Sell, add photos, describe the item, add category, shipping price, brand and condition, set a price and tap Post listing. On the website you click Sell now instead.

You can save a draft and come back to it. Drafts saved in the app show up on the web and the other way round.

### 3. Rewrite the description

**Do not paste the Vinted text as it is.** Depop buyers search in style words. Put the item&rsquo;s name first, then measurements, condition and flaws, then up to five truthful hashtags. Depop&rsquo;s [search page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360050410093-Improving-the-search-ranking-of-your-items) warns that unrelated information, such as a brand you are not selling, &ldquo;can hurt your listing&rsquo;s exposure&rdquo;.

### 4. Set the shipping and item price

Vinted never asked for a postage price. Depop does. Set it from the real cost, and read Depop&rsquo;s [UK shipping guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK) before choosing between Depop Shipping and your own label. Depop also shows a suggested price range and similar sold items.

### 5. Post it and keep a list

Note which items are on both sites, even just on your phone. You need it for the sync step below.

## What are the fees on each side?

**Neither site charges a UK seller a selling fee, but they do not cost the same.**

- **Vinted:** &ldquo;no selling fees on Vinted&rdquo; ([fee page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1653)). The buyer pays the Vinted fee at checkout, which the [price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist) (last updated 8 October 2026) puts at &ldquo;usually 3% to 8% + £0.3 to £0.8&rdquo;. What you set is what you keep. See our [Vinted fees guide](/blog/vinted-fees-uk).
- **Depop:** no selling fee, but a 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing fee, charged on the item price plus shipping and any tax ([seller fees page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges)). The buyer pays a separate [Marketplace fee](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee) of up to 5% of the item price plus up to £1. A buyer buying through a boosted listing adds a 12% boosting fee. See our [Depop fees guide](/blog/depop-fees-uk).

## How do you keep stock in sync?

**The real risk is a double sale.** It sells on Depop on Tuesday, you post it Wednesday, and on Thursday a Vinted buyer pays for it too. You cancel an order, the buyer has waited for nothing, and Vinted&rsquo;s rule against listing a sold item is breached.

**Take the other listing down before you post the parcel.** Our guide to [not selling the same item twice](/blog/stop-selling-same-item-twice) covers habits and tools. If you cannot take listings down for a few days, Depop&rsquo;s Vacation Mode pauses purchases from your shop for 7 days ([help page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12267530258833-Vacation-Mode)).

## Depop to Vinted

The reverse is easier on typing and harder on rules.

- **One listing per item.** The catalogue rules say so, and Vinted&rsquo;s [upload guide](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/375) says identical items cannot be listed twice. For several of the same item, make one listing and give the number in the description.
- **Photos.** Depop allows fewer, so add the extra Vinted angles: labels, flaws and the size tag. Vinted says to show defects in photos and mention them in the description.
- **Postage does not carry over.** Per Vinted&rsquo;s [shipping page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753), the buyer pays and picks the carrier, and you choose a parcel size.
- **Hashtags do not carry over.** Move the useful words into the description in plain sentences.
- **No bulk tools.** Vinted&rsquo;s [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions), applicable from 8 October 2026, say members must not use &ldquo;any kind of external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs, crawling programs, spiders)&rdquo; unless authorised, or &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;. Read them before using any app that touches your Vinted account, and keep Vinted activity to the pace of a person listing by hand. More in our [relisting guide](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted).

## Questions

### Can I cross list from Vinted to Depop for free?

Yes, by hand. Listing is free on Vinted, and Depop does not charge UK sellers a selling fee, so the only cost is your time. Depop does take a 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing fee when an item sells. Apps that do the copying for you charge a monthly fee.

### Do I have to price differently on Depop than on Vinted?

If you want to keep the same amount, yes, slightly. Vinted charges the seller nothing, while Depop takes payment processing of 2.9% plus £0.30, which is 88p on a £20 item. A buyer on each site also pays their own fee on top, so the price they see is higher than the one you set.

### What happens if the item sells on both at once?

You have to cancel one order. Cancel the later one as soon as you see it and message the buyer. Vinted forbids listing an item that is already sold, so hide the Vinted listing the moment a Depop sale comes in.

### Why does my Vinted description not work on Depop?

Depop buyers search with style words, and Depop&rsquo;s own tips ask for a searchable title, measurements, condition and up to five hashtags. A short Vinted description with none of that gives the Depop search less to match. Rewrite it for Depop rather than pasting it.

### Is it against Vinted&rsquo;s rules to use a crosslisting app?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms restrict external software tools and separately ban deleting and re-listing items in bulk. They do not mention crosslisting by name. Read the current terms, keep activity on your Vinted account to what a person would do by hand, and never let a tool bulk delete and re-list your Vinted items.
