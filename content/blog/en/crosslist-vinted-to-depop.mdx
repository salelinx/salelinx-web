To cross list from Vinted to Depop, you make a second listing for the same item on Depop, with the same photos and details, so it is in front of both sets of buyers. Neither site has a button for it. You either rebuild the listing by hand in Depop&rsquo;s sell form, or use a crosslisting app that reads your Vinted listing and creates the Depop copy. The copying is the easy part. The parts that catch people out are the fields the two forms ask for differently, and taking the item down on one site the moment it sells on the other.

This guide covers Vinted to Depop first, then Depop to Vinted, then fees and stock sync. Every rule and fee links to the marketplace&rsquo;s own page, checked on 6 November 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
Copy the photos, title, description, brand, size and condition across, then fill in what Depop adds: a postage price, up to five hashtags and a style. A seller keeps the full price on Vinted, but pays 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing on Depop, so price the Depop copy a little higher if you want to take home the same. Whenever the item sells on one site, take it down on the other that day.
</Callout>

## Before you start: is the item allowed on both?

Vinted is the stricter side. Its [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say a standard account cannot be used to list items you have &ldquo;specifically bought or sourced to resell&rdquo;, and that you should &ldquo;only have one listing per item&rdquo;. Your own second-hand clothes are fine. Stock you bought to flip is a Vinted Pro matter, wherever it is also listed.

The same rules make it &ldquo;forbidden to upload an item that is not available or already sold&rdquo;. That is the rule that turns stock sync from a nicety into a requirement. Depop&rsquo;s [selling guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360009418014-Selling-on-Depop) asks for original photos, so use your own pictures rather than anyone else&rsquo;s.

A few details behind that table:

- **Photos.** Vinted takes far more pictures than Depop does. If your Vinted listing has 12, pick the best four to eight for Depop, and lead with the clearest full-item shot. Neither help page states a required shape, such as square or portrait. Depop&rsquo;s [web listing page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/8608273715217-Listing-on-web) lets you choose a cover image and crop it, so check how the first photo looks after cropping before you post.
- **Hashtags and description.** Depop&rsquo;s description tips ask for a title with words a buyer would search, measurements, condition and flaws, and say to keep it &ldquo;short and to the point&rdquo;. Vinted has no hashtag field, so a Vinted description will not come with tags to copy. Neither page publishes a character limit, so I have not quoted one.
- **Category and size.** Vinted&rsquo;s categories and sizes do not match Depop&rsquo;s word for word. Pick the closest Depop category, then check the size reads the same as the label.
- **Condition and style.** Choose the nearest Depop condition to the Vinted one and describe any flaw in words. Depop&rsquo;s style field is extra. Pick the one that fits.

## Vinted to Depop by hand

### 1. Gather the listing

Open the Vinted listing and copy the title, description, brand, size, condition and colour somewhere you can see them. Use the original photos from your phone if you still have them.

### 2. Start a new listing on Depop

Depop&rsquo;s [listing guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360032716413-How-to-list-an-item) has the steps. In the app, tap Sell, add your photos, describe the item, add the category, shipping price, brand and condition, set a price and tap Post listing. On the website you click Sell now instead, and you can save a draft and come back to it. Drafts saved in the app show up on the web and the other way round.

### 3. Rewrite the description for Depop

Do not paste the Vinted text as it is. Depop buyers search in style words, so put the item&rsquo;s name and what it is first, then measurements, condition and flaws, then up to five hashtags that describe the item truthfully. Depop&rsquo;s [search page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360050410093-Improving-the-search-ranking-of-your-items) warns that adding unrelated information, such as a brand you are not selling, &ldquo;can hurt your listing&rsquo;s exposure&rdquo;.

### 4. Set the shipping price and the item price

Vinted never asked you for a postage price. Depop does. Set it from the real cost of the way you plan to send the item, and read Depop&rsquo;s [UK shipping guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK) before you choose between Depop Shipping and your own label. Then price with the fees in the next section in mind. Depop also shows a suggested price range and similar sold items as a guide, which is useful if you have never sold that item there.

### 5. Post it, then note it down

Once it is live, write down which items are on both sites. Even a note on your phone works. That list is what makes the next section possible.

## Fees on each side

Neither site charges a UK seller a selling fee, but they do not cost the same.

**Vinted.** Its [fee page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1653) says there are &ldquo;no selling fees on Vinted&rdquo;. The buyer pays the Vinted fee at checkout, which the [price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist) (last updated 8 October 2026) puts at &ldquo;usually 3% to 8% + £0.3 to £0.8&rdquo;. What you set is what you keep. Our [Vinted fees guide](/blog/vinted-fees-uk) has the detail.

**Depop.** UK sellers pay no Depop selling fee, but Depop&rsquo;s [seller fees page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) lists a 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing fee, charged on the item price plus shipping and any tax. The buyer pays a separate [Marketplace fee](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee) of up to 5% of the item price plus up to £1. If a buyer buys through a boosted listing, a 12% boosting fee applies too. Our [Depop fees guide](/blog/depop-fees-uk) works through each one.

## Keep stock in sync, or sell the same thing twice

The one real risk of listing on both is a double sale. It sells on Depop on Tuesday, you post it on Wednesday, and on Thursday a Vinted buyer pays for the same item. Now you are cancelling an order, and the buyer has waited for nothing. Vinted&rsquo;s rule against listing an item that is already sold makes that a breach in its own right.

Make the takedown the first thing you do after a sale, not the last. Our guide to [not selling the same item twice](/blog/stop-selling-same-item-twice) covers the habits and tools that make it routine. Depop also has a Vacation Mode that pauses purchases from your shop for 7 days (see its [help page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12267530258833-Vacation-Mode)), which is useful if you cannot take listings down for a few days.

## Depop to Vinted

The reverse direction is easier on typing and harder on rules.

- **One listing per item.** Vinted&rsquo;s catalogue rules say you should only have one listing per item, and its [upload guide](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/375) says identical items cannot be listed twice. If you have several of the same item, make one listing and say how many in the description.
- **Photos.** Depop allows fewer, so add the extra Vinted angles: labels, flaws and the size tag. Vinted says to show defects in the photos and mention them in the description.
- **Postage.** Your Depop shipping price does not carry over. On Vinted, per its [shipping page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753), the buyer pays and picks the carrier, and you choose a parcel size when you list.
- **Hashtags.** They do not carry over either. Move the useful words into the description in plain sentences.
- **No bulk tools.** Vinted&rsquo;s [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions), applicable from 8 October 2026, say members must not use &ldquo;any kind of external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs, crawling programs, spiders)&rdquo; unless authorised, and must not &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;. Read them before you use any app that touches your Vinted account, and keep what you do on Vinted to the pace of a person listing by hand. Our [relisting guide](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) goes further.

## Using an app instead

Doing all of the above for one item takes about ten minutes. For fifty items it is a weekend, which is why crosslisting apps exist. They read a listing on one site, map the category, brand, size and condition to the other site&rsquo;s lists, copy the photos and create the listing. Our [best crosslisting apps](/blog/best-crosslisting-apps-uk) post compares the ones that cover Vinted and Depop.

Whatever you use, check what the app does after a sale. The sync rule above is still your job unless the app links the two listings and takes the other one down.

## Questions

### Can I cross list from Vinted to Depop for free?

Yes, by hand. Listing is free on Vinted, and Depop does not charge UK sellers a selling fee, so the only cost is your time. Depop does take a 2.9% plus £0.30 payment processing fee when an item sells. Apps that do the copying for you charge a monthly fee.

### Do I have to price differently on Depop than on Vinted?

If you want to keep the same amount, yes, slightly. Vinted charges the seller nothing, while Depop takes payment processing of 2.9% plus £0.30, which is 88p on a £20 item. A buyer on each site also pays their own fee on top, so the price they see is higher than the one you set.

### What happens if the item sells on both at once?

You have to cancel one order. Cancel the later one as soon as you see it and message the buyer. Vinted forbids listing an item that is already sold, so hide the Vinted listing the moment a Depop sale comes in.

### Why does my Vinted description not work on Depop?

Depop buyers search with style words, and Depop&rsquo;s own tips ask for a searchable title, measurements, condition and up to five hashtags. A short Vinted description with none of that gives the Depop search less to match. Rewrite it for Depop rather than pasting it.

### Is it against Vinted&rsquo;s rules to use a crosslisting app?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms restrict external software tools and separately ban deleting and re-listing items in bulk. They do not mention crosslisting by name. Read the current terms, keep activity on your Vinted account to what a person would do by hand, and never let a tool bulk delete and re-list your Vinted items.
