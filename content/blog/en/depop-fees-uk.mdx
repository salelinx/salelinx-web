UK sellers pay one Depop fee they always pay and one they can avoid. The 10%
figure on many search results is the old fee, which ended for UK listings in
March 2024.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **No selling fee for UK sellers.**
- **Payment processing: 2.9% + £0.30** on every Buy button sale. On £20 you pay £0.88 and keep £19.12, before postage costs.
- **Boosting fee: 12%,** only if the buyer came through a boosted listing. On £20 that is £2.40, so you keep £16.72.
- The buyer pays a separate Marketplace fee of up to 5% plus up to £1, which does not come out of your money.
- Fees are taken before the payout and refunded if you refund the sale.

</Callout>

## What do UK sellers pay on Depop?

Depop's [seller fees and charges](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges)
page says: "No Depop Selling fees will be charged to sellers based in the UK,
US, or AUS." That leaves two charges, checked on 6 November 2026.

- **Payment processing: 2.9% + £0.30.** Listed for Depop Payments (via
  Stripe) and "charged on the item sale price plus shipping costs and any
  applicable taxes", so the percentage also applies to postage the buyer pays.
- **Boosting fee: 12%, only on boosted sales.** Explained below.

These apply to Buy button sales. All fees are deducted before proceeds reach
your bank, and if a sale is refunded the selling, processing and boosting fees
are reversed too. For Depop Payments sales that happens automatically.

Listing is free, and the Depop pages cited here show no withdrawal fee. Depop's
[payout page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK)
says funds can take up to 5 days to appear in your bank after they are sent.

## Is the Depop fee still 10%?

**Not for UK sellers.** Depop's own
[announcement](https://news.depop.com/evolving-our-fee-structure-with-zero-selling-fees-on-depop/)
says the selling fee was dropped on "new listings created from 20th March
onwards", with a buyer Marketplace fee starting "from 15th April 2024". The
current fees page charges the 10% selling fee only to sellers outside the UK,
US and Australia.

If a page tells you to price in a 10% UK fee, check its date. It describes the
structure from before March 2024.

## How much is the boosted listing fee?

[Boosted Listings](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/10110299060753-Boosted-Listings)
is Depop's paid promotion. **Boosting is optional, and the fee is only charged
when it leads to a sale.**

- In the UK, Depop charges 12% of the item sale price (excluding taxes), plus
  any shipping costs if you arranged your own. It applies whether you boost one
  listing or your whole shop.
- You pay it if a buyer saw the boosted version and bought within 28 days.
  Depop's
  [Boosted Listings Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9253129255569-Boosted-Listings-Policy)
  says simply seeing the boosted tile counts, and unboosting does not cancel a
  fee on a buyer who saw it before.
- If the buyer did not come through a boosted tile, there is no boosting fee.
- Boosting fees are not charged on Depop Shipping labels.

The 12% rate applies to new listings from 23 March 2026, the date the policy
took effect. Treat an older figure as out of date.

**Cost it before you switch on Boost Shop.** At 12% plus processing, a £20
item leaves £16.72. If your margin is thin, boost single items you would
struggle to move, not everything.

## What do buyers pay?

Depop's [Marketplace fee page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee)
says UK buyers pay "up to 5% of the item purchase price, plus a fixed amount of
up to £/$1", excluding taxes and shipping. The exact amount shows at checkout,
and it is refunded in full if the item is refunded.

You do not pay it, but the buyer sees your price plus this fee plus postage,
and compares that total with other shops. On a bundle, the percentage is worked
out on the combined price and the fixed amount is charged once.

## Who pays postage?

It depends on how you set the item up. Depop's
[How to ship - UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK)
page gives two routes:

- **Depop Shipping (via Evri):** Depop emails a QR code for an Evri drop-off,
  with a printable label too. It is tracked, so you add no tracking by hand.
- **Your own way:** your shipping method shows as "Other". You arrange and pay
  for postage, use a tracked service and enter the tracking in Depop.

**Only Depop Shipping gets seller protection.** Depop's
[seller protection](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001845367-Depop-Protection-for-sellers)
covers lost or damaged parcels only when you ship with a Depop Shipping label
within 5 days, up to £250 including postage. The boosting fee is not charged on
a Depop Shipping label, but the 2.9% processing fee still covers the postage
the buyer pays.

## When do you get paid?

Sales go into your Depop Balance, then to your bank. Per Depop's
[UK payout page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK):

- **Depop Shipping:** payment 2 business days after delivery shows, or 10
  business days after the sale, whichever comes first.
- **Your own shipping:** 10 business days after delivery.
- Funds can take up to 5 days to reach your bank.

You must add a bank account and verify your identity before money can leave
your balance.

## Questions

### Does Depop charge sellers a fee in the UK?

There is no selling fee for UK sellers. You pay 2.9% + £0.30 payment
processing on sales made with the Buy button. If the buyer came through a
boosted listing, you also pay a 12% boosting fee. Both are taken before your
payout.

### Is the Depop seller fee still 10%?

Not for UK sellers. Depop dropped the 10% selling fee for UK listings created
from 20 March 2024. It still applies to sellers outside the UK, US and
Australia. Pages quoting 10% for the UK are out of date.

### How much does a boosted listing cost on Depop?

Nothing up front. Depop charges 12% of the sale price in the UK only if a buyer
sees your boosted listing and buys the item within 28 days. The fee is on top of
payment processing, and it is refunded if the sale is refunded.

### Who pays the Depop Marketplace fee?

The buyer. It is up to 5% of the item price plus up to £1, shown at checkout. It
does not reduce what you receive and it is refunded if the order is refunded.

### When do I get paid on Depop?

With Depop Shipping, Depop sends your payment 2 business days after delivery or
10 business days after the sale, whichever is first. With your own shipping it is
10 business days after delivery. The money can take up to 5 more days to reach
your bank.
