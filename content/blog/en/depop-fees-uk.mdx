Depop fees for UK sellers in 2026 come down to one fee you always pay and
one you can avoid: 2.9% + £0.30 payment processing on every sale made with the
Buy button, and a 12% boosting fee only on sales that come through a boosted
listing. There is no selling fee. The 10% figure still shown on many search
results is the old Depop fee, and it ended for UK listings in March 2024. This
guide sets out each charge with Depop's own page linked, as checked on 6
November 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
UK sellers pay no Depop selling fee. On a £20 sale you pay £0.88 in payment
processing and keep £19.12, before postage costs. If the buyer found the item
through a boosted listing you also pay 12%, £2.40 on £20, so you keep £16.72.
The buyer pays a separate Marketplace fee of up to 5% plus up to £1, which
does not come out of your money. Fees are taken before the payout and are
refunded if you refund the sale.
</Callout>

## Depop fees for UK sellers: what you pay

Depop's [seller fees and charges](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges)
page says: "No Depop Selling fees will be charged to sellers based in the UK,
US, or AUS." That leaves two charges.

- **Payment processing: 2.9% + £0.30.** The page lists this for Depop
  Payments (via Stripe) and says it is "charged on the item sale price plus
  shipping costs and any applicable taxes". So the percentage also applies to
  the postage the buyer pays.
- **Boosting fee: 12%, only on boosted sales.** Explained below.

These apply to sales made through the Buy button. Depop says all fees are
deducted before your sale proceeds reach your bank account, and that if a sale
is refunded, the selling, processing and boosting fees are reversed too. For
Depop Payments sales that happens automatically.

Listing is free. There is no fee to withdraw your balance on the Depop pages
cited here, though Depop's
[payout page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK)
says it can take up to 5 days for funds to appear in your bank after they are
sent.

## The 10% fee is the old one

Many guides still say Depop takes 10%. That was true until 2024. Depop's own
[announcement](https://news.depop.com/evolving-our-fee-structure-with-zero-selling-fees-on-depop/)
says the selling fee was dropped for UK sellers on "new listings created from
20th March onwards", with a buyer Marketplace fee starting "from 15th April
2024". Depop's current fees page now charges the 10% selling fee only to
sellers outside the UK, US and Australia.

So if a page tells you to price in a 10% UK fee, check its date. It is
describing the structure from before March 2024.

## Boosted listings: the 12% fee

[Boosted Listings](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/10110299060753-Boosted-Listings)
is Depop's paid promotion. Boosting is optional, and the fee is only charged
when it leads to a sale:

- In the UK, Depop charges 12% of the item sale price (excluding taxes), plus
  any shipping costs if you arranged your own. It is charged whether you boost
  one listing or your whole shop.
- You pay it if a buyer saw the boosted version of your listing and bought the
  item within 28 days. Depop's
  [Boosted Listings Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9253129255569-Boosted-Listings-Policy)
  says simply seeing the boosted tile counts, and that unboosting does not
  cancel a fee on a buyer who saw it before.
- If the buyer did not come through a boosted tile, there is no boosting fee.
- Boosting fees are not charged on Depop Shipping labels.

The fees page says the 12% rate applies to new listings from 23 March 2026, and
the policy is effective from that date. If you read an older figure for the
boosting fee, treat it as out of date.

Boosting is worth costing before you switch on Boost Shop. At 12% plus
processing, a £20 item leaves you £16.72 as the table shows. If your margin is
thin, boost single items you would otherwise struggle to move, not everything.

## What buyers pay: the Marketplace fee

Depop's [Marketplace fee page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee)
says UK buyers pay "up to 5% of the item purchase price, plus a fixed amount of
up to £/$1", excluding taxes and shipping. The exact amount is shown at
checkout. It is refunded in full if the item is refunded.

You do not pay it, but the buyer sees your price plus this fee plus postage.
That total is what they compare with other shops, so it matters for pricing.
On a bundle, the percentage is worked out on the combined price and the fixed
amount is charged once.

## Postage

Postage is a cost for the buyer or you, depending on how you set the item up.
Depop's [How to ship - UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK)
page gives two routes:

- **Depop Shipping (via Evri).** Depop emails a QR code for an Evri drop-off,
  with a printable label too. It is tracked, so you do not add tracking by hand.
- **Your own way.** Your shipping method shows as "Other". You arrange and pay
  for postage, use a tracked service and enter the tracking in Depop.

The choice affects more than cost. Depop's
[seller protection](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001845367-Depop-Protection-for-sellers)
covers lost or damaged parcels only when you ship with a Depop Shipping label
within 5 days, up to £250 including postage. The boosting fee is not charged on
a Depop Shipping label, but the 2.9% processing fee still covers the postage
paid by the buyer.

## Payouts

Sales go into your Depop Balance and then to your bank account. Depop's
[UK payout page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK)
says that with Depop Shipping it sends payment 2 business days after the item
shows as delivered, or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.
With your own shipping it is 10 business days after delivery. Funds can take up
to 5 days to reach your bank. You must add a bank account and verify your
identity before money can leave your balance.

## Questions

### Does Depop charge sellers a fee in the UK?

There is no selling fee for UK sellers. You pay 2.9% + £0.30 payment
processing on sales made with the Buy button. If the buyer came through a
boosted listing, you also pay a 12% boosting fee. Both are taken before your
payout.

### Is the Depop seller fee still 10%?

Not for UK sellers. Depop dropped the 10% selling fee for UK listings created
from 20 March 2024. It still applies to sellers outside the UK, US and
Australia. Pages quoting 10% for the UK are out of date.

### How much does a boosted listing cost on Depop?

Nothing up front. Depop charges 12% of the sale price in the UK only if a buyer
sees your boosted listing and buys the item within 28 days. The fee is on top of
payment processing, and it is refunded if the sale is refunded.

### Who pays the Depop Marketplace fee?

The buyer. It is up to 5% of the item price plus up to £1, shown at checkout. It
does not reduce what you receive and it is refunded if the order is refunded.

### When do I get paid on Depop?

With Depop Shipping, Depop sends your payment 2 business days after delivery or
10 business days after the sale, whichever is first. With your own shipping it is
10 business days after delivery. The money can take up to 5 more days to reach
your bank.
