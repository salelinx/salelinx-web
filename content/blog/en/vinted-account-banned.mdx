Vinted bans an account when it decides you broke its terms or catalogue rules, and a hidden listing is a much smaller thing. Every rule below links to its Vinted page and was checked on 6 November 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **Temporary bans (7 to 30 days)** cover repeatedly selling prohibited items, selling counterfeits, and promoting sales outside Vinted&rsquo;s payment system.
- **Permanent bans** cover multiple accounts, fraud, repeated counterfeits and ignoring Vinted&rsquo;s team.
- **You can appeal once, within 6 months of the decision notice,** using the link in that notice.
- **A hidden listing is not a ban.** Fix it and carry on, no appeal needed.

</Callout>

## Does relisting get you banned on Vinted?

**One relist done by hand is not what the rules aim at.** The risk grows with repetition and volume. See [relisting on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted).

Vinted&rsquo;s [terms and conditions](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) (new version applicable from 8 October 2026) list in section 6 what members &ldquo;must not&rdquo; do. Two items matter here:

- &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;
- using &ldquo;any kind of external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs&rdquo;

Section 7 says Vinted can respond to a breach by blocking a feature on your account, or blocking the account temporarily or definitively.

**Vinted does not say how many relists count as &ldquo;multiple&rdquo;, or which breach gets which response,** so nobody can tell you a safe number.

## What can get a Vinted account banned?

Vinted&rsquo;s help page on [why an account was banned](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/92-why-was-my-account-blocked) splits reasons into temporary and permanent. The terms and [catalogue rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) add detail.

Notes on the main ones:

- **Prohibited items:** the catalogue rules name illegal items (including counterfeits), restricted items that are not clothing or fashion, and unsafe items such as recalled products.
- **Off-platform payment:** help page 92 lists &ldquo;promoting selling outside the Vinted payment system&rdquo;. Listings and profiles must not link to external websites or platforms. A link to your own social profile is allowed if it is not tied to commercial activity.
- **Multiple accounts:** the terms say &ldquo;You&rsquo;re allowed to have only one Account.&rdquo; The exception is one standard and one Pro seller account, with separate emails and phone numbers.
- **Duplicates:** the catalogue rules say &ldquo;You should only have one listing per item.&rdquo; Duplicates get removed, and repeat breaches can lead to a ban (help page 62).
- **Blocked accounts:** under section 7 your items are delisted and you can only contact support. Vinted may also stop you opening a new account.

## Is a hidden listing the same as a ban?

**No.** Vinted&rsquo;s page on [hidden or removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) separates three levels:

- **Hidden:** the listing breaks a catalogue rule or has poor photos. Vinted says &ldquo;you&rsquo;ll need to update it&rdquo;, and &ldquo;You don&rsquo;t need to contact us or submit an appeal for a hidden listing.&rdquo; You are told what to fix.
- **Removed:** the item goes against the rules, for example it is counterfeit, or has been hidden multiple times for the same reason. You can appeal only if the message has an appeal link.
- **Account banned:** Vinted will ban an account, temporarily or permanently, &ldquo;if you repeatedly violate&rdquo; the catalogue rules. One hidden listing is not that.

A hidden listing will not stop you selling. If the same item type keeps getting hidden, read the rule it cites.

## How do I appeal a Vinted ban?

Section 7 of the terms says the notice tells you of your right to appeal, &ldquo;by submitting an appeal through our system&rdquo;. Vinted&rsquo;s [appeal page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/1599-appeal-a-moderation-decision) gives the steps.

1. **Find the decision notice** in your Vinted inbox or email. It contains the appeal link. If you only got a message that the account is &ldquo;under review&rdquo;, there is no decision to appeal yet.
2. **Use the link** in that notice.
3. **Explain why the decision was wrong,** and attach evidence if you have it.
4. **Wait.** The terms say Vinted will review it &ldquo;as soon as we can&rdquo;, with qualified staff supervising. No review time is given.

**You get one appeal, within 6 months.** Vinted says you can appeal &ldquo;within 6 months of receiving the decision notice&rdquo; and a decision &ldquo;can only be appealed once&rdquo;. Three Vinted pages (the appeal page, help pages 62 and 92) agree on this, so put your best evidence in it.

Temporary bans end after 7 to 30 days, and the end date shows when you log in, so appeals are mostly for permanent bans or mistakes.

**Do not delete your data before you appeal.** The appeal page says that if your data is deleted under GDPR, you may be unable to file new appeals.

## How do I lower the risk of a ban?

General guidance from the rules above, not a guarantee. Vinted decides and does not publish its detection methods.

- One account, one login, one set of details.
- Keep payment and conversation on Vinted. Do not give out PayPal or bank details to move a sale off the app.
- One listing per item. Delete the old listing when you upload the new one.
- Relist rarely, by hand, and only once per item.
- Check the catalogue rules for anything branded, electrical, a cosmetic or a child&rsquo;s product.
- Answer Vinted&rsquo;s messages. Repeatedly ignoring them is on the permanent ban list.
- Keep proof of authenticity for branded items, such as receipts and tags.

## Questions

### Why was my Vinted account banned?

Vinted&rsquo;s help page lists the reasons: repeatedly selling prohibited items, counterfeits, promoting sales outside Vinted&rsquo;s payment system, offensive behaviour, having multiple accounts, fraud and repeatedly ignoring its messages. The decision notice in your inbox or email gives the reason for your case.

### Does relisting get you banned on Vinted?

One relist by hand is not what the rules target. The terms do say you must not delete and re-list the same item multiple times, or multiple items in bulk, and they restrict external software tools. Vinted does not say how many relists is too many, so rare and by hand is the careful approach.

### How long do I have to appeal a Vinted ban?

Vinted&rsquo;s help pages say 6 months from receiving the moderation decision notice, and only one appeal is allowed. Use the link in the notice and include evidence.

### How long does a Vinted ban last?

A temporary ban lasts 7 to 30 days depending on the reason, and the end date shows when you log in. A permanent ban has no end date.

### Is a hidden listing a warning that my account will be banned?

Not by itself. A hidden listing means you need to fix it to follow the catalogue rules, and no appeal is needed. Vinted says repeatedly breaking the rules can lead to a ban.

### Can I open a new account after a ban?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms say it may prevent you from creating a new account, and having multiple accounts is itself a permanent ban reason. Appeal instead.
