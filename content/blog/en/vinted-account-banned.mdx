A Vinted account is banned when Vinted decides you have broken its terms or its catalogue rules, and the usual causes are selling banned or fake items, pushing buyers to pay outside Vinted, running more than one account, and breaking the platform rules on tools and bulk activity. A hidden listing is a much smaller thing, and most of the time it is not a warning about your account at all. This guide sets out what Vinted&rsquo;s own pages say about each, how to appeal, and where the rules do not give a clear answer. Every rule below links to the page it comes from and was last checked on 6 November 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
Vinted&rsquo;s help page lists temporary bans (7 to 30 days) for repeatedly selling prohibited items, selling counterfeits, and promoting sales outside Vinted&rsquo;s payment system. It lists permanent bans for having multiple accounts, fraud, repeated counterfeits and ignoring Vinted&rsquo;s team. If you are banned, you can appeal once, within 6 months of the decision notice, using the link in that notice. A hidden listing is different: you fix it and carry on, with no appeal needed.
</Callout>

## Does relisting get you banned on Vinted?

On its own, one relist done by hand is not what the rules aim at. Our guide to [relisting on Vinted](/blog/how-to-relist-on-vinted) covers that case. The risk grows with repetition and volume.

Vinted&rsquo;s [terms and conditions](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) (new version applicable from 8 October 2026) list what members &ldquo;must not&rdquo; do in section 6. Two items matter here: &ldquo;delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk&rdquo;, and using &ldquo;any kind of external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs&rdquo;. Section 7 says Vinted can respond to a breach by blocking a feature on your account, or by blocking the account temporarily or definitively.

Two things are not written down anywhere we could find. Vinted does not say how many relists count as &ldquo;multiple&rdquo;, and it does not say which breach gets which response. So nobody can tell you the safe number. What the pages do say is that breaches can lead to action, and that tools and bulk delete and re-list are named.

## What the rules say can get an account restricted or banned

A few notes on the main ones.

- **Prohibited items.** The catalogue rules name illegal items (including counterfeits), restricted items that are not clothing or fashion, and unsafe items such as recalled products. Read them before listing anything unusual.
- **Off-platform payment.** Help page 92 lists &ldquo;promoting selling outside the Vinted payment system&rdquo;. The catalogue rules add that listings and profiles must not contain links to external websites or platforms. A link to your own social profile is allowed if it is not tied to commercial activity.
- **Multiple accounts.** The terms say &ldquo;You&rsquo;re allowed to have only one Account.&rdquo; The exception is one standard and one Pro seller account, with separate emails and phone numbers.
- **Duplicates.** The catalogue rules say &ldquo;You should only have one listing per item.&rdquo; Duplicates get removed, and repeat breaches can lead to a ban, according to help page 62.
- **Blocked accounts.** Under section 7 your items are delisted and you can only contact support. Vinted may also stop you opening a new account.

## A hidden listing is not a banned account

These get mixed up. Vinted&rsquo;s page on [hidden or removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62) separates three levels.

- **Hidden.** The listing breaks a catalogue rule or has poor photos. Vinted says &ldquo;you&rsquo;ll need to update it&rdquo; to follow the rules, and that &ldquo;You don&rsquo;t need to contact us or submit an appeal for a hidden listing.&rdquo; The catalogue rules add that you are told what to fix.
- **Removed.** The item goes against the rules, for example it is counterfeit, or has been hidden multiple times for the same reason. You can appeal only if the message you got has an appeal link.
- **Account banned.** Help page 62 says Vinted will ban an account, temporarily or permanently, &ldquo;if you repeatedly violate&rdquo; the catalogue rules. One hidden listing is not that.

A hidden listing will not stop you selling. If you see a pattern, such as the same item type hidden again and again, read the rule it cites before you upload more.

## How to appeal a Vinted ban

Section 7 of the terms says the notice of a corrective action will tell you of your right to appeal, and that you can challenge it &ldquo;by submitting an appeal through our system&rdquo;. Vinted&rsquo;s [appeal page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/1599-appeal-a-moderation-decision) gives the steps.

1. **Find the decision notice** in your Vinted inbox or email. It contains the appeal link. If you only got a message that the account is &ldquo;under review&rdquo;, there is no decision to appeal yet.
2. **Use the link** in that notice.
3. **Explain why the decision was wrong**, and attach supporting evidence if you have it.
4. **Wait.** The terms say Vinted will review it &ldquo;as soon as we can&rdquo;, with qualified staff supervising. No review time is given.

The limits matter. Vinted&rsquo;s pages say you can appeal &ldquo;within 6 months of receiving the decision notice&rdquo;, and that a decision &ldquo;can only be appealed once&rdquo;. We checked this wording on three Vinted pages (the appeal page, help page 62 and help page 92), and they agree. Because there is one appeal, put your best evidence in it. Temporary bans end by themselves after 7 to 30 days, and the end date shows when you log in, so an appeal is mostly for permanent bans or bans you think were a mistake.

Appeals are also not the only route. The appeal page says that if your data is deleted under GDPR, you may be unable to file new appeals. Do not delete your data before you have appealed.

## Habits that reduce the risk

This is general guidance based on the rules above, not a guarantee. Vinted decides, and it does not publish its detection methods.

- One account, one login, one set of details.
- Keep payment and conversation on Vinted. Do not give out your PayPal or bank details to move a sale off the app.
- One listing per item. Delete the old listing when you upload the new one.
- Relist rarely, by hand, and only once per item.
- Check the catalogue rules for anything that is branded, electrical, a cosmetic or a child&rsquo;s product.
- Answer Vinted&rsquo;s messages. Ignoring them repeatedly is on the permanent ban list.
- Keep proof of authenticity for branded items, such as receipts and tags.

## Questions

### Why was my Vinted account banned?

Vinted&rsquo;s help page lists the reasons: repeatedly selling prohibited items, counterfeits, promoting sales outside Vinted&rsquo;s payment system, offensive behaviour, having multiple accounts, fraud and repeatedly ignoring its messages. The decision notice in your inbox or email gives the reason for your case.

### Does relisting get you banned on Vinted?

One relist by hand is not what the rules target. The terms do say you must not delete and re-list the same item multiple times, or multiple items in bulk, and they restrict external software tools. Vinted does not say how many relists is too many, so rare and by hand is the careful approach.

### How long do I have to appeal a Vinted ban?

Vinted&rsquo;s help pages say 6 months from receiving the moderation decision notice, and only one appeal is allowed. Use the link in the notice and include evidence.

### How long does a Vinted ban last?

A temporary ban lasts 7 to 30 days depending on the reason, and the end date shows when you log in. A permanent ban has no end date.

### Is a hidden listing a warning that my account will be banned?

Not by itself. A hidden listing means you need to fix it to follow the catalogue rules, and no appeal is needed. Vinted says repeatedly breaking the rules can lead to a ban.

### Can I open a new account after a ban?

Vinted&rsquo;s terms say it may prevent you from creating a new account, and having multiple accounts is itself a permanent ban reason. Appeal instead.
