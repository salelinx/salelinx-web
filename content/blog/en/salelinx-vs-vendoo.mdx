Vendoo is one of the best known crosslisting apps. SaleLinx does a narrower
job: it covers Depop, Vinted and eBay only. Which one fits depends mostly on
whether you sell on Vinted.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **You sell on Vinted:** Vendoo does not list it, so SaleLinx is the one of the two that will.
- **You sell on Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or Facebook Marketplace:** Vendoo covers them and SaleLinx does not.
- **You work from a phone:** Vendoo has a mobile app. SaleLinx needs desktop Chrome.

## Where Vendoo is stronger

- **More marketplaces.** If your stock goes to Etsy, Poshmark or Mercari as
  well as eBay and Depop, Vendoo lists them all from one place.
- **A mobile app,** free for subscribers, on iOS and Android.
- **Unlimited items** on every plan, from $14.99 a month.
- **Bulk tools and AI listing help** on its Growth plan and up, and offers and
  sharing on Pro.

## Where SaleLinx is stronger

- **Vinted.** It crosslists between Vinted, Depop and eBay in any direction.
- **Cheaper to start,** from $9.99 or £7.99 a month.
- **Routine jobs on Depop and Vinted:** relisting, refreshing Depop listings,
  offers to likers, price drops and shipping labels. On eBay it adds bulk
  editing.
- **Copies to Depop and Vinted wait as drafts,** so you check each one before
  buyers see it.

## Delisting when an item sells

Both apps can take the other copies down when an item sells, and both need your
computer on with the browser open. Neither is instant.

- **Vendoo** labels it beta. Its help page says Vendoo must stay open in a
  browser tab and it scans every 10 minutes. It covers eBay, Poshmark, Mercari,
  Depop and Whatnot, so not Vinted.
- **SaleLinx** checks Depop and Vinted about every 5 minutes, with a tab open
  for each, and eBay about every 15. It needs the Pro or Business plan and
  listings you have [linked](/docs/automate/linking) as the same item.

## Who should not pick SaleLinx

- **You sell on Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or Facebook Marketplace.** Vendoo
  covers them.
- **You list mostly from your phone.** SaleLinx needs a desktop or laptop.
- **You never sell on Vinted** and want one app for many US marketplaces.

## Questions

### Does Vendoo work with Vinted?

No. Vinted is not on the marketplace list in Vendoo's help centre, checked 6 November 2026. Its list covers eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, Vestiaire Collective and Whatnot.

### Is SaleLinx cheaper than Vendoo?

At the entry level, yes. SaleLinx starts at $9.99 or £7.99 a month and Vendoo at $14.99 a month on monthly billing. Vendoo's Starter plan has unlimited items, while SaleLinx Starter has 150 crosslists a month.

### Can I use SaleLinx on my phone?

No. SaleLinx is a Chrome extension, so it needs Chrome on a desktop or laptop. Vendoo has iOS and Android apps.
