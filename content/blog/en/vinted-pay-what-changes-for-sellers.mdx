Vinted Pay affects UK sellers. Under Vinted&rsquo;s updated terms, which apply from 8 October 2026, your Vinted Balance is now provided by Vinted Pay instead of Adyen, and you have to accept those terms to keep using Vinted. Your money moves for you.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **In the UK,** the Vinted Balance provider changes from Adyen to Vinted Pay Limited, which the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) authorises as an electronic money institution.
- **Accept the updated terms** when Vinted asks. In most cases that is all, and your available and pending funds and payment history transfer automatically.
- **A few members may need to verify their identity.** Vinted says it will tell you if that is you.
- **Do not accept** and you are limited to withdrawing funds, contacting support, downloading your data or deleting your account. Accounts that never accept are eventually closed.
- **No new seller fee.** The buyer-paid Buyer Protection fee is now called the Vinted fee, and Vinted says the way it is calculated is not changing.

</Callout>

## Does Vinted Pay affect UK sellers?

**Yes. Vinted&rsquo;s UK help page says your Balance &ldquo;will now be provided by Vinted Pay instead of Adyen&rdquo;.** The page, [Vinted Pay: the new Vinted Balance provider](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1548), says it is part of the updated terms, so a new digital wallet is created for you.

The UK terms behind it, [version 12](https://legal-assets.vinted.com/legal/terms/gb/en-GB/terms-and-conditions-v12.pdf), are marked &ldquo;New version applicable from 08-10-2026&rdquo;. They name Mangopay and Vinted Pay Limited as wallet providers. The previous version, applicable from 30 April 2026, also listed ADYEN N.V.

Some secondary coverage says the Mangopay to Vinted Pay move is a European change and that the UK change is different, and on the evidence it is. In the UK, Vinted says the provider being replaced is Adyen.

## What is Vinted Pay?

**Vinted Pay is Vinted&rsquo;s own payments company, and it holds the money in your Vinted Balance.** Vinted&rsquo;s [payment provider page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1039-payment-service-provider-on-vinted) says Vinted Pay Limited &ldquo;is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) as an electronic money institution under firm reference number 1037693&rdquo;.

- **UK licence:** Vinted announced the FCA authorisation on [16 March 2026](https://company.vinted.com/newsroom/vinted-pay-receives-UK-EMI-license). The [Vinted Pay terms](https://vintedpay.com/uk/terms-and-conditions) say the licence was issued on 13 March 2026.
- **Not a bank:** it is an e-money firm. The same terms say wallet funds &ldquo;are not covered under the Financial Services Compensation Scheme&rdquo; and are held in safeguarding accounts of UK credit institutions, separate from the company&rsquo;s own money.
- **A separate contract:** you become a Vinted Pay customer and agree to its terms, as you did with the previous provider. Vinted Pay says Vinted &ldquo;doesn&rsquo;t provide any regulated financial services to you&rdquo;.
- **Its own fees:** none. Vinted Pay&rsquo;s [fees page](https://vintedpay.com/uk/fees) says &ldquo;we do not charge any fees for our services&rdquo; and that it will notify you in advance if that changes.

## What do you have to do?

**Accept the updated terms, and complete an identity check only if Vinted asks.** Vinted&rsquo;s [help page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1548) says: &ldquo;As long as you accept the updated Terms &amp; Conditions, you probably won&rsquo;t need to do anything.&rdquo;

1. **Look for the prompt.** If you have not accepted yet, Vinted shows a prompt asking you to.
2. **Accept.** The terms ask you to do it within 15 days of receiving Vinted&rsquo;s notice.
3. **Wait for the email.** Vinted opens the Vinted Pay wallets gradually and sends a confirmation email once your new Balance is in use.
4. **Verify if asked.** &ldquo;In rare cases, some members may need to verify their identity (pass the KYC procedure).&rdquo; Vinted says it will let you know. KYC means Know Your Customer, the anti-money-laundering check wallet providers must run, and the terms say providers can ask for more checks at later thresholds too. If you fail one, the terms say your wallet may be suspended and you may not be able to pay or withdraw.

## What happens to your balance and withdrawals?

**Your balance, pending money and history move across automatically.** Vinted says &ldquo;all your funds (available and pending balance), payment history and wallet information will automatically be transferred to this new wallet&rdquo;, and that Balance &ldquo;will look and work the same as before&rdquo;.

- **Timing:** the move itself is quick, but Vinted warns that withdrawing or spending during it &ldquo;might cause errors&rdquo;. Its advice is to try again in an hour.
- **Withdrawals:** unchanged. Vinted&rsquo;s [provider page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1039-payment-service-provider-on-vinted) still says UK sellers withdraw to a pound sterling bank account at a UK bank.
- **History:** past transactions stay in your payment history, and Vinted notes they remain subject to online-platform tax rules &ldquo;such as DAC7&rdquo;. Our guide on [whether Vinted reports to HMRC](/blog/does-vinted-report-to-hmrc) covers what that means.
- **Refunds:** Vinted says the support, secure payments and refunds that apply to Buy now orders are not changing. If a parcel goes missing, [that process](/blog/vinted-parcel-lost-or-damaged) is the same.

## What if you do not accept?

**You can still get your money out, but you cannot keep selling.** Vinted says that to continue using Vinted &ldquo;you need to accept the updated Terms and Conditions and switch to Vinted Pay as the wallet provider&rdquo;. Without that, your actions are limited to:

- withdrawing funds to your bank account;
- contacting support;
- downloading the personal data Vinted holds on you;
- deleting your account.

Vinted adds that you can still accept later and carry on as normal, but &ldquo;accounts which don&rsquo;t accept the terms are eventually closed&rdquo;. The help page gives no deadline, so withdraw anything you want to keep.

This is a different thing from a ban. If your account is restricted for another reason, see [what to do if your Vinted account is banned](/blog/vinted-account-banned).

## How do the UK and Europe differ?

**The European move is described differently, and Vinted&rsquo;s own pages say less about it.** The European terms are marked as applying from 5 October 2026 (we read the [French](https://legal-assets.vinted.com/legal/terms/fr/fr-FR/terms-and-conditions-v13.pdf) version). They list MANGOPAY SA, ADYEN N.V. and Vinted Pay UAB as wallet providers, and say that if you are moved to a new provider you must follow the transfer process &ldquo;by the deadline we give you&rdquo; or you might not be able to use the Site or Services.

## Did any fees change?

**Not for sellers.** From 8 October 2026 the UK terms call the buyer-paid charge the Vinted fee, where the previous terms called it the Buyer Protection fee.

## Questions

### Does Vinted Pay apply to UK sellers?

Yes. Vinted's UK help page says your Vinted Balance is now provided by Vinted Pay instead of Adyen, as part of the updated terms that apply from 8 October 2026. You need to accept those terms to keep using Vinted.

### Do I lose my balance if I switch to Vinted Pay?

No. Vinted says your available and pending balance, payment history and wallet information transfer automatically to the new wallet. If a withdrawal or purchase fails during the move, it suggests trying again in an hour.

### What if I do not accept the new Vinted terms?

Your actions are limited to withdrawing funds, contacting support, downloading your data and deleting your account. You can accept later and carry on, but Vinted says accounts that do not accept are eventually closed.

### Do I have to verify my identity for Vinted Pay?

Most sellers will not. Vinted says only in rare cases do members need to pass the identity (KYC) procedure, and that it will tell you if you do. Wallet providers can ask for more checks later, for example at certain transaction thresholds.

### Did Vinted add a new seller fee in October 2026?

No. Vinted renamed the buyer-paid Buyer Protection fee as the Vinted fee and says it is not new or additional, that the calculation is unchanged and that there are no selling fees. Vinted Pay says it charges no fees at present.

### Does Vinted Pay change how Vinted reports sales to HMRC?

Vinted has not said so. Its help page says past transactions stay in your payment history and remain subject to online platform tax rules such as DAC7. Reporting depends on the rules, not on which company holds your wallet.
