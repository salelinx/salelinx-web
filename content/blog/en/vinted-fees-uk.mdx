Vinted charges sellers no fee. Not to list, not when an item sells, and not
to withdraw the money. The buyer pays instead: a Vinted fee at checkout,
usually 3% to 8% of the price plus £0.30 to £0.80, which until October 2026
was called the Buyer Protection fee. That fee is the one number that shapes
what you can charge, because the buyer sees your price plus the fee plus
postage, and that total is what they compare with everyone else. This guide
sets out what each side pays, with Vinted&rsquo;s own page linked for every
figure, all checked on 30 October 2026.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">
You keep the full price you set. The buyer pays your price, a Vinted fee of
roughly 3% to 8% plus £0.30 to £0.80, and the postage. On a £20 item that is
about £21 to £22.40 before postage. The fee pays for Vinted&rsquo;s refund
policy and support, so it is what makes a nervous buyer press Buy now. Vinted
Pro, for businesses, is also free, but Pro sellers must accept 14-day
returns and wait 14 days for the money.
</Callout>

## What sellers pay: nothing

Vinted&rsquo;s [selling basics](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/26) page says
it plainly: &ldquo;There are no fees for selling on Vinted, and you can list
as many items as you like.&rdquo; The page on the
[fee rename](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1653) repeats it: &ldquo;There are
no selling fees on Vinted.&rdquo;

That covers the whole journey:

- **Listing** is free, with no monthly cap. eBay gives private sellers 300
  free listings a month; Vinted sets no limit.
- **Selling** takes nothing off the price. Set £20 and £20 lands in your
  balance.
- **Withdrawing** is free. Vinted&rsquo;s
  [withdrawal page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/73) lists no fee, only a
  minimum of £0.10 and a wait of up to 5 business days for the money to reach
  your bank.
- **Postage** is paid by the buyer, every time. The
  [shipping page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753) says &ldquo;The
  shipping is always paid by the buyer&rdquo;, and Vinted emails you a
  prepaid label after the sale.

The things Vinted does charge sellers for are optional promotions: a
[Bump](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340) on one listing, or
[Showcase](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452) for up to 15. Both are priced
at checkout and neither is needed to sell. Our
[Bump guide](/blog/vinted-bump-cost-and-does-it-work) goes through when they
pay.

## What buyers pay: the Vinted fee

Every order placed through Buy now carries a Vinted fee. Vinted&rsquo;s
[Vinted fee page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1654) calls it &ldquo;a
mandatory platform fee paid at checkout&rdquo; and says it &ldquo;typically
includes a percentage of the item&rsquo;s or bundle&rsquo;s price, as well as
a fixed fee&rdquo;, &ldquo;usually 3% to 8% + £0.3 to £0.8&rdquo;. The
[price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist), last updated 8 October
2026, gives the same range and adds that all prices include VAT.

Vinted does not publish the exact formula. It says the amount depends on
the item, the order value and whether the order is a single item or a bundle.
In practice the percentage is higher on cheap items and lower on dear ones,
so the table below shows the published range rather than one figure.

Two things follow from this for a seller:

- **Price to the total, not to your number.** A buyer deciding between your
  £20 jumper and someone else&rsquo;s £19 one sees £21.50 against £20.40,
  plus postage. Check what the same item sells for, then remember the buyer
  is paying a pound or two more than the listed price.
- **Bundles carry one fee.** The fee is worked out on the bundle price, so a
  buyer taking three of your £5 items as a bundle pays one fee on £15 rather
  than three fees on £5. That is one reason bundle discounts shift cheap
  stock: the buyer saves on the fee as well as the price.

## What the fee buys the buyer

The fee is not pure margin for Vinted; it funds the guarantee that makes
buyers trust a stranger. The
[Vinted fee page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1654) says it covers
connecting buyers and sellers, member support, and applying the refund
policy &ldquo;if the item is lost, damaged, or significantly not as
described&rdquo; when bought from a non-professional seller.

The [refund policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465) sets out what that
means. A buyer has 2 calendar days from the delivery notification to report
a problem. If the item is lost, never arrives or arrives damaged, the refund
covers the item price, the shipping fee and the Vinted fee. If it is
significantly not as described and goes back, the refund covers the item
price and the Vinted fee, and &ldquo;Buyers pay for return shipping&rdquo;
unless the seller chooses to provide a Vinted label for the return.

For a seller the useful points are:

- **The buyer is refunded by Vinted, and you are paid by Vinted.** The
  money does not move between you directly. Vinted&rsquo;s page on
  [getting paid](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/235) says the order completes
  when the buyer presses &ldquo;Everything is OK&rdquo; or 2 days after
  delivery with no report, and your payment then moves from pending to
  available &ldquo;within 2 days&rdquo;.
- **Change of mind is not covered.** The refund policy covers lost, damaged
  and not as described. A buyer who simply went off the item cannot use the
  fee to force a return on a standard seller.
- **A lost parcel is Vinted&rsquo;s problem, not yours,** provided you used
  the Vinted label. Our guide to
  [lost or damaged Vinted parcels](/blog/vinted-parcel-lost-or-damaged)
  covers what to do when one goes missing.

## Vinted Pro: free, with strings

If you buy stock to resell, make things to sell or run a shop, Vinted
expects you on a Vinted Pro account. The
[Pro page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pro) says &ldquo;Vinted Pro is entirely
free&rdquo; and that sole traders, non-profits and companies can sign up; you
need to be registered as a business. There is still no selling fee.

What changes is the buyer&rsquo;s rights, and your cash flow. The
[Pro help page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/918) says a buyer has
&ldquo;14 calendar days to report any issues or decide if they want to return
the item&rdquo;, that Pro sellers must accept those returns, and that your
payment is released after the 14 days with no way to get it sooner, even if
the buyer says they are happy. So a Pro seller waits 14 days where a standard
seller waits 2, and carries the cost of change-of-mind returns that a
standard seller can refuse.

The Vinted fee page says the refund policy it funds applies to purchases
from non-professional sellers. On Pro purchases the buyer&rsquo;s cover comes
from consumer law and the 14-day return right instead. Whether you should be
Pro is the same question as whether you are trading for tax, which our guide
to [tax on Vinted and Depop sales](/blog/tax-on-vinted-and-depop-sales)
walks through.

## The costs sellers still carry

No selling fee does not mean no cost. The ones that are real:

- **Returns you agree to.** The policy makes the buyer pay return postage
  unless you offer a label. If you do offer one, that comes out of your
  pocket.
- **Postage you refund.** Some sellers refund postage to settle a complaint.
  That is a choice, not a fee, but it is money gone.
- **Bumps and Showcase.** Priced per item at checkout, VAT included, no
  refund if the item sells on day one or the listing is removed for a rule
  breach.
- **Packaging.** A mailing bag is pennies, but on a £3 top it is a bigger
  share than any fee.
- **Item Verification.** Vinted&rsquo;s price list says buyers pay £10 for
  Item Verification on eligible luxury items and £5 for Electronics
  Verification. The buyer pays it, but the item goes to Vinted&rsquo;s hub
  first, so you wait longer for the money.
- **Your time.** Listing, photographing, replying to offers and printing
  labels. If you sell a lot, batching the admin is the saving that is actually
  in your control. Printing the day&rsquo;s labels as
  [one merged PDF](/features/print-shipping-labels) rather than one by one
  is the kind of thing that adds up.

Vinted&rsquo;s [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms-and-conditions)
restrict external software tools and bulk deleting and re-listing, so keep
anything a tool does on your Vinted account to what you would do by hand.

## Questions

### Does Vinted charge a fee to sell?

No. Vinted says there are no fees for selling and no limit on how many items
you list. The buyer pays a Vinted fee at checkout instead. The only things a
seller can pay for are optional promotions such as Bumps and Showcase.

### What is the Vinted fee and who pays it?

It is the buyer-side fee Vinted adds to every Buy now order, usually 3% to
8% of the price plus £0.30 to £0.80, VAT included. It used to be called the
Buyer Protection fee; Vinted renamed it in October 2026 without changing how
it is worked out. The buyer pays it, and it funds Vinted&rsquo;s refund
policy and support.

### Is the Vinted fee refunded?

Yes, when Vinted refunds the order. The refund policy says a refund for a
lost, damaged or not-as-described item covers the item price and the Vinted
fee, and for lost items the shipping fee too. A buyer who returns an item
pays the return postage unless the seller provides a label.

### Does Vinted take a cut of postage?

No. The buyer pays postage at checkout and Vinted sends you a prepaid label.
You never set a postage price and nothing is deducted from your sale for it.

### Does Vinted Pro cost money?

No. Vinted says Pro is entirely free and there is no selling fee. The cost is
in the rules: Pro sellers must accept returns for 14 days after delivery and
are paid only once those 14 days have passed.

### How long does it take to get your money from Vinted?

The order completes when the buyer confirms or 2 days after delivery, your
payment reaches your available balance within 2 days of that, and a
withdrawal takes up to 5 business days to reach your bank. Posting promptly
with a tracked Vinted label is the only part you control.
