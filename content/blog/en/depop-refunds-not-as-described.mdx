A Depop buyer has 30 days from purchase to say an item is not as described,
and Depop's decision on a dispute is final. Rules checked 6 November 2026
against Depop's and GOV.UK's own pages.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **The buyer has 30 days** to report a problem, if they paid through Depop Payments.
- **You have 48 hours** to answer in the app. If you do not, the claim goes to Depop automatically.
- Depop only steps in for "significantly" not as described: wrong size, wrong colour, counterfeit, severe damage or major flaws you did not list.
- It does not cover a buyer who changed their mind. That is your choice as a private seller, but a business seller has legal duties to take returns.
- Photos and honest condition notes are your best protection.

</Callout>

## What counts as not as described?

Depop's page on
[what it considers "significantly not as described"](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360038455993-What-does-Depop-consider-significantly-not-as-described)
says the item received is "significantly different compared to the photos or
description used in the original listing".

**Covered:**

- Incorrect sizes and wrong colours
- Counterfeit items
- Severe damage
- Major wear or flaws not detailed on the listing
- Incorrect items, or missing items in a bundle

**Not covered:**

- Smells
- Minor damage on second hand or vintage items
- Material that looks different from the images
- An item that "doesn't fit or doesn't look right"
- Items bought outside the Depop Buy button

The seller must make sure the description and photos "accurately reflect the
item". **Major flaws you did not detail count against you, so what you wrote
is the whole case.**

## How does a claim run?

Depop's [My item is not as described](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791507-My-item-is-not-as-described)
page sets the steps. The buyer must raise the issue "within 30 days of the
transaction date". [Depop Protection for buyers](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360038461713-Depop-Protection-for-buyers)
says the same 30 days from purchase for anyone who paid through Depop Payments
(card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna).

1. **The buyer raises an issue in the app,** picks the outcome they want,
   writes a description and uploads photos. Without enough evidence, Depop says
   it may not be able to proceed.
2. **You have 48 hours** to respond with a solution that takes their preferred
   outcome into account. No response at all means automatic escalation to Depop.
3. **If you cannot agree,** the buyer can escalate. Depop reviews the proof
   against your original listing and may contact you for more.
4. **Depop decides.** Its help page says "Depop's decision on item disputes is
   final". In some cases it may give a full or partial refund without the buyer
   returning the item.

Bundles differ: the buyer cannot raise the issue with you directly and goes to
Depop's support team instead. Check notifications, and answer inside the app,
not only by message.

## What if the buyer paid by PayPal?

Depop Payments is the default. For PayPal, the buyer has to open a "Not as
described" dispute in PayPal's Resolution Centre, and the seller resolves it
there. Depop advises settling with the buyer before a dispute is raised,
finding your photos of the item's condition, watching your emails and keeping
PayPal updated with proof.

Payments made outside the Buy button, such as a bank transfer or in-person
meet-up, are not covered by Depop Protection.

## How do returns and refunds work?

If a return is agreed, the
[refund page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001772468-How-do-I-get-a-refund)
and the not as described page set these rules:

- The buyer must send the item back within 7 days of the return being agreed,
  with a tracked service. Depop says it will not extend this.
- Depop reimburses return postage up to £20 for UK buyers who keep their
  receipt.
- The refund is not automatic. The buyer confirms the return arrived, you
  confirm it arrived safely, then Depop processes the refund.
- You can refund the buyer yourself at any time: in the app, go to the sold
  item and choose Refund. A partial refund is possible too.
- If you refund a buyer who paid with Depop Payments, the fees you paid are
  refunded automatically.

**Depop's guidance is to issue a full refund if the item is returned to you.**

[Depop Protection for sellers](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001845367-Depop-Protection-for-sellers)
is different. It covers items lost or damaged in transit, for sales up to £250
shipped with a Depop Shipping label within 5 days. It does not cover a buyer's
not as described claim.

## What evidence helps a seller?

If a buyer claims not as described, check your in-app notifications and provide
proof of the item's condition or authenticity if asked. **Depop reviews proof
against your original listing, so what you showed and wrote when you listed it
is what matters.**

What to keep:

- **Your listing photos and text.** Do not edit them after a sale. Take a
  screenshot of the sold listing.
- **Photos taken before posting.** Date-stamped photos of the item and any flaw,
  and of it packed.
- **Tracking and the label.** It proves what was sent and when.
- **The message thread.** Keep it inside Depop, so it can be seen.
- **For authenticity claims,** receipts, tags, serial numbers or authentication
  records, if you have them.

**Be polite and quick.** A reply that offers a fix, such as a partial refund
for a flaw you missed, often ends a claim faster than a debate.

## Do I have to accept a return?

**Not for a change of mind.** Depop's refund page says "sellers don't have to
offer you a refund": a registered business has its own returns policy, and for
everyone else "it'll be the seller's choice whether to accept a return". A
change of mind is not a not as described claim.

**Business sellers have legal duties.** GOV.UK's guidance on
[accepting returns and giving refunds](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds)
says: "You must offer a full refund if an item is faulty, not as described or
does not do what it's supposed to."

- Online customers can cancel "within 14 days of receiving their item".
- They then have another 14 days to return it.
- You must refund within 14 days of receiving it back.
- Its page on
  [online and distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses)
  says you must tell customers they can cancel up to 14 days after delivery.

**Private sellers** must make sure the item matches its description
([Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/54/section/13)).
Depop adds that its protection does not limit a buyer's rights against a seller
under local consumer law.

Whether you count as a business depends on how you trade. GOV.UK says that if
you buy or make goods to sell at a profit you are likely to be trading
([selling on a digital platform](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-goods-or-services-on-a-digital-platform)).

If that sounds like you, read it and check with Citizens Advice or an adviser.
This is general information, not legal advice. Our guide to
[tax on Depop and Vinted sales](/blog/tax-on-vinted-and-depop-sales) covers the
trading side.

## How do I prevent claims?

**Most claims are a gap between the listing and the item.**

- **Photograph every flaw close up,** and show the label, size tag and the back.
  Natural light, plain background.
- **Write the flaw in the text,** not only the photo. "Small hole near left
  hem, see photo 5" beats "good condition".
- **Give measurements.** Depop's list says incorrect sizes are covered, and
  "doesn't fit" is not, so measurements put a fit complaint on the right side.
  Say whether the size is the tag size or your measurement.
- **Describe colour honestly.** Screens vary, and "material looks different"
  is not covered, but a wrong colour is.
- **Disclose smells and wear.** Pet hair, smoke, fading, bobbling.
- **For branded or designer pieces,** keep proof of where you got them.
- **Pack and ship tracked,** and photograph the item packed.

## Questions

### How long does a buyer have to claim not as described on Depop?

30 days from the date of purchase, per Depop's help pages. The buyer must have
paid through Depop Payments to get Depop Protection. If they paid by PayPal,
they have to raise a dispute in PayPal instead.

### How long do I have to respond to a Depop claim?

48 hours from when the buyer raises the issue. If you do not respond, the
dispute is escalated to Depop automatically. If you respond and you disagree,
the buyer can escalate it to Depop for review.

### Do I have to accept a return on Depop?

If the buyer just changed their mind, a private seller can choose, and a
registered business follows its own returns policy. For a business seller, UK
law says you must offer a full refund if an item is faulty or not as described.
If Depop finds a claim valid, it can arrange a return or refund.

### Does Depop Protection cover sellers?

Only for items lost or damaged in transit, sent with a Depop Shipping label
within 5 days, on sales up to £250. It does not cover a buyer's not as described
claim. Depop has the final say on those.

### What evidence should I send Depop?

Your original listing photos and text, photos of the item before posting,
tracking, and the message thread. For authenticity claims, receipts or tags.
Depop compares the evidence with the listing, so what you wrote at the time is
what counts.

### Do I get my fees back if I refund a buyer?

Yes, for sales paid through Depop Payments. Depop says all fees are reversed
when a sale is refunded, automatically for those payments. PayPal sales need a
form submitted to Depop support.
