eBay International Shipping (eIS) is replacing the Global Shipping Programme (GSP) for eligible UK sellers, with accounts moved in phases from August 2026. Separately, the currency conversion charge for UK-registered sellers rises from 2.5% to 2.75% on 1 December 2026. Both are on eBay&rsquo;s own pages.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short answer">

- **You still post to a UK hub, and eBay does the international leg.** It handles customs paperwork and delivery, and the buyer pays international postage and import charges.
- **eBay says there are no extra fees for sellers on eIS**, and the international selling fee is waived on eligible items.
- **If a buyer returns an eIS item, eBay refunds them and you keep your payout.** The item is not sent back to you.
- **Once your account moves, GSP is gone, even if you opt out of eIS.** eBay emails you when it happens, and it has not published a date per seller.
- **The currency conversion charge only applies when eBay converts your funds**, for example on an item you list on eBay.de. It goes from 2.5% to 2.75% on 1 December 2026.

</Callout>

Everything below was checked on 8 November 2026 against eBay UK&rsquo;s own pages. Where eBay has not said something, I say so.

## Is eBay International Shipping replacing the Global Shipping Programme?

**Yes, for eligible UK sellers, in phases from August 2026.** eBay&rsquo;s [GSP page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/global-shipping-programme) says: &ldquo;We&rsquo;re upgrading GSP sellers to eBay International Shipping in phases starting August 2026.&rdquo; Its [help page for GSP](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/global-shipping-programme?id=4646) says eIS &ldquo;will replace the Global Shipping Programme for eligible UK sellers&rdquo;.

eBay announced it on [22 July 2026](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/press-room/uk/ebay-boosts-international-selling-support-for-uk-entrepreneurs-side-hustlers-and-small-businesses/), after launches in the US and Canada, and says it is open to everyone from individuals to established businesses.

What eBay has not published: a date for your account, or an end date for GSP. Its [announcement](https://community.ebay.co.uk/forum/announcements-28/topic/ebay-international-shipping-is-coming-to-the-uk-50871/) says sellers get a separate notification with their enrolment date, and its [eIS page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) says you get an email when your account is enrolled. No action is needed beforehand.

## How does eBay International Shipping work for a UK seller?

**You send the parcel to an eBay hub in the UK, and eBay takes it from there.** Your own set-up barely changes.

- **The buyer sees one price at checkout**, made up of your domestic postage plus the international leg. If your own international options are cheaper, [eBay says](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) they show first.
- **eBay handles customs.** Its eIS page says it covers customs documentation and HS code classification. The press release adds tax and duty calculations, carrier management and post-sale support.
- **The buyer pays** international postage and import duties and taxes.
- **You still owe accurate item details**, including country of origin, and any certificate of origin you need.
- **The new hub address differs from the GSP one.** eBay&rsquo;s page does not print it, so use the address in your account.

The [seller terms](https://pages.ebay.co.uk/internationalshippingprogram/seller/terms/) (updated 23 July 2026) are blunter about who is responsible: &ldquo;you are the exporter and remain responsible for all export reporting obligations.&rdquo; Accurate listings matter here, and our guide to [eBay item specifics](/blog/ebay-item-specifics) covers filling them in.

## How is eIS different from the GSP?

**The hub routine is the same. What eBay adds is reach, returns cover and a lower value cap.**

The lower value cap is the one to watch if you sell higher-priced items abroad. Anything over £900 is outside eIS, according to eBay&rsquo;s page, so you would need your own international postage for it.

## What does eBay International Shipping cost?

**eBay says there are no additional fees for sellers.** Its eIS page says: &ldquo;There are no additional fees charged to sellers while using eBay International Shipping.&rdquo; Final value fees, where they apply, are the same as GSP.

- **No international fee on eligible items.** On the [business fees page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/business-selling-fees?id=4809), eBay says you &ldquo;won&rsquo;t be charged an international fee when your eligible item sells&rdquo;. Other UK sellers pay one when the delivery address is outside the UK. For business sellers it is 1.05% for the eurozone and Northern Europe, 1.8% for the US and Canada and 2.0% elsewhere.
- **Postage is out of the fee base.** The same page says your final value fee on international postage &ldquo;is calculated on your cost to post to the UK shipping hub&rdquo;, and if your domestic postage is free you pay none on it.
- **eBay says eIS rates can be up to 30% cheaper than GSP**, for items up to 3kg on the top 10 lanes, against June 2026 GSP rates. That is eBay&rsquo;s own comparison, and the buyer pays that postage, not you.

Our [eBay fees guide](/blog/ebay-fees-uk) covers the rest of what eBay charges.

## Which items and countries does eIS cover?

**Items in the UK, listed on ebay.co.uk, worth up to £900.** The eIS page says only listings on ebay.co.uk are eligible, and it covers 195+ countries, though it does not list them.

- **Restricted and banned items are out.** The eIS FAQ gives examples including alcohol, most batteries, e-bikes, plants and seeds, supplements and weapons, and says the list is not exhaustive. Check eBay&rsquo;s [prohibited and restricted items policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/prohibited-restricted-items?id=4207).
- **Size limits sit in the terms.** They do not permit packages over 31.5kg billable weight or longer than 120cm.
- **There is no eligibility checker.** eBay&rsquo;s FAQ says there is no dashboard that shows whether a listing qualifies.
- **You can exclude countries**, at account or listing level, in My eBay.

For UK VAT, eBay says items sent under eIS are treated as exports, as with GSP. If eBay does not charge you VAT, you may still need to account for UK VAT on the proceeds, so ask an accountant.

## What happens if an eIS parcel is returned or lost?

**eBay refunds the buyer and you keep your payout.** The [announcement](https://community.ebay.co.uk/forum/announcements-28/topic/ebay-international-shipping-is-coming-to-the-uk-50871/) says that after the hub scans the parcel, eBay handles lost, damaged or delayed claims, and if a buyer opens a return it refunds them at no cost to you. You do not receive or inspect the item.

Two details to know:

- **The item does not come back.** The eIS page says returned items may go to a third-party provider or be liquidated, and that &ldquo;we are not able to return items to Sellers&rdquo;.
- **Cover starts at the hub.** The terms say you stay responsible for loss or damage until the hub accepts the parcel, unless it was sent with a Simple Delivery label. Pack well and keep proof of postage.

## Can I opt out of eBay International Shipping?

**Yes, but it will not bring the GSP back.** The eIS page says: &ldquo;Once enrolled in eIS, GSP will no longer be available, even if you opt out of eIS.&rdquo; Orders already in flight at enrolment finish under GSP.

To opt out of everything, go to My eBay, then Postage Preferences, then eBay Shipping Options, and choose &ldquo;Opt out of the eIS Programme&rdquo;. The terms say an opt-out can take up to 72 hours. You can also change country exclusions in the same place after enrolment.

If you opt out, you handle international postage yourself, with your own options. The trade-off is simple: eIS gives you returns cover and no customs paperwork, and your own postage gives you control over price and carriers.

## What should I check before Christmas?

**Check your account before the busiest parcels go out.** A short list:

- **Look for the enrolment email** and note the date. Your own account is the only place that tells you.
- **Find the new hub address** and update any saved label or routine that used the GSP one.
- **Review country exclusions** and any items over £900 that need their own postage.
- **Fill in item specifics and country of origin** accurately on listings you sell abroad.
- **Decide whether to opt out**, knowing GSP will not return.
- **Check the 2.75% charge below** if you list on other eBay sites.

## Is the eBay currency conversion charge going up to 2.75%?

**Yes, for UK-registered sellers, on 1 December 2026.** eBay&rsquo;s [fees for business sellers](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/business-selling-fees?id=4809) says: &ldquo;If your registered address is in the UK, the seller currency conversion charge applied and retained by eBay is 2.5%.&rdquo; It adds: &ldquo;From December 1, 2026, the fee for sellers registered in the UK will increase to 2.75%.&rdquo; The [private sellers page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822), updated 5 November 2026, says the same.

**It applies only when eBay converts your funds.** eBay says that when you list or sell on an international eBay site, it &ldquo;may convert your funds to a different currency&rdquo;. Its example is a UK seller on eBay.de, where fees are converted from pounds to euros. A sale on ebay.co.uk to a UK buyer paid in pounds involves no conversion.

It is a mark-up on the exchange rate, not a percentage of the sale. eBay says it is &ldquo;a fixed percentage applied to the base exchange rate and retained by eBay&rdquo;, and charged by eBay Commerce UK Ltd. The [selling internationally page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/selling/selling-internationally?id=4132) says the rate is fixed at the time of sale, shown in the order details, and applies to refunds too.

One gap: eBay&rsquo;s pages do not say how conversion works for eIS orders placed on ebay.co.uk. Check your order details rather than assuming.

## Questions

### Is eBay International Shipping replacing the Global Shipping Programme?

Yes, for eligible UK sellers. eBay is enrolling accounts in phases from August 2026 and emailing each seller. Once your account moves, GSP is no longer available, even if you opt out of eIS. eBay has not published an end date for GSP.

### Who pays customs and import charges on eIS orders?

The buyer pays international postage and import duties and taxes. eBay handles the customs paperwork and classification, but the seller terms say you remain the exporter, responsible for export reporting and for accurate item details such as country of origin.

### Does eBay International Shipping cost sellers anything?

eBay says there are no additional fees for sellers on eIS, and the international selling fee is waived on eligible items. Final value fees, where they apply, are the same as under GSP. Domestic postage to the hub is still your cost.

### Can I opt out of eIS and keep using the GSP?

No. You can opt out in My eBay, but eBay says that once you are enrolled in eIS, GSP is no longer available even if you opt out. After opting out you handle international postage yourself.

### When does the eBay currency conversion charge go up?

On 1 December 2026, from 2.5% to 2.75% for sellers registered in the UK. It applies only when eBay converts your funds, for example when you list or sell on another eBay site such as eBay.de.
