**UK sellers are not affected yet.** Depop&rsquo;s own help pages still let UK sellers choose Depop Shipping via Evri or arrange their own postage. US sellers are affected: new US listings must use Depop Shipping from 28 October 2026, and Australia has made the same move.

## What changed with Depop shipping in 2026?

**Depop stopped letting US sellers create new listings with their own postage.** Depop&rsquo;s [How to ship - US](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523051318033-How-to-ship-US) page says &ldquo;Other&rdquo; is being phased out: from 28 October 2026 it is no longer available for new listings, and everything listed from then on uses Depop Shipping via USPS. [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/depop-ends-independent-shipping-us-au/), a seller news site, reported the same date on 5 November.

Depop Shipping means the buyer pays for a prepaid label and Depop gives you a label or QR code after the sale. Depop&rsquo;s blog says that for UK and US sellers [shipping costs the seller nothing](https://www.depop.com/blog/how-does-depop-work/) because the buyer pays for the label.

## Is eBay behind it?

**eBay owns Depop now, and it has said shipping is a priority, but we found no statement that links the two.** [eBay announced the purchase](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-to-acquire-depop-from-etsy/) from Etsy in February 2026, and eBay&rsquo;s [press release of 30 July 2026](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-completes-acquisition-of-depop/) says the deal is complete. It says Depop keeps &ldquo;its distinct brand, platform, customer experience, and culture&rdquo; and that eBay&rsquo;s shipping services will be applied to Depop.

On the [Q3 earnings call on 28 October 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/29/ebay-ebay-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) (a transcript, not an official eBay page), CEO Jamie Iannone said access to eBay&rsquo;s carrier rates &ldquo;has already lowered Depop&rsquo;s shipping and parcel rates&rdquo;. That is about Depop&rsquo;s carrier costs. We found no published new Depop price list, so we cannot say what buyers or sellers now pay per parcel.

## Who is affected: the US, Australia or the UK?

**The US and Australia, not the UK.**

For Australia, Depop&rsquo;s Shipping Policy says sellers listing there from the effective date must use Depop Shipping. Its How to ship page adds that the rollout is in phases, so it may not be available to everyone yet.

## What must US sellers do with existing and new listings?

**New listings need nothing extra: pick your parcel size and Depop handles the label.** Per the US help page, you set the parcel size on the listing, which calculates the shipping cost, and after a sale you get the label from Selling Hub under Sold.

- **Older listings set to Other:** they keep that setting until they sell. Depop says if you delete and relist an item it switches to Depop Shipping, so relisting now means giving up own postage on that item.
- **Orders on Other:** you arrange and pay for postage, must use tracked shipping and add the tracking in Depop. You cannot change tracking once added.
- **Use the Depop label on Depop Shipping orders.** The help page says shipping another way means you are not covered by Depop Protection, and the order may be cancelled and refunded.

If you sell cheap items and used to charge less postage than the label costs, check the label price on each parcel size before you relist anything.

## How does Depop Shipping work and what does it cost?

**You pack the item and drop it off with the label or QR code; the buyer pays the postage.** The label is fully tracked, so you do not add tracking or mark the order shipped. In the US the carrier is USPS, and Depop&rsquo;s [USPS Shipping Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11282781352465-USPS-Shipping-Policy-US) says labels are fulfilled by USPS.

**Official rates: we could not verify any.** The help pages we could read give no price table, and Depop&rsquo;s terms say rates may change. Seller guides quote figures that conflict with each other, so we have not repeated them. Look at the parcel-size price in the Depop app, and see our [Depop fees guide](/blog/depop-fees-uk) for what else comes off a sale.

Payment processing is charged on the shipping the buyer pays as well as the item price, per the fees page we cite there. Depop also lists a [New User Free Shipping Offer in the US](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/45540198769937-New-User-Free-Shipping-Offer-in-US-Terms-Conditions), but we could not read its terms, so check them before relying on it.

## What about bundles and large or heavy items?

**A bundle goes in one parcel and gets one label.** Depop&rsquo;s [bundle shipping guide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360017746274-How-to-ship-bundles-with-Depop-Shipping-US-UK) says that if every item uses Depop Shipping you can send them together, and the label size follows the combined weight. Two small-parcel items might get a medium label.

**Heavy items are where you can lose money.** Depop&rsquo;s [Terms of Service](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001773148-Terms-of-Service) say sellers must reimburse Depop for extra carrier charges, for example when an item weighs more than the label allows. We could not read the US size and weight limits, so confirm them in the USPS policy before listing something bulky.

## What are the options for UK sellers now?

**You can still choose.** Depop&rsquo;s [How to ship - UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK) page gives two routes, and this is the same guidance as in our [fees guide](/blog/depop-fees-uk).

- **Depop Shipping via Evri:** a QR code or printable label from Sold in Selling Hub, fully tracked.
- **Other:** you arrange and pay for postage, use a tracked service and add the tracking yourself.

Evri limits come from the older [UK Shipping Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360044352033-UK-Shipping-Policy): mainland UK only, no more than 120cm long and 15kg. That page is about three years old, so check it before sending anything large.

**Only Depop Shipping gets seller protection.** [Depop Protection for sellers](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001845367-Depop-Protection-for-sellers) covers a sale only when you use a Depop label, and in the UK it is up to £250. That matters for claims too: see our guide to [not as described refunds](/blog/depop-refunds-not-as-described).

Because the US has already moved, it is sensible to assume UK own-postage could change, but Depop has not said so. If you are weighing platforms, our [Depop or Vinted guide](/blog/depop-vs-vinted-uk) covers postage on each, and [how to get more sales on Depop](/blog/how-to-get-more-sales-on-depop) covers the rest.

## Questions

### Is Depop ending ship on your own?

Only for new US listings from 28 October 2026, and for new Australian listings from October 2026. US listings set to Other before that date keep it until they sell. UK sellers can still choose Evri or their own postage.

### Are UK Depop sellers affected by the shipping changes?

Not at the time of checking on 8 November 2026. Depop&rsquo;s UK help page still offers Depop Shipping via Evri or Other, where you arrange and pay for tracked postage yourself. We found no announced UK change.

### What happens to my US listings set to Other?

They keep the Other option until the item sells. If you delete and relist one, Depop says it switches to Depop Shipping. Orders on Other need tracked postage and the tracking added in Depop, and are outside Depop Protection.

### How much does Depop Shipping cost in the US?

We could not find an official price list. The buyer pays for the prepaid label, and the price depends on the parcel size you set on the listing. Check the price shown in the Depop app, because Depop says rates can change.

### Did eBay change Depop shipping rates?

eBay&rsquo;s CEO said on its 28 October 2026 earnings call that eBay&rsquo;s carrier rates have lowered Depop&rsquo;s shipping and parcel rates. We found no published new price list, so buyer and seller prices are unconfirmed.

### How are bundles shipped with Depop Shipping?

If every item in the bundle uses Depop Shipping, you send them in one parcel with one label. Depop sets the label size from the combined weight, so two small items may get a medium label.
