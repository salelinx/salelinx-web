# Crosslist your first item

The crosslister takes a listing you&rsquo;ve already written on one marketplace
and posts it to the other. It fills in the target form for you: title,
description, photos, price, and the right category, size, condition, brand and
colour.

You stay in the driver&rsquo;s seat. You can review and tweak any field before
publishing.

## Before you start

- Both marketplaces connected. See [Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace).
- The source listing should already be live (or in your drafts) on one platform.
- You&rsquo;ll need to be on a SaleLinx plan that includes crosslisting (every
  paid tier does; the Free tier has a 25/month limit for trial use).

## 1. Pick the source listing

Open the **Crosslist** tab in the SaleLinx panel. The picker shows your
existing listings from both marketplaces. Search or filter to find the item
you want to copy across.

You can also crosslist from a single listing: open any of your items on Depop
or Vinted, and the SaleLinx panel will show a **Crosslist to...** button.

## 2. Choose the target

Pick the destination platform: Depop or Vinted. SaleLinx opens a new tab on
the target marketplace and the panel switches into fill mode.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Don't touch the target tab">
While SaleLinx is filling the form, leave the target tab alone. Clicking
inside it can interrupt React&rsquo;s controlled inputs and corrupt the form
state. If the fill stalls for more than a minute, click **Cancel** in the
panel and try again.
</Callout>

## 3. SaleLinx fills the form

The panel works through the target form field by field:

- **Title and description** copy across as-is. (You can edit either before
  publishing.)
- **Price** copies across in the target&rsquo;s currency. SaleLinx does not do
  FX conversion, it just moves the number.
- **Photos** download from the source listing and upload to the target.
- **Category, condition, size, colour, brand** are translated through
  SaleLinx&rsquo;s mapping tables. Depop&rsquo;s &ldquo;Used &mdash; like new&rdquo;
  becomes Vinted&rsquo;s &ldquo;New without tags&rdquo;, etc.

If a field can&rsquo;t be mapped (rare brand, very specific size), the panel
flags it for you to set manually.

## 4. Review and publish

Once the form is filled, you can:

- Edit any field before publishing.
- Add or remove photos.
- Override the category if the auto-mapping picked something off.
- Click **Publish** to post the listing.

The panel records the new listing in your dashboard so the source and the new
copy stay linked. That linkage powers the cross-platform [restocker](/docs/sales/restocker).

## Bulk crosslist

To do more than one at a time, select multiple items in the Crosslist picker
grid. SaleLinx will queue them up and crosslist them sequentially with safe
pacing between items (the safety pacing avoids tripping marketplace rate
limits). Plan caps apply.

## What can go wrong

**The form fills but doesn&rsquo;t submit.**
Some fields (especially brand on Vinted) require an exact match against the
platform&rsquo;s search index. If you see a stuck field, click into it
yourself and pick from the dropdown, then click **Publish**.

**Photos are missing.**
The marketplace sometimes blocks photo downloads from a fresh page load.
Refresh the source listing once, then retry.

**Category is wrong.**
Open the category dropdown on the target form and pick the right one. The
auto-mapping is correct in most common cases, but the long tail (vintage
militaria, sports memorabilia, collectibles) often needs a manual nudge.
