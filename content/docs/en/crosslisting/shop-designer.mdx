# Shop Designer (Depop)

Shop Designer is a visual layout tool for your Depop shop. It lets you pin,
re-order, and group your active listings without dragging items around inside
Depop&rsquo;s own UI, which is fiddly on the web and slow on mobile.

This feature is **Depop only**. Vinted doesn&rsquo;t expose a public way to
re-order a shop, so the equivalent doesn&rsquo;t exist for Vinted listings.

## What it does

- **Reorder listings** by dragging cards in the panel preview. Saving pushes
  the new order to Depop in one batch.
- **Pin items to the top** so featured pieces always show first.
- **Preview** the resulting shop layout before you commit.

The panel reads your active listings from Depop, shows them as a draggable
grid, and lets you save the result back to Depop in one go.

## Opening the designer

Inside the SaleLinx panel, open the **Shop Designer** tab. The first time
you open it on a new account, it pulls your active listings (this can take a
few seconds for shops with hundreds of items).

The grid mirrors the order Depop currently shows on your shop page.

## Reordering

Drag any card to a new position. The preview updates instantly. Drop targets
highlight as you drag.

Use the toolbar:

- **Sort by date** &mdash; resets the preview to newest-first.
- **Reverse** &mdash; flips the current order.
- **Reset** &mdash; throws away changes and reloads from Depop.

## Saving

Click **Save layout**. SaleLinx pushes the new order to Depop one item at a
time, with safe pacing between calls so the marketplace doesn&rsquo;t flag the
burst as automated activity.

The save can take a minute or two for a large shop. The panel shows progress
and you can keep working in other tabs &mdash; nothing else is blocked.

<Callout variant="tip" title="When ordering matters">
Depop&rsquo;s search and feed surface listings partly based on shop activity,
so a recently re-ordered shop can briefly get a small visibility bump. Don&rsquo;t
re-order daily &mdash; the bump diminishes if Depop sees the same items being
shuffled in a tight loop.
</Callout>

## Common tasks

**Featuring a new collection.**
Drag the new pieces to the top, save. They&rsquo;ll be the first thing
shoppers see when they land on your profile.

**Cleaning up a messy shop.**
Use **Sort by date** to reset, then nudge the items you want featured to the
top. Saves you the click-by-click drag in Depop&rsquo;s native shop editor.

## Limitations

- **Depop only** &mdash; Vinted has no equivalent.
- **Active listings only** &mdash; sold and draft items aren&rsquo;t shown.
- **No grouping** &mdash; Depop doesn&rsquo;t support sections, so neither
  does the designer. Order is a single flat list.

## Plan and access

Shop Designer is included on Starter and above. The Free tier shows the tab
locked.
