# Shipping labels

The **Labels** tab gathers your sold orders from both marketplaces and lets
you download, print or email the label PDFs in one go &mdash; instead of
clicking each order in the marketplace UI and saving labels one at a time.

## What it shows

Two sections:

1. **To ship** &mdash; sold orders that have a label ready (Depop) or that
   need one ordered first (Vinted &ldquo;needs label&rdquo; flow). Each card
   shows the buyer, item title, carrier badge, and the order date.
2. **Shipped** &mdash; orders you&rsquo;ve marked sent, kept around so you
   can leave Vinted feedback in bulk from the same tab.

Filters at the top narrow by carrier (Evri, Royal Mail, etc.) or by free-text
search across buyer name and item title.

## Selecting orders

Click a card to select it. Click again to deselect. The toolbar shows the
selected count and enables **Print** and **Email**.

The header checkbox selects everything visible (respecting the current
filter), so you can do the day&rsquo;s shipping in one selection.

## Downloading and printing

Click **Print labels** with one or more cards selected. SaleLinx:

1. Downloads each individual label PDF from the marketplace.
2. Merges them into one combined PDF in the order they appear in the panel.
3. Saves it to your downloads folder with a name like `labels-2026-05-10-12.pdf`.
4. Opens the merged PDF in a new tab so you can hit Print.

For Vinted&rsquo;s Evri labels (which come in A4 with extra whitespace),
SaleLinx auto-crops to the label box before merging so you don&rsquo;t waste
half a page per label.

## QR vs PDF (Depop)

Depop offers some carriers as a QR code instead of a PDF. For those orders,
the card shows a **PDF / QR** toggle. Pick whichever your local drop-off
point accepts.

## Emailing labels

Sometimes you want the labels on a different device (phone for printing at
the post office, partner&rsquo;s computer). Click **Email labels** with your
selection.

A small modal asks for an email address, then SaleLinx sends the merged PDF
straight from the extension. The subject and body are pre-written; you can
tick &ldquo;Remember this address&rdquo; so it pre-fills next time.

If your account doesn&rsquo;t have email send configured (or it fails),
SaleLinx falls back to a regular `mailto:` link with the PDF attached as a
download.

## Vinted &ldquo;needs label&rdquo; flow

For Vinted orders where you haven&rsquo;t ordered the label yet, the card
shows **PDF** and **QR** buttons. Click one and SaleLinx asks Vinted for that
label type via the API. The card updates to **Label ready** within a few
seconds and joins the rest of the printable batch.

## Leaving feedback (Vinted)

In the **Shipped** section, each Vinted order with feedback still pending has
a **Leave feedback** button. Click it to post the buyer feedback directly
from the panel &mdash; same as the in-marketplace flow but without leaving
the tab.

Bulk feedback works too: select multiple shipped Vinted cards and click
**Leave feedback** in the toolbar. SaleLinx queues the posts with safe
pacing.

## Plan and access

Available on Pro and above. The Free and Starter tiers see the tab locked.
