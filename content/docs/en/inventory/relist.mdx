# Relist

Relist is a refresh tool with teeth. It posts a brand-new copy of a
stale listing, then deletes the original. The photos are saved again
with small changes (and, if you like, the title, description and
price) so the copy goes up as a new listing rather than a duplicate of
the old one.

The result: your item gets a fresh listed date and goes back out to
buyers as new stock, instead of sinking further down as old stock.

Relist works on Depop, Vinted and eBay. On eBay it ends the listing and
relists it, with no photo changes. An auction relists as an auction, and
relisting one that has bids ends it early and cancels every bid. You can
also put an ended eBay listing back on sale from
[My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings): select it, click **Edit** and
pick **Relist** in its status.

## When to use it

- A listing has been live for weeks with no traction.
- A [refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh) hasn&rsquo;t moved it.
- You want to change the price at the same time as reposting.

The new copy is a separate listing with its own link, so likes and
views on the old one don&rsquo;t carry over. If you just want to nudge
a Depop listing up without recreating it, use the
[Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh) instead.

## How it works

For each Depop or Vinted listing you relist, SaleLinx:

1. Reads the listing live from Depop or Vinted (title, description,
   price, category, size, brand, condition, shipping and photos) and
   keeps a backup copy in your browser.
2. Downloads each photo, applies the photo changes, and uploads the
   new versions one at a time.
3. Posts the new listing with the title, description and price
   changes you chose, or saves it as a draft if you asked for that.
4. If the listing is saved to the cloud, moves its cloud copy (and any
   link to its matches on your other marketplaces) over to the new
   listing.
5. Deletes the original.

Posting comes first and deleting second. If the new listing
can&rsquo;t be created, the original is left alone and nothing is
lost. If the new listing goes up but the original can&rsquo;t be
deleted, you&rsquo;ll have both live, so check your shop and delete
the old one by hand. On Vinted the copy is made as a draft and then
published, so if publishing fails you may find a leftover draft in
your Vinted drafts.

On eBay it works the other way round: the listing is ended first and
then relisted. If that relist fails, the listing stays ended, and the
activity log says so. Put it back on sale from
[My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings).

A photo that fails to download or upload is left out of the new
listing. The relist only fails if none of the photos upload.

## Running a relist

Open the **Relist** tab. It shows your live Depop, Vinted and eBay listings
in one list, oldest first, with how long each has been listed: red for
under 3 days, orange for 3 to 7 days and green after that.
Marketplaces you&rsquo;ve switched off in Settings are left out. If a
marketplace you sell on isn&rsquo;t open in a tab (eBay included), a
popup asks you to open it.

1. Narrow the list if you need to: **All**, Depop, Vinted or eBay, the
   search box (it matches titles and brands), and the age filter:
   **Any age**, **More than**, **Less than** or **Between**, in hours,
   days or weeks.
2. Tick the listings you want to relist. The box at the left of the
   search row ticks everything on the current page.
3. Click **Relist**. The **Relist Confirmation** window opens.
4. Check the options under **For every item** (below), then the row
   for each listing under them, where you can leave a listing out or
   change its options.
5. Click **Relist**. It reads **Relist as drafts** with your count when
   every item is set to save as a draft, and **Cancel bids and relist**
   when an eBay auction you ticked has bids. It stays greyed out while
   nothing is ticked or a price is invalid.

While it runs, a progress bar shows how far the run has got and about
how long is left, and each listing in it shows **Waiting**,
**Relisting&hellip;**, **Relisted**, **Failed** or **Skipped**. If
another SaleLinx job is running, the bar says **Waiting in queue**
until it finishes. When the run ends, a summary counts what was
relisted, skipped, failed or not done. It stays until you close it or
tick another listing. **Stop** ends the run early and also removes
relists still waiting in the queue; SaleLinx asks first.

The list is read live from your marketplaces, so it only shows
listings that are on your shop right now. The reload button next to
the search box fetches it again.

You can also start a relist from the
[Dead Stock](/docs/automate/dead-stock) tab. It opens the same
confirmation window.

### Cloud items and sold items

**Show cloud items** swaps the list for listings you saved to the
cloud that are no longer live on the marketplace (**In cloud (not
live)**). That view is for reference only; you can&rsquo;t relist from
it.

Sold items, and listings you deleted, aren&rsquo;t in the Relist tab.
If you saved one to the cloud, relist it from **My Listings** instead:
select it and use **Relist** in the selection toolbar. See
[Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor).

## The Relist Confirmation window

The options sit under **For every item** and apply to every listing
you ticked. Below them, each listing has its own row. Untick a row to
leave that listing out, or use its icons to turn photo, title,
description, price or draft on or off for that listing alone. Flipping
a **For every item** switch changes every row you haven&rsquo;t
changed yourself.

eBay rows have no photo icon, and when every listing is on eBay the
photo options are hidden. An eBay auction&rsquo;s row says it relists
as an auction, with the same starting price, length and any Buy It Now
price. If it has bids, the row warns that relisting ends the auction
early and cancels them all. eBay won&rsquo;t end an auction with bids
in its last 12 hours, so that one stays live.

### Photo Modifications

On by default, and recommended. Each photo is turned, scaled and
brightened (or darkened) by a small random amount, up to the limits
you set:

- **Rotation** - up to &plusmn;2&deg; by default (you can set up to
  10&deg;).
- **Scale** - up to &plusmn;2% by default (up to 15%).
- **Brightness** - up to &plusmn;1.8% by default (up to 25%).

Every photo also gets a very slight colour shift and is saved again as
a new JPEG file. A tiny zoom covers the corners a rotation would
expose, so a sliver around the edges is trimmed. Photo order, and
which photo leads, stay as they were. The **Before** and **After**
preview shows your first photo with half of each limit applied, and
updates as you change the numbers.

Turning **Photo Modifications** off greys out its options and removes
the rotation, scale and brightness changes. Each photo still gets the slight colour shift, the
tiny zoom and a fresh save, so the new files are never exact copies
of the originals.

### Title and Description Modifications

Both on by default. Each one swaps the case of the first letter of one
random word (for example, `vintage` becomes `Vintage`), so the text
isn&rsquo;t identical to the original. Both work on eBay too, where
only the description&rsquo;s text changes, never its formatting. On
eBay the text and price changes are made after the relist; if that
step fails, the relist still counts but keeps the old text and price.

Depop has no separate title field. On Depop, **Title Modifications**
changes the first line of the description and **Description
Modifications** changes the rest.

### Price Modifications

Off by default. Turn it on to change prices as you relist, then pick
one change for every listing, and the amount:

- **No bulk change**
- **Reduce by %** or **Increase by %**
- **Reduce by fixed** or **Increase by fixed** (an amount in your shop
  currency)

Each listing&rsquo;s row then shows its new price in a box. Type over a
box to price that listing by hand. Your price stays if you change the
bulk change later, and the &#8634; button next to it puts the bulk
price back. An empty or zero box turns red (**Enter a price above
0**), and **Relist** stays greyed out until you fix it. An eBay
auction&rsquo;s box starts from its starting price. Depop doesn&rsquo;t
accept prices under 1.00, so a change that lands lower is raised to
1.00 on Depop.

### Save as draft

**Save as draft** makes the new copy a draft instead of a live
listing, on Depop, Vinted or eBay. The original is still deleted
(ended on eBay), so the item is off sale until you publish the draft.
An eBay draft is saved through an eBay tab, so keep one open and
signed in; without one the run doesn&rsquo;t start.

## Sale discounts on Depop

If a Depop listing is on sale (the grid shows its original price
struck through), SaleLinx re-applies the same discount percentage to
the new listing once it&rsquo;s posted. The discount is worked out
from the new listing&rsquo;s price, including any price change you
made. It isn&rsquo;t re-applied when:

- you save as a draft (set the discount again after you publish), or
- the discounted price would fall below 1.00.

If re-applying the discount fails, the relist still goes through and
the new listing stays at full price.

## Pacing

**Relist settings** is a card in the **Settings** tab, under
**Advanced settings**. It holds **Seconds between listings**: how long
SaleLinx waits after each Depop or Vinted listing, on average. The
default is 10 seconds and the minimum is 5. eBay relists run one after
another without this wait.
The first few listings and the later ones in a long run get a little
extra, and long runs take the odd longer break (the job queue shows
**On break**), so a big run can sit idle for several minutes partway
through. That&rsquo;s expected.

Each relist also takes a while on its own, longer for listings with
lots of photos, because the photos upload one at a time with short
pauses. You can close the panel while it runs, but keep a tab open on
each marketplace you&rsquo;re relisting on. Other SaleLinx jobs wait in
the queue until the run finishes.

## Presets

The preset picker at the top of **Relist settings** saves the relist
settings as a named set: the seconds between listings, plus the
**For every item** options in the Relist Confirmation window (photo,
title and description changes, the price change, and save as draft).
Choices you make on single rows aren&rsquo;t saved. Picking a preset
fills in all of them at once. Two are built in:

- **Default** - 10 seconds between listings; photo modifications on,
  at &plusmn;2&deg; rotation, &plusmn;2% scale and &plusmn;1.8%
  brightness; title and description changes on; price change off;
  save as draft off.
- **Careful** - 20 seconds between listings, and every relist lands as
  a draft. Slower, and nothing goes live until you publish it (the
  originals are still deleted).

Change any setting by hand and the picker keeps the preset&rsquo;s
name with a dot next to it, to show the values have changed. Its menu
then offers **Save as new&hellip;**, **Revert**, and, for one of your
own presets, **Save changes**. Saving
under the name of one of your existing presets asks before
overwriting it, and the built-in names can&rsquo;t be reused. Your
own presets can be deleted after a confirmation; the built-ins
can&rsquo;t.

Your saved presets are stored with your SaleLinx account, so they
survive a reinstall and follow you to another computer. See
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription)
for how syncing, sign-out and per-device selection work.

## Plan and allowance

Relist is on every plan, including the 14-day free trial.
There&rsquo;s no tab-level lock. What varies by plan is your monthly
relist allowance; higher plans raise it. See [pricing](/pricing) for
current numbers.

- Each relist that goes through counts once, on any marketplace.
  Relists that fail don&rsquo;t count.
- The allowance resets at the start of each calendar month (UTC).
- If you select more listings than you have left, the run is cut to
  what&rsquo;s left and the panel tells you.
- The **Membership** card in the Settings tab shows what you have
  left. See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings).

If you only need a lighter visibility bump (no delete and repost), the
[Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh) is the lower-touch alternative
on Depop.
