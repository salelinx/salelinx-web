# Relist

Relist is a refresh tool with teeth. It deletes a stale listing and
posts a brand-new copy with the same content, optionally with subtly
modified photos so the marketplace&rsquo;s duplicate-detection
doesn&rsquo;t flag it.

The result: your item gets a fresh listed-date and re-enters the
search feed as new content, instead of being buried by the algorithm
as old stock.

## When to use it

- A listing has been live for weeks with no traction.
- The Depop refresher and Vinted re-uploads aren&rsquo;t enough.
- You want to swap the lead photo or update wording without losing
  the price history showing in your shop activity.

If you just want to nudge a listing up without recreating it, use the
[Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh) instead.

## How it works

For each item you select:

1. SaleLinx reads the source listing&rsquo;s title, description,
   price, category, condition, brand, size and photos from your cloud
   backup.
2. Optionally applies photo modifications (crop, brightness or hue
   tweak, re-order) so the photos aren&rsquo;t byte-identical to the
   originals.
3. Posts a fresh listing on the same marketplace with all that
   content.
4. Once the new listing is live, deletes the original.
5. Updates your unified [Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) view to
   point at the new ID.

This sequence is the safer order: post first, delete second. If
posting fails partway, your original is still live and you
haven&rsquo;t lost anything.

## Photo modification

Marketplaces aggressively de-duplicate listings by perceptual photo
hash. SaleLinx&rsquo;s photo modifier tweaks each photo just enough
to break the hash without changing how the photo looks to a buyer:

- **Crop** - trims a small uneven border off each edge.
- **Hue / brightness** - nudges colour balance by a couple of
  percent.
- **Order shuffle** - reorders photos beyond the first one (the lead
  stays where you put it for buyer recognition).

Modification is on by default. If you toggle it off, photos are
re-uploaded verbatim and you may end up with a duplicate-flagged
listing.

## Running it

From the **Listings** tab:

1. Select the rows you want to relist (single or multiple).
2. Open the bulk-action toolbar and click **Relist**.
3. Confirm the photo-modification settings (or accept defaults).
4. Click **Start**.

SaleLinx queues the relists and processes them with safe pacing
between items so the marketplace doesn&rsquo;t flag the burst as
automated activity. Progress shows in the panel; you can keep working
in other tabs.

## Presets

The preset picker at the top of the Settings panel saves the relist
settings as a named set: the delay between relists, plus every option
in the relist window (photo modifications, title and description
changes, the price change, and save as draft). Picking a preset fills
in all of them at once. Two are built in:

- **Default** - 10 seconds between relists; photo modifications on,
  at &plusmn;2&deg; rotation, &plusmn;2% scale and &plusmn;1.8%
  brightness; title and description changes on; price change off;
  save as draft off.
- **Careful** - 20 seconds between relists, and every relist lands as
  a draft first. Slower, and does less per run.

Change any setting by hand and the picker marks the current values as
unsaved; from there you can save them as a preset of your own. Saving
under the name of one of your existing presets asks before
overwriting it, and the built-in names can&rsquo;t be reused. Your
own presets can be deleted after a confirmation; the built-ins
can&rsquo;t.

Your saved presets are stored with your SaleLinx account, so they
survive a reinstall and follow you to another computer. See
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription)
for how syncing, sign-out and per-device selection work.

Available on every paid plan, including the 14-day free trial.
