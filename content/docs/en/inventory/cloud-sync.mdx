# Cloud sync

SaleLinx keeps your listing catalogue in your browser. Cloud sync
copies a listing, with its full details and photos, to your SaleLinx
account. It works per listing: you choose which listings to save, and
only those are copied.

When you save a listing, SaleLinx reads its full details from the
marketplace (condition, colour, size, the whole description, the
category and the full-size photos), backs up the photos, then stores
the copy in your account. That is why a save takes a moment to
settle.

## Why you&rsquo;d save listings to the cloud

- **Cross-device** - your saved listings appear on every browser
  signed into your SaleLinx account.
- **Backup** - if a listing is removed from the marketplace, your
  cloud copy still has the photos and details.
- **Relist sold items** - select a sold item that was saved to cloud
  in My Listings and choose **Relist**. It goes back up as a live
  listing or a draft, with its photos and details intact, even after
  the marketplace has stopped showing it. See
  [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist).
- **Linking and the Restocker** - a [link](/docs/automate/linking)
  between a Depop and a Vinted listing is stored on their cloud
  copies, and the [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) reposts from
  the cloud copy. Both only work on saved listings.

## Saving, updating and removing

Everything happens in the **My Listings** tab.

- **One listing** - each row has a switch in the **Cloud** column. A
  linked row has one switch per marketplace, so you can see which side
  is saved. Switch it on to save. A spinner shows while it saves, and
  the row can&rsquo;t be selected until the save finishes. Switch it
  off to remove the cloud copy.
- **Several listings** - select the rows, click **Cloud** in the
  toolbar, and choose **Save to Cloud**, **Remove from Cloud** or
  **Update Cloud**. **Remove from Cloud** asks you to confirm first.
  See [Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor) for the rest of the
  toolbar.

Only active listings can be saved from My Listings. Sold and draft
listings in a selection are skipped, though one that is already saved
can still be removed. The Linking tab&rsquo;s **Save to cloud** picker
can also save sold listings, because a sold item can still be part of
a linked pair.

The cloud filter button above the grid switches between showing all
listings, only cloud-saved ones, and only the ones that aren&rsquo;t
saved.

### While a save runs

SaleLinx needs the listing&rsquo;s marketplace open in a tab to read
its details. If the tab isn&rsquo;t open, that listing waits and the
save carries on with the others.

Saves run one listing at a time in the background, with
**Saving to cloud** and a count showing progress. They give way to
anything else SaleLinx is doing on the marketplace. If the
marketplace starts refusing requests, saving stops and the remaining
listings stay queued for later. If the marketplace tab is signed into
a different account from the one linked to SaleLinx, saving stops
completely, so another shop&rsquo;s listings can&rsquo;t end up in
your backup.

### Keeping the copy up to date

When a saved listing sells or otherwise changes status, the cloud copy
is updated automatically. Edits you make through SaleLinx are saved
to the cloud copy as well.

Edits you make directly on Depop or Vinted aren&rsquo;t copied over
by themselves. Select the listing and choose **Cloud** then
**Update Cloud** to read its latest details and photos again. If that
fails, the existing cloud copy is kept.

### What removing does

Removing a listing from the cloud deletes its cloud copy and photo
backups. The listing on the marketplace and its row in My Listings
are untouched. It also switches off restock tracking for that listing
and removes its link to a partner listing, because both of those live
on the cloud copy.

## What gets stored where

1. **Your browser** holds every listing SaleLinx has seen, with basic
   details like title, price, photos and status.
2. **Your SaleLinx account** holds only the listings you&rsquo;ve
   saved, plus their photo backups.

Your browser is the main copy. The cloud is an extra backup on top of
it.

## How your backup is protected

Cloud copies travel to SaleLinx over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection
and are stored with Supabase, the database provider SaleLinx uses,
which encrypts its storage at rest. Access rules tie every saved
listing and photo to your account, so no other SaleLinx user can see
them.

## On more than one device

A browser that signs into your account pulls down your saved
listings, and every open SaleLinx panel updates when you save or
remove something, so two tabs or two computers stay in step.

## Listings that only exist in the cloud

Sometimes a listing drops out of your browser&rsquo;s list (for
example an old sold item the marketplace no longer returns) while its
cloud copy is still there. SaleLinx still shows these in the
My Listings grid so you can see and manage them, and they follow your
platform and status filters like any other row.

## If SaleLinx is temporarily down

If SaleLinx can&rsquo;t reach its servers several times in a row, the
panel shows **SaleLinx is temporarily down** with **We should be back
up in a moment.** Your listings on Depop and Vinted aren&rsquo;t
affected, and there is nothing you need to do: the message clears by
itself as soon as SaleLinx can reach its servers again.

From version 1.1.12 this screen covers the whole panel until the
connection is back, with a **Close SaleLinx** button to close the
panel. In earlier versions the same message shows as a banner at the
top of the panel.

## Plan and access

- **Pro and Business** include cloud sync.
- **Free trial and Starter** see the cloud switches and toolbar
  options with a padlock. Clicking them opens the upgrade prompt. If
  you move to a plan without cloud sync, the switches lock again,
  including on listings that are already saved.

Each plan has a cloud storage limit for photos and details; see
[pricing](/pricing) for the current amounts. Your usage shows as
**Cloud storage** on the **Membership** card in the Settings tab (see
[Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings)). When you reach the
limit, new saves stop and the upgrade prompt appears. Removing
listings from the cloud frees space.
