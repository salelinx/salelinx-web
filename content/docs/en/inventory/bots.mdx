# Grow

The **Grow** tab (called **Bots** in earlier versions) is your
audience-growth toolkit. From one place you can follow or unfollow the
people around any profile, and on Depop also your own buyers, a
shop&rsquo;s reviewers or the people who liked a listing. It works on
Depop and Vinted: the tab acts on whichever marketplace the panel is
open on.

## Why follow at all

- Following someone usually notifies them, which increases the chance
  they look at your shop.
- Following sellers in your niche surfaces you in their network and
  builds reciprocal follows.
- Following your own buyers and reviewers keeps you in their feed for
  repeat purchases.

## Picking a target

The card at the top of the tab shows the **Target Profile**. SaleLinx
picks it up from the page you&rsquo;re on:

- On someone&rsquo;s profile page, that account is the target.
- On a Depop listing page, the seller is the target, and the listing
  is what **Likers** works on.
- Anywhere else, the card reads **No profile detected**, and the
  **Followers** and **Following** tiles stay greyed out until you open
  a profile.

There&rsquo;s no box to type a username in: browse to the profile you
want, then open the panel. Your own profile works as a target too.

## Follow

- **Followers** - follows everyone who follows the target profile.
  Depop and Vinted.
- **Following** - follows everyone the target profile follows. Depop
  and Vinted.
- **Buyers** - follows everyone who has bought from you. This is
  always your own buyers, whichever profile is showing. Depop only.
- **Reviewers** - follows everyone who has left a review on the target
  shop, or on your own shop if no profile is showing. Depop only.
- **Likers** - follows everyone who liked the listing you have open.
  Needs a Depop listing page. Depop only.

A follow run skips you, accounts you already follow and, on Depop,
anyone on your blacklist.

## Unfollow

- **Followers** - unfollows the accounts in the target profile&rsquo;s
  followers list. Depop and Vinted.
- **Following** - unfollows the accounts in the target profile&rsquo;s
  following list. Depop and Vinted.
- **Buyers** - unfollows everyone who has bought from you, whether or
  not SaleLinx followed them. Depop only.
- **Reviewers** - unfollows the reviewers of the target shop, or of
  your own shop if no profile is showing. Depop only.

Accounts you don&rsquo;t follow are skipped. There&rsquo;s no single
&ldquo;unfollow everyone&rdquo; button: to unfollow the accounts you
follow, open your own profile and use **Following** under
**Unfollow**.

On Vinted, only **Followers** and **Following** work, for both follow
and unfollow.

## Blacklist (Depop)

The **Blacklist** buttons build a list of accounts that Depop follow
runs will never follow:

- **Blacklist Following** adds every account you follow right now.
- **Blacklist Followers** adds every account that follows you.

Each button reads your list from Depop and adds anyone who isn&rsquo;t
already on the blacklist, so pressing it again is safe. The result
shows in the Activity Log.

A common pattern: press **Blacklist Following** before you unfollow a
batch of accounts, so later follow runs don&rsquo;t pick the same
people up again.

The blacklist is kept in this browser and there&rsquo;s no screen in
the panel to view or edit it. It isn&rsquo;t used on Vinted, and
unfollow runs ignore it.

## Settings

The **Settings** section under the tiles controls when a run stops and
how fast it goes:

- **Stop after this many actions** - the run ends once this many
  people have been followed or unfollowed. Default 70.
- **Stop after this many minutes** - an optional time limit. 0, the
  default, means no limit.
- **Choose my own wait time** - off by default. Off uses a safe wait:
  5 to 8 seconds between actions on Depop, and a natural rhythm of
  about 7 seconds on Vinted. On, each action waits a random time
  between the two numbers in **Wait between (seconds)**. Going faster
  raises the risk of a block.

Your daily allowance can also end a run early (see below).

### Presets

The preset picker at the top of **Settings** saves these as a named
set: the maximum actions per run, the optional time limit, and the
custom wait toggle with its minimum and maximum seconds. Picking a
preset fills in all of them at once. Two are built in:

- **Default** - 70 actions per run, no time limit, custom wait off.
- **Careful** - 40 actions per run, custom wait on at 10 to 15
  seconds. Slower, and does less per run.

Change any setting by hand and the picker marks the current values as
unsaved; from there you can save them as a preset of your own. Saving
under the name of one of your existing presets asks before
overwriting it, and the built-in names can&rsquo;t be reused. Your
own presets can be deleted after a confirmation; the built-ins
can&rsquo;t.

Your saved presets are stored with your SaleLinx account, so they
survive a reinstall and follow you to another computer. See
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription)
for how syncing, sign-out and per-device selection work.

## Pacing and safety

**On Depop**, runs go at the steady pace above, without long breaks.
If Depop rate-limits an action, SaleLinx waits 5 seconds and tries
that account once more; if it&rsquo;s refused again, the run stops.
The run also stops straight away if Depop says you&rsquo;ve hit its
24-hour limit, or if it denies access. When that happens, give it a
few hours before the next run, and if access was denied, sign out of
Depop and back in first.

**On Vinted**, runs go through SaleLinx&rsquo;s pacing engine, which
starts a little slower and takes the odd longer break (the job queue
shows **On break**). If Vinted pushes back, the run backs off and
retries a few times, and stops if it keeps happening.

Click **Stop** under the tiles to end a run at any time.

## Depop&rsquo;s following limit

Depop limits how many accounts one person can follow, and SaleLinx
treats that limit as 7,500. Before any Depop follow run, SaleLinx
checks how many accounts you follow. If you&rsquo;re at the limit, the
run doesn&rsquo;t start; if you&rsquo;re close, the run is shortened
to fit. To make room, unfollow some accounts first, for example with
**Following** under **Unfollow** on your own profile.

SaleLinx doesn&rsquo;t check a following limit on Vinted.

## What you&rsquo;ll see

The **Completed**, **Skipped** and **Failed** counters next to the
target count up as the run goes. The **Activity Log** lists each
account as it&rsquo;s followed, unfollowed or skipped, with the reason
for a skip (for example, already following or blacklisted).

## Plan and allowance

The Grow tab is on every plan, including the 14-day free trial.
There&rsquo;s no tab-level lock. What varies by plan is your daily
follow allowance and your daily unfollow allowance, which are counted
separately; higher plans raise or remove them. See [pricing](/pricing)
for current numbers.

Only accounts actually followed or unfollowed count; skips
don&rsquo;t. The allowances reset at midnight UTC. If you start a run
that asks for more than you have left, it&rsquo;s shortened to fit.
The **Membership** card in the Settings tab shows what you have left
today; see [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings).
