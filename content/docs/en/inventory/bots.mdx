# Bots

The **Bots** tab is your audience-growth toolkit. From one place you
can follow accounts in a target&rsquo;s network, follow your own
buyers and reviewers, bulk-unfollow, and curate a blacklist of people
you never want to target. It&rsquo;s cross-platform: the same tab
works on Depop and Vinted, with SaleLinx picking the right backend
based on the page you&rsquo;re on.

## Why follow at all

- Following someone notifies them, which increases the chance they
  look at your shop.
- Following sellers in your niche surfaces you in their network and
  builds reciprocal follows.
- Following your own buyers and reviewers keeps you in their feed for
  repeat purchases.

## Available actions

### Both Depop and Vinted

- **Follow followers of a target** - follows everyone who follows
  another user.
- **Follow following of a target** - follows everyone another user
  follows.
- **Unfollow all** - bulk unfollow from your own following list.
- **Target unfollow** - unfollow accounts that appear in another
  user&rsquo;s followers or following list.

### Depop only

Vinted doesn&rsquo;t expose &ldquo;buyers&rdquo; or
&ldquo;reviewers&rdquo; as queryable audiences the way Depop does, so
these actions are Depop-side only:

- **Follow buyers** - follows every account that has ever bought
  from you.
- **Follow reviewers** - follows every account that has left a
  review on a target shop (defaults to your own).
- **Follow likers** - on a product page, follows every account
  that has liked that listing.
- **Unfollow buyers / reviewers** - mirror of the Follow
  versions, removes accounts you previously followed via those flows.
- **Blacklist all current following** - one-click action that
  adds every account in your following list to your blacklist, so the
  Follow bots never re-target them.

## Picking targets

You can run a follow bot in a few ways:

- **By username** - type a target&rsquo;s handle into the input,
  choose **Followers** or **Following**, and start the run.
- **From a profile page** - navigate to a user&rsquo;s shop, click
  the Bots tab, and the input prefills with their handle.
- **From a product page (likers only)** - on a Depop product page,
  click **Follow likers** and SaleLinx pulls the liker list for that
  exact listing.
- **From your own data** - **Follow buyers** and **Follow reviewers**
  default to your own account; no target needed.

## Blacklist and whitelist

Two text boxes in the **Settings** section govern who the bots will
skip:

- **Blacklist** (used by follow actions) - usernames you never want
  to follow. One per line or comma-separated. Case-insensitive.
- **Whitelist** (used by unfollow actions) - usernames you never want
  to unfollow. Same format.

Two convenience buttons populate the blacklist in bulk:

- **Blacklist all following** walks your own following list and adds
  every username. Useful before running **Follow buyers** so you
  don&rsquo;t accidentally re-follow accounts you&rsquo;ve already
  curated.
- **Blacklist all followers** does the same for your followers list.

Both deduplicate against existing entries, so it&rsquo;s safe to run
them multiple times.

## Pacing and safety

The bots run on SaleLinx&rsquo;s pacing engine so the burst
doesn&rsquo;t look automated to the marketplace. You don&rsquo;t need
to configure anything; sensible defaults are applied per platform.
Power users can tweak the delay range in Settings if they want a
faster or slower cadence.

If the marketplace pushes back temporarily, the bot quietly waits and
retries. If your session gets flagged outright (a `403`), the run
stops immediately and you should sign out, sign back in, and give
things a few hours before resuming.

## Hitting your following limit (Depop)

Depop enforces a ceiling on how many accounts a single user can
follow. Before any Depop follow run, SaleLinx checks your current
following count and stops cleanly if you&rsquo;re at the cap. To
free up headroom, run **Unfollow all** (or **Target unfollow** for
something more selective) and try again.

Vinted doesn&rsquo;t enforce an equivalent cap.

## What you&rsquo;ll see in the log

The activity log shows one line per action: `Followed @username`,
`Skipped @username (blacklisted)`, `Already following @username
(skipped)`, and so on. At the end of a run you get a summary line
with done / skipped / failed counts.

## Plan and access

The Bots tab is available on every paid plan, including the 7-day
free trial. There&rsquo;s no tab-level lock. What varies by plan is
your **daily follow and unfollow allowance** - the higher tiers raise
or remove the cap entirely. See the pricing page for current numbers,
or check the Membership card at the top of the panel for your
remaining allowance today.
