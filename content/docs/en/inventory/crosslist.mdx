# Crosslist

The **Crosslist** tab copies listings you&rsquo;ve already made on one
marketplace to the other. You pick the items, check them in a review step,
and SaleLinx creates each copy in the background. There&rsquo;s no sell form
to fill in twice and no tab to babysit.

From version 1.1.12, every copy is saved as a draft for you to check and
publish. In version 1.1.11 an **Auto-post** switch on the tab could publish
copies straight away instead.

## When to use it

- You list something new on Depop and want it on Vinted too, or the other
  way round.
- You&rsquo;re tidying an old shop and want everything that isn&rsquo;t on
  the other marketplace yet copied across.
- You&rsquo;re new to SaleLinx and want to mirror your existing catalogue in
  one go.

## What you need

- **Both accounts linked to SaleLinx.** Crosslisting needs your Depop and
  your Vinted account linked (see
  [Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace)).
- **A Depop tab and a Vinted tab open**, signed in to those same accounts.
  SaleLinx works through both sites at once. If one is missing, the tab says
  so and offers a button to open it.
- **A plan with crosslists.** Every plan from the 14-day free trial up
  includes a monthly crosslist allowance.

## How Crosslist works

The **How Crosslist works** card at the top of the tab sums it up in three
steps: **Tick items**, **Press Crosslist**, **Drafts appear on the other
site**. Close it with the &times; once you know the drill and it stays
hidden.

## Picking listings

The tab shows a grid of your own listings on the source marketplace, newest
first. Each card has the photo (with the marketplace logo and price on it),
the title, a **Draft** tag for drafts, size and category tags, the brand,
the condition, and likes and views.

Above the grid:

- **Direction** - the button with two logos shows which way you&rsquo;re
  copying, source first. Click it to swap. It starts on whichever site
  you&rsquo;re browsing.
- **Link filter** - **All**, Linked or Unlinked. It starts on Unlinked,
  which hides listings that are already linked to a partner on the other
  marketplace.
- **Status filter** - **All**, **Active** or **Drafts**. It starts on
  **All**: drafts can be crosslisted as well as live listings.
- **Search**, **Refresh listings** (re-reads your listings from the
  marketplace), a select-all checkbox for the current page, and the page
  size (10 to 200 cards).

Tick the cards you want, or press on one and drag across the others. Your
selection is kept when you change page. The button then reads
**Crosslist N to** with the target logo. Once you scroll past the toolbar, a
copy of that button floats in the bottom-right corner so you don&rsquo;t
have to scroll back up.

A card that is already queued for another job (an edit, delete or duplicate
from My Listings, a relist, or another crosslist) is greyed out with a
**Queued** badge. It can&rsquo;t be selected and drops out of select-all, so
two jobs can&rsquo;t collide on the same listing.

## Add from URL

To crosslist something that isn&rsquo;t in the grid, add it by its link:

1. Click **Add from URL**.
2. Paste one or more listing links, one per line. If you opened the panel on
   a Vinted listing, you can leave the box empty to use that listing.
3. Click **Fetch**. Each listing appears in a list where you can **Edit** or
   **Remove** it. Any link that couldn&rsquo;t be fetched stays in the box.
4. Click **Add to List**. The listings go to the front of the grid, already
   ticked.

Only crosslist your own listings. The link decides the direction: a Vinted
link is copied to Depop and a Depop link to Vinted.

## Review before crosslisting

Clicking **Crosslist N to** opens **Review before crosslisting**, with one
row per item.

- **Edit** opens the item&rsquo;s details: title, description, category,
  brand, price, condition, colour (up to two) and size. Category, brand and
  size come from the target marketplace&rsquo;s own lists, so you can fix a
  mapping before anything is sent. Click **Save** to keep your changes.
  Prices are in the source listing&rsquo;s currency.
- **Remove** takes the item out of this batch.
- A warning appears under an item when something looks off, such as a title
  that names a different category from the one picked, or a kids&rsquo; size
  that had to be estimated.

When everything looks right, click **Crosslist to** with the target logo.
The items join the crosslist queue.

You can also crosslist straight from [My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings):
select active listings from one marketplace and click **Crosslist** in the
selection toolbar. That route skips the review step. See
[Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor).

## How the crosslist runs

For each item, SaleLinx reads the source listing, translates its category,
condition, colour, brand, size and shipping into what the other marketplace
expects, copies the photos across, and creates the copy on the target. It
all happens in the background; nothing is typed into the marketplace&rsquo;s
own sell form. Depop has no separate title field, so on a copy to Depop the
title becomes the first line of the description.

Common edge cases are handled for you:

- **Brand the target doesn&rsquo;t recognise** - a Depop copy gets
  Depop&rsquo;s **Other** brand, and a Vinted copy is left with no brand.
  Pick the right one on the draft before publishing.
- **Colour the target doesn&rsquo;t offer** - mapped to the nearest colour
  it accepts (mustard becomes yellow on Depop, for example).
- **Size missing from the target&rsquo;s sizes** - left blank for you to set.
- **Different currencies** - if your two shops use different currencies,
  the price is converted at the day&rsquo;s exchange rate. If no rate can be
  fetched, the copy keeps the same number in the new currency (a
  &pound;20 item is listed at &euro;20), so check the price on the draft.
- **A photo that can&rsquo;t be copied** - it&rsquo;s skipped and the rest
  go through. If no photos can be copied, that item fails.

If a crosslist looks off, the reason is usually on this list.

## Batches

There&rsquo;s no limit on how many items you select. Your monthly allowance
is the only cap: if you select more than you have left, only that many are
queued and the panel tells you how many made it.

A batch:

- Runs one item at a time, with pauses in between so the marketplaces
  don&rsquo;t see a burst of activity.
- Shows its progress in the panel&rsquo;s job queue, and keeps running if
  you close the panel.
- Pauses if the browser restarts part-way through. Press **Resume** in the
  job queue to carry on from where it stopped.
- Skips an item that fails and moves on to the next. Select it again later
  to retry.
- Re-reads the target marketplace when it finishes, so the new drafts show
  up in My Listings.

From version 1.1.12, each copy is counted against your allowance as it is
created and failed items don&rsquo;t count. If the allowance runs out
part-way, the batch stops. In version 1.1.11 the batch is counted when it
finishes, failed items included.

## Copies are saved as drafts

Every copy lands as a **draft** on the target marketplace, never as a live
listing. Any mapping mistake (wrong category, missing brand, a size left
blank) sits in a draft you can check rather than on a listing buyers can
see.

Open the draft on the marketplace, fix anything that looks wrong and publish
it. You can also publish drafts from My Listings with **Post**.

## The original and the copy aren&rsquo;t linked

Crosslisting doesn&rsquo;t link the original and its copy, so the source
listing stays under the Unlinked filter afterwards. Check before you
crosslist the same item twice.

To pair them, publish the copy, save both listings to the cloud, then link
them in the [Linking tab](/docs/automate/linking), or select both in My
Listings and click **Link**. Linked pairs are what the
[Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) works from. Cloud saving and the
Linking tab are part of the Pro and Business plans.

## Troubleshooting

**&ldquo;Both Depop and Vinted tabs must be open to crosslist.&rdquo;** Use
the button to open the missing site, or open it yourself. Make sure each tab
is signed in to the account you linked to SaleLinx.

**&ldquo;Both Depop and Vinted accounts must be linked before
crosslisting.&rdquo;** Link the missing account first. See
[Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace).

**The brand is &ldquo;Other&rdquo; or missing.** The source brand
isn&rsquo;t on the target marketplace&rsquo;s brand list. Pick a brand on the
draft before publishing, or set it in the review step next time.

**The category looks off.** SaleLinx&rsquo;s matching covers common items
well, but unusual ones can land in the wrong place. Watch for the category
warning in the review step, and pick the right category on the draft before
publishing.

**Photos are missing.** A photo that couldn&rsquo;t be copied is skipped.
Add it on the draft, or delete the draft and crosslist the item again.

**The price is wrong.** Your shops use different currencies and no exchange
rate could be fetched, so the number was kept as it was. Correct the price
on the draft.

**A Vinted listing won&rsquo;t fetch.** For a listing that isn&rsquo;t in
your synced shop, SaleLinx reads the listing&rsquo;s public page, and if
that fails it opens the listing briefly in a background tab. A listing that
has been deleted or has sold can&rsquo;t be fetched. Check the link, click
**Refresh listings** and try again.
