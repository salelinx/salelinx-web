# My Listings

**My Listings** is the first tab in the SaleLinx panel. It puts your Depop,
Vinted and eBay listings in one grid, with a marketplace logo on every row so you
always know where an item lives. From here you can search, filter and sort
your whole catalogue, then select listings and act on them in bulk.

## Where the listings come from

SaleLinx reads your listings from Depop and Vinted through your open
marketplace tabs. If the grid is empty the first time you open it, browse
your shop or open one of your listings on Depop or Vinted and it fills in.
eBay listings come straight from eBay once your account is linked (see
*eBay listings* below).

Above the grid there is a sync indicator for each marketplace. The dot tells
you how fresh that marketplace&rsquo;s data is:

- **Green** - read in the last five minutes. While a marketplace tab is
  open, SaleLinx re-reads your listings in the background to stay inside
  that window, and the dot pulses.
- **Yellow** - between 5 and 30 minutes old.
- **Orange** - more than 30 minutes old, or the last read came back
  incomplete.
- **Red** - more than two hours old, never read, or your marketplace session
  has expired.
- **Grey** - that marketplace account isn&rsquo;t linked to SaleLinx yet.

When something needs fixing, the indicator says what and gives you a
one-click fix such as **open site**, **reload tab** or **link account**.

From version 1.1.12 the dot stays green for the whole five minutes. In
version 1.1.11 it turns grey after the first minute.

Each indicator has its own refresh button, and **Refresh All** re-reads
both marketplaces. These only update what SaleLinx knows about your
listings. They don&rsquo;t bump anything up the marketplace&rsquo;s search
feed; for that, use the [Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh).

## What the grid shows

Each row is one listing, with these columns:

- **Item** - the title, with the brand and size underneath. A chain-link
  icon marks a linked pair, and a **Queued** badge appears while the listing
  is waiting on another job.
- **Site** - the Depop or Vinted logo. A linked Depop and Vinted pair shares
  one row with both logos, and the other columns show a value for each side.
- **Status** - active, sold, draft, hidden or delayed (Vinted is still
  reviewing it). **Not synced** means SaleLinx couldn&rsquo;t read it; open
  a tab for that marketplace.
- **Price** - a Depop listing on sale shows its original price struck
  through above the sale price.
- **Discount** - the Depop sale percentage, if there is one.
- **Views** and **Likes**.
- **Boost** - a rocket icon when the listing is boosted.
- **Restock** and **Qty** - the Restocker switch and how many restocks are
  left (see below).
- **Listed** - the date and time the listing went up. Vinted doesn&rsquo;t
  share this for every listing, so some Vinted rows show an estimated date
  with no time. Hover over it for details.
- **Cloud** - the cloud backup switch, one per marketplace on a linked row.

Click a column header to sort by it. Use the page controls above the grid to
move between pages and to show between 10 and 200 rows at a time.

Double-click a row to open the listing on the marketplace in a new tab. A
Vinted draft opens in Vinted&rsquo;s edit form, and a linked pair asks which
marketplace to open.

## Searching

The search box narrows the grid as you type. It matches the title and the
brand of the listings already in SaleLinx. It doesn&rsquo;t search Depop or
Vinted themselves.

## Filtering

The toolbar above the grid has quick filters:

- **Platform** - **All**, or Depop, Vinted or eBay on its own.
- **Linked** - the chain-link button next to them shows only listings that
  are linked across both marketplaces.
- **Status** - **All**, **Active**, **Sold**, **Draft**, **Hidden** or
  **Delayed**.
- **Cloud** - the cloud button cycles through all listings, cloud-saved
  listings only, and listings that aren&rsquo;t saved to the cloud.

Filters stack with each other and with the search box. Vinted + **Active**
+ &ldquo;jeans&rdquo; narrows the grid to active Vinted listings with
&ldquo;jeans&rdquo; in the title or brand.

### Filter & Sort

**Filter & Sort** opens two extra rows for more precise views:

1. On the **Filter:** row, click **+ Add** and build a rule from a field, a
   comparison and a value. Number fields (**Price**, **Discount**,
   **Views**, **Likes**, **Quantity**, **Age (days)**) take **at least**,
   **at most**, **exactly**, **more than** or **less than**. **Title** takes
   **contains** or **excludes**. **Restock** and **Boosted** take **Yes** or
   **No**. A listing has to match every rule to stay in the grid.
2. On the **Sort:** row, click **+ Add** to add sort levels, for example
   price, then listed date. **Clear** puts the sort back to newest listed
   first.

For example, &ldquo;Age (days) more than 30&rdquo; plus &ldquo;Likes at most
2&rdquo; finds old listings nobody is interested in.

### Presets

**Presets** saves a view you use often so you can get it back in one click.

1. Set up the filters and sort you want.
2. Open **Presets** and click **+ Save current view**.
3. Give it a name and press Enter.

A preset remembers the platform, **Linked**, status and cloud filters, your
filter rules and your sort. It doesn&rsquo;t keep search text. Pick a preset
from the menu to apply it. While one is applied the button shows its name,
and the &times; beside it takes you back to the default view. You can keep up
to 20.

These presets are saved in your browser on this computer. They don&rsquo;t
follow your account to another browser or computer.

## Selecting listings

- **Click a row** to select it, and click it again to deselect it.
- **The checkbox in the header** selects every row on the current page.
- **Drag** - press on a row and drag up or down to select a run of rows.
  Start the drag on a row that is already selected to deselect instead.

Your selection is kept when you change page. Rows that are queued for
another job, or are still saving to the cloud, can&rsquo;t be selected until
they finish.

As soon as something is selected, a toolbar appears with the number
selected and every action that applies to it: edit, discount, duplicate,
crosslist, cloud, link, export, delete and more. Each one is covered step by
step in [Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor).

## Cloud sync

The **Cloud** switch on each row saves that listing&rsquo;s details and
photos to your SaleLinx account. It is part of the Pro and Business plans.
Only active listings can be switched on. Sold and draft listings that are
already saved can be switched off to tidy up.

Cloud-saved listings are what make linking, relisting sold items and the
Restocker possible. See [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync) for the
full picture.

## eBay listings

Once eBay is connected (see
[Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace)),
your active eBay listings and the last 60 days of sold ones appear in
the grid. eBay syncs at most every 15 minutes; **Refresh All** or the
eBay refresh button fetches them straight away.

Open an eBay listing to edit its title, description, price, quantity,
photos, condition, category and item specifics, and save straight to
eBay. Choose **Unlist** to end the listing. eBay has no delete, so
deleting an eBay row ends the listing and removes it from the grid.
An ended listing opens with a **Relist** option.

## Restocker columns

The **Restock** switch and **Qty** stepper start and manage restocking for a
row. They need the Business plan and a listing that is saved to the cloud
and still active (or already being restocked). See
[Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) for the full flow.

## Language and theme

The panel is available in English, French, Spanish and German, with light
and dark themes. Your listings stay in whatever language you wrote them in.
SaleLinx never translates your listing text.
