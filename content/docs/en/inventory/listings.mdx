# My Listings

**My Listings** is the first tab in the SaleLinx panel. It puts your Depop,
Vinted and eBay listings in one grid, with a marketplace logo on every row
so you always know where an item lives. From here you can search, filter and sort
your whole catalogue, select listings and act on them in bulk, or make a
new listing on several marketplaces at once with **Create listing**.

## Where the listings come from

SaleLinx reads your listings from Depop and Vinted through your open
marketplace tabs. If the grid is empty the first time you open it, browse
your shop or open one of your listings on Depop or Vinted and it fills in.
eBay listings come straight from eBay once your account is linked (see
*eBay listings* below).

Above the grid there is a sync status for each marketplace. It says how
long ago that marketplace was read, such as **Just now** or **12m ago**,
and its colour tells you how fresh that is:

- **Green** - read in the last five minutes. While a marketplace tab is
  open, SaleLinx re-reads your listings in the background to stay inside
  that window.
- **Amber** - between 5 minutes and two hours old, or the last read came
  back incomplete.
- **Red** - more than two hours old, never read (**Not synced**), or your
  marketplace session has expired.
- **Grey** - that marketplace account isn&rsquo;t linked to SaleLinx yet.

When something needs fixing, the status gives you a one-click fix instead:
**Open tab**, **Reload**, **Log in**, **Link**, **Reconnect**, **Retry** or
**Wrong account**.

Click a marketplace&rsquo;s status to re-read just that marketplace, and the
update button (&ldquo;Update listings from all platforms&rdquo;) re-reads
every marketplace you sell on. These only update what SaleLinx knows about your
listings. They don&rsquo;t bump anything up the marketplace&rsquo;s search
feed; for that, use the [Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh).

## What the grid shows

Each row is one listing, with these columns:

- **Item** - the title, with the brand and size underneath. A chain-link
  icon marks linked listings, and a **Queued** badge appears while the
  listing is waiting on another job.
- **Site** - the Depop, Vinted or eBay logo. Linked listings share one row,
  with one line for each marketplace they&rsquo;re on (two or all three), and
  the other columns show a value for each.
- **Status** - active, sold, draft, hidden or delayed (Vinted is still
  reviewing it). A listing you deleted on the marketplace shows as
  **Deleted**, and its cloud backup is kept so you can relist it. An eBay
  listing that has ended shows as **Unlisted**, and an ended auction as
  **Auction ended**. A **Sync** button means SaleLinx hasn&rsquo;t read it
  yet; click it to sync that marketplace now.
- **Price** - a Depop listing on sale shows its original price struck
  through above the sale price. A live eBay auction shows an **Auction**
  tag beside its price, with the number of bids underneath. Hover over the
  tag for the bids, the end date, the starting price and any Buy It Now
  price.
- **Discount** - the Depop sale percentage, if there is one.
- **Views** and **Likes**.
- **Boost** - a rocket icon when the listing is boosted.
- **Restock** and **Qty** - the Restocker switch and how many restocks are
  left (see below).
- **Listed** - the date and time the listing went up. Vinted doesn&rsquo;t
  share this for every listing, so some Vinted rows show an estimated date
  with no time. Hover over it for details.
- **Cloud** - the cloud backup switch, one per marketplace on a linked row.

Click a column header to sort by it. Use the page controls above the grid to
move between pages and to show between 10 and 200 rows at a time.

Double-click a row to open the listing on the marketplace in a new tab. A
Vinted draft opens in Vinted&rsquo;s edit form, an active eBay listing opens
in eBay&rsquo;s own edit form, and a linked pair asks which marketplace to
open.

## Searching

The search box narrows the grid as you type. It matches the title and the
brand of the listings already in SaleLinx. It doesn&rsquo;t search the
marketplaces themselves.

## Filtering

The toolbar above the grid has quick filters:

- **Platform** - **All**, or any of Depop, Vinted and eBay. Pick more than
  one to see them together.
- **Linked** and **Unlinked** - the chain-link buttons next to them show
  only listings that are, or aren&rsquo;t, linked across marketplaces. With
  **Linked** on, **Only these** keeps listings linked on exactly the
  marketplaces you picked.
- **Status** - **All**, **Active**, **Sold** or **Draft**. The arrow after
  them (&ldquo;More statuses&rdquo;) opens **Hidden** and **Delayed**.
- **Cloud** - the cloud button cycles through all listings, cloud-saved
  listings only, and listings that aren&rsquo;t saved to the cloud.

Filters stack with each other and with the search box. Vinted + **Active**
+ &ldquo;jeans&rdquo; narrows the grid to active Vinted listings with
&ldquo;jeans&rdquo; in the title or brand.

### Advanced filters and sorting

**Advanced** opens two extra rows for more precise views:

1. On the **Filter:** row, click **+ Add** and build a rule from a field, a
   comparison and a value. Number fields (**Price**, **Discount**,
   **Views**, **Likes**, **Quantity**, **Age (days)**) take **at least**,
   **at most**, **exactly**, **more than** or **less than**. **Title** takes
   **contains** or **excludes**. **Restock** and **Boosted** take **Yes** or
   **No**. A listing has to match every rule to stay in the grid. **Clear
   filters** removes them.
2. On the **Sort:** row, click **+ Add** to add sort levels, for example
   price, then listed date. **Reset sort** puts the sort back to newest
   listed first.

For example, &ldquo;Age (days) more than 30&rdquo; plus &ldquo;Likes at most
2&rdquo; finds old listings nobody is interested in.

### Presets

**Presets** saves a view you use often so you can get it back in one click.

1. Set up the filters and sort you want.
2. Open **Presets** and click **+ Save current view**.
3. Give it a name and press Enter.

A preset remembers the platform, **Linked**, status and cloud filters, your
filter rules and your sort. It doesn&rsquo;t keep search text. Pick a preset
from the menu to apply it. While one is applied the button shows its name,
and the &times; beside it takes you back to the default view. You can keep up
to 20.

These presets are saved in your browser on this computer. They don&rsquo;t
follow your account to another browser or computer.

## Selecting listings

- **Click a row** to select it, and click it again to deselect it.
- **Shift-click** - click a row, then Shift-click another to select every
  row in between.
- **The checkbox in the header** selects every row on the current page.
- **Drag** - press on a row and drag up or down to select a run of rows.
  Start the drag on a row that is already selected to deselect instead.

Your selection is kept when you change page. Rows that are queued for
another job, or are still saving to the cloud, can&rsquo;t be selected until
they finish.

As soon as something is selected, a toolbar appears with the number
selected and every action that applies to it: edit, discount, duplicate,
crosslist, cloud, link, export, delete and more. Each one is covered step by
step in [Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor).

## Create listing

**Create listing**, at the top of the tab, makes one new listing on any of
Depop, Vinted and eBay in one go.

1. Under **Where to list**, pick the marketplaces. Only the marketplaces
   you sell on appear, and one you haven&rsquo;t linked yet is greyed out.
2. Add **Photos**: drag them in, or click to choose. The first one is the
   cover. Depop takes up to 8 photos, Vinted 20 and eBay 24, and a
   marketplace that takes fewer than you added gets the first ones.
3. Fill in the **Details**: **Title**, **Description**, **Category**,
   **Price**, **Condition**, **Brand**, **Colour** and **Size**. With Vinted
   picked there is also **Parcel size**, and with eBay picked, any item
   specifics eBay needs for the category.
4. Check each marketplace&rsquo;s row, then click **Create listing** (or
   **Create listings** for more than one marketplace).

You pick the category and size from one marketplace&rsquo;s lists, chosen
under **Categories from**, and SaleLinx matches the others to it. Each
marketplace&rsquo;s row shows the category it matched. Click **Edit** on a
row to correct its category and size (for eBay, its condition and item
specifics), then **Done**. If nothing matched, the row says **No match
found. Pick one with Edit.**

The button stays greyed out until the listing is ready, and the form shows
the first thing to fix: **Pick at least one marketplace.**, **Add at least
one photo.**, **Add a title.**, **Add a description.**, **Add a price.**,
then any marketplace limit, such as **Vinted needs a title and description
of 5 characters or more.**, then **Pick a category.** Hover over the button
to see everything at once. If a marketplace is only missing some details,
the first click notes them under each one, and the button becomes **Create
anyway**.

Below the marketplaces are three switches, all on each time you open the
form:

- **Save as drafts** - the listings are made as drafts for you to check.
  Off posts them: eBay goes live straight away, and Depop and Vinted are
  posted once they are made. With eBay picked and this off, eBay&rsquo;s
  fee shows on its row first.
- **Back up to the cloud** - keeps every copy in your cloud backup, so you
  can relist or restock them later.
- **Link them together** - links the new listings, so a sale on one updates
  the others. It needs the backup on and two or more marketplaces.

The last two need a plan with cloud sync, and don&rsquo;t appear without
one.

The listings are made in the background, and show in the
[queue](/docs/getting-started/queue) as **Create Listing**. Depop and
Vinted need a tab open, and eBay drafts need a signed-in eBay tab. The form
keeps what you&rsquo;ve entered if you close it; **Reset** clears it. If a
marketplace fails, the form shows **Not made on** with the reason and a
**Try again** button, which makes only what&rsquo;s missing.

To start from a draft you already have, select its row on its own and click
**Create listing** in the selection toolbar. The form opens filled in from
the draft. A Depop or Vinted draft is updated in place, and an eBay draft is
replaced by the new one.

The first marketplace a listing is made on is free. Each further one counts
as a crosslist, checked against your monthly allowance before anything is
made. If you don&rsquo;t have enough left, the form says so, for example
**Only 2 crosslists left this month**, with an **Upgrade** button.

## Cloud sync

The **Cloud** switch on each row saves that listing&rsquo;s details and
photos to your SaleLinx account. It is part of the Pro and Business plans.
Active and draft listings can be switched on. Sold and deleted listings
that are already saved can be switched off to tidy up. Turning off a
linked listing also unlinks it, so SaleLinx asks first (**Turn off and
unlink**).

Drafts made by [Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist) or Create listing
with **Back up to the cloud** on are saved for you.

Cloud-saved listings are what make linking, relisting sold items and the
Restocker possible. See [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync) for the
full picture.

## eBay listings

Once eBay is connected (see
[Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace)),
your active eBay listings appear in the grid, along with your eBay drafts
and the ones that sold or ended in the last 60 days. The eBay sync status
and the eBay filter button only appear once eBay is linked. SaleLinx checks
eBay at most every 15 minutes, so its status is often amber between checks;
the update button or the eBay status fetches your listings straight away.
If the status says **Reconnect**, click it and link eBay again in Settings.

To edit eBay listings, select them and click **Edit** (see
[Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor)). An eBay card has the title,
description, price, quantity, photos, category, condition and item
specifics, and **Save Changes** sends them straight to eBay. Each card also
has **Allow offers** with a **Minimum offer**; business sellers can add an
auto-accept price, and private sellers on eBay UK see a **Buyer will pay**
box with eBay&rsquo;s Buyer Protection fee added.

- **End listing**, in the card&rsquo;s status, ends the listing.
- An auction can&rsquo;t be edited in SaleLinx. Its card links you to eBay,
  and bulk changes skip it.
- An **Unlisted** listing can only be relisted: pick **Relist** in its
  status and save, then edit it once it&rsquo;s back on sale.
- eBay has no delete, so **Delete** ends the listing and removes it from
  the grid.
- If eBay took a listing down, its card says so and why. It can&rsquo;t be
  edited or relisted; delete it to clear it from the grid.

## Restocker columns

The **Restock** switch and **Qty** stepper start and manage restocking for a
row. They need the Business plan and a listing that is saved to the cloud
and still active (or already being restocked). They don&rsquo;t work on
eBay rows. See [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) for the full flow.

## Language and theme

The panel is available in English, French, Spanish and German, with light
and dark themes. Your listings stay in whatever language you wrote them in.
SaleLinx never translates your listing text.
