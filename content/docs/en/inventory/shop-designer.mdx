# Shop Designer

Shop Designer lets you rearrange the listings on your Depop shop by
dragging them into the order you want, then applies that order for
you. It&rsquo;s handy for putting featured pieces first and pushing
older stock further down.

It works because Depop shows your shop with the most recently saved
listing first. When you apply a layout, SaleLinx re-saves your
listings in the right order so each one lands where you put it.

This feature is **Depop only**.

## Opening the designer

Open the **Shop Designer** tab. It works from any page, as long as a
Depop tab is open somewhere in Chrome. Each time you open it, it loads
your active listings straight from Depop, in the order your shop shows
them now. Large shops can take a few seconds.

Your shop appears twice, side by side:

- **Mobile** - your shop as it looks on a phone.
- **Desktop** - your shop as it looks in a browser.

Both show your profile picture, bio and follower, following and sold
counts above the grid, so you can judge the layout the way a buyer
sees it. A change in one preview shows in both.

## Rearranging

- **Drag** any listing to a new spot.
- **Bring to top** and **Bring to bottom**, the two arrow buttons on
  each listing, send it straight to the first or last spot.
- Listings that are no longer in their original spot are marked, so
  you can see what has changed.
- **Reset** puts everything back in your shop&rsquo;s current order.

The reload button next to the item count fetches your listings from
Depop again.

## Applying the layout

Click **Apply Layout**. SaleLinx re-saves the listings that need to
move, starting with the lowest and working up, so the last one it
saves lands on top. Listings at the bottom of your shop that are
already in the right order are left alone.

That makes moving a listing up cheap and moving one down expensive.
Bringing one listing to the top re-saves just that listing. Sending
one to the bottom means re-saving every listing that should sit above
it.

A progress bar shows how far it has got, and **Stop** ends it early.
If you stop partway, the listings re-saved so far sit at the top of
your shop and the rest keep their old order below them.

Each listing that moves takes several seconds, because SaleLinx paces
the saves like a person working through them, so a big rearrangement
can take a while. You can close the panel and carry on browsing; keep
the Depop tab open. Other SaleLinx jobs wait until it finishes. When
it&rsquo;s done, the designer reloads your shop from Depop so you can
check the result.

Re-saving is the same thing a [refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh) does,
so the listings that moved also get a fresh &ldquo;last edited&rdquo;
time. Applying a layout doesn&rsquo;t use your daily refresh
allowance.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keeping your layout">
Your layout lasts until something else saves a listing. A new listing,
an edit or a refresh puts that listing at the top of your shop. Refresh
All and Auto-Refresh keep your order when they cover your whole shop;
when a run is cut short (by its cap, your allowance or the skip
setting), the listings it reached move to the top. Apply your layout
again after that.
</Callout>

## Common tasks

**Featuring a new collection.** Drag the new pieces to the top (or use
**Bring to top** on each, starting with the one you want last), then
click **Apply Layout**. They&rsquo;ll be the first thing shoppers see
on your profile.

**Bringing older pieces back up.** Older listings sink as you list new
ones. Move the ones you want seen to the top and apply. Because moving
up is cheap, this is quick even in a large shop.

## Limitations

- **Depop only.**
- **Active listings only** - sold items and drafts aren&rsquo;t shown.
- **One flat order** - there are no sections or groups.

## Plan and access

Shop Designer is included on Starter, Pro and Business, and on the
14-day free trial. Without one of those plans, the tab shows a lock.
