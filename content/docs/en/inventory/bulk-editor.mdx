# Bulk editor

Select one or more listings in [My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) and a
toolbar appears with the number selected and the actions you can take. It
only shows the buttons that make sense for what you picked, so the set
changes as your selection does. This guide walks through each one.

Most actions are queued and run in the background, one listing at a time,
with progress shown in the panel&rsquo;s job queue. You can keep working or
close the panel while they run. A listing that is waiting on a job shows a
**Queued** badge in the grid and can&rsquo;t be selected again until the job
reaches it.

## The toolbar at a glance

These are always there: **Edit**, **Refresh**, **Duplicate**, **Link** (or
**Unlink**), **Cloud**, **Export**, **Delete** and **Deselect**, which
clears the selection. The rest depend on what you&rsquo;ve selected:

- **Discount** - at least one active Depop listing.
- **Crosslist** - every listing is active and on the same marketplace.
- **Relist** - at least one sold listing that was saved to the cloud.
- **Post** - every listing is a draft.
- **Hide** or **Unhide** - every listing is an active or hidden Vinted
  listing.

**Plans.** **Save to Cloud** and **Update Cloud** are part of the Pro and
Business plans and show a padlock on lower plans. **Relist** and **Link**
work on cloud-saved listings, so in practice they need Pro or Business too.
**Crosslist** uses your monthly crosslist allowance (see
[pricing](/pricing)). The other actions aren&rsquo;t tied to a plan.

## Edit

**Edit** opens an editor with one card per listing, so you can change each
one by hand, or make the same change to many at once with **Bulk Edit**.

1. Select the listings and click **Edit**. Sold listings, listings Vinted is
   still reviewing and reserved Vinted listings can&rsquo;t be edited. They
   are left out, with a note at the top of the editor saying why.
2. Change any field on any card. Depending on the marketplace, a card has:
   - **Title** (Vinted only; on Depop the first line of the description is
     the title).
   - **Description**, **Category**, **Brand**, **Price**, **Condition** and
     **Colour** (up to two).
   - **Size**, loaded from the sizes that listing&rsquo;s category allows.
   - **Quantity** (Depop) or **Parcel size** (Vinted).
   - A status chip (see Status below).
   - The photo strip (see Photos below).
3. Click **Save Changes**. The edits are queued and sent to Depop and Vinted
   in the background.

On Depop the brand has to be one Depop recognises. If it isn&rsquo;t, the
editor says so: pick one from the list, or leave it empty for no brand.

The bar at the top of the editor also has a select-all checkbox,
**Remove** (takes the ticked cards out of this edit without touching the
listings), **Split photos** (see below), **Bulk Edit**, and a button that
shows two cards per row. Click anywhere on a card to tick it.

### Bulk Edit

1. In the editor, click **Bulk Edit**. A panel opens on the right.
2. Pick a **Field**: **Price**, **Title**, **Description**, **Brand**,
   **Condition**, **Colour**, **Size**, **Quantity**, **Category**,
   **Parcel size**, **Status** or **Image**.
3. Enter or choose the new value.
4. Click **Apply** to change only the cards you&rsquo;ve ticked, or **All**
   to change every card in the editor.
5. Check the cards, then click **Save Changes**.

Bulk changes only land where they make sense. **Title** and **Parcel size**
skip Depop cards, **Quantity** skips Vinted cards, and a category only
applies to cards from its own marketplace (the list offers both Depop and
Vinted categories). Changing the category clears the size, because sizes
depend on the category. If nothing in the editor can take the value, the
panel says **No eligible items for this field**.

**Price** has a mode next to the value: **Set to**, **Increase by**,
**Decrease by**, **Increase by %** or **Decrease by %**. Each mode works
from the price the card is currently showing, so applying it twice stacks.
A card whose price would fall to zero or below is left alone.

From version 1.1.12, the Price field can raise or lower prices by an amount
or a percentage. In version 1.1.11 it can only set a new price.

### Status

The status chip on each card offers the moves that listing can make:

- **Vinted** - switch between **Active** and **Hidden**, or **Publish** a
  draft.
- **Depop** - an active listing can be set to **Sold**. Depop has no hide,
  so this sets the stock to zero, which Depop treats as sold. Depop locks
  sold listings, so this can&rsquo;t be undone; relisting is the only way
  back.

In **Bulk Edit**, use the **Status** field for Vinted listings. It offers
**Active**, **Hidden** and **Publish** (for Vinted drafts), and each card
only takes a move it can make. To publish Depop drafts, use **Post**.

### Photos

Each card has a photo strip. The first photo is marked **Cover**.

- The **Add** tile uploads a new photo, and the &times; on a photo removes
  it.
- Drag photos to reorder them.
- The pencil on a photo opens **Edit Photo**, with **Rotation**,
  **Scale** and **Brightness** sliders, **Rotate 90&deg;**, **Flip**,
  **Crop**, **Randomise** (a slight random tweak) and **Reset**. Click
  **Save Photo** when the after-preview looks right.

To adjust many photos at once:

1. In **Bulk Edit**, pick **Image** and click **Configure&hellip;**.
2. **Bulk Edit Photos** shows every photo from the cards you ticked (or from
   all cards if none are ticked). Click a photo to leave it out.
3. Set the sliders, **Rotate 90&deg;** or **Flip**, then click the
   **Apply to** button, which shows how many photos it will change. Or
   click **Randomise** to give every chosen photo its own slight random
   tweak.
4. Click **Save Changes** in the editor to upload the edited photos.

### Split photos (Depop)

**Split photos** turns each photo of a Depop listing into its own draft,
for a bundle you want to sell piece by piece.

1. In the editor, tick one or more Depop listings with at least two photos.
2. Click **Split photos**. The confirmation says how many drafts it will
   create.
3. Click **Create drafts**.

Price, brand, condition and shipping are copied to every draft. Only the
first line of the description carries over, so you can write each one. The
original listing isn&rsquo;t deleted.

## Discount (Depop)

**Discount** puts active Depop listings on sale with Depop&rsquo;s own
discount, so buyers see the original price struck through. Buyers who liked
or saved the item are told about the drop. Vinted has no per-item discount,
so Vinted listings in the selection are left out.

1. Click **Discount**.
2. Set the percentage with the slider (5 to 95&#37; in steps of 5) or type
   any whole number from 1 to 95.
3. Check the list. Each listing shows its new price, and any listing that is
   already at that discount, or whose sale price would fall below Depop&rsquo;s
   1.00 minimum, is marked as skipped.
4. Click **Apply discount**.

To end a sale, select the listings, click **Discount**, then
**Remove discount**.

## Refresh

**Refresh** re-reads the selected listings from Depop and Vinted, so the
grid shows their current status and details. Use it after editing a listing
on the marketplace itself, or to check whether a listing is still up. It
doesn&rsquo;t bump anything up the search feed; for that, use the
[Refresh tab](/docs/inventory/refresh).

## Crosslist

**Crosslist** sends the selection straight to the crosslist queue, the same
queue the [Crosslist tab](/docs/inventory/crosslist) uses, and creates a
copy of each listing on the other marketplace.

1. Select active listings from one marketplace only. (Drafts, sold listings
   and mixed selections don&rsquo;t get the button.)
2. Click **Crosslist**. You&rsquo;ll need a Depop tab and a Vinted tab open,
   and both accounts linked to SaleLinx.

There is no review step from here. If you want to check or edit the copies
before they&rsquo;re sent, start from the Crosslist tab instead. If the
selection is bigger than what&rsquo;s left of your monthly allowance, only
that many are queued and the panel tells you.

From version 1.1.12 every copy is saved as a draft. In version 1.1.11 the
Crosslist tab&rsquo;s **Auto-post** switch decided whether copies were
published straight away.

## Duplicate

**Duplicate** creates a draft copy of each listing on the same marketplace.
The originals aren&rsquo;t touched.

1. Click **Duplicate**.
2. If every selected row is a linked pair, choose **Depop only**,
   **Vinted only** or **Both**.
3. If the selection includes sold Vinted listings that weren&rsquo;t saved
   to the cloud, SaleLinx warns that only a partial draft can be made for
   them and you may need to fill in some details. Click **Continue** to go
   ahead.

The drafts appear in the grid with the **Draft** status. Use **Post** to
publish them.

## Post drafts

**Post** appears when every selected listing is a draft, on either
marketplace.

1. Click **Post**. The **Post drafts** window opens.
2. Choose **Post all now**, or **Schedule posts** and pick an interval
   between posts (1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes, or type your own).
3. Click **Start**.

Scheduled posts keep running even if you close the panel.

## Hide and Unhide (Vinted)

**Hide** takes active Vinted listings out of view without deleting them.
When every selected listing is already hidden, the button reads
**Unhide** and puts them back. The button only appears when the whole
selection is active or hidden Vinted listings.

Click it and a progress window works through the listings one by one, then
reports how many succeeded.

## Cloud

**Cloud** opens a small menu:

- **Save to Cloud** - backs up the details and photos of the selected
  listings to your SaleLinx account. Only active listings can be saved;
  others are skipped and the panel tells you how many.
- **Update Cloud** - re-sends the latest details and photos of listings
  that are already saved. Use it after editing a listing on the marketplace.
- **Remove from Cloud** - deletes the saved details and photo backups for
  the selected listings, after you confirm. The listings themselves stay up.

**Update Cloud** and **Remove from Cloud** only appear when at least one
selected listing is saved. Rows lock with a spinner on their cloud switch
while they save. See [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync).

## Link and Unlink

**Link** pairs a Depop listing with its Vinted twin so SaleLinx knows
they&rsquo;re the same item. That is what the
[Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) works from. A linked pair then shows as
one row with both logos.

1. Select exactly two listings: one from Depop and one from Vinted.
2. Make sure both are active or sold, and both are saved to the cloud (and
   have finished saving). Drafts can&rsquo;t be linked from here.
3. Click **Link**. If the two are set up in the Restocker with different
   restock counts, choose which count both should use.

To undo it, select the linked row; the button reads **Unlink**. If the pair
is tracked by the Restocker, SaleLinx tells you both listings will stay
tracked on their own.

To find matching pairs across your whole catalogue, use the
[Linking tab](/docs/automate/linking).

## Export

**Export** downloads a CSV file of the selected listings in SaleLinx&rsquo;s
own format, the same format [CSV import](/docs/inventory/csv-import) reads.
To export everything, use the CSV button in the My Listings toolbar and
choose **Export All**.

## Delete

**Delete** removes listings from the marketplace for good. There is no undo.

1. Click **Delete**.
2. If any of the listings are saved to the cloud, the confirmation has a
   box to delete their cloud backups too. It starts unticked: leave it that
   way and the backups stay, so you can relist the items later.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm.

If you&rsquo;ve selected a whole linked pair, the confirmation shows a card
for each side instead. Each side has its listing ticked and its
**Cloud backup** unticked. Untick a listing to keep that side live, for
example to delete only the Vinted copy and keep selling on Depop. Tick a
**Cloud backup** to remove that backup too. Ticking only a backup removes
the backup and leaves the listing up.

## Relist sold items

**Relist** appears when the selection includes a sold listing that was
saved to the cloud before it sold. It recreates the item from the cloud
copy as a new listing on the same marketplace. To relist active listings
instead, use the [Relist tab](/docs/inventory/relist).

1. Click **Relist** and choose how:
   - **Quick relist** reposts with the recommended settings.
   - **Relist with config** lets you set photo, title, description and
     price changes first.
2. If every selected row is a linked pair, choose **Depop only**,
   **Vinted only** or **Both**.
3. Choose where the cloud save goes:
   - **Move to the new listing** carries its link, restock settings and
     saved details over to the new listing. This is the usual choice.
   - **Keep on the sold item** leaves that record where it is.
4. For **Quick relist**, choose **Relist to post** to publish it now or
   **Relist as draft** to publish it yourself later. For
   **Relist with config**, set your changes in the **Relist Confirmation**
   window (it has its own save-as-draft option) and click
   **Confirm Relist**.
