# Offers

The **Offers** tab gathers your pending offers from Depop and Vinted
in one place, in two columns side by side. The marketplace you are
browsing has its column on the left. Each card shows the item, the
buyer&rsquo;s username, when the offer came in, the offer amount, your
listed price and the discount the buyer is asking for, with three
buttons: **Accept**, **Decline** and **Counter**.

The tab also hosts the [Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers)
block at the top, for accepting offers above a threshold without you
clicking through.

## What shows up

- **Depop** - offers on listings you are selling that are waiting for
  your answer.
- **Vinted** - offers buyers have made in your inbox conversations
  that you haven&rsquo;t answered yet. Offers you made as a buyer are
  not shown.

Bundle offers (several items in one Vinted offer) list each item in
the bundle inside the card.

A column with nothing waiting says **No new offers**. If SaleLinx
couldn&rsquo;t reach a marketplace, for example because no tab is open
for it, the column tells you that instead of showing an empty list.
Open a tab for that marketplace and refresh. A column for a
marketplace you haven&rsquo;t linked is blurred, with a prompt to link
the account in Settings.

## Age filter (Vinted)

The dropdown at the top of the tab sets how far back SaleLinx looks
through your Vinted inbox: 1, 3, 5, 7, 14 or 30 days (default 3).
Most sellers want to act on fresh offers; older ones are usually
irrelevant. The filter applies to Vinted only. Depop always shows
every offer still waiting for your answer.

## Acting on an offer

Each card has three buttons:

- **Accept** - accepts the buyer&rsquo;s offer on the marketplace.
- **Decline** - declines the offer.
- **Counter** - opens a price box, already filled in halfway between
  the offer and your listed price. Type your own price, or use a
  quick-counter button: **-10%** fills in 10% off your listed price,
  and **-£1** fills in 1 off it (in the offer&rsquo;s currency). Then
  press **Send**. **Cancel** closes the box without sending.

After you act, the card stays where it is and shows the result:
**Accepted**, **Declined**, or **Countered at** with your price.
Answered offers drop off the list the next time it loads.

Offer actions go ahead of other SaleLinx jobs waiting on the same
marketplace, so they go through promptly even while a bot is running.

## Refreshing

Offers load when you open the tab. The **Refresh** button (the
circular arrow at the top of the tab) loads both marketplaces again.
There&rsquo;s no auto-refresh on a timer. To avoid repeat requests,
SaleLinx reuses offers it fetched in the last two minutes, so a
brand-new offer can take a couple of minutes to appear.

## Shared with Messages

On Vinted, Offers and [Messages](/docs/buyers/messages) share the
inbox and conversations SaleLinx has already loaded, so opening both
tabs doesn&rsquo;t fetch the same chats twice. Accepting, declining or
countering clears that saved copy, so you never see a stale view.

## No bulk decline

There is no button to decline many offers at once. Answer offers one
card at a time, or leave the ones you don&rsquo;t want to expire.

## Auto-accept presets

The [Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers) settings have a
preset picker at the top. A preset saves the minimum percentage of the
asking price, the price floor, which platforms (Depop, Vinted) to
check, and how often to check. Picking a preset fills in every setting
at once, and for auto-accept the picked preset is the live rule
immediately. Two are built in:

- **Default** - 90% of asking, no price floor, both platforms,
  checked every 15 minutes.
- **Careful** - 95% of asking, no price floor, both platforms,
  checked every 30 minutes.

A preset deliberately does not include the auto-accept on/off switch.
Turning auto-accept on is always a separate, explicit step, whichever
preset is selected.

Change any setting by hand and the picker keeps the preset&rsquo;s
name with a dot next to it, to show the values have changed. Its menu
then offers **Save as new&hellip;**, **Revert**, and, for one of your
own presets, **Save changes**. Saving under the name of one of your
existing presets asks before overwriting it, and the built-in names
can&rsquo;t be reused. Your own presets can be deleted from the menu
after a confirmation; the built-ins can&rsquo;t.

Your saved presets are stored with your SaleLinx account, so they
survive a reinstall and follow you to another computer. See
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription)
for how syncing, sign-out and per-device selection work.

## Plan and access

The Offers tab is available on **Starter, Pro and Business**, and on
the free trial. The [Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers)
block at the top of the tab is **Pro and Business** only.
