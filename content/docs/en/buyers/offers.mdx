# Offers

The **Offers** tab gathers your pending offers from Depop, Vinted and eBay
in one place, in a column each, side by side. The marketplace you are
browsing has its column on the left. Each card shows the item, the
buyer&rsquo;s username, when the offer came in, the offer amount, your
listed price and the discount the buyer is asking for, with three
buttons: **Accept**, **Decline** and **Counter**.

The toolbar shows how many offers are pending, and the same count sits
next to **Offers** in the sidebar. Its **Auto-Accept** button opens
[Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers), for accepting offers
above a threshold without you clicking through. A green dot on the
button means it&rsquo;s on.

Offers covers Depop, Vinted and eBay. On eBay it shows Best Offers,
which you can accept, decline or counter here. You still set eBay&rsquo;s
offer options on each listing in its editor in
[My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings).

## What shows up

- **Depop** - offers on listings you are selling that are waiting for
  your answer.
- **Vinted** - offers buyers have made in your inbox conversations
  that you haven&rsquo;t answered yet. Offers you made as a buyer are
  not shown.
- **eBay** - Best Offers waiting for your answer. eBay gives each
  one a 48 hour window. Its cards take the title and photo from the
  listing in My Listings.

Bundle offers (several items in one Vinted offer) list each item in
the bundle inside the card. An eBay offer for more than one of an item
says how many (**for 2**, for example). If the buyer has made offers
before, **Earlier** on the card lists them, with your counters and
whether each was accepted, declined or countered.

A column with nothing waiting says **No new offers**. When you open
the tab, SaleLinx checks that Depop and Vinted are open in a tab, and
a popup asks you to open any that isn&rsquo;t. eBay needs no tab. If a
marketplace still can&rsquo;t be reached, its column says so instead of
showing an empty list. A column for a marketplace you haven&rsquo;t
linked is blurred, with a prompt to link the account in Settings. A
marketplace you&rsquo;ve switched off in Settings has no column.

## Time window and sorting

The **Time window** dropdown sets how far back SaleLinx looks: 1, 3,
5, 7, 14 or 30 days (default 3). It applies to every column, but it
matters most on Vinted, where old conversations pile up. Depop and
eBay offers expire within a day or two anyway.

**Sort offers** orders each column by **Newest first** (the default),
**Oldest first** or **Best offers**, which puts the smallest discount
first. Both go back to their defaults when the panel reloads.

## Acting on an offer

Each card has three buttons:

- **Accept** - accepts the buyer&rsquo;s offer on the marketplace.
- **Decline** - declines the offer.
- **Counter** - opens a price box. Type a price above the
  buyer&rsquo;s offer and no higher than your listed price, then press
  **Send** (or Enter). **Cancel** closes the box without sending.

After you act, the card shows the result for two seconds
(**Accepted**, **Declined**, or **Countered at** with your price),
then leaves the list. A short line at the bottom of the column keeps a
record, and a column you&rsquo;ve cleared says **All caught up**. If
the offer had already expired or been answered, the card says
**Expired or already answered** instead.

Offer actions go ahead of other SaleLinx jobs waiting on the same
marketplace, so they go through promptly even while a bot is running.

## Refreshing

Offers load when you open the tab. The **Refresh** button (the
circular arrow in the toolbar) loads every marketplace again. The tab
doesn&rsquo;t reload on a timer while you&rsquo;re looking at it, but
it does after Auto-Accept accepts an offer. While you&rsquo;re on
another tab, SaleLinx checks every 5 minutes so the count next to
**Offers** stays current. A brand-new Vinted conversation or eBay
offer can take a couple of minutes to appear, because SaleLinx reuses
those for two minutes to avoid repeat requests.

## Shared with Messages

On Vinted, Offers and [Messages](/docs/buyers/messages) share the
inbox and conversations SaleLinx has already loaded, so opening both
tabs doesn&rsquo;t fetch the same chats twice. Accepting, declining or
countering clears that saved copy, so you never see a stale view.

## No bulk decline

There is no button to decline many offers at once. Answer offers one
card at a time, or leave the ones you don&rsquo;t want to expire.

## Auto-accept presets

The [Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers) popup has a
preset picker below its settings. A preset saves the minimum
percentage of the asking price, the price floor, which marketplaces
(Depop, Vinted, eBay) to check, and how often to check. Picking a
preset fills in every setting at once, and for auto-accept the picked
preset is the live rule immediately. Two are built in:

- **Default** - 90% of asking, no price floor, no marketplace ticked,
  checked every 10 minutes.
- **Careful** - 95% of asking, no price floor, no marketplace ticked,
  checked every 30 minutes.

A preset deliberately does not include the auto-accept on/off switch.
Turning auto-accept on is always a separate, explicit step, whichever
preset is selected.

Change any setting by hand and the picker keeps the preset&rsquo;s
name with a dot next to it, to show the values have changed. Its menu
then offers **Save as new&hellip;**, **Revert**, and, for one of your
own presets, **Save changes**. Saving under the name of one of your
existing presets asks before overwriting it, and the built-in names
can&rsquo;t be reused. Your own presets can be deleted from the menu
after a confirmation; the built-ins can&rsquo;t.

Your saved presets are stored with your SaleLinx account, so they
survive a reinstall and follow you to another computer. See
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription)
for how syncing, sign-out and per-device selection work.

## Plan and access

The Offers tab is available on **Starter, Pro and Business**, and on
the free trial. [Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers) (the
**Auto-Accept** button in the toolbar) is **Pro and Business** only.
