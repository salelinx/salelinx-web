# Labels

The **Labels** tab gathers your sold orders from both marketplaces
and lets you download, print or email the label PDFs in one go -
instead of clicking each order in the marketplace UI and saving
labels one at a time. The same tab also hosts the Vinted
**feedback bot** for posting bulk seller feedback on completed
sales.

## What it shows

Two sections:

1. **To ship** - sold orders that have a label ready (Depop) or
   that need one ordered first (Vinted &ldquo;needs label&rdquo;
   flow). Each card shows the buyer, item title, carrier badge,
   and the order date.
2. **Shipped** - orders you&rsquo;ve marked sent, kept around so
   you can leave Vinted feedback in bulk from the same tab.

Filters at the top narrow by carrier (Evri, Royal Mail, etc.) or
by free-text search across buyer name and item title.

## Selecting orders

Click a card to select it. Click again to deselect. The toolbar
shows the selected count and enables **Print** and **Email**.

The header checkbox selects everything visible (respecting the
current filter), so you can do the day&rsquo;s shipping in one
selection.

## Downloading and printing

Click **Print labels** with one or more cards selected. SaleLinx:

1. Downloads each individual label PDF from the marketplace.
2. Merges them into one combined PDF in the order they appear in
   the panel.
3. Saves it to your downloads folder with a name like
   `labels-2026-05-10-12.pdf`.
4. Opens the merged PDF in a new tab so you can hit Print.

For Vinted&rsquo;s Evri labels (which come in A4 with extra
whitespace), SaleLinx auto-crops to the label box before merging so
you don&rsquo;t waste half a page per label.

## QR vs PDF (Depop)

Depop offers some carriers as a QR code instead of a PDF. For
those orders, the card shows a **PDF / QR** toggle. Pick whichever
your local drop-off point accepts.

## Emailing labels

Sometimes you want the labels on a different device (phone for
printing at the post office, partner&rsquo;s computer). Click
**Email labels** with your selection.

A small modal asks for an email address, then SaleLinx sends the
merged PDF straight from the extension. The subject and body are
pre-written; you can tick &ldquo;Remember this address&rdquo; so it
pre-fills next time.

If your account doesn&rsquo;t have email send configured (or it
fails), SaleLinx falls back to a regular `mailto:` link with the
PDF attached as a download.

## Vinted &ldquo;needs label&rdquo; flow

For Vinted orders where you haven&rsquo;t ordered the label yet,
the card shows **PDF** and **QR** buttons. Click one and SaleLinx
asks Vinted for that label type via the API. The card updates to
**Label ready** within a few seconds and joins the rest of the
printable batch.

## Vinted feedback bot

The **Shipped** section also surfaces orders that are eligible for
seller feedback. Each row carries a **Leave feedback** button. The
bot posts your feedback - with an optional follow-up DM - in bulk
or one-at-a-time.

### Eligibility

A sold Vinted order shows up as feedback-eligible only when **all**
of these are true:

- The transaction&rsquo;s user status is `completed` (the buyer
  has received the item).
- You haven&rsquo;t already left feedback on it.
- Vinted still exposes the feedback action (i.e. you&rsquo;re
  inside the feedback window).

The strict completion gate is deliberate: Vinted technically
exposes the feedback action earlier (in transit, on cancelled
orders, etc.), but acting on it then would risk reviews on orders
the buyer never received.

### Configuring the message

Click the gear icon in the Shipped section header to open the
feedback settings card:

- **Rating** - 1 to 5 stars. Defaults to 5.
- **Feedback message** - the public review text. Supports the
  `@username` placeholder, which is replaced with the
  buyer&rsquo;s Vinted login. Use `\@` to write a literal `@`.
- **Send follow-up DM** - toggle. When on, SaleLinx also sends a
  private chat message after the feedback lands.
- **DM message** - body of the optional DM, same `@username`
  substitution.

Settings are stored locally and apply to every run; there&rsquo;s
no per-order override (matching the simpler dotb-style flow).

### Running it

For a single order: click **Leave feedback** on the row. The row
disappears once the post lands.

For bulk:

1. Tick the rows you want to feedback.
2. Click **Leave feedback on selected** in the toolbar.
3. SaleLinx queues the posts through the task queue with standard
   pacing.

Selected rows are removed from the list as soon as the bot accepts
the job. If the run fails partway through, the next manual refresh
of the tab brings any un-fed-back rows back into view.

### Pacing

Feedback posts (and the optional DMs, queued separately) go through
SaleLinx&rsquo;s standard pacing so a bulk run doesn&rsquo;t look
automated to Vinted.

A failed DM doesn&rsquo;t invalidate the feedback. The row still
flips, and the failure is logged so you can chase it manually if
needed. If the marketplace pushes back hard the bot stops cleanly;
re-sign-in and try again later.
