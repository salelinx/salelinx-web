# Labels

The **Labels** tab gathers your sold orders from Depop and Vinted
and turns the labels you pick into one PDF you can print or email,
instead of opening each order in the marketplace and saving labels
one at a time. The same tab lets you order labels for, extend or
cancel Vinted orders, and leave Vinted feedback on completed sales
in bulk. eBay support is coming.

## What it shows

The tab has two lists:

1. Orders to ship, at the top: Depop sales awaiting shipment and
   Vinted sales waiting for you to post them.
2. **Completed orders**, below: completed Vinted sales that you can
   still leave feedback on. See Vinted feedback further down.

Each order card shows the item photo and title, the buyer, the
price, how long ago it sold and the carrier. Orders with several
items list each one underneath. Vinted orders also show a countdown
to the posting deadline, estimated as five working days from the
sale, which turns into **Overdue** once that passes.

Above the list:

- Platform chips (All, Depop and Vinted) with a count on each.
- A carrier dropdown (**All providers** by default) showing each
  carrier&rsquo;s logo and how many orders use it, so you can pull
  up just the Evri pile before a drop-off run.
- A search box that matches the item title or the buyer.
- The refresh button.

Changing the platform or carrier filter clears your selection.

A **Pending payout** card adds up the price of the orders still to
ship in the current view, per currency, with a Depop and Vinted
split when both have orders.

Depop leaves sold orders marked as awaiting shipment indefinitely,
so orders whose label has expired are hidden rather than left in
the tab offering a label that will no longer scan.

### Loading

Vinted order details load one at a time, so a progress line counts
them in (&ldquo;Loading order details 3 of 8&rdquo;) and cards still
waiting say **Loading details**. When you come back to the tab, it
shows the last list straight away and updates it in the background.
Click the refresh button to load everything again.

## Label status on each card

- **Label ready**: the label can be printed. Select the card.
- **QR**: the label only exists as a QR code. It prints as a QR slip
  you can show at the drop-off point.
- **PDF / QR** toggle (Depop): the carrier offers both. See QR or
  PDF below.
- **Needs label** on a Vinted order: no label has been ordered yet.
  See Ordering a Vinted label below.
- **Needs label** on a Depop order: create the label on Depop. There
  is no order-label button for Depop orders in SaleLinx. Once the
  label exists, refresh the tab.

## Selecting orders

Click a card, or its checkbox, to select it. Click again to deselect.
Only cards with a label ready can be selected for printing. The
toolbar shows how many are selected.

**Select all** selects every visible order with a label ready,
respecting the current filters and search, so you can do the
day&rsquo;s shipping in one selection.

## Printing labels

Click **Print Labels** with one or more cards selected. SaleLinx:

1. Downloads each label from the marketplace.
2. Merges them into one PDF, in the order they appear in the tab.
3. Opens the PDF in a new tab, in Chrome&rsquo;s PDF viewer, where
   you can print it.

Nothing is saved to your Downloads folder. Use the viewer&rsquo;s
download button if you want a copy. The PDF is named after the date
and the number of labels, like `labels-13-Oct-2026-3`.

If a label can&rsquo;t be downloaded, SaleLinx leaves it out and
merges the rest, so check the page count against your selection.

Vinted labels that come on a full A4 sheet (Evri, InPost and Relay)
are cropped to the label itself, so you don&rsquo;t waste a page per
label.

## QR or PDF (Depop)

Depop offers some carriers as a QR code as well as a PDF. For those
orders, the card shows a **PDF / QR** toggle. Pick whichever your
local drop-off point accepts: some scan a code rather than taking a
printed label.

When your selection contains more than one of these orders, a
second **PDF / QR** toggle appears in the toolbar and sets all of
them at once, so you don&rsquo;t have to flip each card. It shows
QR only when every one of them is already set to QR. Otherwise it
shows PDF, and one click on either side sets the whole selection.

## Ordering a Vinted label

For Vinted orders that still need a label, the card shows a **PDF**
button, a **QR** button, or both, depending on what the carrier
offers. Click one and SaleLinx orders that label type from Vinted.
A **Label ordered** notice confirms it.

Vinted doesn&rsquo;t always update the order straight away, so the
card can stay on **Needs label**, and it can&rsquo;t be selected for
printing until Vinted moves the order on. Click the refresh button
later, or get the label from the order on Vinted. If you press
**PDF** or **QR** again on an order that already has a label,
SaleLinx warns **Label already ordered** and only orders another if
you click **Order again**. Do that only if you meant to change the
label type.

If Vinted doesn&rsquo;t have your address, you&rsquo;ll see **Add
your address on Vinted** instead: Vinted needs your address and
phone number before it can make a label. Add them in your Vinted
settings and try again.

## Managing Vinted orders

### Extend the posting deadline

**Extend** appears on Vinted orders once a label has been ordered.
It opens **Extend posting deadline**, which shows your
current deadline and the extensions Vinted offers, each with its new
date. Pick one, edit the **Reason for the buyer** if you like, and
click **Extend**. Some orders can&rsquo;t be extended, and the window
says so.

The reason starts as your **Default extension reason**, which you
set in the feedback settings (see Configuring the message below).
Out of the box it reads: &ldquo;Hi! Sorry for the delay, I need a bit more time
to post this. Thanks for your patience!&rdquo;

### Cancel an order

Vinted orders that haven&rsquo;t shipped have a **Cancel** button.
It opens **Cancel order**, where you choose one of Vinted&rsquo;s
cancellation reasons. Some reasons let you add a note for the
buyer. Click **Cancel order** to confirm, or **Keep order** to back
out. The buyer is notified and refunded. Cancelling isn&rsquo;t
available for Depop orders.

### Message the buyer

Most cards have a **Message** button that opens the buyer&rsquo;s chat in
the [Messages](/docs/buyers/messages) tab. Vinted orders open the
exact conversation. Depop orders are matched by the item and the
buyer. If the chat can&rsquo;t be found, SaleLinx says **No chat
found** and suggests a longer date range in Messages.

### Select in My Listings

**Select in My Listings** switches to the My Listings tab with the
items from your selected orders already selected, so you can relist
them in a couple of clicks. It works with orders from either list.

## Emailing labels

Sometimes you want the labels on a different device (your phone for
printing at the post office, or a partner&rsquo;s computer). Select
your orders and click **Email Labels**.

The **Email shipping labels** window asks where to send them. Type
any address in **Send to**: yours, a print shop&rsquo;s or a
helper&rsquo;s. Tick **Remember this address** to pre-fill it next
time. Click **Send Email** and SaleLinx emails the merged PDF as an
attachment. The subject and message are fixed.

There is a daily limit on label emails per account. If you reach it,
the window says so and you can send again the next day (UTC).

If the email service isn&rsquo;t available, SaleLinx downloads the
PDF instead and opens your mail client with a pre-written email, so
you can attach the file yourself. If sending fails for another
reason, the window shows **Send failed** with the reason, and you
can try again.

Email Labels is a Business feature. On Pro, the button shows a lock
and clicking it opens the upgrade prompt.

## Vinted feedback

The **Completed orders** list holds completed Vinted sales that you
can still leave feedback on. Each card has a **Leave feedback**
button. You can post feedback one order at a time or in bulk, with
an optional follow-up chat message.

The dropdown in the section header sets how far back to look, from
**Last 7 days** to **Last 90 days** (7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days). It
starts at 30 days and remembers your choice.

### Which orders appear

A sold Vinted order shows up only when **all** of these are true:

- Vinted marks the order as completed (the buyer has received the
  item).
- You haven&rsquo;t already left feedback on it.
- Vinted still offers feedback on it (you&rsquo;re inside the
  feedback window).

Orders still in transit or cancelled don&rsquo;t appear, so you
never review an order the buyer hasn&rsquo;t received. If the list
is empty, it says why, for example that no completed orders in the
chosen period need feedback.

### Configuring the message

Click **Feedback** in the section header to open the settings:

- **Rating** - 1 to 5 stars. Defaults to 5.
- **Feedback message** - the public review text. Defaults to
  &ldquo;Thank you for your purchase!&rdquo;.
- **Also send a follow-up message in chat** - when ticked, SaleLinx
  also sends the buyer a private chat message after the feedback
  lands. Off by default.
- The follow-up message itself, in the box under the toggle.
- **Default extension reason** - the text that pre-fills the reason
  when you extend a posting deadline.

In both messages, `@username` is replaced with the buyer&rsquo;s
Vinted username. Write `\@` for a literal `@`.

Click **Save** to keep your changes. **Reset** fills the form with
the defaults, which also need a **Save**. Settings are stored in
your browser and apply to every order. There&rsquo;s no per-order
override.

### Running it

For a single order, click **Leave feedback** on its card. The card
disappears once the feedback is posted.

For several:

1. Tick the orders you want, or use **Select all** in the Completed
   orders toolbar.
2. Click **Leave feedback on selected**.
3. SaleLinx posts them one after another with its usual pacing, so
   a bulk run doesn&rsquo;t look automated to Vinted.

The selected cards leave the list as soon as you start the run. If
the run stops partway, the next refresh of the tab brings back any
orders that didn&rsquo;t get feedback.

A failed follow-up message doesn&rsquo;t undo the feedback: the
order still counts as done. If Vinted starts refusing or
rate-limiting requests, the run stops. Check that you&rsquo;re still
signed in to Vinted and try again later.
