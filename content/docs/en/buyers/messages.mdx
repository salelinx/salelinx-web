# Messages

The **Messages** tab puts your Depop, Vinted and eBay chats in one
inbox, so you can read and reply without opening each
marketplace&rsquo;s inbox. The list of conversations sits on the left
and the conversation you&rsquo;re reading on the right. Messages covers
Depop, Vinted and eBay.

Each row shows the item&rsquo;s photo with the marketplace logo on it,
the other person&rsquo;s avatar and username, how long ago the last
message was sent, a preview of that message, and the item the
conversation is about. Conversations with unread messages carry an
unread badge, and a **Needs reply** tag marks the ones where the other
person spoke last and is waiting on you.

## What the inbox shows

The inbox lists every conversation with a message inside the time
window, read or unread, whether or not you&rsquo;ve already replied.
Conversations waiting on you come first, under **Waiting on you**, then
the rest under **Everything else**, each with the newest at the top.
The first conversation waiting on you opens by itself.

- **Open**, **Offers** and **Sold.** These tabs above the list split
  your conversations. **Open** holds your everyday chats and is where
  the tab starts. **Offers** holds conversations that are only an
  offer, with no words from the other person. **Sold** holds
  conversations about a listing of yours that has sold.
- **Marketplace filters.** **All**, then a button for each marketplace
  you sell on. Click one to see only that marketplace, or several to
  see them together. **All** shows every marketplace again. The row
  only appears when you sell on two or more.
- **Time window.** The buttons in the toolbar set how far back to
  look: **1d**, **3d**, **5d**, **7d**, **14d** or **30d**. It starts
  at **7d**.
- **Vinted.** SaleLinx reads the 20 most recent Vinted conversations
  in the window one at a time, because reading lots at once can get
  you temporarily blocked, so Vinted takes longer to fill in than
  Depop. Older Vinted conversations in the window are still listed,
  and load when you open them.
- **Depop** shows up to the 72 most recent conversations in the
  window.
- **eBay** shows its conversations with the messages already loaded,
  and needs no open eBay tab.

The line above the list says how many buyers are waiting on you, for
example **3 buyers waiting on you**, and the number beside it is how
many conversations are listed. Neither counts offer or sold
conversations, and neither is an unread count.

From version 1.1.12, conversations where you are the buyer also
appear, with a **Buying** chip, and the username on the card is the
seller&rsquo;s. Earlier versions show only chats about your own
listings.

The **&times;** on a row hides it until the next refresh.

## Opening a thread

Click a row to open the conversation on the right. Vinted threads are
usually already loaded, so they open straight away. Depop threads load
when you open them, so the first open takes a moment.

Photos the other person sends show in the thread. An offer shows as
**Offer** with its price, followed by **Accepted** or **Declined** once
it&rsquo;s answered. On Vinted, the menu on one of your own messages has
**Delete**, which removes it from the chat.

## Replying

The reply box sits under the open conversation. Type your message, then
press Enter or click the send button. Shift+Enter adds a new line.

On Vinted and eBay, the attach button adds photos to a reply, up to 5
in a message (JPEG, PNG or GIF). On eBay a photo needs a message with
it. Depop replies are text only. Replies to eBay can be up to 2000
characters.

Replies jump ahead of any background work SaleLinx is doing on that
marketplace. If a reply can&rsquo;t be sent, the conversation says so,
for example **Vinted didn&rsquo;t take your reply. Check you&rsquo;re
signed in there, then try again.**, and your text stays in the box so
you can try again.

**Open in Depop** (or **Open in Vinted**, **Open in eBay**) at the top
of the conversation opens the chat on the marketplace for anything the
panel doesn&rsquo;t do, such as blocking or reporting.

## Refreshing

The tab loads when you open it. There&rsquo;s no automatic refresh
on a timer. Coming back to the tab within two minutes (five for eBay)
reuses the last results instead of asking the marketplaces again.

To fetch fresh results, click the refresh button in the toolbar or
change the time window. The list stays on screen while it reloads,
and each marketplace updates as soon as it answers.

## When the list is empty

- **No new messages** means nothing falls inside the time window. Try
  a longer window. If your filters hide everything, the list says
  **Nothing matches these filters.** and **Show all** clears them.
- If SaleLinx can&rsquo;t reach a marketplace, it says so instead of
  showing an empty list, for example when no tab for that marketplace
  is open or you&rsquo;re signed out. Fix that, then click refresh. For
  eBay, it says **Reconnect eBay to see your eBay messages here.** with
  a **Reconnect** button, because only reconnecting eBay can fix it.
- **Depop account not linked. Its items aren&rsquo;t shown here.** (or
  the same for Vinted) with a **Link account** button means you
  haven&rsquo;t linked that marketplace yet.
