# Messages

The **Messages** tab puts your Depop and Vinted chats side by side
in two columns, so you can read and reply without opening each
marketplace&rsquo;s inbox. The column for the site you&rsquo;re on
sits on the left. Messages covers Depop and Vinted; eBay support is
coming.

Each card shows the other person&rsquo;s avatar and username, how
long ago the last message was sent, a preview of that message, and
the item the conversation is about. Conversations with unread
messages carry an unread badge.

## What the columns show

Both columns list every conversation with a message inside the
time window, read or unread, whether or not you&rsquo;ve already
replied. The newest conversation sits at the top of each column.

- **Time window.** The dropdown in the toolbar sets how far back to
  look: 1, 3, 5, 7, 14 or 30 days. It starts at 3 days.
- **Vinted** shows up to the 20 most recent conversations in the
  window. SaleLinx loads each Vinted conversation one at a time,
  because loading lots at once can get you temporarily blocked, so
  this column takes longer to fill than the Depop one.
  Conversations with no written messages (only an offer or an order
  update, for example) are left out.
- **Depop** shows up to the 72 most recent conversations in the
  window.

The number in the toolbar is how many conversations are listed
across both columns, and each column header shows its own count in
brackets. It is not an unread count.

From version 1.1.12, conversations where you are the buyer also
appear, with a **Buying** chip, and the username on the card is the
seller&rsquo;s. Earlier versions show only chats about your own
listings.

The **&times;** on a card hides it until the next refresh.

## Opening a thread

Click a card to open the thread inside it. Only one thread is open
at a time: opening another closes the first. Vinted threads are
already loaded, so they open straight away. Depop threads load when
you open them, so the first open takes a moment.

## Replying

The reply box sits under the open thread. Type your message, then
press Enter or click the send button. The box is a single line, so
you can&rsquo;t add line breaks.

Replies jump ahead of any background work SaleLinx is doing on that
marketplace. If a reply can&rsquo;t be sent, you&rsquo;ll see
**Failed to send message.** and your text stays in the box so you
can try again.

The button next to send opens the chat on the marketplace (on Depop,
your inbox) for anything the panel doesn&rsquo;t do, such as sending
photos, blocking or reporting.

## Refreshing

The tab loads when you open it. There&rsquo;s no automatic refresh
on a timer. Coming back to the tab within two minutes reuses the
last results instead of asking the marketplaces again.

To fetch fresh results, click the refresh button in the toolbar or
change the time window. Both columns clear to loading placeholders,
and each one fills in as soon as its marketplace answers.

## When a column is empty

- **No new messages** means nothing in that column falls inside the
  time window. Try a longer window.
- If a column can&rsquo;t reach its marketplace, it says so instead
  of showing an empty list, for example when no tab for that
  marketplace is open or you&rsquo;re signed out. Fix that, then
  click refresh.
- A blurred column with a **Link account** button means you
  haven&rsquo;t linked that marketplace yet. The button takes you to
  Settings.
