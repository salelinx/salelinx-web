# Auto-Accept Offers

Auto-Accept Offers checks your pending Depop and Vinted offers on a
schedule and accepts any that meet a threshold you set. It removes the
manual step for offers you would always say yes to, while still
letting you handle bigger or more interesting offers by hand.

It doesn&rsquo;t cover eBay. There, business sellers set an auto-accept
price on each listing instead, in its editor in
[My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings).

## Where it lives

It is the **Auto-Accept Offers** block at the top of the **Offers**
tab, above the list of pending offers.

It is not the **Auto-Offers** tab. That tab sends offers *to* people
who liked your Vinted items, and is covered in
[Send Offers](/docs/automate/send-offers).

## How it decides

An offer is accepted only when it clears both checks:

1. The offer is at least your chosen percentage of the listed price.
2. The offer is at or above your price floor (if you set one).

If either fails, the offer is left alone for you to handle manually.
Both checks are applied to each offer on its own, so a shop with
mixed prices behaves correctly.

## Setting it up

1. On the **Auto-Accept Offers** row, click **Settings** to open the
   rule. You can set it up before switching anything on.
2. Next to **Accept if offer &ge;**, enter a percentage of the asking
   price. For example, 90 means &ldquo;accept any offer at 90% of
   asking or higher&rdquo;. You can choose from 50 to 100.
3. Next to **And &ge;**, enter an optional price floor. For example,
   £5 means &ldquo;ignore anything below £5, whatever the
   percentage&rdquo;. Leave it at 0 for no floor.
4. **Platforms** - tick Depop, Vinted, or both. The same rule applies
   to each one you tick.
5. **Check every** - how often to check: 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or
   1 hour (default 15 minutes).
6. Flip the switch next to **Settings** to turn auto-accept on.

Turning it on runs a check straight away, so you don&rsquo;t have to
wait a full interval to see it work. If neither platform is ticked
when you switch it on, SaleLinx ticks Depop for you. Changing the rule
while it is on takes effect from the next check.

A line under the title sums up the current rule, for example
&ldquo;Accept offers &ge; 90% of asking on Depop &amp; Vinted, every
15m.&rdquo;

Steps 2 to 5 can be filled in at once from a preset (**Default** is
90%, no floor, both platforms, every 15 minutes; **Careful** is 95%,
every 30 minutes), and you can save your own. The on/off switch is
never part of a preset. See [Offers](/docs/buyers/offers) for how
presets work and sync.

## Keep the marketplace open

Checks run in the background while Chrome is open, even when the
panel is closed. SaleLinx reads and accepts offers through your
signed-in marketplace tabs, so keep a Depop tab and a Vinted tab open
for the platforms you ticked. If a marketplace can&rsquo;t be reached,
that check finds nothing there and tries again at the next interval;
the other marketplace is still checked.

On Vinted, each check looks at conversations from the last 7 days.

## Status and recent accepts

Once auto-accept is on, the block shows:

- **Last run** - when the last check finished.
- **Next run** - a countdown to the next check.
- **Recently auto-accepted** - the five most recent offers SaleLinx
  accepted for you, with the marketplace, item, price and when, so you
  can spot-check its decisions.

## Trade-offs

Auto-accept will sometimes accept offers you would have countered.
Set the threshold conservatively; 90% is a sensible default. If you
set it to 70%, you&rsquo;ll auto-accept lowballs you could have
nudged up by a few pounds.

The price floor is the safer lever for mixed-price shops. A 90%
threshold on a £5 item still accepts £4.50; a £5
floor stops that.

## What it doesn&rsquo;t do

- **Doesn&rsquo;t counter** - it only accepts. Counter-offering needs
  your judgement on the right step-down amount.
- **Doesn&rsquo;t decline** - offers below the threshold stay in the
  pending list for you. Marketplaces expire unanswered offers on their
  own schedule.
- **Doesn&rsquo;t touch offers you have already answered** - it only
  looks at pending offers.

## Plan and access

Auto-Accept Offers is included on **Pro and Business**. On Starter and
the free trial the block is locked, while the rest of the Offers tab
(accept, decline and counter on each card) works as normal. If your
plan stops including it, or you sign out, auto-accept switches itself
off at its next check.
