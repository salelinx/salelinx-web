# Linking

The Linking tab pairs listings for the same item across Depop, Vinted
and eBay so the rest of SaleLinx knows they&rsquo;re the same physical
item. Once listings are linked:

- When one sells, SaleLinx takes the others down (see
  &ldquo;Taking the others down when one sells&rdquo; below).
- The [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) keeps the stock of Depop and
  Vinted listings in step.
- [My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) shows the pair as one row
  with both marketplace logos, and the **Linked** filter finds them.
- The Restocker tab&rsquo;s **Linked** / **Unlinked** filter
  recognises them as a pair.

Crosslisting doesn&rsquo;t link the original and the new copy for
you. After a [Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist), pair the two
listings here (or from My Listings). The same goes for items you
listed on more than one marketplace yourself, and for pairs whose link was
removed.

## What you need

- **Both listings saved to the cloud.** The link is stored on the
  cloud copies, so a listing that isn&rsquo;t saved can&rsquo;t be
  linked. The tab&rsquo;s **Save to cloud** button helps with this
  (see below), and [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync) explains
  the rest.
- **A tab open for Depop and Vinted.** Each column fills in from a
  fresh read of that marketplace. If one isn&rsquo;t open, or its
  session has expired, the column shows a short note with a link to
  open it instead of the listings. eBay needs no open tab, but if its
  connection has lapsed the column asks you to reconnect it in Settings.
- Active, sold and draft listings can all be linked here. A sold
  item stays linkable because the Restocker still needs the pair.

## Reading the tab

Along the top:

- **Include sold** - on by default. Turn it off to show only active
  listings (it hides drafts too).
- **Save to cloud** - appears with a count when listings on screen
  aren&rsquo;t saved to the cloud yet.
- **Recommended links** - suggests pairs to link in one go.
- **Refresh** - re-reads your marketplaces.

Below that is one column for each marketplace you have linked. Each lists the
cloud-saved listings that aren&rsquo;t linked yet, with a search box
and a count. A card shows the photo, title and price, plus a **Sold**
or **Draft** badge where that applies.

At the bottom, **Already linked** is a collapsed section listing your
existing pairs, each with an **Unlink** button. Click the arrow to
open it.

A dismissible **What is linking?** card sits at the top on your first
visit.

## Saving listings to the cloud from here

Click **Save to cloud** to open **Save listings to cloud**. It lists
the listings on screen that still need a cloud save, in two groups:

- **Active** - ticked by default.
- **Sold** - unticked by default, because saving them uses cloud
  storage. Drafts appear in this group too.

Tick what you want and press the save button, which shows how many
listings it will save. Each card shows **Saving&hellip;** until its
save finishes, then it can be linked.

## Pairing manually

1. **Click a card in either column.** The other column re-sorts by
   match score and puts a percentage badge on each card: green for
   likely matches, amber for maybes, grey for weak ones.
2. **Click the matching listing in the other column.** The
   **Link these listings?** dialog shows both photos, titles and
   prices with the match score between them.
3. **Click Link.** The pair leaves the columns and moves to
   **Already linked**.

Click a selected card again to deselect it.

## The match score

The match score runs from 0 to 100 and is based on how similar the
two titles are, with a small boost when the prices are close.
SaleLinx lowercases both titles, strips punctuation and accents, and
ignores common filler words such as &ldquo;the&rdquo;,
&ldquo;size&rdquo;, &ldquo;vintage&rdquo;, &ldquo;new&rdquo; and
&ldquo;y2k&rdquo; before comparing, so cosmetic differences
don&rsquo;t throw it off.

- **80 to 100**: green badge, a confident match.
- **50 to 79**: amber badge, worth a look.
- **Below 50**: grey badge, probably unrelated.

The match isn&rsquo;t perfect. Treat a green badge as a strong
suggestion you should still check, not as proof.

## Recommended links

Click **Recommended links** to open **Review matches to link**. It
lists every pair scoring 80 or more, best first, with each listing
used only once. Each row shows both photos, titles, prices and
marketplaces.

Pairs you can link are ticked. A pair where one listing isn&rsquo;t
saved to the cloud is greyed out with a **Needs cloud save** tag and
can&rsquo;t be ticked. Untick any pair you don&rsquo;t want (or use
**Select all**), then press the link button, which shows how many
pairs it will link. If nothing scores high enough you&rsquo;ll see
**No recommended matches found.**

Recommendations only consider listings the columns are showing, so
with **Include sold** off, sold listings and drafts are left out.

## Refresh

**Refresh** re-reads your listings on your marketplaces at the same
time, then reloads the columns. Use it after you&rsquo;ve added new
listings on either side. The button shows **Refreshing&hellip;** while
it runs.

## Linking from My Listings

You can also link without opening this tab. In **My Listings**, select
two listings from different marketplaces and click **Link** in the
toolbar. With a linked row selected, the same button reads **Unlink**.
See [Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor) for the rest of the
toolbar.

From My Listings, both listings must be saved to the cloud (and
finished saving), not already linked, and active or sold. Drafts
can&rsquo;t be linked from there. If both are tracked by the
Restocker with different restock counts, SaleLinx asks which count to
use for the pair.

## Unlinking

In **Already linked**, click **Unlink** on the pair. The link is
removed on both sides without touching the listings themselves, and
the two listings go back into the columns. If either one was tracked
by the Restocker, both stay tracked, each with the higher of the two
restock counts.

Removing either listing from the cloud also removes its link.

## Taking the others down when one sells

Added in version 1.2.2. When a linked listing sells on Depop, Vinted or
eBay, SaleLinx takes the other listings in the group down so the item
doesn&rsquo;t sell twice:

- **Depop**: the quantity is set to 0, so it shows as sold out.
- **Vinted**: the listing is hidden by default, or deleted if you chose
  that in the Restocker&rsquo;s **When a linked item sells out** setting.
- **eBay**: the listing is ended. It stays in My Listings, so you can
  relist it.

It is on once you have linked listings. The **Take linked listings down
when one sells** switch in **Linking settings** turns it off. If you
turn it back on, it does not act on sales from while it was off.

It has limits:

- **It is not instant.** SaleLinx notices a sale on its next check, about
  every 5 minutes for Depop and Vinted and about every 15 for eBay.
  Another buyer could still buy the item in between.
- **It only runs while Chrome is open with SaleLinx running.** Checking
  Depop and Vinted needs a Depop or Vinted tab open. eBay does not.
- **It needs Pro or Business.** On other plans, nothing is taken down.
- **It leaves a listing up when it shows more than one in stock.**
- **It gives up after repeated failures.** SaleLinx retries a failed
  take-down up to 12 times, then tells you to end the listing
  yourself. A cancelled sale does not put the other listings back up.
- **It ignores sales from before it was switched on.**

If the [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) is running for an item,
the Restocker handles the Depop and Vinted copies (it counts stock down
and reposts), and SaleLinx still takes down the eBay copy.

When it acts, a card tells you what was taken down, what was left up and
what failed.

## Plan and access

Linking is included on **Pro and Business**. The free trial and
Starter see the tab locked, with a prompt to upgrade.

Cloud sync, which linking needs, is also Pro and Business, so the
gates line up. The [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker), the main
user of linked pairs, is Business only.
