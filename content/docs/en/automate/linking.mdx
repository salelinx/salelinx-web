# Linking

The Linking tab pairs an individual Depop listing with its Vinted
counterpart so the rest of SaleLinx knows they&rsquo;re the same
item. Once two listings are linked:

- [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) can auto-repost the partner
  when one side sells.
- The unified [Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) view collapses the
  pair into one row with stacked platform badges.
- The Crosslist tab&rsquo;s Linked / Unlinked filter recognises them
  as a pair.

A successful [Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist) sets the link
automatically. The Linking tab is for the case where you crosslisted
manually outside SaleLinx, lost a link due to an account reset, or
need to pair pre-existing inventory after migrating in.

## When to use it

- You have inventory that exists on both Depop and Vinted but
  wasn&rsquo;t crosslisted via SaleLinx.
- You want to turn on [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) and need
  the pairs set up first.
- A previous link broke (a listing was deleted and recreated, or an
  edge case desynced the pair).

## What you need

- **Cloud sync on for both items.** Linking only works on
  cloud-synced listings, because the link state lives on your cloud
  record. See [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync).
- Both items active on their marketplace.
- Both Depop and Vinted tabs open in the browser.

## Reading the tab

The tab is a two-column layout:

- **Left column** - unlinked items on whichever platform you&rsquo;re
  currently browsing.
- **Right column** - unlinked items on the other platform.
- **Already linked** - a collapsed section at the bottom shows
  existing pairs with per-pair Unlink buttons.

Each card has: a search box per column, the thumbnail, title,
brand, price and platform badge.

A dismissible &ldquo;What is linking?&rdquo; card sits at the top to
remind you what the tab does on first visit.

## Pairing manually

1. **Click a card in the left column.** The right column re-sorts by
   match probability and adds a colour-banded percentage badge to
   each card (green for likely matches, amber for maybes, grey for
   weak).
2. **Click the suggested partner on the right.** A confirmation
   modal shows both thumbnails, titles, and the match score side by
   side.
3. **Click Link.** Both records get a cross-reference (link_id on
   each side). The cards disappear from the unlinked columns and
   move to the Already linked section.

The right-column reshuffle uses a quick shake animation so you can
see which items moved.

## The fuzzy match

The match score is a 0-100 integer based on how similar the two
titles are, with a small nudge if the prices are close to each other.
SaleLinx normalises both titles (lowercase, strip punctuation, drop
common stopwords like &ldquo;the&rdquo;, &ldquo;vintage&rdquo;,
&ldquo;new&rdquo;) before comparing, so cosmetic differences
don&rsquo;t throw it off.

The score bands map to badges:

| Band  | Visual                  |
| ----- | ----------------------- |
| Good  | Green, confident match  |
| Maybe | Amber, worth a look     |
| Weak  | Grey, probably unrelated |

The match isn&rsquo;t perfect. Treat &ldquo;Good&rdquo; as a strong
suggestion you should still confirm, not as truth.

## Link 100%

Above the columns is a **Link 100%** button. Click it to open a
review modal listing every unlinked pair with a perfect-confidence
match. Each row is pre-checked; uncheck any you don&rsquo;t want,
then **Accept** to link the remaining pairs in one batch. Faster
than clicking through one at a time when your titles are clean
matches.

## Refresh

The **Refresh** button at the top of the tab re-fetches your
listings on both Depop and Vinted in parallel, then reloads the
columns. Use it after you&rsquo;ve added new listings on either
side and want them to show up in the unlinked columns.

The button disables and shows &ldquo;Refreshing&hellip;&rdquo; while
running.

## Unlinking

In the **Already linked** section, each pair has an Unlink button.
Clicking it removes the cross-reference on both sides without
touching the listings themselves. The pair reappears in the
unlinked columns.

## Plan and access

Linking is included on **Pro and Business**. The free trial and
Starter see the tab locked with an Upgrade card.

Cloud sync (a prerequisite) is also Pro and Business, so the gates
align.
