# Linking

The Linking tab links listings for the same item across Depop, Vinted
and eBay so the rest of SaleLinx knows they&rsquo;re the same physical
item. One item can be linked on two of them or on all three. Once
listings are linked:

- When one sells, SaleLinx takes the others down (see
  &ldquo;Taking the others down when one sells&rdquo; below).
- The [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) keeps the stock of Depop and
  Vinted listings in step.
- [My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) shows them as one row with a
  line for each marketplace, and the **Linked** filter finds them.
- The Restocker tab&rsquo;s **Linked** / **Unlinked** filter
  recognises them as linked.

A [Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist) links the original and the
new copy for you while **Link to the original** is on, which it is to
start with, and [Create listing](/docs/inventory/listings) can link the
listings it makes. Use this tab (or My Listings) for items you listed on
more than one marketplace yourself, copies made with linking off, and
listings whose link was removed.

## What you need

- **Cloud sync on your plan.** The link is stored on the cloud copies.
  Linking here saves the listings to the cloud for you, and a card that
  isn&rsquo;t saved yet carries a **Saves to cloud** tag.
  [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync) explains the rest.
- **A tab open for Depop and Vinted.** Each column fills in from a
  fresh read of that marketplace. If one isn&rsquo;t open, or its
  session has expired, the column shows a short note with a link to
  open it instead of the listings. eBay needs no open tab, but if its
  connection has lapsed the column asks you to reconnect it in Settings.
- Active listings and drafts can be linked here. Sold listings
  don&rsquo;t appear in the columns, but a link stays in place after a
  sale, because the Restocker still needs it.

## Reading the tab

Along the top:

- **All**, **Active** and **Drafts** - which listings the columns show.
  It starts on **All**.
- **Recommended links** - suggests matches to link in one go.
- **Refresh** - re-reads your marketplaces.

Below that is one column for each marketplace you have linked. Each lists
the listings that aren&rsquo;t linked yet, cloud-saved ones first, with
a search box and a count. A card shows the photo, title and price, plus
a **Draft** badge where that applies. A listing linked on one other
marketplace stays in its column while a third is missing, marked with
its partners&rsquo; logos (&ldquo;Linked on&rdquo; when you hover).

At the bottom, **Already linked** is a collapsed section listing your
existing links, each with an **Unlink** button, and buttons to sort
them by title, A to Z or Z to A. Click the arrow to open it.

A dismissible **What is linking?** card sits at the top on your first
visit.

## Linking manually

1. **Click a card in any column.** The other columns re-sort by match
   score and put a percentage badge on each card: green for likely
   matches, amber for maybes, grey for weak ones. If nothing on a
   marketplace looks like it, that column says so and offers a
   **Crosslist to** button for that marketplace.
2. **Click the matching listing in another column.** The
   **Link these listings?** dialog shows the photos, titles and
   prices with the match score between them.
3. **Click Link.** The listings are saved to the cloud if they
   aren&rsquo;t already, and linked.

Click a selected card again to deselect it.

## The match score

The match score runs from 0 to 100 and is based on how similar the
two titles are, with a small boost when the prices are close.
SaleLinx lowercases both titles, strips punctuation and accents, and
ignores common filler words such as &ldquo;the&rdquo;,
&ldquo;size&rdquo;, &ldquo;vintage&rdquo;, &ldquo;new&rdquo; and
&ldquo;y2k&rdquo; before comparing, so cosmetic differences
don&rsquo;t throw it off.

- **80 to 100**: green badge, a confident match.
- **50 to 79**: amber badge, worth a look.
- **Below 50**: grey badge, probably unrelated.

The match isn&rsquo;t perfect. Treat a green badge as a strong
suggestion you should still check, not as proof.

## Recommended links

Click **Recommended links** to see very close matches side by side,
scoring 90 or more, with each listing used only once. Each row shows
the photos, titles, prices and marketplaces, and has its own **Link**
button.

**Link all**, with the number of matches, links every row at once. It
asks first (**Link all these matches?**): each one is saved to the
cloud first, then linked, so check the matches look right. Click
**Done** to go back to the columns. If nothing scores high enough
you&rsquo;ll see **No recommended matches found.**

Recommendations only consider listings the columns are showing, so
with **Active** picked, drafts are left out.

## Refresh

**Refresh** re-reads your listings on your marketplaces at the same
time, then reloads the columns. Use it after you&rsquo;ve added new
listings on either side. The button shows **Refreshing&hellip;** while
it runs.

## Linking from My Listings

You can also link without opening this tab. In **My Listings**, select
two or three listings, one on each marketplace, and click **Link** in
the toolbar. With a linked row selected, the same button reads
**Unlink**. See [Bulk editor](/docs/inventory/bulk-editor) for the rest
of the toolbar.

From My Listings, each listing must be saved to the cloud (and
finished saving), and active, sold or a draft. If they are tracked by
the Restocker with different restock counts, SaleLinx asks which count
to use.

## Unlinking

In **Already linked**, click **Unlink** on the link. The link is
removed on every side without touching the listings themselves, and
the listings go back into the columns. For an item linked on all three
marketplaces, the &times; beside one listing (&ldquo;Unlink this listing
only&rdquo;) takes just that one out. If any of them was tracked by the
Restocker, they stay tracked, each with the higher restock count.

Removing either listing from the cloud also removes its link.

## Taking the others down when one sells

Added in version 1.2.2. When a linked listing sells on Depop, Vinted or
eBay, SaleLinx takes the other listings in the group down so the item
doesn&rsquo;t sell twice:

- **Depop**: the quantity is set to 0, so it shows as sold out.
- **Vinted**: the listing is hidden by default, or deleted if you chose
  that in the Restocker&rsquo;s **When a linked item sells out** setting.
- **eBay**: the listing is ended. It stays in My Listings, so you can
  relist it.

It is on once you have linked listings. The **Take linked listings down
when one sells** switch in **Linking settings** (in **Settings**, under
**Advanced settings**) turns it off. If you turn it back on, it does not
act on sales from while it was off.

It has limits:

- **It is not instant.** SaleLinx notices a sale on its next check, about
  every 5 minutes for Depop and Vinted and about every 15 for eBay.
  Another buyer could still buy the item in between.
- **It only runs while Chrome is open with SaleLinx running.** Checking
  Depop and Vinted needs a Depop or Vinted tab open. eBay does not.
- **It needs Pro or Business.** On other plans, nothing is taken down.
- **It leaves a listing up when it shows more than one in stock.**
- **It gives up after repeated failures.** SaleLinx retries a failed
  take-down up to 12 times, then tells you to end the listing
  yourself. A cancelled sale does not put the other listings back up.
- **It ignores sales from before it was switched on.**

If the [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker) is running for an item,
the Restocker handles the Depop and Vinted copies (it counts stock down
and reposts), and SaleLinx still takes down the eBay copy.

When it acts, a card tells you what was taken down, what was left up and
what failed.

## Plan and access

Linking is included on **Pro and Business**. The free trial and
Starter see the tab locked, with a prompt to upgrade.

Cloud sync, which linking needs, is also Pro and Business, so the
gates line up. The [Restocker](/docs/automate/restocker), the main
user of linked listings, is Business only.
