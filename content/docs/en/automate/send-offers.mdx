# Send Offers

Someone who liked an item is the warmest buyer you have: they have
seen it, they want it, and something is holding them back. Usually
it is the price. The **Auto-Offers** tab in the panel finds the people
who liked your Vinted items and sends each of them an offer, so you
turn likes into sales without messaging anyone by hand.

This is a different feature from
[Auto-accept](/docs/automate/auto-offers), which accepts offers
buyers send *you*. Send Offers is outgoing: you make the offer.

## When to use it

- An item has plenty of likes but no sales.
- You want to clear stock before a restock or a season change.
- You would rather offer a small discount to interested buyers than
  drop the price for everyone with a
  [Price Drop](/docs/automate/price-drops).

## How it works

1. **Scan Likers.** SaleLinx reads your recent Vinted notifications
   and groups the likes by item, so you see each item with the people
   who liked it.
2. **Pick items.** Tick the items you want to send offers on, or use
   Select all.
3. **Set the price.** Choose a **Discount %** and a **Min price
   floor**. You can also type a custom offer price for any item, which
   overrides the discount for that item.
4. **Send Offers.** SaleLinx sends one offer to each liker of each
   selected item, in the background.

The offer is the listed price minus your discount, but never below
your floor. If the discount would take an item under the floor, the
offer goes out at the floor price instead. An item whose discounted
price would not be lower than its listed price is skipped.

## Built-in safeguards

- **One offer per liker per item.** Someone who already received an
  offer on an item is not sent another for 30 days, however many
  times you scan.
- **Human pacing.** Offers are not fired the instant a like arrives.
  SaleLinx waits a natural-looking interval and paces sends through
  the same queue as every other job, with breaks and rate-limit
  handling.
- **Runs in the background.** The job keeps going if you close the
  panel, and you can pause or cancel it from the queue.

## Stats

The tab tracks how the offers are doing:

- **Offers sent** in the last 30 days.
- **Items sold** among the items you sent offers on.
- **Sell rate**, items sold as a share of offers sent.
- **Recently sent**, a list of the latest offers.

## Plan and access

Send Offers is Vinted only, and needs a linked Vinted account. It is
included on **Pro and Business**; on Starter and the free trial the
tab is locked.
