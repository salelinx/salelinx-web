# Send Offers

Someone who liked an item is the warmest buyer you have: they have
seen it, they want it, and something is holding them back. Usually
it is the price. The **Auto-Offers** tab in the panel finds the people
who liked your Vinted items and sends each of them an offer, so you
turn likes into sales without messaging anyone by hand.

This is a different feature from
[Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers), which accepts
offers buyers send *you*. Auto-Offers is outgoing: you make the offer.

## When to use it

- An item has plenty of likes but no sales.
- You want to clear stock before a restock or a season change.
- You would rather offer a small discount to interested buyers than
  drop the price for everyone with a
  [Price Drop](/docs/automate/price-drops).

## How it works

1. **Scan Likers.** SaleLinx reads your recent Vinted notifications
   and groups the likes by item, so you see each item with the number
   of new likers. Items whose likers have all had an offer already are
   left out, and the items with the most new likers come first.
2. **Pick items.** Every item starts ticked. Untick the ones you want
   to leave out, or use **Select all** to tick or untick them all.
3. **Set the price.** Choose a **Discount %** (5 to 50%, default 10%)
   and a **Min price floor** (default 1). When SaleLinx knows an
   item&rsquo;s price, its **Offer** box is filled in from those two
   settings and updates as you change them. Type your own price into
   any item&rsquo;s **Offer** box to override it for that item.
4. **Send Offers.** SaleLinx sends one offer to each new liker of each
   ticked item, in the background.

The offer is the item&rsquo;s price minus your discount, but never
below your floor. If the discount would take an item under the floor,
the offer goes out at the floor price instead, and the same goes for a
price you typed yourself. If the offer would not
be lower than the item&rsquo;s current price on Vinted (for example
because the floor is at or above it), that liker is skipped.

**Rescan** reads your notifications again and refreshes the list.
While offers are going out, a progress bar shows how many have been
sent and how many failed, and **Stop** ends the run.

## Built-in safeguards

- **One offer per liker per item.** Someone who already received an
  offer on an item is not sent another for 30 days, however many
  times you scan. A liker who was skipped because the offer would not
  have been a discount is also left alone for 30 days. SaleLinx keeps
  this record in your browser.
- **Human pacing.** Offers go out one at a time with natural gaps,
  through the same queue as every other SaleLinx job, with regular
  breaks. If Vinted asks SaleLinx to slow down, the job pauses and
  picks up again once the wait is over.
- **Runs in the background.** The job keeps going if you close the
  panel, and you can pause or cancel it from the queue. Keep a Vinted
  tab open while it runs, because the offers are sent through it.

## Stats

The top of the tab shows how the offers are doing:

- **Offers sent** in the last 30 days.
- **Items sold** among the items you sent offers on.
- **Sell rate**, items sold as a share of offers sent.

Below the **Scan Likers** button, **Recently sent** lists your latest
offers with the buyer, the item&rsquo;s price and your offer.

## Plan and access

Auto-Offers is Vinted only for now (eBay support is coming), and needs
your Vinted account linked in SaleLinx. It is included on **Pro and
Business**; on Starter and the
free trial the tab is locked.
