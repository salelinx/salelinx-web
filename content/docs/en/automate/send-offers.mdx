# Send Offers

Someone who liked an item is the warmest buyer you have: they have
seen it, they want it, and something is holding them back. Usually
it is the price. The **Send Offers** tab in the panel finds the people
who liked your Vinted items, or who are watching your eBay listings,
and sends each of them an offer, so you turn interest into sales
without messaging anyone by hand.

This is a different feature from
[Auto-Accept Offers](/docs/automate/auto-offers), which accepts
offers buyers send *you*. Send Offers is outgoing: you make the offer.

## When to use it

- An item has plenty of likes or watchers but no sales.
- You want to clear stock before a restock or a season change.
- You would rather offer a small discount to interested buyers than
  drop the price for everyone with a
  [Price Drop](/docs/automate/price-drops).

## How it works

1. **Scan.** If you sell on both Vinted and eBay, pick **All**,
   **Vinted** or **eBay** at the top, then press the scan button
   (**Scan Likers** for Vinted, **Scan Watchers** for eBay, **Scan**
   for both). The choice is remembered.
   - On Vinted, SaleLinx reads your recent notifications and groups
     the likes by item, so you see each item with the number of new
     likers. Items whose likers have all had an offer already are left
     out, and the items with the most new likers come first.
   - On eBay, SaleLinx asks eBay which listings have people watching
     them or holding them in their basket, and shows how many are
     watching each one. A listing you sent an offer on in the last 48
     hours is left out.
2. **Pick items.** Every item starts ticked. Untick the ones you want
   to leave out, or use **Select all** to tick or untick them all.
3. **Set the price.** Choose a **Discount** (5 to 50%, default 10%)
   and a **Min price** (default 1.00, and never below 1). When SaleLinx
   knows an item&rsquo;s price, its **Offer** box is filled in from
   those two settings and updates as you change them. Type your own
   price into any item&rsquo;s **Offer** box to override it for that
   item.
4. **Add a message (optional).** Turn on **Message** and type a note
   to send with every offer. On Vinted it goes in the chat, and on
   eBay it goes to the watchers with the offer. The box starts empty,
   so nothing is sent until you type something.
5. **Send.** The button shows how many offers will go out, for example
   **Send 12 offers**, and a summary shows what they are worth. On
   Vinted that is one offer per new liker. On eBay it is one offer per
   listing, which reaches everyone watching it.

The offer is the item&rsquo;s price minus your discount, but never
below your Min price. A price you type yourself must be at least your
Min price and below the item&rsquo;s price; if it isn&rsquo;t, a note
appears under the box and that item isn&rsquo;t sent. If your Min price
is at or above an item&rsquo;s price, that item is left out too. If the
price has changed by the time the offer goes out and the offer would no
longer be a discount, that buyer is skipped.

**Rescan** reads again and refreshes the list. While offers are going
out, a progress bar shows how many were sent, skipped and failed, and
**Stop** ends the run.

Your discount, Min price and message are remembered in this browser
the next time you open the tab. There are no presets for this tab.

## Built-in safeguards

- **One offer per liker per item.** Someone who already received an
  offer on an item is not sent another for 30 days, however many
  times you scan. SaleLinx keeps this record in your browser.
- **eBay&rsquo;s own limits.** eBay allows one offer per listing every
  48 hours. Its offers last 2 days, and buyers can&rsquo;t counter
  them.
- **Human pacing.** Offers go out one at a time with natural gaps,
  through the same queue as every other SaleLinx job, with regular
  breaks. If Vinted asks SaleLinx to slow down, the job pauses and
  picks up again once the wait is over.
- **Runs in the background.** The job keeps going if you close the
  panel, and you can pause or cancel it from the queue. Keep a Vinted
  tab open while it runs, because Vinted offers are sent through it.
  eBay offers need no eBay tab.

## Stats

The top of the tab shows how the offers are doing:

- **Offers sent** in the last 30 days. On eBay, each listing you sent
  an offer on counts once.
- **Items sold** among the items you sent offers on.
- **Sell rate**, items sold as a share of offers sent.

**Recently sent** lists your latest 30 offers with the buyer (on eBay,
how many buyers it reached), the item&rsquo;s price and your offer.

## Plan and access

Send Offers works on Vinted and eBay, and needs your account on that
marketplace linked in SaleLinx. If it isn&rsquo;t, the tab shows a
**Link account** button. It is included on **Pro and Business**; on
Starter and the free trial the tab is locked.
