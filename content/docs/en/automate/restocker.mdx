# Restocker

The Restocker keeps your stock honest across marketplaces. Two
scenarios it handles:

1. **Multi-quantity Vinted seller.** You have 3 of a t-shirt. Set it
   to **2 restocks**: as each of the first two sells, the Restocker
   reposts a fresh Vinted listing for the next unit, so the item stays
   listed without you doing anything. The third sale closes it out.
2. **Linked items.** The same item is listed on both Depop and Vinted
   and [linked](/docs/automate/linking). Both sides share one stock
   count. When the last unit sells on either side, the Restocker takes
   the other side down so you don&rsquo;t sell the same item twice.

Only Vinted listings are reposted. Vinted has no quantity field, so a
fresh listing is how an item stays up there. On Depop, SaleLinx writes
your stock into Depop&rsquo;s own quantity field instead, and Depop
keeps the listing live until it runs out.

## Where it lives

The **Restocker** tab in the panel is where you choose what to track:

- **Open Restocker** opens the Restocker itself (see Running the
  Restocker below). While it runs, a **Stop Restocker** button appears
  here too.
- **Export Stock** and **Import Stock** move your restock numbers in
  and out as a CSV file.
- **Add Items** lists your cloud-saved listings that aren&rsquo;t
  tracked yet.
- **Tracked Items** lists everything being tracked, with a
  **Restocks** number on each row. You can search it and filter by
  marketplace or by linked and unlinked items.
- **Drafts (locked)** appears when any of your cloud-saved listings
  are drafts (see below).

## Adding an item

An item has to be [saved to the cloud](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync)
before it can be tracked, because reposts are rebuilt from the cloud
copy. Then either:

- **From My Listings** - switch **Restock** on in an active
  listing&rsquo;s row and set the number of restocks in the **Qty**
  column. The two are separate: typing a number doesn&rsquo;t start
  tracking until the **Restock** switch is on.
- **From the Restocker tab** - under **Add Items**, click **Add** next
  to a listing, or tick several and click **Add selected**. Items
  added this way start at 1 restock unless they already had a number
  saved. Change it in **Tracked Items**.

The number is how many times you want SaleLinx to put the item back
up after it sells. That is one fewer than the number of units you
have, and it is worth getting the right way round:

- **0 restocks** is the default in My Listings, and it is the
  ordinary one-of-a-kind case. The item stays tracked, Depop
  advertises it as 1, and the first sale closes it out and takes the
  linked partner down. It is never reposted.
- **2 restocks** means 3 units: two reposts, then the item closes out.

### Drafts

Drafts can&rsquo;t be tracked. They sit in the **Drafts (locked)**
section until you publish them. If a draft already had a restock
number saved, it comes back automatically once the listing is live.

### Export and import stock

**Export Stock** downloads your tracked items as a CSV with each
listing&rsquo;s ID, marketplace, title, number of restocks and whether
tracking is on. Change the numbers in a spreadsheet, then use
**Import Stock** to apply them all at once. Each row in the file
switches tracking on with its number, unless the file has an enabled
column, in which case only rows marked true are switched on. Import
also accepts the CSV that **Export All** in My Listings produces.

## Running the Restocker

1. In the **Restocker** tab, click **Open Restocker**. SaleLinx opens
   your Vinted sold orders page in a new tab and shows the Restocker
   there, with its status, settings and activity feed.
2. Click **Start Restocker**. You need a Vinted tab open, plus a Depop
   tab if any tracked item is on Depop or linked, both signed into the
   accounts linked to SaleLinx. If something is missing, the
   Restocker tells you what to open instead of starting.
3. It first catches up: it picks up Depop sales made while it was
   stopped, checks every tracked Vinted listing, and sends your stock
   numbers to Depop. Then it switches to **Live monitoring**, checking
   for new sales every 60 to 105 seconds by default.
4. Click **Stop Restocker** to stop it, either on that page or in the
   panel&rsquo;s Restocker tab.

Keep the Restocker tab open while it works. Closing it stops the
Restocker, and so does Chrome or SaleLinx restarting (after an update,
for example). Sales that happen while it is stopped are picked up the
next time you press **Start Restocker**.

While a repost runs, the page shows each step as it happens (waiting,
fetching data, photos, creating the draft, publishing). The
**Activity** feed lists sales, restocks, stock updates and listings
taken down.

## How it works (Vinted reposts)

1. A tracked Vinted listing sells.
2. On its next check, the Restocker confirms the sale with Vinted
   directly rather than trusting a cached status, then checks once
   more just before reposting.
3. It reposts a fresh copy built from the cloud copy (title,
   description, photos, price and item details), with the changes
   from your settings (below) applied.
4. The restock count drops by 1. The link and tracking move over to
   the new listing.
5. A sale with no restocks left closes the item out instead of
   reposting it, so you never end up with a live listing and no stock
   behind it. Tracking then switches off for that item.

## How it works (linked pairs)

When a Depop and a Vinted listing are [linked](/docs/automate/linking),
they share one stock count, and Depop shows it as the listing&rsquo;s
quantity. The Restocker only acts on linked pairs while it is
running.

- **A unit sells on Vinted** - the Vinted listing is reposted if there
  are restocks left, and the Depop quantity goes down to match.
- **A unit sells on Depop** - Depop lowers its own quantity and keeps
  the listing up, and the Vinted listing stays live. SaleLinx brings
  the shared count down the next time it checks your Depop stock.
- **The last unit sells** - the other side comes down. Depop only
  marks a listing as sold once its quantity reaches zero, so a Depop
  listing showing as sold is treated as a full sell-out.

The partner listing only comes down when the stock reaches 0. On
Depop, that means setting its quantity to 0 so it shows as sold out.
On Vinted, it follows the **When a linked item sells out** setting:
the listing is hidden (the default, and reversible) or deleted.

## Settings

The Restocker page has a **Settings** section. Changes save as you
make them and apply to this browser only.

- **Photo modifications** (on by default) - makes small random
  changes to each photo so every repost goes live as a fresh listing.
  You can set the range for **Rotation (°)**, **Scale (%)** and
  **Brightness (%)**.
- **Text modifications** - **Toggle random letter case in title** and
  **Toggle random letter case in description** are both on by
  default. Each swaps the case of one random letter on every repost.
- **Price modification** - **None** by default. Choose **Reduce %** or
  **Reduce fixed** to knock a little off the price of each repost.
- **Timing** - **Delay before reposting** (0 minutes by default),
  **Min delay between reposts** and **Max delay between reposts**
  (40 to 80 seconds by default), and **Time between checks** (1 minute
  by default, from 30 seconds up to 30 minutes).
- **When a linked item sells out** - **Hide the Vinted listing**
  (default) or **Delete the Vinted listing**. Hiding can be undone,
  deleting is permanent.
- **Advanced** - **Publish as drafts** (off by default) saves each
  repost as a Vinted draft for you to publish yourself.

If you&rsquo;d rather each new listing looked completely different,
use [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist) instead.

## What it won&rsquo;t do

- **Won&rsquo;t restock without a confirmed sale.** SaleLinx waits
  for a clear sold signal before acting, so a passing marketplace
  glitch doesn&rsquo;t trigger a phantom repost.
- **Won&rsquo;t repost something you already put back up.** If the
  listing turns out to be live again when it is about to repost, the
  repost is cancelled.
- **Won&rsquo;t repost on Depop.** Depop listings stay up through
  their quantity until they sell out.
- **Won&rsquo;t act while stopped.** Nothing happens until you press
  **Start Restocker**, and it then catches up on what it missed.

## Plan and access

Restocker is **Business only**. Pro doesn&rsquo;t include it, and on
other plans the Restocker tab and the **Restock** column in My
Listings are locked.

Restocker also needs [Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync) (for
the reposted content) and, for cross-platform pairs,
[Linking](/docs/automate/linking). Both are included with Business.
