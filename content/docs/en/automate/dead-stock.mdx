# Dead Stock

The Dead Stock tab scores every active listing in your shop against
its age, view count, and like count, then sorts them into four
health tiers. It surfaces the items that are tying up capital
without producing sales, so you can decide whether to drop the
price, relist, or delete them.

## When to use it

- Your shop is large enough that you can&rsquo;t eyeball stale items
  yourself.
- You want a regular &ldquo;triage&rdquo; pass before listing new
  stock.
- You&rsquo;re about to set up [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops)
  and want to pick the right candidates.
- You want to know how much money is sitting in stock that&rsquo;s
  unlikely to sell.

## How the classification works

Each active listing is placed in one of four tiers. The thresholds
differ by marketplace because Depop and Vinted have different
baseline engagement rates.

**Depop**

- **Dead Stock**: 21+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 30
  views.
- **Stale**: 10+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 30 views.
- **Slow Mover**: 5+ days old, and fewer than 3 likes or fewer than 15
  views.
- **Healthy**: anything above those bars.

**Vinted**

- **Dead Stock**: 30+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 40
  views.
- **Stale**: 14+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 40 views.
- **Slow Mover**: 7+ days old, and fewer than 3 likes or fewer than 20
  views.
- **Healthy**: anything above those bars.

Sold, hidden and draft listings are excluded. Age is measured from
the date the listing was created on the marketplace, not the last
refresh or price change. A relisted item counts as new, because
relisting creates a fresh listing.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why two thresholds (likes OR views)?">
A listing with 50 views but zero likes is failing differently from
one with 5 likes but no views. Either is a warning sign. The OR
catches both failure modes.
</Callout>

## Reading the tab

The Dead Stock tab has four blocks, top to bottom:

1. The capital banner - how much money is tied up in flagged listings,
   for example &ldquo;£420 stuck in 18 items &middot; oldest 64
   days&rdquo;. The total is the combined price of every Dead Stock,
   Stale and Slow Mover listing. If you sell in more than one
   currency, you see one total per currency.
2. **Inventory Health** - cards counting **Total Active** listings and
   how many sit in each tier.
3. **Worst Performers** - cards for your five worst-performing
   listings, ordered first by tier (Dead Stock, then Stale, then Slow
   Mover) and then by likes per day (lower is worse). Each card has a
   **Relist** button.
4. **All Flagged Listings** - every Dead Stock, Stale and Slow Mover
   listing, with its photo, title, price, age, views, likes and tier
   badge. Healthy listings are only counted, never listed.

The filters above the list narrow it by tier (**All**, **Dead**,
**Stale**, **Slow**, **Price dropped**) and by marketplace. They only
change the list. The capital total, the tier counts and the Worst
Performers cards always cover both marketplaces.

Double-click any card or row to open the listing on the marketplace.

## Taking action

Each row in the list has three buttons:

- **-10%** - lowers the listing&rsquo;s price on the marketplace by
  10% straight away. See &ldquo;Price dropped&rdquo; below.
- **Relist** - opens the Relist window with that listing. See
  [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist).
- **Delete** - deletes the listing on the marketplace. The first click
  changes the button to **Click again to confirm**; click again within
  a few seconds to go ahead.

**-10%** and **Delete** change your real listings, so use them
deliberately. All three only work on listings from the marketplace
you are currently browsing. To act on the others, open the panel on
the other marketplace.

To relist several listings at once, tick their boxes (or click the
rows) and press **Relist Selected** in the bar that appears at the
bottom. Only listings from the marketplace you are browsing are
relisted; the others are skipped with a message telling you to switch.
**Clear** empties the selection.

### Price dropped

After a **-10%**, the listing leaves its tier and rests under the
**Price dropped** filter for 7 days, so your to-do list visibly gets
shorter. Its row shows a badge with the size of the drop and when it
happened; hover over it to see the old and new prices and how many
days are left. A second **-10%** inside those 7 days adds to the
first (the badge then reads -19%) and restarts the 7 days.

While it rests, the listing is left out of the tier counts, the
capital total and the Worst Performers cards. If it still has not
sold after 7 days, it returns to its tier.

## How the data is gathered

Dead Stock uses the same listing data as **My Listings**, which
SaleLinx keeps in sync in the background while the panel is open.

If SaleLinx has not recorded view or like counts for a listing yet,
it counts them as zero, so an older listing can show as flagged until
the next sync fills them in.

The **Reload listings** button above the list fetches fresh data from
both marketplaces and re-classifies everything, rather than waiting
for the background sync. Use it after a burst of sales or price
changes, when the counts are behind what your shop actually looks
like.

## Recommended workflow

A weekly triage that uses the tab to drive other tools:

1. Open the Dead Stock tab and check how much money is stuck.
2. Filter to **Dead** items. For each:
   - If you still want to sell at the current price, **Relist** it to
     give it a fresh listing date.
   - If it&rsquo;s priced too high, press **-10%**, or add it to
     [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops) for a schedule of
     markdowns.
   - If you&rsquo;ve already tried both, **Delete** it and free the
     slot for new stock.
3. Filter to **Stale** items and give them a lighter bump with
   [Refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh).
4. Leave **Slow** items alone until the next pass; they&rsquo;re too
   young to act on.
