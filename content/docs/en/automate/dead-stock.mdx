# Dead Stock

The Dead Stock tab scores every active listing in your shop against
its age, view count, and like count, then sorts the ones that
aren&rsquo;t selling onto a board. It surfaces the items that are
tying up capital without producing sales, so you can decide whether
to drop the price, relist, or delete them.

Dead Stock works on Depop, Vinted and eBay listings.

## When to use it

- Your shop is large enough that you can&rsquo;t eyeball stale items
  yourself.
- You want a regular &ldquo;triage&rdquo; pass before listing new
  stock.
- You&rsquo;re about to set up [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops)
  and want to pick the right candidates.
- You want to know how much money is sitting in stock that&rsquo;s
  unlikely to sell.

## How the classification works

Each active listing is placed in one of four tiers. The thresholds
differ by marketplace because each has different baseline
engagement rates. These are the defaults; **Dead Stock settings**
(see below) let you change them.

**Depop**

- **Dead**: 21+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 30
  views.
- **Stale**: 10+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 30 views.
- **Slow**: 5+ days old, and fewer than 3 likes or fewer than 15
  views.
- **Healthy**: anything above those bars.

**Vinted**

- **Dead**: 30+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 40
  views.
- **Stale**: 14+ days old, and fewer than 5 likes or fewer than 40 views.
- **Slow**: 7+ days old, and fewer than 3 likes or fewer than 20
  views.
- **Healthy**: anything above those bars.

**eBay**

eBay listings run until they sell, so they take longer to count as
old. eBay has watchers rather than likes, and its views cover the last
90 days. Few buyers watch a listing, so on eBay both numbers have to
be low.

- **Dead**: 60+ days old, and fewer than 2 watchers and fewer than
  60 views.
- **Stale**: 30+ days old, and fewer than 2 watchers and fewer than 60
  views.
- **Slow**: 14+ days old, and fewer than 1 watcher and fewer than
  30 views.
- **Healthy**: anything above those bars.

If eBay has no view figure for a listing, it is judged on watchers
alone. Auctions are never flagged, because they end on their own.

Sold, hidden and draft listings are excluded. Age is measured from
the date the listing was created on the marketplace, not the last
refresh or price change. A relisted item counts as new, because
relisting creates a fresh listing.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why two thresholds (likes OR views)?">
A listing with 50 views but zero likes is failing differently from
one with 5 likes but no views. Either is a warning sign. On Depop and
Vinted the OR catches both failure modes. You can change this for
each marketplace in Dead Stock settings.
</Callout>

## Reading the tab

At the top, a headline says how many listings aren&rsquo;t selling,
for example &ldquo;18 listings aren&rsquo;t selling&rdquo;, with how
much money they tie up and the oldest one&rsquo;s age underneath,
such as &ldquo;£420 tied up &middot; oldest 64 days&rdquo;. If you
sell in more than one currency, you see one total per currency. When
nothing is past your limits, it reads **Everything is selling fine**.

Below it, the board has four columns:

- **Dead** - &ldquo;Old, with almost no attention. These need the
  most help.&rdquo;
- **Stale** - &ldquo;Getting old, and buyers are losing
  interest.&rdquo;
- **Slow** - &ldquo;Newer listings off to a quiet start.&rdquo;
- **Price dropped** - listings resting after a **-10%**. See Price
  dropped below.

Each column shows how many listings it holds and what they add up to,
with the oldest first. A card shows the listing&rsquo;s photo, title,
price, age, views and likes (watchers on eBay). Healthy listings
aren&rsquo;t on the board.

The marketplace buttons above the board (**All**, or Depop, Vinted or
eBay) narrow the whole page, headline included.

Double-click any card to open the listing on the marketplace.

## Taking action

Each card has three buttons:

- **-10%** - lowers the listing&rsquo;s price on the marketplace by
  10% straight away. See &ldquo;Price dropped&rdquo; below.
- **Relist** - opens the Relist window with that listing. See
  [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist).
- **Delete** - deletes the listing on the marketplace (on eBay it ends
  the listing). The first click
  changes the button to **Click again to confirm**; click again within
  a few seconds to go ahead.

**-10%** is not offered for eBay listings with variations.

**-10%** and **Delete** change your real listings, so use them
deliberately. Changes to Depop and Vinted listings go through an open
tab for that marketplace, in any window.

### A whole column

When a column holds more than one listing, buttons at its top act on
all of them, with the count on each: **Relist all**, **-10% all** and
**Delete all**. Listings already waiting on another job are left out.

- **Relist all** opens the Relist window with the column&rsquo;s
  listings.
- **-10% all** asks first (&ldquo;Take 10% off 18 listings?&rdquo;):
  the new prices go live straight away, and the listings move to
  **Price dropped** for 7 days. Click **Take 10% off** to go ahead.
- **Delete all** asks first too, because the listings come off their
  marketplaces for good.

If the column spans several marketplaces, the window has a button for
each so you can leave one out. Listings on a marketplace with no tab
open are left out, and the window says to open a tab to include
them. Both run in the background through the
[queue](/docs/getting-started/queue).

### Several listings

Click a card to select it, drag across several, or Shift-click to
select every one between. Then press **Relist Selected** in the bar
that appears to open the Relist window with all of them. **Clear**
empties the selection.

### Price dropped

After a **-10%**, the listing leaves its column and rests in **Price
dropped** for 7 days, so your to-do list visibly gets shorter. Its
card shows a badge with the size of the drop and when it happened;
hover over it to see the old and new prices and how many days are
left. A second **-10%** inside those 7 days adds to the first (the
badge then reads -19%) and restarts the 7 days.

While it rests, the listing is left out of the headline and the money
tied up. If it still has not sold after 7 days, it returns to its
column.

## Dead Stock settings

**Dead Stock settings** is in **Settings**, under **Advanced
settings**. It has a grid for each of Depop, Vinted and eBay, with a
row each for **Dead**, **Stale** and **Slow** and these columns:

- **Days listed** - how old a listing has to be.
- **Likes under** (**Watchers under** on eBay) and **Views under** -
  how low its numbers have to be.

Under **Flag a listing when**, pick **Either number is low** or **Both
numbers are low** for that marketplace. A listing takes the first
level it matches, from Dead down. Leave the days empty to turn a level
off (the box shows **Off**), or leave likes and views empty to flag on
age alone.

The **Default** preset holds the numbers above. Change any of them and
you can save your own preset.

## How the data is gathered

Dead Stock uses the same listing data as **My Listings**, which
SaleLinx keeps in sync in the background while the panel is open.

If SaleLinx has not recorded view or like counts for a listing yet,
it counts them as zero, so an older listing can show as flagged until
the next sync fills them in.

The **Reload listings** button above the board fetches fresh data from
your marketplaces and re-classifies everything, rather than waiting
for the background sync. Use it after a burst of sales or price
changes, when the counts are behind what your shop actually looks
like.

## Recommended workflow

A weekly triage that uses the tab to drive other tools:

1. Open the Dead Stock tab and check how much money is stuck.
2. Start with the **Dead** column. For each:
   - If you still want to sell at the current price, **Relist** it to
     give it a fresh listing date.
   - If it&rsquo;s priced too high, press **-10%**, or add it to
     [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops) for a schedule of
     markdowns.
   - If you&rsquo;ve already tried both, **Delete** it and free the
     slot for new stock.
3. Move on to the **Stale** column and give those a lighter bump with
   [Refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh).
4. Leave **Slow** items alone until the next pass; they&rsquo;re too
   young to act on.
