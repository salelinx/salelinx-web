# Dead Stock

The Dead Stock tab scores every active listing in your shop against
its age, view count, and like count, then sorts them into four
health tiers. It surfaces the items that are tying up capital
without producing sales, so you can decide whether to refresh, mark
down, relist, or delete them.

## When to use it

- Your shop is large enough that you can&rsquo;t eyeball stale items
  yourself.
- You want a regular &ldquo;triage&rdquo; pass before listing new
  stock.
- You&rsquo;re about to run [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops)
  and want to pick the right candidates.
- You want to know how much money is sitting in stock that&rsquo;s
  unlikely to sell.

## How the classification works

Each active listing is placed in one of four tiers. The thresholds
differ by marketplace because Depop and Vinted have different
baseline engagement rates.

**Depop**

| Tier        | Rule                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dead**    | 21+ days old AND (fewer than 5 likes OR fewer than 30 views) |
| **Stale**   | 10+ days old AND (fewer than 5 likes OR fewer than 30 views) |
| **Slow**    | 5+ days old AND (fewer than 3 likes OR fewer than 15 views)  |
| **Healthy** | Anything above those bars                                    |

**Vinted**

| Tier        | Rule                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dead**    | 30+ days old AND (fewer than 5 likes OR fewer than 40 views) |
| **Stale**   | 14+ days old AND (fewer than 5 likes OR fewer than 40 views) |
| **Slow**    | 7+ days old AND (fewer than 3 likes OR fewer than 20 views)  |
| **Healthy** | Anything above those bars                                    |

Sold and hidden listings are excluded. Age is measured from the
listing&rsquo;s original creation date, not the last refresh.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why two thresholds (likes OR views)?">
A listing with 50 views but zero likes is failing differently from
one with 5 likes but no views. Either is a warning sign. The OR
catches both failure modes.
</Callout>

## Reading the tab

The Dead Stock tab has four blocks, top to bottom:

1. **Tier counts** - how many listings sit in each tier across the
   filtered marketplace(s).
2. **Capital at risk** - the combined value of every Dead and Stale
   item, plus the age of the oldest such listing. This is the money
   you could free up by selling, marking down, or removing.
3. **Worst five** - cards showing your five worst-performing
   listings. Ordered first by tier (Dead beats Stale beats Slow) and
   then by likes-per-day (lower is worse). Each card has a one-click
   **Relist** button.
4. **Full list** - the complete classified inventory, filterable by
   tier and by marketplace. Each row shows the photo, title, price,
   age, tier badge, and engagement stats.

The filters at the top of the list let you narrow to a specific
tier or marketplace without losing the row selection underneath.

## Taking action

Selecting one or more rows opens the action bar at the bottom of
the tab. From there you can:

- **Relist** - opens the relist overlay pre-filled with the chosen
  listings. See [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist).
- **Clear selection** - drops everything from the action bar.

For each individual listing you can also click straight through to
the marketplace page to delete it manually, or jump to the **Price
Drops** tab to add it to your markdown schedule.

The detector itself never edits or deletes anything. It&rsquo;s
purely diagnostic; every action is opt-in.

## How the data is gathered

The detector reads from your **Listings** cache, which SaleLinx
keeps in sync in the background while the panel is open and tops up
with listing data picked up from your normal browsing.

If a listing was created very recently and view/like counts
haven&rsquo;t been recorded yet, it&rsquo;s treated as having zero
of each, which can briefly flag it as Slow.

## Recommended workflow

A weekly triage that uses the detector to drive other tools:

1. Open the Dead Stock tab and check the **Capital at risk** number.
2. Filter to **Dead** items. For each:
   - If you still want to sell at the current price, send it to
     [Relist](/docs/inventory/relist) to give it a fresh listing
     date.
   - If it&rsquo;s priced too high, add it to
     [Price Drops](/docs/automate/price-drops) with an aggressive
     percentage.
   - If you&rsquo;ve already tried both, delete it on the
     marketplace and free the slot for new stock.
3. Filter to **Stale** items and run [Refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh)
   on the lot.
4. Leave **Slow** items alone until the next pass; they&rsquo;re too
   young to act on.
