# Price Drops

Price Drops lowers the price of listings you&rsquo;ve opted in, on a
schedule you set per item. It turns slow-moving stock over without
you having to remember to revisit it, and it never goes below the
floor you choose.

## How the maths works

Drops are **cumulative**, not compounding. Cycle N targets:

```
new_price = original_price × (1 − N × percent / 100)
```

So at 10% per cycle, an item priced at £30 drops:

- Cycle 1: £30 &rarr; £27 (10% off original)
- Cycle 2: £27 &rarr; £24 (20% off original)
- Cycle 3: £24 &rarr; £21 (30% off original)
- ...

The percentage is always &ldquo;N% of the original price&rdquo;,
never of the current price. That means the per-cycle pound amount
stays constant rather than shrinking each round. The original price
is the one the listing had when its first drop happened.

If a drop would take the price below the item&rsquo;s **floor
price**, the price is set to the floor instead. Once an item sits at
its floor, drops stop. Without a floor, drops stop when the
cumulative percentage reaches 100%.

## Which listings you can track

Price Drops works from the listings you have saved to the cloud. Only
active, cloud-saved listings appear in the tab, so save a listing to
the cloud in **My Listings** first if it&rsquo;s missing. See
[Cloud sync](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync).

## Setting it up

In the **Price Drops** tab:

1. Under **Available listings**, tick the listings you want and click
   **Add selected**, or click **Add** on a single row. The search box,
   marketplace filter and age filter help you find stale items. Added
   listings move to **Tracked listings**.
2. Each tracked listing has its own rule, written as a sentence you
   edit in place: **Drop** (a percentage) **every** (a number of)
   **days** **down to** (a floor price) **counting from** **Listing
   age** or **Now**.
   - The percentage is 1 to 90 per cycle.
   - The days between drops is 1 to 180. It is also how old the
     listing must be (counted from the start point) before the first
     drop.
   - The floor is optional. Leave it empty for no floor.
   - **Listing age** counts from when the listing was created, so an
     item older than its interval is due straight away. **Now**
     counts from the moment you click it, so the first drop waits a
     full interval.
3. Switch on **Run price drops** at the top of the tab, and pick how
   often SaleLinx checks for due drops under **Check every**: 12h,
   24h (1 day), 48h (2 days), 3 days, 7 days or 14 days. The default
   is 24h.

Listings you add start at 15% every 3 days, with a floor at half the
current price, counting from the listing&rsquo;s age. Change any of
these before the next check if they don&rsquo;t suit you.

To change several items at once, tick them in **Tracked listings** and
click **Bulk edit**. Fill in only the fields you want to change
(discount, how often, floor price, start from) and click **Apply**;
anything left blank stays as it is on each item.

## The drop schedule

Under each tracked listing, a row of price steps shows what will
happen: the current price (**Now**), then each upcoming drop with its
date, ending at **floor** when the floor is what stops it. It shows up
to 12 drops. Above it, the listing&rsquo;s line shows its price, how
many days it has been active, how many drops have happened so far, and
when the next one is due.

## When drops run

Once **Run price drops** is on, SaleLinx checks your tracked listings
at the interval you picked; the first check comes one interval after
you switch it on. **Next run** shows when the next check is, and
**Run now** starts one straight away.

Each check uses the prices and statuses SaleLinx last synced from
your shop, rather than asking the marketplace again. For each tracked
listing it:

- Skips the listing if it has sold or is no longer active.
- Skips it if its interval hasn&rsquo;t passed since the last drop
  (or, for a listing that hasn&rsquo;t dropped yet, since its start
  point).
- Skips it if it is already at its floor.
- Skips a Depop listing that is in a Depop sale, because changing the
  price would end the sale.
- Otherwise, sets the new price on the marketplace.

At most one drop per item per run. An item that&rsquo;s been on for
60 days with a 14-day interval drops once on the next run. It
doesn&rsquo;t catch up by firing 4 drops back-to-back.

A drop always sets a price lower than the one SaleLinx last synced.
If you lower a price by hand on the marketplace, let SaleLinx sync
your listings (open the panel on Depop or Vinted) before the next
check, so a drop is never worked out from the old, higher price.

Keep a Depop or Vinted tab open for the listings you track, because
SaleLinx changes prices through those tabs. If another SaleLinx job is
running when a check is due, it waits and tries again a few minutes
later.

## History

Click **History** to open **Recent drops**, a log of the last 500
price changes SaleLinx made or tried to make: which listing, which
marketplace, the price before and after, the percentage, and when.
A change that failed (for example because the listing is gone) is
marked with the error.

**Clear history** empties the log without affecting tracked items.

## Stopping markdowns on an item

Click the &times; on a tracked listing (**Stop tracking**), or tick
several and click **Remove selected**. The listing goes back to
**Available listings** and no further drops happen. Drops already
applied stay in place; the listing just stops moving. If you add it
again later, it starts a fresh schedule from its price at that time.

To stop everything at once, switch off **Run price drops**.
