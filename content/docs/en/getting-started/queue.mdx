# The queue

Most of the work SaleLinx does for you on Depop and Vinted goes through the
queue: Grow runs, Refresh, Relist, Crosslist, Shop Designer, Price Drops
(listed in the queue as **Auto Markdown**), Auto-Offers, CSV import, and
bulk actions from **My Listings** such as editing, deleting or posting
drafts.

Each run waits its turn, and the requests it sends are spaced out like a
person using the site. Marketplaces limit how fast an account can act, and
an account that goes faster than that can get slowed down or blocked. The
queue is how SaleLinx stays under those limits.

## How it works

- **One run at a time.** Start three things and the second and third wait
  until the first one finishes.
- **Spaced-out actions.** There are a few seconds between actions, a little
  slower at the start of a run and as a long run goes on, with the odd
  short pause at random.
- **Planned breaks.** After a stretch of work, SaleLinx stops for a while.
  See Breaks below.
- **Depop and Vinted are paced separately.** A break or slowdown on one
  doesn&rsquo;t stop syncing, offer replies or messages on the other. Queued
  runs still take turns, whichever marketplace they&rsquo;re for.
- **No duplicates.** Starting exactly the same run twice doesn&rsquo;t add a
  second copy.

## Where to find it

The queue sits at the bottom of the panel&rsquo;s left sidebar, under
**Settings**. The **Queue** heading shows how many tasks are in it, and each
row shows the task&rsquo;s name and progress (for example `12/50`). It reads
**No tasks** when there&rsquo;s nothing to do.

Below the list are two buttons: **Pause** (or **Resume**) and
**See more...**, which opens the full Queue window. If you&rsquo;ve
collapsed the sidebar, the same controls appear as play, pause and
&ldquo;more&rdquo; icons.

At the very bottom, the status line tells you what&rsquo;s happening right
now:

- **Idle** - nothing is running.
- The running task and its progress, for example **Refresh 12/50**.
- **Paused** - you paused the queue.
- **Break 12m** - a planned break, with the minutes left.
- **Limited 2m** - a marketplace is limiting requests, with the time left.

Listings that are waiting in a queued task get a badge such as
**Queued &middot; Delete** in the grids, and are locked so you can&rsquo;t
queue a second action on the same item.

## The Queue window

**See more...** opens a larger view of the queue. At the top:

- **Pause** or **Resume** for the whole queue.
- **Clear all**, which asks whether to clear just the history (**Clear
  history**) or also cancel everything running and waiting (**Clear all**).
- A summary of how many tasks are **Running**, **Queued** and **On hold**,
  how many have **Completed**, **Failed** or been **Cancelled**, and any
  break or rate limit with its time left.

Below that, tasks are grouped into **Current**, **Cancelled**, **Failed** and
**Completed**. Each task shows its marketplace, a status, a progress bar and
a line of detail, for example:

- **Running** - &ldquo;Running for 3m &middot; ~2m left&rdquo;.
- **Queued** - &ldquo;Next up #2 &middot; Added 5m ago&rdquo;.
- **Paused** - &ldquo;Waiting to resume&rdquo;.
- **On hold** - why the task is waiting and when it will try again.
- **On break** or **Rate limited** - when the run will continue.
- **Scheduled** - the next scheduled Auto-refresh, with a countdown.

Finished tasks show when they ended and how long they took, plus how many
items were skipped or failed. A failed task shows the reason.

Each group shows five tasks at first, with a button to show the rest, and
has its own **Clear** button. The &times; on a finished task clears just that
one. Clearing **Current** cancels everything running and waiting, so it
asks first. SaleLinx keeps the most recent 100 finished tasks.

## Pause, resume and cancel

**Pause** stops the queue once the item in progress finishes. The button
reads **Pausing...** until then. Nothing is lost: **Resume** carries on
from where the run stopped.

To take a single task out, click the &times; on its row:

- **On a waiting task**, SaleLinx asks to remove it from the queue.
- **On the running task**, it asks you to confirm the cancel. Anything
  already done stays done. The rest is dropped, and the next task starts.

Pausing can also hold back SaleLinx&rsquo;s other activity on that
marketplace, such as syncing and replying to offers, until you press
**Resume**. Don&rsquo;t leave the queue paused longer than you need to.

## Changing the order

Tasks run in the order you started them. To change that:

- **In the sidebar**, pause the queue first. Up and down arrows then appear
  on the waiting tasks.
- **In the Queue window**, the arrows are always there.

If you move a waiting task above the one that&rsquo;s running, the running
task pauses after its current item, the task you moved runs, and the paused
one then carries on by itself.

Quick one-off actions don&rsquo;t join the task list at all. Answering an
offer, sending a message or loading your shipping labels goes out next,
between the steps of whatever is running and ahead of background syncing.
These one-off actions never count toward a break.

## Breaks

After a few dozen actions on a marketplace, SaleLinx takes a planned break.
Most last a few minutes, and the occasional one lasts up to half an hour.
The status line shows **Break** with the minutes left, and the running task
shows **On break** with &ldquo;Taking a planned break&rdquo; and when it
resumes.

You don&rsquo;t need to do anything. The run continues on its own when the
break ends, and the next stretch starts slowly again. Breaks are part of
what keeps your account safe, so there&rsquo;s no button to skip one.

While a marketplace is on a break, nothing else is sent to it either,
including one-off actions such as an offer reply. They go out as soon as
the break ends. The other marketplace isn&rsquo;t affected, apart from runs
waiting their turn behind this one.

## When a marketplace pushes back

Sometimes Depop or Vinted answers &ldquo;too many requests&rdquo;. SaleLinx
handles it in steps:

1. **It retries.** The request is tried again a few times, with a longer
   gap each time.
2. **It waits.** If the marketplace still refuses, SaleLinx stops sending
   to it for a while. The status line shows **Limited** with a countdown,
   and the running task shows **Rate limited** with &ldquo;Depop is
   limiting requests&rdquo; and when it resumes. Work picks up by itself
   afterwards.
3. **It stops the run.** If the marketplace refuses three times in a row,
   SaleLinx ends the current run early to protect your account. What it
   finished stays done, and history shows how far it got (for example
   `12/50`).

If a run stopped early, wait a while before starting it again, and consider
a smaller run or the **Careful** preset that Grow, Refresh and Relist offer.
Restarting straight away is likely to hit the limit again.

**Auto-Offers** works slightly differently. When Vinted limits how fast
offers can be sent, the Auto-Offers task goes **On hold** and carries on by
itself once the marketplace lets offers through again.

If a marketplace refuses access outright, rather than asking SaleLinx to slow
down, the run stops at once. Open the marketplace in a normal tab, check
you&rsquo;re still signed in and can browse your shop, and complete any
check the site shows you. Then start the run again.

## Plan limits and tasks on hold

Some actions count toward a daily or monthly allowance (see the usage
meters in [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings)):

- **If you ask for more than you have left**, SaleLinx queues only what
  fits and tells you, for example &ldquo;Queued 20 of 50&rdquo;.
- **If the allowance runs out before a queued task starts**, the task goes
  **On hold** with a note such as &ldquo;Crosslist limit reached&rdquo;.
  The rest of the queue keeps moving. The held task tries again about once
  an hour, so it runs by itself once your allowance resets. Daily
  allowances reset at midnight UTC and monthly ones on the first of the
  month. Remove it with &times; if you don&rsquo;t want to wait.
- **If the feature isn&rsquo;t in your plan** any more when the task&rsquo;s
  turn comes, the task fails and says so.

The limits for each plan are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Keep a marketplace tab open

SaleLinx does its work through your open Depop and Vinted tabs, using the
session you&rsquo;re signed in with. While there&rsquo;s work queued for a
marketplace, keep at least one tab for it open. It can be in any window and
doesn&rsquo;t need to be the tab you&rsquo;re looking at, and the panel
itself can be closed.

Panel tabs that work with one marketplace only (**Refresh** and **Shop
Designer** for Depop, **Auto-Offers** for Vinted) say so when no browser tab
for it is open: &ldquo;This feature needs a Vinted tab. Open one in any
window and it will unlock here.&rdquo; Click **Open Vinted** (or **Open
Depop**) and the panel tab unlocks as soon as the page loads. Crosslist needs both: &ldquo;Both Depop
and Vinted tabs must be open to crosslist.&rdquo;

## Closing the panel, the tab or the browser

- **Closing the panel** changes nothing. The queue runs in the background.
- **Closing your last Depop or Vinted tab** cuts SaleLinx off from that
  marketplace, so tasks for it can&rsquo;t make progress. Keep one open.
- **Closing Chrome** stops everything. Next time you open it, the queue
  comes back **Paused** with your tasks still listed. A task that was
  running continues from where it stopped when you press **Resume**.
  Nothing restarts without you, and any rate-limit wait is forgotten.
- **Updating SaleLinx** can clear tasks that were waiting rather than carry
  them over to the new version, so check the queue after an update.
- **Logging out** cancels everything in the queue and clears its history.

## Other entries in the queue

Two kinds of entry aren&rsquo;t runs you started yourself:

- **Saving to Cloud** appears while SaleLinx copies listing details into
  your [cloud backup](/docs/inventory/cloud-sync). It steps aside while a
  queued task runs, and has its own pause, resume and cancel buttons.
- **Auto Refresh**, shown as **Scheduled** with a countdown, is your next
  scheduled [Auto-refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh). Its &times; turns the
  schedule off.

Background syncing of your listings, offers and messages doesn&rsquo;t show
in the list, but it goes through the same line to each marketplace as your
runs.

### Depop follows and unfollows

Grow runs on Depop (following and unfollowing) appear in the queue and wait
their turn like everything else, but they pace themselves instead of using
the queue&rsquo;s spacing. They wait a random 5 to 8 seconds between people,
or your own range if you turn on **Choose my own wait time**, and they
don&rsquo;t take planned breaks. Use **Stop after this many actions** and
**Stop after this many minutes** to keep these runs short. Grow runs on
Vinted use the normal queue pacing. See [Grow](/docs/inventory/bots).
