# Connect your first marketplace

SaleLinx works through your marketplace session in the browser. There&rsquo;s
no marketplace password to enter and no API key to copy. You just need to be
signed in to Depop or Vinted in the same browser profile where SaleLinx is
installed.

Each SaleLinx account links one Depop account and one Vinted account. This
is the same on every plan.

## 1. Sign in on the marketplace

Open a regular tab and sign in at [depop.com](https://www.depop.com) or on
your Vinted site. If you sell on both, sign in to both.

SaleLinx supports these Vinted country sites: vinted.com, .co.uk, .ie, .fr,
.de, .at, .be, .nl, .es, .pt, .it, .pl, .cz, .sk, .hu, .ro, .lt, .se, .dk
and .fi.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why no login?">
When you sign in to Depop or Vinted, the marketplace remembers you in your
browser. SaleLinx uses that same session to talk to the marketplace, the way
the marketplace&rsquo;s own pages do. It never asks for, sees or stores your
marketplace password.
</Callout>

## 2. Open the SaleLinx panel

On any Depop or Vinted page, click the SaleLinx button in the bottom-right
corner (or press **Alt+S**). The panel only opens on Depop and Vinted pages,
not from the toolbar icon. If you haven&rsquo;t signed in to SaleLinx or
started a plan yet, see [Installing the SaleLinx extension](/docs/getting-started/install-the-extension)
first.

## 3. Link your shops

Until at least one account is linked, the panel shows the **Link your
shops** screen:

1. Under **I sell on**, pick Depop, Vinted or both.
2. Each marketplace row shows the account SaleLinx can see in your browser,
   with its photo and username. Click **Link** to connect it.
3. If a row shows **Log in** instead of **Link**, you&rsquo;re not signed in
   to that marketplace. Click **Log in**, sign in on the marketplace, then
   come back to the panel and link.

Once an account is linked, the panel opens on **My Listings**, which shows
your items from every linked marketplace in one list.

## If you only sell on one of them

You don&rsquo;t have to use both. Pick **Depop only**, **Vinted only** or
**Both** on the **Link your shops** screen (or in the **Where do you sell?**
box that appears after your first sign-in). You can change the answer any
time in **Settings > Marketplaces**. Until you choose, SaleLinx assumes both.

Switching one off is not a display preference. SaleLinx hides that
marketplace&rsquo;s tabs, filters and account row, stops asking you to link
it, and skips its background work entirely, so there&rsquo;s nothing running
for a marketplace you don&rsquo;t sell on.

Two features need both marketplaces switched on and both accounts linked:
[Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist) and [Linking](/docs/automate/linking).
They stay visible rather than disappearing, and explain which of the two
things is missing.

If you open the panel on a marketplace you&rsquo;ve switched off, it shows a
banner rather than blocking you, with a button to turn that marketplace back
on (for example **Turn on Vinted**) and **Dismiss**.

## Where account linking lives

After setup, linking and unlinking is in **Settings > Marketplace
accounts** (called **Accounts** in versions before 1.1.12). Each
marketplace has a row there showing the account it is
linked to, with an **Unlink** button. When SaleLinx can see a marketplace
account that isn&rsquo;t linked yet, the row asks whether to link it, with
**Link** and **Not now** buttons. See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings)
for the rest of that tab.

Don&rsquo;t confuse it with the [Linking tab](/docs/automate/linking), which
does something different: that one pairs an individual Depop listing with its
Vinted counterpart.

## Troubleshooting

**The account is already linked to a different SaleLinx account.**
A Depop or Vinted account can only be linked to one SaleLinx account at a
time. Log in to the SaleLinx account it is linked to, unlink it in
**Settings > Marketplace accounts**, then try again here.

**The account has already used a free trial on another SaleLinx account.**
Each marketplace account gets one free trial. To link it to this SaleLinx
account, start a paid plan from the [pricing page](/pricing) first. If you
are in a free trial, that ends the trial and starts billing straight away.

**Could not link, no login found.**
SaleLinx couldn&rsquo;t find your session on that marketplace. Sign in to
the marketplace in a normal tab, then try again.

**The panel says the marketplace is signed in as a different account.**
The account you are signed in to on Depop or Vinted is not the one linked to
SaleLinx. Features for that marketplace stay unavailable until you either
sign in to the linked account on the marketplace, or unlink it in
**Settings > Marketplace accounts** and link the account you&rsquo;re using
now.

**My Listings stays empty.**
Reload your Depop or Vinted tab, then open the panel again. If the panel
says your session has expired, sign in on the marketplace again.

**I&rsquo;m on the wrong Vinted country.**
SaleLinx follows the Vinted site you use in your browser. If you switch
countries (for example from .co.uk to .fr), sign in on the new site first.

For anything else, see the [FAQ troubleshooting section](/help/faq#troubleshooting).
