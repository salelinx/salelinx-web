# Connect your first marketplace

For Depop and Vinted, SaleLinx reads your marketplace session straight from
your browser. There&rsquo;s no password to enter, no API key to copy. You just
need to be signed in to Depop or Vinted in the same browser profile where
SaleLinx is installed. eBay connects differently, through eBay&rsquo;s own
sign-in page (see *Connecting eBay* below).

## 1. Sign in on the marketplace

Open a regular tab and sign in at [depop.com](https://www.depop.com) or
[vinted.com](https://www.vinted.com). If you sell on both, sign in to both.
SaleLinx supports any country domain (`vinted.co.uk`, `vinted.fr`, `vinted.de`,
`vinted.es`, etc.).

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why no login?">
Marketplace logins are tied to browser cookies that the marketplace itself
sets. SaleLinx reads those cookies on its own behalf when it makes
authenticated calls, the same way the marketplace&rsquo;s own pages do. We
never see, store, or transmit your marketplace password.
</Callout>

## 2. Open the SaleLinx panel

Navigate to a product page (any item in your shop), a profile page, or your
inbox on Depop or Vinted. Click the SaleLinx icon in your toolbar to open the
side panel.

The first time you open it on each marketplace, the panel runs a quick session
check. When it succeeds, you&rsquo;ll see your shop name and avatar at the top
of the panel.

## 3. Verify the connection

In the panel, open the **Listings** tab. SaleLinx reads your active items from
the marketplace and shows them in a unified grid. If both marketplaces are
connected, you&rsquo;ll see Depop and Vinted items merged together with a small
platform badge on each row.

If the grid stays empty for more than a few seconds, scroll down to the
Troubleshooting section below.

## Connecting eBay

1. Open **Settings** in the panel and find eBay under **Marketplace
   accounts**.
2. Click **Link**. A small SaleLinx window opens.
3. Leave **Show SaleLinx on eBay** ticked if you want the panel on eBay
   pages too. Chrome asks you to allow SaleLinx on eBay sites. This is
   optional: linking works either way.
4. Sign in to eBay and agree to let SaleLinx manage your listings. The
   window closes and your eBay account shows as linked.

SaleLinx never sees your eBay password. eBay gives SaleLinx permission to
act on your listings, which you can remove at any time by clicking
**Unlink** in Settings.

Once linked, your eBay listings appear in **My Listings** and eBay becomes
a source and target in [Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist).

## If you don&rsquo;t sell on all of them

You don&rsquo;t have to use every marketplace. The first time you sign in,
SaleLinx asks which marketplaces you sell on: any mix of **Depop**,
**Vinted** and **eBay**. You can change the answer any time in **Settings >
Marketplaces**. New installs default to all three.

Switching one off is not a display preference. SaleLinx hides that
marketplace&rsquo;s tabs, filters and account row, stops asking you to link
it, and skips its background work entirely, so there&rsquo;s nothing running
for a marketplace you don&rsquo;t sell on.

[Crosslist](/docs/inventory/crosslist) needs at least two marketplaces
switched on and linked, and [Linking](/docs/automate/linking) needs both
Depop and Vinted. Both stay visible rather than disappearing, and explain
what&rsquo;s missing.

If you open the panel on a marketplace you&rsquo;ve switched off, it shows a
dismissible banner rather than blocking you.

## Where account linking lives

Linking and unlinking marketplace accounts is in **Settings > Accounts**.
Each marketplace has a row there showing the account it is linked to, with
a **Link** button whenever SaleLinx can see a live session for that
marketplace.

Don&rsquo;t confuse it with the [Linking tab](/docs/automate/linking), which
does something different: that one pairs an individual Depop listing with its
Vinted counterpart.

If you run more than one shop on the same marketplace, check the
[pricing page](/features#pricing) for which plans cover multiple linked
accounts.

## Troubleshooting

**The panel shows &ldquo;Not signed in&rdquo;.**
You&rsquo;re signed out on the marketplace itself. Open a normal tab, sign in,
then re-open the panel.

**Listings stay empty.**
Sometimes the marketplace returns 0 items if your session is partially
authenticated (e.g. signed in to the homepage but not to the API). Reload the
marketplace tab once and re-open the panel.

**I&rsquo;m on the wrong domain.**
SaleLinx auto-detects which Vinted regional domain you use. If you switch
countries (.co.uk to .fr), sign in on the new domain first.

For anything else, the [FAQ troubleshooting section](/faq#troubleshooting)
covers panel visibility, signed-out states, and listing sync issues.
