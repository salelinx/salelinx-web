# Account and subscription

Your SaleLinx login covers both the website and the extension. The same
account signs you in to billing on salelinx.com and to the panel inside
Chrome, though you sign in to each one separately.

You can sign in two ways: **Continue with Google**, or an email and
password. Google is the quicker route and leaves you with no separate
SaleLinx password to remember. If you signed up with Google and later want a
password too, you can add one from your account page (see Account security
below).

## Using more than one computer

You can stay signed in on several computers, but only one of them can use
SaleLinx at a time. If your account is already in use somewhere else, the
panel shows **In use on another device**. Click **Use here instead** to carry
on from this computer. The other computer is then signed out of SaleLinx.

## Where to find it

- **Website**: your account page is at [salelinx.com/account](/account).
  The **Account &amp; billing** link in the window that opens from the
  SaleLinx toolbar icon takes you there too.
- **Extension**: the **Membership** card in the panel&rsquo;s **Settings**
  tab shows your plan, and its **Manage** button opens your account page.
  See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings) for the rest of that tab.

## Plans at a glance

Plans differ in which features they unlock and how much you can do each day
or month. The current pricing and what each plan includes lives on the
[pricing page](/pricing). The extension reads its limits from the same
place, so what you see there is what you get.

## Seeing what you have used

The **Membership** card in the panel&rsquo;s **Settings** tab shows your
plan, its status, and the date it renews (or the date your trial or plan
ends). Below that is a row for every allowance on your plan, such as
**Crosslists this month** (crosslists to and from eBay count too) or
**Refreshes today**. Each row has a bar showing
how much you have used and how much is left, so you can tell at a glance
whether you are about to run out. Plans that include cloud storage get a
**Cloud storage** row too.

Daily allowances reset at midnight UTC, and monthly ones on the 1st of each
month (UTC).

## Free 14-day trial

New accounts can start a 14-day free trial. It has the same features as
Starter, with lower allowances. Start it with **Start free trial** on the
panel&rsquo;s **Welcome to SaleLinx** screen or on the
[pricing page](/pricing).

A card is required to start the trial, but nothing is charged during it.
When the 14 days are up, the trial converts to a paid Starter subscription
automatically unless you cancel first. You can cancel at any time before
then with **Manage subscription** on your account page, and you will not be
charged.

There is one trial per person. If your SaleLinx account has had a plan
before, or a Depop, Vinted or eBay account you have linked has already used
a trial, the pricing page skips the trial and you subscribe at the normal
price. There is no permanent free plan: to use SaleLinx after the trial you
need an active Starter, Pro or Business subscription.

## Subscribing

1. Open the [pricing page](/pricing). The **Upgrade** and **Go to Pricing**
   buttons in the panel take you there too.
2. Click **Subscribe** on the plan you want. If you aren&rsquo;t signed in on
   the website, it asks you to sign in first.
3. Stripe Checkout opens. Complete payment.

Your plan is active as soon as the payment goes through. You don&rsquo;t
need to reinstall or sign in again: the panel picks up the new plan within a
few minutes, the next time you load a Depop, Vinted or eBay page. If the
panel is showing **Welcome to SaleLinx** or **Your plan has ended**, click
the refresh link under the main button to check straight away.

## Upgrading during your trial

During the trial, the buttons on the pricing page start your paid plan
instead of opening Checkout. Starter&rsquo;s button reads **Start Starter
now**; Pro and Business still read **Subscribe**. Your account page also has
a **Start Starter now** button.

Nothing happens until you confirm. A box explains that this ends your free
trial, charges your card now and every month until you cancel, and that the
new plan&rsquo;s limits apply straight away. The button to go ahead shows
the amount you will be charged. If your card is declined, your plan stays as
it was and you can update your card and try again.

## Changing plans

Once you are on a paid plan, plan changes happen in Stripe&rsquo;s billing
portal. Click **Manage subscription** on your account page and choose the
new plan there. Clicking **Subscribe** on the pricing page takes you to the
same place.

## Managing billing

Click **Manage subscription** on your account page to open Stripe&rsquo;s
billing portal. From there you can:

- Change your plan.
- Update your payment method.
- Download invoices.
- Cancel.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current period. Until then, the
panel works normally on your current plan, and your account page shows the
date it is scheduled to end. You can resume any time before that date
through **Manage subscription**.

When a plan ends, the panel shows **Your plan has ended** with a **See
plans** button. Your listings, settings and cloud data stay in your account,
ready for when you subscribe again.

## What if a payment fails?

If a payment fails, your account page shows **Payment failed** and the
panel&rsquo;s **Membership** card shows **Past due**. To fix it, click
**Manage subscription** and update your card.

- **If you have paid for SaleLinx before**, you keep full access for 7 days
  while you sort it out. If the payment still hasn&rsquo;t gone through after
  7 days, the panel locks until it does.
- **If the first charge after your free trial fails**, there is no grace
  period. The panel locks straight away until a payment goes through.

## Account security

The **Security** card on your account page handles your password and email
address. Both changes are confirmed by a link we email to you.

- **Password**: click **Send reset link** and follow the link to set a new
  password. If you signed up with Google, your account has no password yet.
  Click **Send set-password link** to add one, so you can also sign in with
  your email and password.
- **Email**: click **Change email**, enter the new address and click **Send
  confirmation**. The change finishes when you click the link in the email.

The **Email preferences** card lets you turn tips and occasional offers on
or off. Account emails, such as receipts, password resets and replies to
your support tickets, are always sent.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of your account page, click **Delete account**. Enter your
password (accounts that use Google sign-in skip this step) and click **Send
confirmation email**. Nothing is deleted until you open the link in that
email, within 60 minutes, and click **Permanently delete my account**.

Deleting removes your listings, images, linked marketplace accounts, usage
history and support tickets. Any active subscription is canceled
immediately, with no refund for time left in the billing period. This
cannot be undone.

## Settings presets follow your account

Several tabs in the panel let you save their settings as named presets:
[Grow](/docs/inventory/bots), [Refresh](/docs/inventory/refresh),
[Relist](/docs/inventory/relist) and the **Auto-Accept Offers** controls in
[Offers](/docs/buyers/offers). Each ships with two built-in presets,
**Default** and **Careful**, and the ones you save yourself are stored
with your SaleLinx account rather than only in the browser. Syncing is part
of every plan, including the trial; without an active plan your presets
stay on the device you saved them on, and upload the next time a plan is
active. While a plan is active:

- **They survive a reinstall** and appear on a second computer once you
  sign in there.
- **If two devices disagree**, the most recently changed list wins,
  separately for each tab.
- **Presets you saved before syncing existed** are merged in by name
  rather than lost.
- **Which preset is selected stays per device.** Picking one on your
  laptop does not change the selection on your desktop.
- **Signing out clears presets from that browser**, so the next person to
  sign in there does not inherit yours. They come back the next time you
  sign in.

View presets in the **My Listings** tab (saved filter and sort
combinations) are a separate, older feature. They stay on the device and do
not sync.
