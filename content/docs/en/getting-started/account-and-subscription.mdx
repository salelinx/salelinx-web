# Account and subscription

Your SaleLinx login covers both the website and the extension. The same email
and password sign you in to billing on salelinx.com and to the panel inside
Chrome.

## Where to find it

- **Website** &mdash; your account page is at [salelinx.com/account](/account).
- **Extension** &mdash; the **Settings** tab in the side panel mirrors the
  same plan info and links straight to the billing portal.

## Plans at a glance

Plans are tiered by what features you unlock and what monthly caps you get.
The current pricing and what each tier includes lives on the
[pricing page](/features#pricing). Changes there are the source of truth: the
extension reads its limits from the same place you see them.

## Upgrading

1. Open [the pricing page](/features#pricing) (or click **See pricing** from
   anywhere in the panel).
2. Click **Subscribe** on the tier you want.
3. Stripe Checkout opens in a new tab.
4. Complete payment.

Your account upgrades immediately. You don&rsquo;t need to reload the
extension &mdash; the next time the panel checks your subscription (within
about a minute), the new caps and features become available.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Mid-month upgrades">
Stripe pro-rates the difference, so you only pay for the days remaining in
the current period. The next full bill arrives on your usual renewal date.
</Callout>

## Switching plans

To move between paid plans (e.g. Pro to Business), use the same Subscribe
button on the new tier. Stripe handles the swap and the proration.

To downgrade, open **Manage subscription** (button on your account page),
which takes you to the Stripe billing portal. Choose **Update plan** and pick
the smaller tier. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current period
&mdash; you keep the higher tier until then so you don&rsquo;t lose what
you&rsquo;ve already paid for.

## Managing billing

Click **Manage subscription** on your account page. The Stripe customer
portal opens in a new tab. From there you can:

- Update your payment method.
- Download invoices.
- Change your billing email.
- Cancel.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current period. Until then, the
panel works normally on whatever plan you were on. You can resume any time
before the end date by re-opening the same portal.

## Account security

The **Security** card on the account page handles password resets and email
changes. Both flow through a confirmation link we send to your inbox &mdash;
SaleLinx never asks you to confirm changes inside the panel itself.

## What if a payment fails?

Stripe retries failed payments a few times. While retries are pending, the
panel shows a banner and your access stays active. If all retries fail, your
account drops to the Free tier and the banner switches to a prompt to update
your payment method.

## Free tier and limits

Free is for trying things out, not for daily use. The monthly caps (25
crosslists, 25 relists, 50 refreshes per day) reset on the first of each
calendar month. Free does not include the heavier features &mdash;
auto-offers, restocker, shipping labels, account linking, price drops &mdash;
those start on a paid plan.
