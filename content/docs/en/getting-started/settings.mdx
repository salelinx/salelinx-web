# Settings

**Settings** is pinned to the bottom of the panel&rsquo;s sidebar, below
**Support** and **Refer &amp; Earn**, so you can always reach it. It holds
your SaleLinx account, the Depop, Vinted and eBay accounts you&rsquo;ve
linked, your plan and how much of it you&rsquo;ve used, which marketplaces
you sell on, and a couple of display options.

A few quick controls (language, theme, Pop out) live in the panel header
instead. They&rsquo;re covered at the end of this page.

## SaleLinx account

The first section, **SaleLinx account**, shows the email you&rsquo;re signed
in with and two buttons:

- **Manage account on salelinx.com** opens your account page on the
  website, where you change your password or email and manage billing.
- **Log Out** signs you out of the extension after a confirmation.

Logging out also cancels everything in the [queue](/docs/getting-started/queue)
and clears its history, and removes the listings and saved presets SaleLinx
kept in this browser, so the next person to sign in here can&rsquo;t see
them. Your presets and cloud backups stay with your account and come back
when you sign in again.

## Marketplace accounts

**Marketplace accounts** has a row each for Depop, Vinted and eBay. The
Depop and Vinted rows show the linked shop&rsquo;s @username and a status:

- **Connected** - the shop is linked, or you&rsquo;re signed in there.
- **Disconnected** or **Session expired** - SaleLinx can&rsquo;t see you
  signed in there. An **Open Depop** (or **Open Vinted**) button appears
  next to the status; sign in on the site that opens and the row recovers.

If you&rsquo;re signed in but haven&rsquo;t linked the shop yet, the row
reads **Not linked** and offers to link it.

The eBay row works differently (see The eBay row below). Rows for
marketplaces you&rsquo;ve switched off (see Marketplaces below) are hidden.

In version 1.1.11 and earlier, this section and the one above it are headed
**Accounts** and **Account**.

### Linking an account

Linking tells SaleLinx which shop belongs to your SaleLinx account. To link
one:

1. Sign in on [depop.com](https://www.depop.com) or
   [vinted.com](https://www.vinted.com) in a normal tab.
2. Open the SaleLinx panel on that site and go to **Settings**.
3. Under the marketplace&rsquo;s row you&rsquo;ll see &ldquo;Link
   @yourname (Depop) to your cloud?&rdquo;. Click **Link**.

**Not now** hides the prompt for now.

You can only link Depop or Vinted from a page on that marketplace. From a
Depop tab, the Vinted row asks you to open Vinted and log in there, and the
other way round. The full first-time walkthrough is in
[Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace).

If linking fails, the row says why. The common reasons:

- **No login found.** Sign in on the marketplace and try again.
- **The shop is already linked to a different SaleLinx account.** Sign in
  to that SaleLinx account, unlink it there, then come back.
- **The shop has already used a free trial on another SaleLinx account.**
  Start a paid plan to link it here.

### Signed in as someone else

If the marketplace tab is signed in to a different shop from the one you
linked, a banner at the top of Settings says so. That marketplace&rsquo;s
features stay unavailable until you switch accounts on the marketplace, or
unlink and link the shop you&rsquo;re now using.

### Unlinking

Click **Unlink** on the row and confirm. Unlinking removes that
marketplace&rsquo;s listings from SaleLinx in this browser. Nothing changes
on the marketplace itself.

If you have listings from that shop saved in your cloud backup, the
confirmation offers a choice:

- **Unlink, keep cloud backup** - unlink, but leave the backups in your
  account, ready if you link again.
- **Unlink and delete backup** - unlink and also delete that shop&rsquo;s
  cloud backups, photos included.

### The eBay row

eBay has no browser session for SaleLinx to check, so its row shows your
eBay username with **Linked**, or a **Link** button. Linking opens a small
window where you sign in on eBay&rsquo;s own page, and works from any page
the panel is open on. See
[Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace).

Once eBay is linked, the row also has:

- **Seller type** - **Private** or **Business**. SaleLinx reads it from
  eBay; pick the other one if it&rsquo;s wrong. It decides what the eBay
  listing editor in [My Listings](/docs/inventory/listings) offers:
  business sellers can set an auto-accept price for offers, and private
  sellers on eBay UK see what the buyer will pay with eBay&rsquo;s Buyer
  Protection fee.
- **Postage** - the postage, returns and item postcode SaleLinx adds to
  every listing it [crosslists](/docs/inventory/crosslist) to eBay. The row
  shows what&rsquo;s saved, **From your eBay business policies**, or **Asked
  for on your first crosslist**. **Edit** opens the form. What you save is
  stored with your SaleLinx account.

## Account info

**Account info** shows your **User ID** with a **Copy** button, in case
support asks for it.

## Membership

The **Membership** card shows:

- **Your plan**: **Free trial**, **Starter**, **Pro** or **Business**.
- **Its status**: **Trial**, **Active**, **Past due** or **Canceled**.
- **A date**: when the trial ends, when the plan renews, or, if
  you&rsquo;ve cancelled, when it ends.
- **Manage**, which opens your account page on salelinx.com to change plan,
  update your card or cancel.

While you&rsquo;re on the free trial, a countdown in the panel header also
shows how long is left. Click it to open the pricing page.

### Usage meters

Under the plan, one row for each allowance on your plan shows how much
you&rsquo;ve used, your limit and how many are left:

- **Crosslists this month**
- **Relists this month**
- **Refreshes today**
- **Follows today**
- **Unfollows today**
- **Cloud storage** (on plans that include cloud backups)

A row reads **Unlimited** when your plan has no cap on it. The bar changes
colour as you get close to the limit.

The &ldquo;today&rdquo; allowances reset at midnight UTC. In the UK
that&rsquo;s midnight in winter and 1am during British Summer Time. The
&ldquo;this month&rdquo; allowances reset on the first of each calendar
month (UTC), not on your billing date.

When you run out, SaleLinx shows an upgrade prompt, and tasks already
waiting in the [queue](/docs/getting-started/queue) are put on hold until
the allowance resets. The limits for each plan are on the
[pricing page](/pricing). Billing, upgrades and cancelling are covered in
[Account and subscription](/docs/getting-started/account-and-subscription).

## Marketplaces

**Marketplaces** sets where you sell: &ldquo;Choose where you sell. We hide
everything for the marketplaces you skip.&rdquo; There is one button for
each of Depop, Vinted and eBay, showing its logo (hover over one to see its
name). Click a button to switch that marketplace on or off, then click
**Apply**. Any mix works, as long as at least one stays on.

Turning a marketplace off:

- Hides its tabs, filters and account row. **Refresh** and **Shop
  Designer** are Depop-only tabs, and **Auto-Offers** is Vinted-only.
- Stops its background work, and switches off its schedules: Auto-refresh
  when Depop is off, and automatic offer accepting for that marketplace.
- Can leave **Crosslist** and **Linking** in the sidebar, but locked.
  Crosslist needs two marketplaces (any two of the three), and Linking
  needs Depop and Vinted. Each explains why and offers a **Change
  marketplaces** button that brings you back here.

With only eBay switched on, the sidebar shows **My Listings**,
**Crosslist** (locked until you switch on a second marketplace),
**Settings**, **Support** and **Refer &amp; Earn**, with a **Coming soon**
list of the Depop and Vinted features that don&rsquo;t work with eBay yet.

Your choice is saved to your SaleLinx account. SaleLinx asks the same
question (&ldquo;Where do you sell?&rdquo;) the first time you sign in. If
you chose your marketplaces before SaleLinx supported eBay, eBay starts
switched off, and a one-time pop-up says eBay is here. Its **Go to
Settings** button opens this tab.

If you open the panel on a Depop or Vinted page you&rsquo;ve turned off, a
banner offers **Turn on Depop** (or **Turn on Vinted**) and **Dismiss**.

## Display

- **Page animations** switches the moving scenes at the top of each tab on
  or off. Turn them off if the panel feels slow. This setting is saved in
  this browser only.
- **Panel shortcut** shows the keyboard shortcut that opens and closes the
  panel (**Alt+S** unless you&rsquo;ve changed it, or **Not set**). Chrome
  controls extension shortcuts, so **Change** opens Chrome&rsquo;s shortcuts
  page, where you can pick a different key. The shortcut only works on a
  Depop or Vinted tab.

## Controls in the panel header

These sit in the top-right corner of the panel on every tab:

- Language (the flag): the panel is available in English, French,
  Spanish and German. You can also switch language from the menu that
  appears when you hover over the SaleLinx launcher while the panel is
  closed.
- Tutorial mode (the &ldquo;i&rdquo; icon): hides or shows the info
  cards at the top of each tab. Turning it back on also brings back any
  cards you closed one by one.
- Theme (moon or sun): switches between light and dark. Light is the
  default.
- Log out: the same as **Log Out** in Settings.
- **Pop out**: shrinks the panel into a window you can drag by its header
  and resize from any edge or corner, so the page underneath stays usable.
  Click **Fill screen** to go back to full size. SaleLinx remembers which
  you used last, and the window&rsquo;s size and position.
- Close (&times;): closes the panel. Anything in the queue keeps
  running.

## The other bottom tabs

- **Refer &amp; Earn** gives you a link to share. Your friend gets a
  discount, and you earn free time on your plan when they subscribe.
- **Support** is where you report a bug or send feedback to the SaleLinx
  team.
