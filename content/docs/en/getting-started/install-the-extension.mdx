# Installing the SaleLinx extension

SaleLinx is a Chrome extension that works inside your Depop and Vinted tabs.
Install it once, pin it to your toolbar, and sign in with your SaleLinx
account.

You&rsquo;ll need Google Chrome or another Chromium browser (Microsoft Edge,
Brave, Arc or Opera). Safari and Firefox are not supported.

## 1. Install from the Chrome Web Store

<InstallExtensionButton label="Add to Chrome" className="my-6 inline-flex items-center gap-2 rounded-full bg-zinc-900 px-5 py-2.5 text-sm font-medium text-white shadow-[0_1px_0_rgba(255,255,255,0.08)_inset,0_10px_30px_-12px_rgba(0,0,0,0.45)] transition hover:bg-zinc-800 dark:bg-white dark:text-zinc-900 dark:hover:bg-zinc-100" />

Or open the [SaleLinx listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/salelinx/oichfmfogobecihmhlgkfcfbdceomenj) in the Chrome Web Store and click **Add to Chrome**.
Chrome will ask you to confirm the permissions the extension needs. Click
**Add extension** to continue.

<Callout variant="tip" title="What the permissions mean">
SaleLinx asks for access to Depop and to the Vinted country sites it
supports. It uses that access to read your own listings, orders and
messages, and to list, edit and relist items for you through the same
connections the marketplace&rsquo;s own website uses. It also talks to a few
supporting services: the SaleLinx cloud (your account and backups), the
marketplaces&rsquo; photo servers, and the shipping label provider. The
SaleLinx panel only appears on Depop and Vinted pages.
</Callout>

## 2. Pin the extension

After install, Chrome hides new extensions behind the puzzle icon in the
toolbar. Pin SaleLinx so you can reach it with one click.

<InstallPinCallout />

## 3. Sign in

Click the pinned SaleLinx icon. A small window opens where you can sign in
with **Continue with Google**, or with your email and password and **Log
in**. If you don&rsquo;t have an account yet, click **Sign up** to create
one right there. If you sign up with an email address, confirm it from the
email we send, then come back and log in.

You can also sign in from the panel itself: if you open it on Depop or
Vinted while signed out, it shows the same sign-in options.

Once you are signed in, the window shows which account you are using, with
buttons to open Depop or Vinted, an **Account &amp; billing** link to your
account page on salelinx.com, and **Log out**.

<Callout>
The website and the extension use the same account, but you sign in to each
separately. If you reset your password on the website, use the new password
in the extension too.
</Callout>

## 4. Open the panel

Go to [depop.com](https://www.depop.com) or your Vinted site. A small
SaleLinx button sits in the bottom-right corner of the page. Click it to
open the panel. The panel lives on Depop and Vinted pages only; it does not
open from the toolbar icon.

If you already had Depop or Vinted tabs open before installing, reload them
once so the button appears.

There&rsquo;s also a keyboard shortcut: **Alt+S** opens and closes the panel
while you are on a Depop or Vinted tab. It does nothing on other sites. If
another extension already uses Alt+S, set your own shortcut under
`chrome://extensions/shortcuts`, or open **Settings** in the panel and click
**Change** next to **Panel shortcut**.

## 5. Your first run

The first time you open the panel, it walks you through two screens:

1. **Welcome to SaleLinx.** If your account doesn&rsquo;t have a plan yet,
   click **Start free trial**. This opens the pricing page on salelinx.com,
   where you start your 14-day trial (sign in there with the same account
   if asked; a card is required, but nothing is charged during the trial).
   When you&rsquo;re done, come back to the panel and click **Already have a
   plan? Refresh**.
2. **Link your shops.** Under **I sell on**, pick Depop, Vinted or both,
   then click **Link** next to each account. If a row shows **Log in**
   instead, you are not signed in to that marketplace in this browser yet.

After that the panel opens on **My Listings**. The full linking walkthrough
is in [Connect your first marketplace](/docs/getting-started/connect-your-first-marketplace).
